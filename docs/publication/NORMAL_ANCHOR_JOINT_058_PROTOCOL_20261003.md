# 058 normal-anchored joint model: prospective development readout

The 019 model has a useful correct-pose tissue mapper but still selects the wrong plane too often (2.591 mm identity-equal mapped error; 1.166 mm best-eight rigid oracle on the 185-section synthetic DEV panel). The frozen 057 control showed that atlas patch-bank geometry degrades local matching, while even an exact rigid true-location patch scores below distant lookalikes. More patch-bank density alone is therefore not the next training strategy.

Continue 019 batch 18,000 within the same randomly initialized model lineage. Preserve its 16 pose modes, encoder, atlas-conditioned mapper and fitting-to-pose gradient, and add 64 fixed antipodal plane-normal anchors with learned tangent/roll, position, image-scale and reflection residuals. Predict the new branches from the existing multiscale whole-image feature map at 8×8, not from an atlas patch catalogue. Use synthetic physical pose and tissue-map supervision plus a differentiable second finite-thickness atlas render so fitted physical error and candidate ranking update the pose head. Two near-true normal branches and an exact-pose mapper are TRAIN-only aids; inference selects solely from predicted probabilities and fitted scores. This is motivated by the out-of-plane/in-plane factorization and differentiable resampling in [Zheng et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2023.121132), but that MRI method does not establish histology performance.

Train 12,000 batches of two independently drawn arbitrary-plane synthetic sections and one real TRAIN section, maintaining one image/background/artifact draw per physical synthetic plane. Real sections are unique within this run. Previously used TRAIN sections may recur across experiments only after the never-before-used pool is exhausted; the exact counts and animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs are recorded. No external pretrained features, legacy weights or pseudolabels enter.

After the trainer exits, evaluate checkpoints 0, 2,000, 6,000 and 12,000 on the unchanged 185 eligible synthetic sections from eight held-out deformation identities and 64 weak-reference real sections from six separate donors. Primary development endpoint: identity-equal selected physical tissue-to-atlas error. Also report best-eight rigid pose capture, plane-normal angle, exact-pose mapper error and weak-real donor disagreement. A useful continuation requires selected mapped error ≤2.1 mm (at least 0.49 mm below 019), best-eight rigid error ≤0.9 mm, exact-pose mapper error ≤0.2 mm and no >0.2 mm worst-donor weak-real regression. These are development gates, not biological-animal validation or shipping thresholds. The Allen real references are weak affines; synthetic identities are not independent animals. Do not use final-test animals, the DeepSlice public benchmark or calibrated-probability language here.
