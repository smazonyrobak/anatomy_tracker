# 305 acquired-fluorescence capture: bounded matched TRAIN test

The 305 joint selector failed on its frozen synthetic TRAIN-inner panel, and its scratch pose checkpoint has only 9/240 top-16 candidates within 1.5 mm of approximate VisuAlign references on four Lupori TRAIN animals. The already completed 185 label preflight found good mask/atlas-support overlap after checking the QuickNII axis convention; do not repeat that audit or treat the human planes as exact biological truth. The 187 scratch-lineage result shows that acquired fluorescence can supply useful pose supervision, but its weights cannot initialize 305.

Start both arms from the identical randomly initialized-project-lineage 305 pose step-26,000 weights, with fresh identical AdamW states and seeds. Optimize only the shared encoder/lateral features and direct/anchor pose heads; keep the map, atlas pair, refinement, and uncertainty heads frozen. Each of 600 batches uses two freshly and independently sampled full-sphere 305 synthetic sections from TRAIN bases 0–55, plus one valid Lupori fluorescence section. Both arms use identical cases, augmentations, site draws, learning-rate schedule and synthetic loss. The control gives the real case zero loss; the adaptation arm adds one weak-reference probabilistic physical-pose loss at weight 1.0. The mask selects supervised tissue sites but is never an input channel. No physical synthetic plane is deliberately repeated with changed appearance; chance collisions are neither sought nor rejected.

Use only `CC2B`, `CC4B`, and `CC4A` for acquired gradients. Keep all 52 valid `AL1A` sections as an inner TRAIN animal guard; do not use its labels or images in optimizer steps. At steps 0/200/400/600, record first-choice and top-16-oracle five-site gap to the approximate AL1A human plane, unsigned normal gap, and the unchanged 64-case synthetic full-sphere TRAIN-inner pose panel (including >=45-degree and <10%-visible strata). Compare both arms on the same sections. The primary step-600 advancement gate is an AL1A first-choice mean reduction of at least 1.0 mm **and** 25% versus control, with synthetic full-panel first-choice mean no more than 0.20 mm worse and top-16 capture no more than five cases lower. Any improvement remains a weak-reference TRAIN-only result, not measured electrode accuracy, arbitrary-oblique physical validation, or a calibrated probability. All checkpoints and raw rows stay on I:; independently audit completion before selecting a later training direction. Do not touch official DEV/CAL/FINAL animals or a public DeepSlice benchmark.
