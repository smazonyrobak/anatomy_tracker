# 097: make the existing matcher discriminate anatomy, not merely pose geometry

Freeze this before the next TRAIN draw or 097 DEV score. The 096 read-only control showed that the 094 learned local matches reduced the best candidate's truth-correct tissue sites from 0.892 at its unshifted plane to 0.605 after matching; the unshifted plane also ranked candidates slightly better (plan-equal ρ 0.816 versus 0.791). In 094, the descriptor received only 5e-6 learning rate amid joint pose/warp losses, and its single nearest-bin cross-entropy supplied no anatomical-negative gradient when truth was outside a wrong candidate's local search. Another quality readout is not the next bottleneck.

Continue the same scratch-only 094 checkpoint lineage. For this isolated stage, freeze its image encoder, direct probabilistic pose, fitter, local map, verifier and correlation temperature. Use a separate immutable copy of 094 to produce fitted states; update *only* the existing 083 image and atlas descriptor projections with a candidate-contrastive anatomical objective. This is internal staged training of the intended model, not a separately sourced model or a deployable checkpoint.

Train for exactly 4,000 accepted independently randomized eligible synthetic TRAIN sections with one independently sampled geometry and appearance per section, without deliberately reusing a physical section in altered background. Form the unchanged blind 8-old/6-anchor beam. Choose four existing branches using 094's recorded rule: rigid-physical best; nearest-support branch ≥0.5 mm worse; highest-prior branch ≥1 mm worse; one distinct random remaining branch, with documented fallbacks. Synthetic true CCF coordinates enter TRAIN target construction only. For each visible 32×32 source site, consider the 225 fine atlas bins in each chosen branch after frozen fitting. A supported bin within 1.5 mm of the exact observed-site CCF coordinate is a positive, a supported bin at least 2.0 mm away is a negative, and bins between are ignored. Positives may occur in more than one candidate; never mislabel a physically matching bin as a negative because its branch is nominally wrong. Use a numerically stable multi-positive log-sum-exp contrastive loss across the four candidate banks on raw normalized descriptor similarity, with the fixed atlas-support penalty removed and unsupported cells masked. Average over visible sites with both positive and negative bins. Keep the atlas-support policy and finite-thickness rendering unchanged. Record the accepted-section IDs, donor/subject-plan provenance, draw attempts, source hashes, eligible-site counts, loss and hard-negative margin; save initial and fixed step-4,000 checkpoints on I:.

Before TRAIN, freeze an independent evaluator. On the exact 096 182-section complementary synthetic DEV panel and its 14 unchanged fitted 094 branches, recompute learned-match correctness with the frozen 097 descriptor and the same centre-bin geometry null, visible denominator, support condition and 1.5-mm tolerance. Verify step-0 parity with 096. Report plan-equal learned-minus-centre correct fraction, best-candidate matched versus centre fraction, centre-wrong recoveries, centre-correct corruptions, pairwise positive/negative similarity margins, and raw/black/brush plus atlas-support strata. The focused gate is: learned-minus-centre fraction >0 overall and in at least six of eight plans, and physically best matched fraction ≥the unchanged centre-bin 0.892. Do not select checkpoints on DEV or use oracle fraction/error rank correlation alone as a success metric. If the gate fails while TRAIN contrastive loss falls, investigate descriptor context/capacity and domain mismatch before feeding match quality back into pose. If it passes, jointly continue this *same* model and test whether anatomical-fit feedback improves selected pose, warp and point mapping on a fresh synthetic DEV panel before real animal-separated development. This stage does not authorize GUI promotion, probability calibration or public DeepSlice benchmarking.
