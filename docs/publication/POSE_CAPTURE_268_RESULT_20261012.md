# 268: scratch arbitrary-plane proposal capture improves, but fails its gate

The frozen `_002` run completed 8,000 optimizer batches of four independently drawn v8 synthetic TRAIN sections (32,000 accepted; 37,313 attempts). It initialized the 80-mode image-to-pose model randomly and trained only its image encoder and pose heads. The 64-case TRAIN-inner panel, eight cases from each of eight held-out synthetic deformation bases, was frozen before initialization. No prior weights/features/pseudolabels, acquired images, animal CAL/FINAL data, or public benchmark data were used. The failed `_001` preflight contains no trained checkpoint and is not part of this readout.

The blind model ranks 160 pose/reflection branches from the slice image alone. “Top-16 hit” means at least one of its first 16 branches maps sampled valid tissue sites within 1.5 mm of their synthetic CCF target. This is a *candidate-capture* test, not a usable alignment or electrode-localization accuracy claim.

At 8,000 batches, only **5/64 first choices** meet the 1.5-mm site threshold. The first-choice median is 2.945 mm; its mean antipodal plane-normal error is 43.24°, with 30/64 above 45°. Of the 33 top-16 misses, 15 have a near-enough branch somewhere among all 160 but are ranked outside the first 16; the other 18 have no branch within 1.5 mm. Thus both proposal generation and selection remain inadequate. Increasing the image-only beam to 32/64/160 branches captures 41/44/46 cases, respectively. The low-visible-tissue stratum (<15% of the image) has only 3/11 top-16 hits and 5.177-mm mean first-choice error. The >45°-from-cardinal stratum has 3/6 hits, too few for a strong angle-specific conclusion. All eight held-out bases improved their top-16 count over initialization. No selected minor FOV was ≤1.2 mm, so the earlier catastrophic span-collapse symptom is absent here.

The prespecified pass conditions were ≥45/64 top-16 hits at batch 8,000, improvement in at least six bases, median minor FOV ratio ≥0.5, and at most four selected FOVs ≤1.2 mm. **Only the first failed (31/64); the whole gate failed.** The model must not be promoted or extended unchanged. A post-exit, read-only counterfactual using the *existing* composite training cost to rank its fixed branches captures all 46 site-oracle cases within 16 and puts 44 at rank one. That rules out the suspected composite-vs-site target mismatch as the immediate ranking bottleneck; changing only that target would be an unmotivated experiment. The image-only network has not learned to infer that anatomical cost. The next decision should test whether actual atlas intensity (against support-only and shifted-intensity controls) adds information for ranking the 41 site-capturing top-32 proposals, while separately addressing the 18 cases with no near proposal. Sparse visible tissue and only 56 synthetic training bases remain plausible coverage limits, not established causes.
