# Frozen protocol — candidate-aligned oriented-slab ranker 299

## Question and scientific boundary

The frozen 275 pose model often includes a physically near plane in its blind top 16, but does not reliably rank it first. The 296/298 image-correlation score failed even on clean exact atlas-derived images. Can a learned source-to-atlas *spatially coherent* match score extract candidate-specific anatomy beyond support silhouette? This is a TRAIN-inner synthetic mechanism test, not real-histology validation, a calibrated probability model, a deployable replacement, or a DeepSlice comparison.

The design draws on oriented 2D/3D patch matching and geometric consensus in [SLIV-Reg](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18683v1), but does not borrow its weights, features, data, or claims. Here the atlas is explicitly re-rendered in each frozen candidate plane with the known 9-point finite-thickness PSF. All neural weights in the 299 ranker start randomly. The 275 proposer is frozen, including all candidate states, reflections, and prior log scores. No atlas registration or local deformation is updated in this pilot.

## Frozen design

- Use 1,200 independently drawn and accepted v9 sections from synthetic TRAIN base animals 0–55. Each draw randomizes plane, subject variant, appearance, brush availability, and artifacts independently; no paired black/nonblack or deliberately repeated plane. Refit the observable section's affine pose gauge only for synthetic truth measurement. Do not use the new eight TRAIN-inner readout bases 56–63 for gradients.
- Freeze a new 64-section TRAIN-inner panel: eight draws from each base 56–63, generated before gradients. Store the full 16-way blind beam and all atlas banks before any ranker training. No checkpoint selection on this panel; read only at step 1,200.
- For each candidate render five adjacent atlas planes at normal offsets −500, −250, 0, +250, +500 µm with that section's 9-point PSF, atlas intensity, and support. The 48×48 atlas lattice is exactly centred on 4×4 source-pixel blocks. The observed 192×192 five-channel input is averaged into those blocks before a fresh source encoder. A separate fresh atlas encoder produces 32-D normalized features. An image-only reliability head estimates which source sites survived. Its training target is surviving non-displaced tissue; no truth mask enters inference.
- Compare 64 spatially stratified source sites with candidate-aligned atlas features. A single through-plane offset and single 2-D shift within ±2 feature cells apply to all sites together. Unsupported atlas sites receive a penalty in the fixed source-weight denominator. The score is a soft maximum over this small shared-rigid hypothesis set, never a per-site free warp, support-count top-k, or unconstrained pose adjustment.
- Train near (≤1.5 mm rigid physical-site error) against a prior-preferred far candidate that is ≥2 mm, ≥1 mm worse, and within 0.10 atlas central-support fraction of the near candidate. Include top-first and a random far candidate when eligible. Use direct pairwise evidence and frozen-prior-plus-4×evidence margin losses, plus a 0.10-weight source-reliability loss. The fixed 4× coefficient is used unchanged in both arms and every readout. AdamW 1e-4, clipping 3, fixed step 1,200. No step-based selection or adaptive tuning from this panel.
- Matched null: an identical ranker with identical initial weights, draws, pairs, optimizer settings and source input, but atlas intensity set to zero while atlas support remains. A full-atlas advantage over this arm is required. No external weights, pretrained descriptors, pseudolabels, reserved real animals, DEV/CAL/FINAL cases, or public benchmark examples enter training or readout.
- Hard stop after two wall-clock hours; an interrupted/failed run does not count as a scientific result and must not be resumed by silently changing the frozen protocol. Store all draws, panel, train losses, all 16 evidence scores per case, source/checkpoint/input hashes, and raw readout rows on I:.

## Predeclared conjunctive gate

On the 64 new sections, require (1) at least 30 support-matched near/far pairs; (2) full-atlas evidence chooses the near candidate in at least 75% of those pairs; (3) this exceeds the independently trained support-only rate by at least ten percentage points; (4) full-atlas selected mean rigid physical-site error improves by at least 0.20 mm over frozen prior-first and by at least 0.15 mm over support-only; (5) at least six of eight base-animal means are non-worse than frozen prior-first. Report no-brush/brush and ≥45°/other-angle strata, failures, and oracle-best top-16 error even if the gate fails. The selected candidate is **not** permission to move tissue or assert electrode probabilities.

If the gate passes, the next stage is a separate acquired-TRAIN weak-family guard and fresh animal-separated, physically annotated oblique validation before considering any model integration. If it fails, do not scale this ranker unchanged. Neither outcome alone proves arbitrary-plane registration impossible: synthetic intensity may lack sufficient modality realism, and no independently ground-truthed physical steep-oblique cohort exists yet.
