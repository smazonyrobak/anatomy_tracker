# Substantial same-model exposure continuation 019

The 018 continuation improved frozen selected synthetic mapping only from 2.789 to 2.734 mm after 8,000 batches; its descriptor loss decreased but the fitted score/error correlation did not improve. This is not a successful model. At the same time, it has seen far fewer distinct arbitrary-plane examples than the [DeepSlice training scale](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-41645-4), so a saturation claim would be premature. The next test is **exposure, not another architectural gadget**.

Continue the complete randomly initialized-lineage model from 018 batch 8,000, including its optimizer moments and RNG state. Keep direct probabilistic pose, finite-thickness atlas rendering, atlas-conditioned tissue map, two-pass fitted pose correction, and the fitted-score gradient into global pose. Keep the paired anatomy descriptor loss and ranking weights unchanged. Halve the continuation learning rates to reduce the early regression seen after the 018 optimizer restart. Train 30,000 further batches with two independently sampled arbitrary-plane synthetic sections per batch (60,000 presentations) and one previously unused TRAIN real section per batch. At protocol freeze, the reserved TRAIN pool had 263,754 images across 1,885 donors; 233,159 unique sections appeared in prior pinned schedules, leaving 30,595 available for this non-replay stage. The training schedule must reject every prior real schedule and be unique within this stage. Preserve all specimen/experiment/animal IDs and input-source hashes. All data, caches and outputs remain on `I:`.

After the run exits, evaluate every 2,000-batch checkpoint on the unchanged 185-case arbitrary-plane synthetic DEV panel and 64-section/six-donor real DEV panel. Use identity-equal physical mapping error and real weak Allen affine five-point error. The desired change is at least 0.25 mm lower selected mapping error than 018's 2.734 mm, correct-pose map below 0.2 mm, and no >0.2 mm real weak-label regression. These are development criteria only. Preserve any smaller honest improvement, but do not waive failure into a deployment claim. Do not use the DeepSlice Ground Truth benchmark, calibration animals or final-test animals during checkpoint selection.
