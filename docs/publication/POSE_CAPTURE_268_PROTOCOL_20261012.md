# 268: scratch full-sphere pose-capture stage

Train a fresh-random `OneShotJointSliceModel(modes=16, normal_anchor_count=64)` on v8 synthetic TRAIN sections only. No project or external checkpoint, pretrained features, real affine label, pseudolabel, DEV/CAL/FINAL animal or public benchmark enters this stage. Optimize the shared image encoder, lateral pose features, direct 16-mode head and 64 anchor heads; leave warp, atlas matcher, uncertainty and other non-pose modules untrained. The output is an **uncalibrated** mixture over 80 full-frame poses and two horizontal-reflection branches, not a deployed joint model.

Before the first optimizer batch, freeze eight independently drawn eligible v8 sections from each synthetic TRAIN deformation base 56–63 (64 distinct physical-section IDs) in an I:-drive panel. Draw from bases 0–55 for gradients only. Every accepted example has one independently sampled physical plane, warp, damage and exterior style; do not deliberately pair a plane with a second appearance. Record rejected attempts, full v8 provenance, panel tensors and hashes. The virtual variants of one base are not independent biological animals. Preserve the v8 full-sphere normal/roll/offset sampler and its optional smart-brush input.

Use 192×192 input, batch four, at most 8,000 optimizer batches (32,000 accepted presentations). Follow 154's pose-capture curriculum: valid fraction ≥0.12 for batches 1–2,000 at AdamW `1e-4`, ≥0.08 for 2,001–4,000 at `7e-5`, then any v8-eligible section for 4,001–8,000 at `5e-5`; weight decay `1e-4`, gradient clip 2. For each section sample 128 valid observed pixels. At pixel index `(x,y)`, chart coordinates are `(x/192,y/192)`; a horizontal reflection maps x to `191/192 − x/192`. Five fixed sites are the four pixel centres at `(0,0)`, `(191,0)`, `(0,191)`, `(191,191)` and the raster centre `(95.5,95.5)`, all divided by 192. Adapt 154's differentiable 75% valid-site/25% five-site physical distance, unsigned normal penalty `4 mm × (1−|cos θ|)`, mixture/soft-target KL, free-mode minimum and four-nearest-anchor supervision to this convention. This stage trains pose heads only; it does not predict or qualify a local deformation field.

Save scratch step 0 and readouts/checkpoints at batches 2,000, 4,000 and 8,000 on the same frozen inner panel. For every case rank all 160 pose/reflection branches **blindly** by the model's mixture/reflection probabilities, then record best-of-top-16 within 1.5 mm, top-one mean physical-site rigid error, all-branch oracle, selected normal-axis error and selected/true minor FOV. Also report equal-base and visible-tissue fraction (<0.15, 0.15–0.40, ≥0.40) and nearest-cardinal-angle (≤20°, 20–45°, >45°) strata. The 1.5-mm endpoint uses synthetic target rigid pose at valid pixel sites, not post-warp local tissue-to-CCF error. Select a trained checkpoint lexicographically by more blind top-16 hits, then lower equal-base mean top-one rigid site error. Step 0 is a baseline, not selectable.

The continuation gate is evaluated specifically at batch 8,000: blind top-16 capture ≥45/64; capture improves versus step 0 in at least six of eight held-out synthetic bases; median of per-case selected/true minor-FOV ratios ≥0.5; and no more than four selected minor FOVs ≤1.2 mm. If any condition fails, record failure and do not extend this unchanged recipe. If it passes and the inner curves are still improving, a separately predeclared scratch-lineage continuation can be considered; 32,000 presentations are a checkpoint, not a claim of sufficiency. Passing is only a TRAIN-inner proposal-capture signal. It is not animal-level accuracy, calibrated uncertainty, deformation success, GUI readiness or authorization for DeepSlice benchmarking. Keep the panel, draw IDs, source/config/protocol hashes, all checkpoints and completion receipt on I:.
