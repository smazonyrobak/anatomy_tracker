# Blind local-pose information gate before another joint updater

The 305 top-16 beam contains a ≤1.5-mm candidate in 57/64 fixed TRAIN-inner synthetic sections, yet its fitted selector loses to support-only and shuffled-intensity controls. A scalar fit-weight search does not repair that. Earlier 257 found local intensity information around the *true* synthetic pose, but that privileged centre does not establish a useful gradient around an actual blind guess. Test this distinction directly, with no training or deformation.

Freeze the fluorescence-plus-old-domain rehearsed 305 pose checkpoint at local step 400. Draw eight new independently sampled eligible v9 physical sections from each of TRAIN-inner synthetic bases 56–63, without deliberately carrying a plane between samples or manufacturing background pairs. Preserve every accepted/rejected draw and physical-section ID. For each image, take the checkpoint's highest-probability pose/reflection branch **without truth or mask**, hold that branch's reflection fixed, and render paired ± local tangent probes in all nine full-frame coordinates: 12° rotations about each of three local axes; 1.0-mm translations along each local axis; ±log(1.1) in each raster span; and ±0.1 shear. Use the sample's finite-thickness PSF metadata as supplied, identically across probes. The model's source-only reliability map supplies a candidate-independent pixel weight; warp is disabled.

For each ± pair, score sign-invariant smoothed atlas/source intensity correlation on their shared eroded atlas support, with one fixed source weight map and denominator. Compare against (a) the difference in source-weighted atlas support only and (b) independently spatially shuffled atlas intensities with the same supports. Synthetic truth is used **only after inference** to determine which probe has lower surviving-site physical CCF error. A pair is informative only if its physical-error difference is ≥0.10 mm, source-weighted shared support is ≥64 of 96² cells, support IoU is ≥0.45, and support-area gap is ≤0.15. Report excluded counts, equal-base directional win fractions, actual counts, and results by angle/visible-tissue fraction. Also measure source reliability overlap with known valid tissue diagnostically, never as a score input.

The no-training advancement gate requires ≥40 informative pairs in ≥6/8 bases, ≥70% equal-base atlas-intensity directional correctness, and ≥15 percentage points over **both** support-only and shuffled-intensity controls. This is only a *local correction-signal* gate; passing it does not show that a trained updater, global proposal selection, mapping, real oblique sections, uncertainty or the GUI works. Failure means do not launch another 305-style joint training stage based on this stencil. Keep DEV/CAL/FINAL animals and public DeepSlice untouched; write all model-development outputs on `I:`.
