# 249: isolate the image-only arbitrary-plane pose signal

The 247 joint pilot left its 64-anchor normal cross-entropy at approximately `ln(64)=4.159` after 12,000 accepted synthetic presentations; the physical-cut target distribution has mean entropy about 2.434 and a median nearest-anchor distance of 7.1°. Thus a diagnostic with the **same full joint-model architecture initialized afresh**, but only its pose pathway supervised, can distinguish a loss-conflict/training problem from an unlearnable image signal at this exposure. It is a training stage candidate, not a deployable separate model.

Train 3,000 batches of four independently drawn eligible 192-pixel v7 TRAIN sections (12,000 presentations) from the 64 TRAIN 3D deformation bases. One appearance and one plane are sampled per presentation; no intentional duplicate planes or paired backgrounds. Do not use acquired weak labels, atlas-rendered match, local-map, refinement, or ranking losses in this isolation. The five-channel image still has optional brush channels; raw inputs have no brush. The pose loss is one unit of 15°-width antipodal 64-anchor cross-entropy plus `0.25 ×` the target-nearest-three-anchor mean surviving-tissue rigid error in millimetres, taking the better reflection. AdamW learning rate `1e-4`; save fresh-random step 0 and steps 500, 1,500, 3,000 with optimizer and RNG states. Record each accepted/rejected draw identity, training curves, source/protocol hashes and exact checkpoint hashes on I:.

This first stage is worth continuing only if the last 500-batch mean TRAIN normal cross-entropy is at least 0.5 below `ln(64)`, and the independent **v7** eight-plan 248 synthetic DEV readout (once fully frozen) improves plan-equal normal-axis error by at least 10° and selected rigid error by at least 0.5 mm over the frozen 247 checkpoint on exactly the same eligible sections. These are development mechanism thresholds, not claims of biological accuracy. A failure stops this stage rather than prompting more of the same batches. A pass permits later *within the same checkpoint lineage* atlas-conditioned joint feedback and deformation training, with an anatomical-intensity null, acquired donor guard, new development plans, and independent animal-level calibration/final testing still required. The old 247 panel, CAL/FINAL animals and public DeepSlice benchmark are not used for training or checkpoint selection here.
