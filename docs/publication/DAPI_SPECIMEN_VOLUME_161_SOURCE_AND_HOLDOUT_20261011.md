# 161: independent DAPI specimen-volume source, rights, and coordinate boundary

The [G-Node DAPI template dataset](https://doi.gin.g-node.org/10.12751/g-node.16wrxa/) provides 12 separately reconstructed adult-mouse DAPI-intensity volumes (`BM03`, `BM04`, `BM06`, `BM10`–`BM18`) under its repository [CC0 license](https://gin.g-node.org/doi/B6_dapi_template/src/10.12751/g-node.16wrxa/LICENSE). The [methods paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-020-0570-z) reports 62–70 physically acquired 100-µm coronal sections per animal and a final DAPI population average interpolated to 33.3 µm through-plane spacing. These are 12 biological specimens, unlike the project's virtual affine descendants of one Allen atlas; they can diversify specimen texture and shape if registration passes QC. They do **not** provide physically cut steep-oblique slices: any arbitrary-plane image resampled from them interpolates a coronal acquisition and is synthetic orientation training, not independent oblique validation.

The immutable repository contains DAPI intensity data and a CCF/DAPI affine, but **not** the per-animal transforms into its final average. Its [README](https://gin.g-node.org/doi/B6_dapi_template/src/10.12751/g-node.16wrxa/README.md) and [template pipeline](https://gin.g-node.org/doi/B6_dapi_template/src/10.12751/g-node.16wrxa/template_creation_pipline/template_creation.py) show the reconstructed animals are registered before averaging. BM03's NIfTI header has an identity selected sform and unknown spatial unit despite 8.6×100×8.6-µm pixdim; the DAPI average has a metric affine and 8.6×33.3×8.6-µm spacing. Central native-grid images look broadly anatomically similar but are **not** registration evidence. No BM volume receives CCF coordinates or a training label by copying voxel indices. The `training/preflight_dapi_bm03_161.py` report and montage on `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/dapi_bm03_preflight_161` freeze this observation.

For each animal, estimate and visually QC a transform from DAPI-average fixed space to the BM moving volume before using it. The published `ccf_to_dapi_affine_transformation.txt` is an *output-to-input* pull transform for resampling the CCF image onto the DAPI grid, as the [repository example](https://gin.g-node.org/doi/B6_dapi_template/src/10.12751/g-node.16wrxa/README.md) and [SimpleITK resampler convention](https://simpleitk.org/doxygen/latest/html/classitk_1_1simple_1_1ResampleImageFilter.html) show. Preserve the original coordinate conventions and verify left/right, three-plane overlays, and anatomical landmarks before composing transforms. Registration-derived CCF labels remain **weak inferred labels**, not expert section truth.

Predeclare the animal split before any DAPI model training: TRAIN `BM03`, `BM04`, `BM06`, `BM10`–`BM14` (8); development `BM15`, `BM16` (2); sealed secondary animal holdout `BM17`, `BM18` (2). All 12 source files may be hashed and header-checked for download integrity, but no images or registration outcomes from the sealed two may guide model design, selection, or calibration. The two-animal secondary holdout will not replace future independent physically oblique, expert-annotated validation. Provenance must retain BM animal ID, DOI/revision, raw file SHA-256, native slice index, registration transform/version, synthetic reslice plane, artifact seed, and section identity. Do not deliberately reuse a plane merely to vary its background.
