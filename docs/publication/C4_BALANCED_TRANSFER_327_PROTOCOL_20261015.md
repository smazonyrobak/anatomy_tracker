# 327: corrected, bounded acquired-orientation transfer probe

Study 326 was stopped after its batch-0 readout and 17 optimizer batches: its random initial model already selected the sagittal family for 6/8 TRAIN-inner sagittal donors, making a preregistered gain of four donors impossible. Its partial files remain on `I:` and are not a result. This replacement uses a new random seed and run ID; it retains the *same* donor split and frozen synthetic panel, so donors are not changed in response to their observed scores. It does not use 326 weights or training draws.

Run exactly 1,000 optimizer batches from a new random C4 initialization, with four independently sampled eligible v10 TRAIN synthetic cuts and one weakly registered acquired TRAIN section per batch. Cycle equally through Lupori coronal, Allen coronal, and Allen sagittal. Synthetic base, variant and seed are independently random on every attempt; chance-similar planes are allowed, with no de-duplication. Use the unchanged 321 pose/map/support objective and tolerant weak-affine real objective. Approximate human/Allen atlas registration is not measured physical pose truth. Preserve animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs and source hashes. The 10 coronal and eight sagittal TRAIN-inner donors are disjoint from acquired gradients, and `AL1A` is withheld. No DEV/CAL/FINAL or public benchmark access.

Read out only at batches 0 and 1,000. Against the identical frozen 321 TRAIN-inner synthetic panels, the batch-1,000 model must have inner first-mapped mean ≤3.920 mm and ≤21/128 cases over 5 mm, steep first-mapped mean ≤4.004 mm, and sparse first-mapped mean ≤5.756 mm. These are 321's batch-1,000 values plus 0.20 mm (tail plus two), not a comparison with its mature batch-12,000 model. For *simultaneous* real-family capture, require at least 8/10 coronal first-choice AP-family and 9/10 AP-family in top 16, and at least 6/8 sagittal first-choice ML-family and 6/8 ML-family in top 16. No improvement-from-random-initialization clause applies. The known physical cutting family is the firm label; exact weak-affine angles remain descriptive.

All clauses must pass for transfer feasibility. Even a pass is not model promotion: the C4 model is smaller than the stronger 309 pose model, has no demonstrated useful local map gain, no measured physical oblique validation, and no calibrated probabilities. Do not extend this run or change the thresholds after the batch-1,000 result. A failure does not establish impossibility: it could reflect model capacity, objective, data, or the short from-scratch budget.
