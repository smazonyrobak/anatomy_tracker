# 090 whole-plane contrast: failed selection, support shortcut

The frozen [protocol](GLOBAL_ATLAS_CONTRAST_090_PROTOCOL_20261004.md) trained 15,000 independently drawn eligible synthetic TRAIN sections (16,469 attempts), one appearance per section, from the project-only 089 lineage. The process exited 0. Its independent evaluator exited 0 on the unchanged 246-section/eight-plan synthetic DEV panel and exact 086 14-branch IDs. A separate raw-row audit reproduced every selected branch, best branch, and top-1/3/5/14 mapped error for all 984 section/checkpoint rows (13,776 candidate rows), and verified the evaluator hashes. No image was revisited, real/final-test or public benchmark label used, or probability calibrated.

At 15,000 batches, plan-equal selected mapped error was **2.828 mm**, worse than the frozen 089 selected **2.592 mm**. Best available among the unchanged 14 was **0.961 mm**. Within-section score-vs-lower-error Spearman was **0.044** (gate ≥0.30), and selected gain was **−0.236 mm** (gate ≥+0.400 mm). The exact-black result was 2.626 mm versus 2.562 mm (within its +0.150 mm nonregression gate), but raw was 2.855 versus 2.630 mm and imperfect brush was 3.041 versus 2.430 mm. Six of eight synthetic plans worsened. The prespecified gate **failed**; do not promote 090 or call its scores probabilities.

The failure is diagnostic, not merely low exposure. On the 15,000-step DEV rows, within-section score/support Spearman was **−0.845**, while support/lower-physical-error Spearman was **0.007** and score/lower-error was **0.043**. The chosen atlas views had mean support 0.197 versus 0.242 for the physically best view. This preference was already present in the random-initialized scorer (score/support −0.929 over the first 1,000 TRAIN draws) and remained −0.821 over the last 1,000. Training positive top-1 frequency rose 49.3%→59.8%, but *among the 14 blind candidates*, score/error Spearman only rose 0.036→0.088. The observed-affine reference has mean support 0.260 versus 0.348 for blind alternatives; its average soft target mass is 0.585. Thus the loss can improve by recognizing support extent instead of discriminating anatomy among inference candidates. This is an inference from the measured associations, not a proof that the encoder learned no anatomy. Subtracting the score/support slope fitted only on the last 1,000 TRAIN draws from DEV scores was exploratory, did not rescue selection (3.008 mm), and was not used to revise the frozen gate.

The next targeted training change should force useful **within-blind-beam anatomical ordering at similar support**, not enlarge the atlas catalogue or train the same 090 loss longer. The existing draws have on average 5.98 blind candidates within ±0.05 support of the observed-affine reference; 87.2% have at least one and 72.8% at least three, so support-matched comparisons are feasible without duplicate slice images. Retain a direct probabilistic pose head and local map, then enable fit/physical supervision to update pose in the same checkpoint after the anatomy-matching signal is established. The project-specific matched-support loss and joint fine-tuning still need a frozen protocol and a fresh DEV readout. [Debiased hard-negative contrast](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Jang_Difficulty-Based_Sampling_for_Debiased_Contrastive_Representation_Learning_CVPR_2023_paper.html) and [anatomy-aware structural contrast](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Mok_Modality-Agnostic_Structural_Image_Representation_Learning_for_Deformable_Multi-Modality_Medical_Image_CVPR_2024_paper.html) motivate the direction, but neither establishes it for this arbitrary-plane task.
