# 310: individual anatomical source and the next decision

The present bottleneck is not a lack of optimizer batches. The frozen 309 model saw 20,000 additional four-image batches, yet its blind first-choice rigid-site error on 256 independent full-sphere TRAIN-inner cuts moved only 2.595→2.469 mm; a ≤1.5-mm proposal existed in its top 16 for 226/256 cuts. An exact-pose local residual is about 0.109 mm on these synthetic data. A separate individual-mouse microCT texture substitution made selection much worse, but mixed individual anatomy with a different imaging modality. Another unchanged 309 continuation or another ad hoc candidate score is therefore a poor use of compute.

The [Allen Connectivity API](https://brain-map.org/support/documentation/api-allen-brain-connectivity-atlas) exposes reference-aligned 25-µm `ImagesResampledTo25MicronARA` ZIPs. Unlike the selective transgenic signal volumes inspected separately, the ZIP's `resampled_red.raw` is background fluorescence carrying general anatomy. The API describes serial two-photon coronal acquisition at approximately 100-µm section sampling, specimen reconstruction, red-channel registration to the CCF template, and ReferenceSpace 9 with positive posterior/inferior/right axes. It reports 25-µm grid dimensions 528×320×456. These are **registered individual-brain images**, not independent physical arbitrary-oblique histology or exact landmark truth.

One bounded ZIP, experiment `156198187`, WellKnownFile `607436902`, was obtained only on `I:/AnatomyTracker/data/allen_stpt_red_310_001/`. Its `resampled_red.mhd` says 528×320×456, 25-µm spacing, zero offset and uint16 little-endian red voxels. The MHD orientation string `RAI` conflicts with the API's `PIR` convention, so the header string alone is not a coordinate proof. Reading raw storage as `(ML,DV,AP)` and transposing to the pinned `(AP,DV,ML)` array gives gross atlas alignment in all three orthogonal views; within-annotation-support log-intensity correlation at 100-µm stride is 0.889 without flips versus 0.730 for the next-best ML flip. The [three-plane QC image](I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/allen_stpt_red_310.png) is a visual check, not a measured registration-error bound. `training/qc_allen_stpt_red_310.py` reproduces it. The current API asset count is not an animal count: repeated experiments may share a donor, so split by donor identity after metadata audit.

A metadata-only RMA audit found 3,113 such file assets. Of those, 3,104 have resolvable SectionDataSets with distinct specimen and donor IDs; eight attachments are BaseImageSeries rather than SectionDataSets and one SectionDataSet has two assets. Filtering to nonfailed datasets with `reference_space_id=9`, `red_channel='background fluorescence'` and donor identity leaves **2,770 assets from 2,769 donors**. This is a discovery count, not 2,769 QC-qualified training brains or a claim of physically acquired oblique sections. A donor-distinct candidate shortlist is: experiment/file/donor `168615344/607628055/160252067`, `257636467/607916488/182849231`, `307742524/315602749/287634601`, `526501457/527105701/517337553`, and `649361916/650149968/642454692`. Inspect images and geometry before using any. The [API documentation](https://brain-map.org/support/documentation/api-allen-brain-connectivity-atlas) supplies the WellKnownFile type and query pattern; exact candidate experiment metadata can be retrieved through `https://api.brain-map.org/api/v2/data/SectionDataSet/{experiment}.json?include=specimen`.

Two additional donor-distinct red ZIPs were then acquired to `I:/AnatomyTracker/data/allen_stpt_red_310_001/` and checked with `training/qc_allen_red_cohort_310.py`. Their archive SHA-256 values are `0e5c93594f05b31b0c7f9e2b2898fc790b9d665c722362ed3a79a729397d054a` (donor `160252067`) and `17d9ac4f2a92a60ec186e2f7817d3387e4c0c0d5a155018853dbdec75b74e3c6` (donor `287634601`). Both have the same asserted grid/header fields. The three donor volumes' no-flip atlas-brain correlations are **0.889, 0.872 and 0.875**; no-flip is best of all eight axis-flip combinations for each. The [three-donor, three-plane QC grid](I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/allen_stpt_red_three_donor_qc_310.png) shows broadly corresponding but nonidentical anatomy and source-specific acquisition patterns. These are gross checks only; no individual landmarks or exact coordinate errors were measured. The JSON QC rows are at `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/allen_stpt_red_three_donor_qc_310.json`.

The short paired source-shift diagnostic `training/preflight_allen_red_source_shift_310.py` keeps the 309 model, 256 TRAIN-inner virtual planes, deformation maps, appearance seeds, support channel and coordinate targets frozen. It substitutes only this red anatomical intensity after within-brain 1st–99th percentile normalization, and compares first choice, harmful tail and top-16 capture with the saved 309 rows. This isolates a useful input-source transfer question more cleanly than the microCT substitution, but real specimen anatomy, fluorescence contrast and registration errors still change together. It is not a test on physically cut sections.

**Result:** the source archive SHA-256 is `19d18b558a44cbe6fbdc72e0d53eeb6d72da9b74f497a8a16ffa3e7b7ef5b6a3`; red-channel within-brain 1st/99th percentiles were 15/604. On all 256 paired cuts, frozen blind first-choice mean rigid-site error changed **2.469→2.773 mm**, the >5-mm count **17→21**, and top-16 capture within 1.5 mm **226→199**. Red-source top-16 oracle mean was 1.141 mm and median unsigned first-choice plane-normal error was 28.4°. The model retains substantial localization ability on this source, but transfer is worse; one donor and one modality cannot establish the effect of source diversity on future training, nor can this be called real-histology accuracy. Raw per-case rows and the normalization/source binding are under `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/allen_stpt_red_source_shift_310_train`. No model weights or reserved validation data changed.

Process after that readout: (1) audit donor counts, registration/rights and a **small** donor-distinct source set, not a bulk 3,113-ZIP ingest; (2) if source diversity is usable, run a short, matched training intervention and evaluate fresh full-sphere cuts plus donor-held-out individual volumes and acquired TRAIN donor guards; (3) advance a single direct pose-and-local-map model only if blind selected pose, >5-mm tail and mapped-site error improve together. Keep the donor-level calibration and final histology sets sealed, and do not benchmark DeepSlice or claim 90% electrode-region coverage before prospective real-animal calibration. Any near-correct atlas slice on a tiny or torn fragment may remain ambiguous; preserve multiple hypotheses/abstention instead of forcing certainty.

For the final architecture, use one shared image encoder with a multimodal plane head and a local tissue-to-atlas map head in the same training graph. The image, chosen atlas plane and local map should be assessed together by a differentiable atlas-agreement term and a deformation-cost term; gradients from these terms must reach the pose head, so an anatomically poor plane cannot be made cheap by an unconstrained warp. This is a direct joint prediction at inference, not two separately optimized deployed models or a repeated atlas-search loop. Synthetic applied warps give inverse-warp supervision, whereas the registered donor red volumes do **not** provide exact biological warp truth. A short matched test must show that the coupled objective improves *blind* pose and mapped-site accuracy over the direct prior; otherwise do not scale it merely because a rendered example looks convincing.
