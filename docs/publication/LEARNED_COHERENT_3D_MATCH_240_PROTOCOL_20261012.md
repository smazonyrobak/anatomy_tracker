# Learned 3D anatomy matching for arbitrary-plane registration

The frozen 226 pose proposer covers most, but not all, fresh TRAIN-inner sections when its entire 160-branch beam is retained (238: 58/64 within 2 mm). Hand-designed 3D patch matches contain an anatomy signal, but robustly fitting them improved near branches by only 0.136 mm (239). The next change is a **new, randomly initialized cross-modal descriptor**, trained to make a single coherent source-to-atlas plane score and bounded pose correction useful on the proposer's actual blind branches. Neither the 226 weights nor synthetic pose truth enters the deployed descriptor inputs. The 226 proposer is frozen only for this staged TRAIN-inner experiment; a successful module must later be trained jointly into one standalone checkpoint before deployment.

## Model and supervision

- A one-channel source CNN extracts 64-dimensional descriptors from the acquired-looking image itself; a separate 2+1D CNN extracts 64-dimensional descriptors from a candidate-aligned, finite-thickness atlas slab. The slab has 17 planes spanning ±4 mm at 0.5-mm spacing. Every plane integrates the actual nine-sample through-plane point-spread function. Both encoders see **intensity only**: no synthetic tissue mask, atlas support, image coordinates, CCF coordinates, branch rank, or pose prior. Atlas support gates comparisons after encoding. Raw/black-background/imperfect-brush appearance is sampled once per independently drawn physical section; no paired appearance variants of one section are training examples.
- Train on source sites that survive the sampled synthetic artifacts. Their known tissue-to-CCF coordinates provide a soft local correspondence target, with same-support atlas positions displaced in 3D as negatives. This truth is a **training label**, never an inference feature. A coherent score compares every source descriptor against features sampled at the 3D points of **one rigid plane hypothesis**. Pairwise/listwise supervision also makes that exact score rank true/near planes above actual blind wrong branches. A no-shift hypothesis always competes with bounded full-pose updates; local tissue warp is not used to explain away a wrong plane in this stage.
- Draw the actual 160 blind 226 proposals for every fresh section; use the truth-nearest reachable branch, a wrong branch from the blind top 16, and a small truth-jittered hypothesis during training. Rank near/wrong pairs only when their supported-site counts are comparable. They are hypotheses for **one image**, not repeated images or dataset duplicates. Mix arbitrary normals, offsets, rolls, reflections, independent v4/v7 appearance, illumination, noise, confusing backgrounds, tissue tears, bubbles, missing fragments, and unequal local warps already present in the frozen generator. Record exact physical-section and base-map lineage.

## Bounded training and readout

Initialize the descriptor from random weights; never load an external encoder, external pseudolabels, or DEV/CAL/FINAL animals. Use TRAIN base maps 0–47 for optimizer draws, 48–51 for checkpoint selection, and 52–55 for a **fresh, sealed 64-section TRAIN-inner readout** (32 v4, 32 v7). One seed defines one independently generated physical section and one appearance. Start with at most 1,024 unique training presentations, inspect the validation curve at 256-presentation intervals, retain the best checkpoint, and extend training only while validation improves. No million-presentation run is authorized by an unpromising pilot. The 226 proposer remains frozen; this is not yet the shipped joint model.

Before opening the 64-case readout, fix the checkpoint from validation only. On the readout, report source/atlas correspondence error, intact versus support-preserving atlas-intensity shuffle and source-interior swap, common-support near/wrong ranking, near-branch rigid correction, known-true pose drift, blind selected rigid error, all-160 corrected capture, v4/v7 strata, and reflection. Measure physical tissue-site error in millimetres, not just a pose parameter. The controls retain identical plane geometry, candidate order, source outline, and support. Also report the fraction of readout cases whose true tissue points lie outside every candidate's 3D bank; those cannot be fixed by descriptor quality.

This stage passes for integration only if all hold on the fresh panel: intact near/wrong ranking ≥75% on matched-support pairs and at least 15 percentage points above each anatomy-destroying control; mean near correction ≥0.25 mm and ≥0.15 mm more than the shuffled-atlas arm; known-true mean error after correction ≤0.20 mm; blind selected mean error improves ≥0.30 mm over the frozen direct proposer; and at least 60/64 cases have a corrected branch within 2 mm. A failure rules out this **unchanged** descriptor/score/correction recipe, not arbitrary-plane registration as such. A pass triggers joint end-to-end pose/deformation training, then animal-separated real validation and probability calibration. No public DeepSlice benchmark or numerical electrode-region probability claim is made in this stage.

Store source hashes, code/checkpoint bindings, raw case rows, all generator draw attempts, best-checkpoint rule, and an independent verifier under `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/learned_coherent_3d_match_240_*`. This is a scientific pilot, not a GUI or animal-generalization claim.
