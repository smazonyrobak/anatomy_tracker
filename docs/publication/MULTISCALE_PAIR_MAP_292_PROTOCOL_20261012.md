# 292: supervised multiscale correspondence mechanism, frozen before training

The 289 exact-pose spatial map gained only 0.0131 mm, and the 290/291 fixed 96-grid intensity cost fails to identify many true local offsets. The 291 hard candidate mask barely changed this; a strong center prior mostly copied zero motion. Test the smallest principled replacement: randomly initialized source/atlas descriptor pyramids and explicitly supervised local matching at 48→96→192 pixels, with coarse-to-fine atlas warping, finite-thickness CCF rendering at the known post-artifact pose, and a small 3-D residual for through-plane/subpixel displacement. The output is one tissue-to-CCF field; detached fragments may map independently and no global diffeomorphism may force them back through a tear. Use PWC-Net's learned pyramid/warping/local-cost principle ([Sun et al., CVPR 2018](https://research.nvidia.com/publication/2018-06_pwc-net-cnns-optical-flow-using-pyramid-warping-and-cost-volume)); this is not a copied pretrained model.

Retain the exact 275 scratch-lineage pose modules and buffers inside the same checkpoint, frozen byte-for-byte for this mechanism stage. The descriptor/mapping modules start randomly. Every accepted training presentation is an independently drawn physical plane/appearance/artifact from the current v9 stream, bases 0–55; do not deliberately reuse a plane to vary only its background. Use one new 64-section TRAIN-inner panel, eight independent eligible draws per synthetic base 56–63, with no exact physical ID overlap with earlier 286/287/289 training or panels. Do not use acquired DEV/CAL/FINAL animals, previous model features as labels, public benchmarks, or any non-I: development artifact. The 289 selected map is the frozen control on the **same new panel**.

Train batch size one for at most 2,400 new sections, with fixed readouts at steps 0, 400, 1,200, and 2,400; retain the lowest whole-panel surviving-site error after step 0. Supervise observed-pixel→CCF endpoint error densely on valid tissue plus extra weight on valid displaced-fragment sites, and supervise correct local offset classification at each scale when the true offset is inside that level's search window and atlas support. Add only low-weight within-piece, image-edge-aware field smoothness and valid-tissue confidence losses. Store per-presentation provenance (physical section, animal, specimen, experiment, draw seed), losses, source hashes, panel/input/target hashes, checkpoints, and readouts. Never tune on or overwrite the frozen panel after seeing a result.

Implementation frozen before the panel draw: three descriptor scales 48/96/192 with integer search radii 6/4/3, 16 descriptor channels, no learned prior weights, full-resolution field output. The total loss is dense endpoint mm + 0.5×displaced-fragment endpoint mm + 0.004×mean eligible scale-offset cross-entropy + 0.01×within-piece smoothness mm + 0.005×validity BCE. At each coarse scale, the CE target is an actual nearest-sampled tissue point, gated out if its downsampling block mixes background or fragments; it is expressed in the reflected atlas-raster coordinates relative to the detached current coarse-to-fine flow. The optimizer is AdamW at 0.0002, reduced to 0.00008 after 1,200 updates. The mapper caps final in-plane displacement at 1.5 mm and through-plane displacement at 250 µm; report fractions of true valid and fragment displacements beyond these caps. Trilinearly sample atlas support at the **final mapped sites**; unsupported outputs still count in endpoint error and their counts are reported, never accepted on candidate-support alone.

Descriptive advance gate for this **synthetic exact-pose mechanism only**: whole-panel mean gain over rigid ≥0.030 mm; displaced-fragment gain ≥0.070 mm on at least 100 fragment sites; gain over frozen 289 control on this panel ≥0.015 mm; at least six of eight synthetic base means positive; intact-site gain nonnegative; all frozen pose hashes and first-choice pose readouts identical; output finite and no mapped site outside atlas support silently treated as correct. Also report 0→400→1,200→2,400 train/panel losses, candidate-offset coverage, accepted physical IDs, and fragment/intact strata. Failing this gate means diagnose correspondence training/data before blind coupling or more scale. Passing it authorizes a larger TRAIN continuation and a separately frozen predicted-pose feedback experiment, **not** GUI deployment or a probability claim.

This is still one-atlas synthetic evidence. The v9 renderer has a known square imperfect-brush artifact, stylized tear/fold/bubble/seam primitives, uncalibrated event prevalence, and an Allen average volume with through-plane acquisition limits. A successful mechanism readout must be followed by versioned generator repairs and donor-separated acquired checks before a high-quality all-angle claim. No evidence yet warrants restricting angle families; low-information images may instead require optional orientation/section-order hints or abstention.
