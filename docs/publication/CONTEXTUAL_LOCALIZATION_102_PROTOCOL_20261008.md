# 102A: make the anatomical matcher localize, not merely separate broad bins

The frozen 099 step-4000 matcher improved raw correspondence ranking but its decoded physical match still lost to leaving a good candidate plane unshifted. The matched 101 swap consequently changed only one selected branch among 64 sections. This experiment changes only the loss used to train the *existing scratch-lineage* 099 context residuals; it does not add a model, train a plane scorer, alter a frozen source/checkpoint, or assume more exposure alone will fix selection.

Initialize from authenticated 099 step 4000. Freeze the 094 image-to-plane predictor, local mapper, fit head and all inherited matcher projections; train only the 099 image/atlas multiscale context residuals. Draw 4,000 new independent TRAIN physical sections (one plane and one appearance per draw, no deliberate paired variants) from the existing 64-deformation/4,096-virtual-subject sampler, seed `20261008102`; retain rejected attempts and identity/geometry/appearance receipts. Use the unchanged frozen 094 beam and four candidate types from 099, including useful near and hard wrong poses. Checkpoint at 0, 2,000 and 4,000 accepted sections. All files and cache remain on I:.

For each supported visible TRAIN site, form a distance-weighted target over *every* supported match class from its exact observed-pixel-to-CCF target: weight proportional to `exp(-distance²/(2σ²))`, with fine σ=0.5 mm and coarse σ=1.0 mm, and zero for unsupported classes. Skip a site when no supported class lies within 1.5 mm fine or 2.0 mm coarse of truth. Minimize soft-label cross-entropy and a small robust coordinate penalty (weight 0.2, 1-mm Huber scale) on the **same argmax-neighborhood softmax coordinate decoder** used in 094 inference, measured in millimetres. Do not use true tissue mask, true CCF target or correct plane as a forward input. Exact targets are synthetic supervision only. Preserve supported-bin and decoder numerics; no implicit probability calibration.

Before training, freeze the sampling seeds and source for a new eight-plan, 32 independent sections/plan synthetic DEV panel (plan indices 10200–10207; no TRAIN or prior DEV deformation realization/plane reuse). Generate and hash it before the first 102A evaluation. On the unchanged frozen 14-branch 094 candidate geometry, compare 099 step 4000 and 102A checkpoints using raw top-1, *decoded* 1.5-mm correct match, decoded 3-D CCF error, and the no-shift candidate-plane control. Report physically best candidate and all candidates, plan-equal plus angle-family, appearance and tissue-visibility strata. Do not train on DEV labels. The prespecified feedback gate requires the selected 102A checkpoint's decoded correct-match fraction to exceed the no-shift control by at least 0.03 absolute on physically best candidates and 0.02 on all candidates, with improvement over 099 in at least six of eight synthetic plans and no family/appearance regression worse than 0.03. Also retain step-zero exact parity with 099 step 4000. A score that merely beats 099 but not the no-shift control **fails** this gate.

If this gate fails, do not let displaced correspondences drive pose or fit feedback. Diagnose localization and domain realism before another large training run. If it passes, separately test a 14-branch blind candidate posterior trained on post-fit *mapped* physical error with atlas-support-matched hard negatives, and explicitly ablate centered match evidence versus bounded deformation/warp difficulty. Only a gain in selected physical error on new identity-disjoint confirmation sections justifies joint pose/mapper fine-tuning. This 102A panel is synthetic development, not real arbitrary-plane validation, calibrated trajectory uncertainty, GUI qualification, or a DeepSlice benchmark.
