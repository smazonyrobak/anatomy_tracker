# 093: the matches contain signal; the reliability head does not recognize it

After 092 failed, this read-only diagnostic examined eight reproducibly sampled frozen synthetic DEV sections from each of eight deformation plans (64 sections), all 14 actual blind candidates per section, and both 16- and 32-cell correspondence grids. It used the unchanged 092 step-6,000 checkpoint and exact 092 candidate states; no synthetic truth entered inference or altered a prediction. Known synthetic observed-pixel-to-CCF coordinates were used only to score whether each predicted local match was actually correct. The 64 sections are synthetic, not independent animals.

On the 32-cell grid, the *actual fraction of visible queries correctly matched within 1.5 mm* was strongly associated with a physically better candidate: mean within-section Spearman **0.735** between that fraction and lower mapped error. The physically best candidate had 57.5% correct visible queries on average; the worst had 10.4%. Coarse-grid correct-match fraction had Spearman 0.676. Thus local correspondence contains a useful geometric signal if correctness can be estimated without truth.

The present learned reliability estimate did **not** recover that signal. Its candidate-mean Spearman with lower error was only 0.035 at fine resolution and −0.006 at coarse resolution. Across visible fine-grid sites, the reliability score's AUROC for an actually correct match was **0.502**, essentially chance, even though 092 training BCE decreased. Atlas support itself had near-zero relationship with physical error here (Spearman −0.007). The 092 final score's much stronger association with support therefore reflects its aggregation formula, not useful anatomical evidence.

This narrows the next intervention. Keep the direct probabilistic pose and local atlas cost volume, but replace the eight-statistic/shallow correct-match head with a spatial candidate-conditioned verifier that sees the *full match distribution* and source/atlas image features. Train it against correct-match labels on the model's own diverse wrong proposals, plus a learned image-only soft tissue-validity signal; score conditional anatomical inliers with explicit coverage accounting rather than averaging empty-atlas penalties. Use the predicted inlier field to weight the constrained plane fit and train its result jointly back into the pose predictor. A longer run of 092's present scalar score is not justified. This is a hypothesis from the diagnostic, not a proven architecture or deployment result.
