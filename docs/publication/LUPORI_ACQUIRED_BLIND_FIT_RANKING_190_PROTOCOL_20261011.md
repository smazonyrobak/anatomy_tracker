# 190: blind-proposal anatomy discrimination on acquired TRAIN sections

Freeze the 188 step-1200 model and the exact 64 TRAIN animal/section identities selected for 189 (16 per animal); rerun its image encoder to obtain the first 16 **blind** pose/reflection proposals for each image. Use the identical full-thumbnail fluorescence preprocessing, Allen 2017 atlas, 48² image grid and five-point through-plane PSF as 189. No model training, mask/brush input, Lupori DEV/CAL/FINAL, public benchmark, or post-hoc score tuning.

Render all 16 rigid candidate planes. For a retrospective *pair diagnostic only*, use the approximate human plane to find the closest branch with five-site error ≤1.5 mm and the highest-prior branch with error ≥3 mm. Require at least 100 pixels in the 7×7-eroded **intersection** of their rendered atlas supports. Score both alternatives on those *identical* pixels with the 189 smooth absolute image/atlas correlation. Repeat after a common circular permutation of atlas intensities within that fixed mask, preserving source image and atlas support. Record whether atlas score, permutation-null score, and direct pose prior prefer the near branch. The pair is selected without looking at any fit score, and all candidate errors and source identities are frozen in the output.

Separately, rank all 16 branches blindly by their individual correlation scores on their own eroded atlas-support masks; branches with fewer than 100 supported pixels are ineligible. Compare first-choice five-site error to the untouched highest-prior branch. This individual-mask ranking is an operational stress test and can be support-biased; do not substitute the truth-selected pair score for blind accuracy.

The *pair* anatomy-signal gate passes only with at least 32 eligible sections, at least 70% actual-intensity near wins, an advantage of at least 10 percentage points over the permutation null, and above-50% near wins in at least three of the four TRAIN animals. The separate *blind* gate passes only if the blind fit-only first choice reduces equal-animal mean five-site error by at least 0.15 mm versus the original direct prior, no animal worsens by more than 0.2 mm, and no more than four sections lack any eligible score. If the pair gate passes but the blind gate fails, only local discrimination is established; the score is not a viable global selector and must not drive joint training unchanged. These weak human plane references and near-coronal TRAIN animals cannot establish held-out accuracy or arbitrary oblique performance.

Bind source, protocol, checkpoint and 189 frozen-receipt hashes; preserve raw per-section scores. Audit only after the runner exits.
