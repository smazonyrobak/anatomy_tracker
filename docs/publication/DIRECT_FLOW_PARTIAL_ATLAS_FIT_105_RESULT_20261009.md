# Direct continuous-flow fit 105: frozen negative result

The 105 TRAIN run finished 4,000 updates in 1,590 s from 4,415 independent v3 synthetic draw attempts. Its parent was the project's own 104 step-1,000 fitter and v3 step-2,000 direct pose model; the new three-component local flow was initialized to zero with a live first-order map-loss gradient. A TRAIN-only exact/jittered true-pose candidate augmented four blind hypotheses for each newly drawn image; it was never available to the blind selector. No external pretrained features, original-app weights, pseudolabels, public benchmark, or expert real labels were used. The independent verifier passed all training, panel, source, checkpoint and evaluation hashes, 2,688 blind candidate rows, 192 section rows and 192 separate oracle-diagnostic rows.

The predeclared 64-section, eight-synthetic-plan DEV gate **failed**. The best trained checkpoint was step 4,000. On the common frozen 094 mapping-error comparator, the 105 score selected **3.910 mm**, better than the original 094 selector's **4.165 mm** but worse than atlas-support-only selection's **3.744 mm**. The physical best of 14 was **1.836 mm** and requires truth. Five of eight plans improved over 094 selection, below the required six. Within-section score/error Spearman correlation was 0.068 for 105, −0.004 for 094 and **0.109 for support-only**. Thus the new fit score is not yet a reliable way to choose the plane.

More decisively, the local flow did not improve spatial mapping. On the three candidates with lowest frozen 094 mapping error per section, 105's map was **2.39766 mm** versus **2.39794 mm** for its own affine-corrected rigid plane—a 0.00028-mm gain, far below the preregistered 0.20 mm—and versus **2.33618 mm** for the frozen 094 map. The selected 105 map itself was 3.927 mm, worse than the common frozen-094 map of its selected candidate (3.910 mm). The independently supplied true target plane was diagnostic only: its input rigid map averaged 0.196 mm, while inherited 094 pose fitting plus 105 flow yielded 0.269 mm (own fitted rigid 0.267 mm). At step zero that diagnostic map was 0.40 mm, so training improved the inherited *affine fitter's* behaviour; it did not make the added local flow useful. This is synthetic mapping error, not electrode-site or biological accuracy.

TRAIN class/dustbin CE fell from 2.50 in the first 500 updates to 2.17 in the last 1,000; map Huber fell 0.74→0.66 mm, and the TRAIN-only true-pose map Huber 0.38→0.29 mm. Mean absolute predicted flow across all cells fell 0.04→0.02 cells, consistent with the held-out near-identity map result. A lower training loss did not translate into local registration. The raw, imperfect-brush and heavy-oblique nonregression condition passed, but four of six scientific gate conditions failed: local map gain, beating support-only selection, ordering margin, and six-of-eight plans. No fitting-derived gradient has been enabled into the direct pose predictor.

The combined evidence of the earlier 022/035 diagnostics and this 105 run argues against further tuning a scalar fit score or this 32-cell local field over millimetre-wrong planes. The 022 exact-surface atlas image was distinguishable, but actual predicted planes lay outside the narrow reliable comparison basin; 035's atlas-patch score gradient was directionless at model proposals and more aligned near truth. The next investment must enlarge **near-true pose capture** and train a structured atlas-conditioned full-frame correction from actual blind proposal errors, while retaining a separate no-warp control and only then allowing useful fitting loss to teach pose. Merely scaling 105's data or calibrating its logits is not supported by the result. Any positive successor needs new identity-disjoint synthetic confirmation and animal-separated acquired validation. GUI replacement and DeepSlice benchmarking remain premature.
