The fixed 015 DEV panel leaves 2.7–2.8 mm error after direct or coordinate-based one-pass pose prediction, despite 0.13 mm correct-pose local mapping. The 016 MIND scalar worsened actual candidate selection even with a privileged tissue mask. The next substantial experiment must therefore train **pose correction using spatial atlas/fit evidence inside the same model**, not append another post-hoc ranker.

Continue the standalone random-initialized lineage from 015 batch 8,000, retaining its encoder, probabilistic 16-mode pose head, finite-thickness atlas renderer and learned local mapper. Remove the failed dense-coordinate auxiliary head, and add the existing randomly initialized shared pose-refiner head. For each of 16,000 batches, draw two independent arbitrary-plane synthetic sections and one previously unused real TRAIN section. Keep direct pose supervision on synthetic physical coordinates and weak real Allen affines. For the image-prior top eight synthetic pose/reflection candidates, render and locally fit an atlas plane at 64 pixels. Use those spatial fit features to predict a bounded full-frame pose update; re-render and re-fit each corrected plane at 96 pixels. Map synthetic visible tissue through each corrected result to its known CCF coordinates. Train the corrected-pose selection and physical mapping jointly, with a training-only exact-pose positive, tissue-reliability and deformation-size/smoothness penalties. The exact pose is not inserted at inference. Reuse image features across candidates, and stop gradients through the *first* feature-extraction fit to control GPU memory; the corrected pose, second atlas render, local fit, score and physical losses must differentiate back to the global pose head. Verify that connection once on an actual predicted branch.

Report image-prior and corrected selection, best-of-eight coverage, true-pose mapper, low-tissue failures and weak-real donor behaviour separately.
