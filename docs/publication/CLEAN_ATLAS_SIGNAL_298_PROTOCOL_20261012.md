# 298: is the missing plane-ranking signal in the atlas, the appearance, or the mask?

This is a bounded, no-training, TRAIN-inner mechanism check on the 32 already frozen sections and blind top-16 branches of 296. It does **not** score a new model or unlock DEV/CAL/FINAL animals. Do not alter the 296 outputs. Use the same candidate finite-thickness atlas renders, same zero-deformation image objective, and same candidate-independent common mask as 296; replay its saved artifact-image zero-step scores first. The score is `1 - |masked Pearson correlation| + 0.1 × candidate atlas-support deficit`, with zero deformation (the other 296 regularizers are zero). Supply the generating true atlas plane only as an unranked source of a clean, support-normalized image—not as a candidate or an inference input.

Evaluate the same 16 blind candidates in a 2×2 control: artifacted observed image versus clean true-atlas rendering, each scored under (a) 296's observable common mask and (b) a **privileged diagnostic mask** containing only the 512 saved truly valid surviving tissue sites. The privileged mask is forbidden at inference. Keep the exact stored 296 candidate order, physical errors, and predeclared near/far support-matched pair. On each arm report mean physical error of the minimum-score candidate, near-over-far wins among the same eligible 296 pairs, and truth-control score. Stratify by brush and obliquity descriptively; do not tune score fusion, mask, or thresholds. For the artifact/common arm, require score parity with every frozen 296 step-zero candidate value to within numerical tolerance.

Interpret the contrasts narrowly. Strong clean/common discrimination with weak artifact/common discrimination points to appearance/deformation mismatch. Improvement from common to privileged mask points to unreliable inference-time tissue support. If clean/privileged still fails, the current scalar intensity objective or atlas-plane distinctiveness is inadequate even under favorable conditions. A clean success does not prove real-histology recoverability: all cases share one atlas template, the clean image is privileged, and the blind candidate set was learned from the corresponding artifacted input. This study cannot justify calibration, GUI replacement, a DeepSlice benchmark, or restriction to cardinal cuts. Freeze source/protocol and I:-only output receipts; independently reaggregate the raw candidate scores after completion.
