# 185 real-section joint-label source preflight

The public [Lupori et al. microscopy record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7419283) is a candidate for *acquired coronal* pose-plus-local-warp supervision. Record version 1.0 (concept DOI `10.5281/zenodo.7419282`) declares CC BY 4.0 and seven adult mouse folders. Each reportedly pairs original microscopy images and thumbnails with per-mouse QuickNII plane XML, VisuAlign nonlinear JSON, displacement fields, masks, and specimen information. Its sole archive is `rawData.zip`, 48,652,184,187 bytes, MD5 `c8c57aa74182207db67aa0b8072be3a7`. Store and inspect it only under `I:/AnatomyTracker/data/lupori_joint_185/`; do not use an incomplete or checksum-mismatched archive. The Zenodo endpoint returned HTTP 200 and the declared length but did not advertise byte ranges, so no defensible small specimen-only download was identified.

Acquisition does **not** admit these registrations as exact anatomical truth. Before training, inspect the archive manifest without extracting all images, bind mouse IDs, confirm image-to-XML/JSON/field pairing, axis/units and transformation direction, and visually review a stratified TRAIN subset. Reserve animals by ID before looking at any candidate DEV/final images or model results; seven animals are too few for precise probability calibration or a universal performance claim. QuickNII/VisuAlign are human atlas registrations and may be valuable supervision, but remain expert approximations rather than physically measured ground truth. The material is coronal, not physically steep-oblique; it cannot establish arbitrary-cut accuracy. It is distinct from the untouched DeepSlice public benchmark.

The authors' [alignment code](https://github.com/LeonardoLupori/brainAlignment/blob/main/Slice.m) provides the coordinate contract to test against the archive: VisuAlign O/U/V overrides QuickNII where present, and atlas planes use normalized image coordinates `x/width, y/height`. The [marker order and triangulation](https://github.com/LeonardoLupori/brainAlignment/blob/main/getDisplacement.m) imply a field on the acquired-image grid with `D(image_xy) = reference_xy - image_xy`; [MATLAB `imwarp`](https://www.mathworks.com/help/images/ref/imwarp.html) then samples the unwarped atlas at `image_xy + D`. But the authors' cell-coordinate code subtracts `D`, and it upsamples the thumbnail field to full image resolution without visibly scaling displacement magnitudes. Treat those point coordinates as unverified. Before using any dense target, check stored fields against marker pairs and atlas-label overlays, then check the sign and pixel-scale conversion against image-to-CCF points. CSV field values require conversion from their downsampled computation grid to thumbnail-pixel units.

CC BY 4.0 applies to this deposited dataset, but it does not automatically grant rights to redistribute Allen atlas content or train/deploy a commercial service using Allen-derived atlas assets; preserve source attribution and review Allen's separate terms before shipping. No pretrained weights, model features, external pseudolabels, or DEV/final sections are authorized by this source preflight.
