# Coherent partial atlas fit 104 — frozen development protocol

This is a TRAIN-only fitter experiment in the existing randomly initialized 094 → v3 pose-capture lineage. It is not a final benchmark, calibration, or deployment claim. The parent pose generator is the selected v3 2,000-update checkpoint; the atlas matcher/fitter is the 094 20,000-update checkpoint. No external weights, features, pseudolabels, or hidden expert labels enter training.

For each independently sampled v3 arbitrary-plane TRAIN section, infer a blind 14-candidate beam. Choose four candidates for fitting: physical best, atlas-support-matched wrong candidate at least 0.5 mm worse where available, high-prior wrong candidate at least 1 mm worse where available, and another candidate. Truth is used only to select TRAIN candidates and supervise learning, never as model input. Record every source physical section and artifact draw; no deliberate near-duplicate pairing or plane rejection.

The 104 fitter inherits finite-thickness atlas correlation and 094 coarse pose fitting. A shared recurrent spatial field predicts a partial correspondence distribution with a dustbin for surviving tissue that cannot match in the finite search window. Normalize match-vs-dustbin evidence by the number of supported atlas bins. Predict a bounded observed-to-atlas map initialized near no shift. Decompose its weighted affine component into the returned pose and retain only nonrigid residual in the local deformation. Penalize physical residual size and strain, without coupling across independently displaced fragments in the supervised smoothness loss.

TRAIN loss: supported match-class cross-entropy; dustbin cross-entropy on valid surviving but unsupported/out-of-window sites; true CCF map error on supported sites; image-validity supervision; piecewise smooth local residual; and physical-error ordering across the four sampled candidates. Background/occluded pixels are not dustbin truth. Parent pose encoder/heads and old fitter heads remain frozen during this stage. New recurrent layers, atlas/image descriptors, image-validity head, and fit-score temperature may learn. Source atlas and generator provenance remain bound by hashes. Save step 0, 1,000, and 4,000 and preserve best development checkpoint rather than selecting the last by default.

The next frozen synthetic DEV evaluation must use blind candidate outputs and compare 104 to unchanged 094 and support-only selection on plan-equal sections. Require both: (1) observed-to-CCF mapping improves on the unshifted/fitted-094 map at good candidates; (2) selected mapped error and within-section fit ordering improve, including raw, brush, oblique, and low-support groups. No pose-gradient feedback is enabled until both pass. If either fails, diagnose from exact frozen outputs before changing architecture. A fresh independently sampled panel is required to confirm any selection; reused DEV does not certify generalization. Real expert animal-separated validation, calibrated electrode/region probabilities, GUI replacement, and public DeepSlice benchmarking remain later gates.
