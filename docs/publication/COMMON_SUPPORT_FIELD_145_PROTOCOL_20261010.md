# Common support anatomy field 145 development protocol

The 143 field's correct-plane score was mostly rigid mapping: the zero-offset null scored 93.76% within 0.5 mm on v4 DEV, versus 95.51% for the full field and 95.69% for support-only. Full and support-only ranked a roughly 2.2-mm wrong plane equally well when only the *fraction* of atlas support was matched. With exact and wrong candidates forced to share the same pixelwise support mask, the full field ranked the exact plane in 53.1% of 64 v4 and 56.3% of 64 v3 exploratory cases. The next intervention must identify interior anatomy under matched shape, then improve physical mapping, not merely lower descriptor loss. Previous 097 contrastive and 099 contextual descriptor changes failed to beat the unshifted-plane map operationally; 101 barely changed selection.

Continue the same internally random-initialized 132→143 lineage; never load legacy application weights, external pretrained features, pseudolabels or public benchmark examples. Initialize two 145 field arms from the *same* 143 full checkpoint and optimizer state. Give them the same independently drawn synthetic sections and ordinary exact/near/wrong correspondence, offset and source-visibility losses. The control receives no new loss. The treatment adds two linked objectives:

1. Generate wrong poses 1–3 mm from each section's true rigid plane, choose a hard candidate with high *pixelwise* atlas-support overlap, and intersect the two candidates' support masks at every rendered depth. Set both candidate support channels to this one intersection and zero atlas intensity outside it. Penalize fit energy unless the exact plane beats the wrong plane by the fixed margin. Atlas-support-only inputs are then identical by construction; a successful comparison must come from the spatial atlas image in relation to the source image.
2. At eroded, intact synthetic source sites, use the known dense CCF point to sample the correct atlas feature under that same mask. Contrast it with the wrong plane's feature at the same grid point and with distant within-plane feature keys; exclude negatives physically near the true CCF point. The field's existing image and atlas encoders supply the embeddings. Keep the ordinary supervised mapping loss so a better descriptor must also produce a usable tissue map.

Use two v4 and one v3 independently sampled physical section per update, 2,000 pilot updates and at least 6,000 accepted sections, with checkpoints at 0, 500 and 2,000. Log rejected attempts, physical section and base-plan identity, source hashes, optimizer/scaler/RNG state, parent checkpoint hash and the frequency/coverage of usable common-support pairs. Save to I: only. Do not deliberately reuse a physical plane with merely different background or artifacts; chance coincidences need no rejection. Preserve raw, exact-black and imperfect-brush variability without requiring a mask at inference.

The same frozen 248 eligible v4 and 243 eligible v3 DEV sections may be used for this **development mechanism gate**; their eight synthetic deformation plans have already informed prior design decisions, so this is not fresh biological validation or a final test. The predeclared primary readout is exact-versus-wrong energy ranking on a deterministic, section-seeded wrong bank with common pixelwise atlas support. Score a tie as one-half. The primary v4 cohort must include at least 120 of the 248 eligible sections, each with central support intersection fraction ≥0.08 and central support IoU ≥0.45; otherwise the mechanism gate is inconclusive, not a pass. Report both the retained and excluded cohorts and the full distribution of overlap. Require treatment full intensity ≥75% and ≥10 percentage points over the matched base-only control on the same retained sections. Require both intensity-zero and spatially scrambled-intensity treatment ablations to score ≤60% and at least 10 points below intact treatment; zero/scrambled arms must keep the same support and image intensity distribution, respectively. On the same sections, require a ≥10-point treatment gain over control on valid correct-plane sites where the rigid zero-offset map misses by >0.5 mm, and ≥5-point gain on the frozen truth-best blind-near branches within 1.5 mm. For both mapping gains, compare hit rates on identical per-section sites and report counts; require at least 100 rigid-null-failing sites or declare that mapping gate inconclusive. Report all-valid/global-supported denominators, error and deformation strain by angle and appearance; a >5-point loss in v3 exact-plane or blind-near hit rate is a material regression and fails the gate. A common-support ranking gain alone is insufficient.

If the gate passes, independently verify that fit-energy gradients or finite perturbations locally favor movement toward the true plane, then run a matched feedback-versus-no-feedback joint continuation of the direct probabilistic pose head and the **same** local deformation field. Do not compose the legacy warp with the field. If it fails, audit descriptor/hard-negative construction and atlas/source image fidelity instead of scaling the unchanged objective. Keep physical steep-oblique expert truth, animal-separated validation, calibration of electrode-site and region probabilities, GUI replacement and the public DeepSlice comparison for later qualification.
