# 118: frozen anatomy-only local pose-energy diagnostic

This is a feasibility gate, not training, deployment, or an independent animal result. Use only the frozen 112 step-512 fitter and 64 identity-disjoint synthetic DEV sections (eight per plan). Do not promote any checkpoint from this assay.

Compare an exact plane with a support-matched wrong plane at least 1.5 mm away, and compare three near planes at 0.4, 0.5, and 0.6 times the same random perturbation. Score only intensity-based local correlation inside a fixed, eroded, donor-valid and common-atlas-support interior. A zero-intensity atlas control checks that the reported score differences arise at the intensity readout; it does not establish that the atlas-conditioned mapper learned support-free features.

For a **predicted** top-14 branch that is within 1.5 mm, truth selects the nearest branch for a conditional local test. Truth does not enter the correction objective: freeze its donor-valid/predicted-support mask, differentiate the intensity energy through the frozen mapper and finite-thickness renderer, and make one prespecified normalized local-state step. Measure the resulting rigid five-point and learned-map tissue-chart errors on the same fixed mask; report post-step support retention. This oracle-near branch choice is **not** a blind inference method.

The local signal would be worth architecture work only if exact energy beats support-matched wrong energy on at least 75% of scored sections, both near-direction slopes favor truth on at least 70%, and the retained-support one-step correction improves rigid error on at least 70% with mean improvement of at least 0.05 mm. Fewer than 16 eligible predicted-near steps leaves that last condition inconclusive. These are diagnostic gates, not a guarantee of improvement in blind pose selection or real tissue. A negative result should redirect work toward better global candidate capture or anatomical correspondence rather than another scalar reranker.
