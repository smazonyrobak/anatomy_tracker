# Prospective 305: scratch-trained joint pose/deformation feedback

The 302 oracle-mask atlas information was real but unavailable at inference; 299/300 learned rankers and 304 image-only texture scoring failed. This experiment changes the *existing whole model*, not a separate post-hoc ranker. From random initialization, its image encoder predicts an antipodal full-sphere plane mixture and one candidate-independent tissue-reliability map. For each blind candidate, it renders the same supplied finite-thickness atlas slab, exposes raw source/atlas/support and spatial residuals to one shared bounded full-frame pose updater and the local mapper, rerenders after updating, and scores the result by explicit sign-invariant spatial fit, source-weighted atlas-support exit, and affine-free bounded-warp magnitude/roughness. The same source-only reliability denominator is used for every candidate; the score cannot erase a bad fit by changing a candidate's mask. The input brush is optional. The existing acquisition-context input remains available but is zero in 305, so constraint benefit is **not** claimed here and requires later explicit training/evaluation. Synthetic sample PSF metadata is supplied just as section thickness is supplied by the GUI; it is identical across candidates. No inference-time synthetic plane/warp truth, external weights/features/pseudolabels or 275/299/300 checkpoint may initialize this model.

Draw one fresh fixed 64-section TRAIN-inner readout before any optimizer batch: eight independent eligible v9 physical sections from each base 56–63, with all attempts and provenance recorded. Use bases 0–55 for gradients. The post-artifact robust fitted affine is the supervised pose gauge, while surviving observed-pixel CCF coordinates supervise local mapping. Do not deliberately repeat a section with altered artifacts/background. Ordinary v9 sampling remains fully random in plane, deformation, contrast, black/near-black/gray exterior, brush availability, fragments/folds/bubbles/seams and other recorded effects. Report denominators, capture and mean errors for ≥45° normal obliquity, <10% visible tissue, ≥2% pixels affected by fragment/fold/bubble damage, and each exterior style. Do not reject difficult but eligible planes to improve a metric. These are synthetic inner-training diagnostics, not animal-independent acquired validation.

Train one checkpoint lineage in stages, with a ceiling of 26,000 four-section pose/reliability batches followed by 4,000 two-section joint feedback batches. Pose batches optimize 275's physical-site/five-point/normal mixture objective plus source-reliability supervision; at every fourth batch also optimize exact-pose atlas-conditioned dense local mapping so the mapper is prepared before blind refinement. Save optimizer/RNG state and frozen readouts at pose batches 0, 2,000, 8,000, 16,000 and 26,000. The joint stage uses on-policy blind top-eight branches plus an exact-pose branch and one independently jittered near-truth branch per example. It retains direct pose supervision, penalizes refined pose/site error and exact-pose drift, trains the source-only reliability map, supervises surviving-fragment CCF mapping, and sends spatial-fit gradients through the rerendered pose and constrained warp. Save/read out at joint batches 0, 1,000, 2,000 and 4,000. Retain the best TRAIN-inner checkpoint without looking at DEV/CAL/FINAL or public benchmark; stop earlier on nonfinite loss, repeated material validation regression, or runtime failure, preserving evidence rather than silently resuming from a different model.

Before the joint stage, the frozen panel must show at least 45/64 cases with a ≤1.5-mm branch in the blind top 16 and mean top-16 oracle rigid-site error ≤1.5 mm. Among pose readouts, select a checkpoint satisfying both clauses if one exists, then maximize capture, minimize oracle mean, and minimize first-choice mean; if none satisfies both, use that same ordering without the satisfaction flag. If the selected checkpoint fails, report capture failure instead of testing a selector with no adequate candidate. The final joint gate is a conjunction on the *same frozen panel and same branches*: full-atlas selected mapped-site mean improves ≥0.40 mm over direct image-prior selection and ≥0.25 mm over each support-only and spatially shuffled-intensity control; at least six of eight base means improve against both controls; at least 40 support-matched near/far pairs with full evidence winning ≥70% and by ≥10 percentage points over support-only; exact-pose mean drift ≤0.25 mm; and on the 16 panel cases with highest truth-measured affine-free residual RMS (the predeclared strong-warp quartile), exact-pose local mapping improves ≥10% over zero warp. Require an explicit nonzero finite score-to-pose and score-to-warp gradient check before interpreting a training result. Selection uses no oracle mask. A failed clause blocks GUI promotion, full-scale training, calibration, and DeepSlice benchmarking; it does not prove arbitrary-plane registration impossible.

After this synthetic gate, separately evaluate donor-distinct acquired TRAIN images and ultimately independently labeled physically cut steep-oblique animals. No numeric electrode-region probability is valid before animal-level calibration and final testing. An all-angle *training distribution* is not a verified all-angle *clinical/biological accuracy claim*.

The validation gap is concrete: [Xiong et al.](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroinformatics/articles/10.3389/fninf.2018.00093/full) report real nonstandard-angle sections but no ground truth for their five real brains; [AMBIA](https://pure.knaw.nl/ws/files/992924990/Sadeghi2023.pdf) studies physical tilted sections but its expert atlas-number/quadrant labels are not independent 3D cut-plane measurements; the public [DeepSlice ground-truth collection](https://figshare.mq.edu.au/articles/dataset/DeepSlice_Source_Data_and_Ground_Truth_datasets/22802411) is coronal, not a steep-oblique test. This search does not prove that a suitable private cohort does not exist. For tiny intersections, even expert regional assignment can be ambiguous ([Bjerke et al.](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroanatomy/articles/10.3389/fnana.2018.00082/pdf)); the eventual model must express uncertainty or abstain rather than force a confident plane.
