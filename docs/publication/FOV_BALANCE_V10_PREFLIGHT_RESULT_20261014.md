# FOV-balance v10 TRAIN-only preflight result (14 October 2026)

**Decision: the limited framing-feasibility gate passed.** This is permission to test a short pose-training continuation, not evidence that the model is accurate or ready for full-scale training or deployment.

The predeclared 0.72 field-of-view rule and script were committed before the run (`2128fa4`). The first attempt (`I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/fov_balance_v10_preflight_001`) stopped on its first accepted section: the artifact wrapper does not retain the original `clean` tensor needed only for the requested image preview. No training took place. The correction (`d69964a`) deterministically replays the source generator for the first six *previewed* sections, checks that each replay has the same physical plane, and keeps their distinct original training/evaluation draws. The failed run was not deleted or silently reused. The completed output is `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/fov_balance_v10_preflight_002`.

The completed run sampled 66 attempts for 64 eligible, distinct physical sections: eight from each of synthetic TRAIN-inner bases 56–63. Its median valid-tissue fraction was **44.57%** (old 306 synthetic panel **24.67%**; three near-coronal Lupori TRAIN animals, 188 masks, **54.08%**). The old and new synthetic fractions use the v9/v10 post-artifact valid mask; the real fractions use supplied masks after nearest-neighbor resize, so they are not perfectly interchangeable. The new panel retained **8/64** sections with under 15% tissue and **7/64** with at least 45° obliquity from the nearest cardinal axis. Thus the declared median 35–65%, sparse ≥4 and oblique ≥4 gate passed. The sampled new range remains very broad: 0.84–80.99% tissue.

The [six actual-generator examples](I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/fov_balance_v10_preflight_002/first_six.png) show each section's original atlas texture, its artifact-bearing input, and the atlas slab at the frozen model's first guessed pose. They include black, gray and optional-brush exteriors, oblique cuts, and substantial crops. Visual inspection does **not** establish histology realism: the atlas-derived contrast still differs from acquired microscopy, and some views cut off much of the brain. First-choice rigid-site errors in these six range from **0.79 to 5.03 mm**; the full new panel mean is **2.91 mm**. This is a new-domain descriptive number, not a matched improvement estimate or a nonrigid/electrode outcome.

An independent post-exit audit passed: all five completed-output hashes, the committed generator/source/protocol hashes, parent checkpoint and old-panel hashes, 66 draw records, 64 distinct accepted IDs, eight sections per intended TRAIN-inner base, case/draw identity equality, quantiles, counts, error mean and gate arithmetic. The source read no acquired DEV/CAL/FINAL animals or public benchmark. No checkpoint was trained, promoted or integrated. This result supports one short, prospectively gated framing-only pose continuation; require improvements on separately sampled synthetic sections **and** no acquired-animal regression before any scaling. Keep the sparse and oblique tails and do not synthesize paired copies of a plane for different appearances.
