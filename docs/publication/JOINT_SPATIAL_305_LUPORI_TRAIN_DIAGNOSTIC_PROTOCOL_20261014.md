# 305 zero-shot acquired fluorescence diagnostic

After the frozen joint synthetic gate, evaluate the already selected 305 scratch pose checkpoint on every valid section in the four prespecified Lupori TRAIN animals (`AL1A`, `CC2B`, `CC4B`, `CC4A`). This is a zero-weight-update, no-checkpoint-selection diagnostic of domain transfer, not animal-held-out confirmation. Inference receives the whole fluorescence thumbnail resized to 192 pixels, `max(red, green)/255` with the historical image-wide p99 normalization, and four zero auxiliary channels. It receives no human alignment, automatic segmentation, brush, atlas plane, or acquisition constraint.

Only after prediction, compare first-choice and top-16 rigid five-site coordinates to the source VisuAlign/QuickNII human reference. Preserve animal, original section ID and archive member path/hash, and source DOI; the archive supplies no separate experiment identifier, so do not invent one. The source O/U/V vectors span the full thumbnail, so the 192-pixel chart uses `(0,0)`, `(191,0)`, `(0,191)`, `(191,191)`, `(95.5,95.5)` divided by 192 without shrinking the QuickNII physical extent. Report per-animal and equal-animal means, medians, ≤1.5-mm candidate counts and unsigned normal gaps. A near-human result is useful but not physical ground truth; a poor result should redirect work toward measured acquired appearance rather than more synthetic-only training. Do not read DEV/CAL/FINAL or a public benchmark and do not attach calibrated probabilities to these scores.
