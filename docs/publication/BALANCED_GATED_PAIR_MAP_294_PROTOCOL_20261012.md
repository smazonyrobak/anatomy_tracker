# 294: train match descriptors before allowing a local move

The frozen 292 matcher had nearly uniform fine-offset probabilities and moved intact tissue incorrectly. The [293 oracle diagnostic](MOVE_GATE_CEILING_293_RESULT_20261012.md) showed that a move/stay gate can reduce harm but cannot by itself supply reliable directions. This is a bounded, predeclared correction: preserve the 275/292 scratch-lineage pose and matcher weights, add a randomly initialized spatial move gate with near-zero initial output, train the source/atlas descriptors on **balanced exact correspondence** first, then jointly train the matcher and gate. It remains one deployable checkpoint containing pose and local map; at inference no fragment label or true warp is available.

Freeze a new 64-section TRAIN-inner panel before training: eight independent eligible v9 physical-plane draws for each of synthetic bases 56–63. Draw 3,000 independent accepted training presentations from bases 0–55 into `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/balanced_gated_pair_map_294_001`. Because only 519/2,400 (21.6%) uniformly drawn sections in 292 contained a displaced fragment, every second accepted training batch requires a displaced-fragment pixel; intervening batches accept any eligible section. All draws still randomize the physical plane and appearance independently, with no deliberate paired-background duplicates and no physical-section ID overlap with the new panel or previous 286/287/289/292 examples. Record the attempted and accepted fragment counts, animal/specimen/experiment IDs, draw seeds, source and target hashes. Use the same v9 atlas, finite-thickness rendering, full-angle planes, raw/black/gray exteriors and optional imperfect brush inputs. Do not use acquired DEV/CAL/FINAL, public benchmarks, external weights/features/pseudolabels, or any output outside I:.

At updates 1–600, optimize only the three-scale supervised offset classification, averaging intact and displaced-fragment losses separately with equal stratum weight whenever both exist, or using the single available stratum. This prevents a sub-percent displaced area from disappearing beneath near-zero intact labels. Keep pose and gate frozen. At updates 601–3,000, jointly optimize the mapper and gate with dense valid-tissue endpoint mm + 0.5×fragment endpoint mm + 0.1×balanced offset cross-entropy + 0.01×within-piece smoothness mm + 0.005×validity BCE + 0.05×move-gate regression. The gate regression target is the synthetic-truth optimal scalar projection of the **detached raw matcher move**, clipped to [0,1], with intact and fragment terms likewise averaged equally when both exist. This label is used only in training. Gate evidence includes observed image, rigid finite-thickness atlas intensity/support, raw local displacement, match-posterior peak/entropy/support and correspondence validity. Initialize its final bias to −4 (approximately 1.8% move); no hard-coded inference fragment mask. Batch size one, AdamW 0.0002 then 0.00008 after update 1,800, fixed readouts/checkpoints at 0, 600, 1,800 and 3,000; select the lowest new-panel mapped error after zero. Save all frozen source/protocol/parent/data hashes and train/readout logs.

On the **same new panel**, evaluate frozen 289 and 292 as controls, plus the gated 294 map and unchanged 275 first-choice pose. Report all/intact/fragment surviving-site error, per-base gains, 48/96/192 offset coverage and classification loss, fine posterior peak, gate mean, true displacement-cap exceedance, and support at final mapped sites. The descriptive advance gate is: whole-panel gain over rigid ≥0.030 mm; fragment gain ≥0.070 mm on ≥100 sites; at least 0.015-mm gain advantage over **289**; at least six of eight base means positive; intact gain nonnegative; final mapped sites outside atlas support counted; pose hashes and first-choice readouts identical; balanced panel offset CE lower at selected step than at zero. Failure stops this line before blind pose feedback or scaling. Success permits a new predicted-pose feedback experiment, not GUI deployment, public DeepSlice comparison or calibrated electrode probabilities.

This pilot still depends on one Allen average atlas and stylized artifacts. Full-angle physical accuracy and severe oblique transfer remain unproved. A later versioned generator/real-slide domain gate and animal-separated validation remain mandatory, regardless of this synthetic result.
