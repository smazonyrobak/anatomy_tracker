# 297: matched, weak-label real-volume pose-mix pilot (TRAIN-inner only)

Question: can a small dose of donor-specific acquired anatomy correct the 275 pose model's poor first-choice plane ranking and sagittal acquired-image transfer without erasing its full-sphere synthetic capture? This is a 500-batch *pilot*, not training for release. Continue the frozen 275 step-8,000 checkpoint and inherited AdamW moments twice: a synthetic-only control and a treatment. At every batch both arms see the **same four freshly and independently drawn eligible v9 synthetic planes**, their same 128 valid-pixel physical-site samples, the same 275 physical-site/five-point/normal loss, optimizer settings, and 500 updates. The treatment alone also sees one independently sampled full-sphere virtual cut from a TRAIN donor serial stack, passed through the already existing v3 slide-artifact sampler. Its Allen-affine pose loss uses exactly the same coordinate objective at **0.10 times one synthetic-batch mean**. There is no atlas render/fit-score feedback, external model weight/feature/pseudolabel, deformation-label claim, or deliberate reuse of a slice with altered artifacts. Repeated geometry can occur by chance.

Use only the frozen 105 geometry-audit donor list, not a newly selected convenient pool. Exactly 63/64 stacks are complete regular lattices; exclude incomplete donor `16207`. Exclude `14855` and `15975` because they overlap the frozen 272/275 odd-hash acquired readout. From the remaining **61 TRAIN donors**, a fixed seeded permutation (seed `20261012297+4`) reserves eight donors for a virtual-oblique weak-label inner readout and leaves **53 gradient donors**. Draw three independently sampled eligible virtual cuts per reserved donor before updates; no reserved donor enters gradients. Bound each donor's `images.npy`, `geometry.npz`, `records.jsonl`, donor receipt, and animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs by the reserved TRAIN manifest and frozen 105 row hashes. No acquired readout donor enters gradients. These non-coronal cuts are interpolated from approximately 100-µm-spaced coronal acquisitions. Their locations inherit **weak Allen section affines**, not expert anatomical truth or measured physical oblique-cut labels. Accordingly, the 0.10 term is a domain-adaptation probe, not permission to call weak affines exact.

Before updates, fix a new 64-case synthetic TRAIN-inner readout: eight independent eligible v9 planes per held-gradient deformation base 56–63, with an attempt and provenance receipt. Also retain the frozen 270-v9 and 268-v8 panels. Fix the 24 virtual-oblique weak-label cuts from the eight held-gradient donors, and the exact 28 coronal and 22 sagittal odd-hash acquired TRAIN donor median sections from 272, image-only at inference; their affines are read solely as weak orientation-family diagnostics. Read both arms at batch 0, 250, and 500 and save both checkpoints. The fixed batch-500 endpoint is assessed; no checkpoint is chosen from weak affine labels. Report synthetic top-1 mean mapped-site error, top-16 ≤1.5-mm capture, normal-axis error, per-base effects and heavy-oblique cases; virtual held-donor weak-affine mapped-site error and per-donor effects; acquired first-choice normal-family counts and weak-affine angle. Keep draw, training, panel, readout, source, checkpoint and config SHA-256 receipts.

The treatment advances to a separately designed experiment only if, at batch 500, versus the matched control, its new synthetic mean top-1 site error falls by **≥0.15 mm**, at least **6/8** synthetic base means improve, new top-16 capture loses at most **2/64**, and both frozen 270-v9 and 268-v8 top-16 captures lose at most **2/64**; the held-donor virtual-oblique weak-reference top-1 error falls by **≥0.15 mm** with at least **6/8** donor means improving; and on the separate acquired readout, sagittal normal-family hits rise by **≥4/22** and coronal hits lose at most **1/28**. All clauses are required. A failed gate blocks scaling this mixture or promoting it to inference. A passed gate licenses only a further TRAIN-inner confirmation and subsequent truly animal-separated, expert-reviewed acquired validation. It does not establish physical arbitrary-angle accuracy, calibrated probabilities, usable local deformation, GUI readiness, or a fair DeepSlice comparison. DEV/CAL/FINAL and public benchmark data remain sealed.
