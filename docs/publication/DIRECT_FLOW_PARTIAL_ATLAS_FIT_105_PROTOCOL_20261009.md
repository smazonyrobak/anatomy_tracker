# Direct-flow partial atlas fit 105 — frozen development protocol

This is a targeted continuation of the standalone, random-initialization 094 → 104 fitter and v3 one-pass pose lineages. The 104 DEV result failed because its discrete correspondence posterior learned but its near-zero gated average did not move the observed-to-atlas map. The one architectural replacement in 105 is a directly supervised, bounded continuous three-component CCF flow from the same recurrent image/atlas correlation field. Its output is zero at initialization, with a nonzero map-loss derivative. The separate identity and unmatched probabilities never multiply or suppress the training flow. Global affine change is decomposed from the field into the fitted full-frame state; the remaining field is local deformation. Existing 094 finite-thickness atlas rendering, image encoder, coarse pose fit, and optional-brush image interface are retained. No original-application or external weights/features/pseudolabels enter.

Use 4,000 independently drawn TRAIN sections with the v3 artifact generator. One synthetic draw has one independently random physical plane, appearance, background, and deformation. Do not manufacture a paired appearance of that same section or reject chance plane similarity. For each draw, fit the same four blind beam candidates as 104 (physical best, support-matched wrong, high-prior wrong, random remaining) **plus one TRAIN-only target-pose teacher**, alternating exact target and small full-frame jitter across draws. These are candidate hypotheses for one image, not additional image examples. Target pose and true dense CCF are used only for TRAIN supervision; neither is present in the blind DEV/inference selection. Compute matched/dustbin labels on surviving valid pixels with atlas support; supervise the direct map on supported survivors and identity probability from physical no-shift proximity. Use fragment-aware smoothness so displaced tissue pieces are not forced together. Score-order pairs use only the four blind hypotheses. The inherited parent pose network remains frozen until mapping and score quality pass the gate.

Initialize from the saved 104 step-1,000 checkpoint; copy its 226 class/dustbin field-output channels and zero-initialize only the new continuous-flow and identity channels. Train the coherent field, validity head, score temperature, and image/atlas descriptor branches (latter from update 1,001). Save updates 0, 1,000, and 4,000 with exact source, checkpoint, sample-draw, protocol, and output hashes. Training loss is 0.5 matched/dustbin cross-entropy + 1.0 supported true-CCF map Huber + 0.05 identity BCE + 0.05 validity BCE + 0.02 fragment-aware local smoothness + 0.5 blind candidate ordering. Teacher map sites receive threefold weight; teacher never enters the ranking pairs.

Evaluate 64 synthetic DEV sections, eight per deformation plan selected by frozen smallest SHA-256 section ID, with the same blind 14-candidate beam and exact common 094 mapped-error comparator as 104. Additionally evaluate the true target pose as an **oracle diagnostic only**, not as a selectable candidate. Choose the best trained checkpoint by plan-equal common 094 mapped error of the new selected blind candidate. Require all of: top-three physically best blind candidates' new map beats their own rigid fit by at least 0.20 mm; the new selector beats both old 094 and atlas-support-only selectors on the common mapped error by at least 0.10 mm; its within-section ordering correlation exceeds the stronger control by at least 0.05; raw, imperfect-brush, and heavy-oblique strata do not regress by more than 0.20 mm; at least six of eight deformation plans beat old 094 selection. Inspect TRAIN curves and identity/flow diagnostics but do not waive these thresholds after seeing DEV. A positive reused-DEV gate requires a separately drawn, frozen confirmation panel before pose-gradient feedback is enabled. These synthetic results cannot establish biological accuracy, calibrated uncertainty, GUI readiness, or superiority to DeepSlice.
