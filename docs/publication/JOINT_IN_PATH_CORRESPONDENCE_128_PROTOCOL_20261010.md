## Fresh development readout and advancement gate

Before training outcomes are inspected, freeze a new 256-physical-section panel from the same eight existing synthetic DEV deformation plans, exactly 32 independent pose/appearance draws per plan, with new section/physical IDs and no deliberate plane/background pairs. Preserve ineligible cases and report them. It tests fresh sections, **not new biological animals or untouched deformation plans**. On every eligible section, compare frozen 122, same-draw control and treatment with the same blind-16 beam, finite-thickness atlas rendering and mapped/rigid valid-tissue metrics. Report prior/all-160 original proposal capture, blind-16 original and corrected capture, selected original-or-corrected action, selected mapped and rigid error, plan-equal and section-weighted means, each of eight plans, raw/exact-black/imperfect-brush modes and 0–15/15–30/30–45/45–55-degree nearest-cardinal-angle bins. Include matched support/area blind candidate-pair ranking with atlas-intensity-zero and source-swap controls, noting these conditional perturbations cannot prove whole-pipeline image independence. Evaluate the existing six coronal and eight sagittal identity-disjoint weak-real DEV donors only as weak-affine guardrails, not anatomical truth. No final animals, calibration set, physical steep-oblique benchmark or DeepSlice comparison.

The primary gate is **treatment versus matched control**: at least 0.30-mm reduction in plan-equal selected mapped-tissue error and at least a ten-percentage-point increase in the fraction selected within 1.5 mm, with at least six of eight plans non-worse and no selected rigid-gauge regression. Blind-16 and all-160 original mapped capture must not each drop by more than five percentage points. Raw/no-brush selected mapped error must not regress, and treatment's support-matched near-versus-wrong pair win fraction must exceed its atlas-intensity-zero and source-swap controls by at least ten percentage points with at least 64 scored pairs. Coronal/sagittal donor-equal weak-affine discrepancies may not regress by >0.20 mm against either frozen 122 or the matched control, nor any donor by >0.50 mm. Report every failed condition without waiver. If these conditions fail, do not scale this CE, infer fit-to-pose benefit, promote the model or calibrate uncertainty from it. If they pass, first check whether the direct pose distribution improves **without** relying on matcher selection, then separately design a trustworthy fitted-anatomy-to-pose-feedback test and independent acquired-animal validation.
