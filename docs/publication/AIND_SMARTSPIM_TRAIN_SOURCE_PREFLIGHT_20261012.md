# AIND SmartSPIM registered-volume TRAIN source: metadata preflight (2026-10-12)

**Decision:** viable as a *small, independent-animal image-source pilot* for randomly initialized synthetic arbitrary-plane TRAIN generation, after orientation and registration QC. These registered volumes add actual specimens' fluorescence texture, not independent native geometry, expert plane truth, or physical oblique-section validation. No image chunks, transforms, model weights, DEV/CAL/FINAL subjects, or benchmark data were downloaded in this preflight.

The [AIND public bucket](https://allenneuraldynamics.github.io/data.html#aind-open-data) is `s3://aind-open-data` (`us-west-2`, anonymous reads). The [AWS registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/allen-nd-open-data/) and each chosen `data_description.json` say **CC BY 4.0**; retain attribution, exact asset names, subject IDs, channels, and access date. AIND identifies raw assets by modality/subject/acquisition and stores derived assets separately. The [SmartSPIM pipeline](https://github.com/AllenNeuralDynamics/aind-smartspim-pipeline) documents `image_atlas_alignment` as registered images plus transforms and `image_tile_fusing` as stitched, unregistered OME-Zarr.

Metadata-only S3 inspection found one chosen processed asset per subject below. Each link points to the actual registered level-0 `.zarray` under `image_atlas_alignment/<channel>/OMEZarr/image.zarr/0/`; each reports shape `[1,1,456,320,528]`, float32, while its `.zattrs` reports spatial scale 25 µm on all three axes. Every listed channel directory also contains `affine_transforms.mat`, `ccf_ls_warp_transforms.nii.gz`, and `ls_ccf_warp_transforms.nii.gz`. All 12 chosen assets' `subject.json` match the subject in the name, and `data_description.json` calls them derived SmartSPIM/SPIM data with CC BY 4.0.

The asset-level `processing.json` cites registration code v0.0.17, [whose registration returns the ANTs warped image](https://github.com/AllenNeuralDynamics/aind-smartspim-ccf-registration/blob/v0.0.17/code/aind_ccf_reg/register.py#L257-L331) and writes it to OME-Zarr.

**Coordinates:** OME metadata names spatial array axes `z,y,x` and gives scale, but *no explicit CCF origin/direction*. The 568105 registration record (also checked for 654302, 659080, 671413) says reference `orientations={left_to_right:0, superior_to_inferior:1, anterior_to_posterior:2}`. Thus the working anatomical interpretation of `[456,320,528]` is `[ML rightward,DV inferior,AP posterior]`, and a candidate array transpose to this project's `[AP,DV,ML]` order is `(2,1,0)`. Do not use that as a proven voxel-to-CCF transform yet: verify origin, handedness, and anatomical landmarks on a small orthogonal QC sample against the pinned CCF and check the actual registration/reference grid. A generic `z,y,x` label is not evidence that `z=AP`. The three transform files exist but their ANTs push/pull direction also needs a separate image-coordinate check before native-space use.

**Pilot, not validation:** freeze these exact source-asset identities and one registration channel per subject, then QC a few coarse chunks/orthogonal planes on `I:` only. Keep all repeated acquisitions/derivatives of one subject together (568105, 651050, 663289 have additional acquisitions; 669749 has two stitched derivatives of one acquisition). `subject.json` identifies 659080/659081, 669748/669749, and 671413/671414 as same-parent pairs; keep each pair together too. One concrete small split is TRAIN `568105,651050,663289,659080,659081,662367,663599`; inner development `671413,671414,669748,669749`; reserve `654302` (only 561/593 channel) for an out-of-channel stress check, not model selection. All sampled planes/augmentations from any animal inherit its split. Compare one-atlas versus multi-subject-source arms from **fresh random initialization** with the same model, slice budget, plane draws, artifacts, and optimizer schedule; use only image pixels and registration-derived weak CCF geometry for TRAIN labels, no prior weights/features/pseudolabels. Report subject- and family-equal internal development errors plus registration QC, not per-slice pseudo-replication. No public benchmark, sealed animal, or `runs/pose_gauge_map_286_001` is involved.

**Limit:** SmartSPIM is cleared-tissue, channel/genotype-dependent fluorescence, not the target histology modality. Registration to one CCF can suppress native-shape differences, imprint alignment bias, and make oblique reslices virtual rather than acquired. Any gain only supports source-diversity feasibility under weak registered labels; independent physical oblique animal validation remains necessary.
