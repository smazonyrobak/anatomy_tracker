# 094: train a spatial verifier of the model's own wrong planes

Freeze this before any 094 TRAIN draw. The 092 score failed on the exact 086/061 synthetic DEV beam: selected mapped error 2.685 mm versus inherited 089's 2.592 mm, and the fit energy followed atlas support (Spearman −0.961) rather than physical accuracy. The read-only 093 diagnostic localized recoverable evidence: actual fine-grid correct-match fraction correlated 0.735 with lower candidate error on 64 fixed DEV sections, while 092's predicted match reliability had site AUROC 0.502 and candidate-level correlation 0.035. Those DEV sections informed the design and are not a fresh test.

Continue the single scratch-lineage 092 model and matcher. Replace the shallow eight-statistic reliability head and support-averaged fit energy, not the direct multimodal arbitrary-plane pose head, finite-thickness renderer or affine-free local tissue map. A new spatial verifier receives the *full* coarse/fine local 3-D match distributions and support, pooled observed-image features, candidate-conditioned atlas/image spatial features, local match displacement and chart position. It predicts a per-query probability of a correct anatomical match. An image-only soft tissue-validity head is trained from synthetic valid masks but neither a mask nor an external segmentation/brush is required at inference. Use the verifier's predicted inliers and validity on alternating coarse grid cells for a robust, ridge-constrained bounded full-plane fit; rerender the atlas and score conditional predicted inlier fraction on held-out fine cells. Include a small explicit atlas-overlap and pose-correction cost, rather than penalizing all empty-atlas cells at a floor value. The fitted atlas spatial evidence feeds the existing local mapper. All fit/score operations needed for learning stay differentiable to the selected pose state, matcher and direct prior logits; synthetic truth is never a model input.

Draw 20,000 independently randomized eligible arbitrary-plane TRAIN sections with one appearance/geometry per draw and preserved physical-section/virtual-subject provenance, batch one. On each section form the existing blind 8-old/6-anchor beam and choose four *existing* branches for TRAIN only: the smallest known physical error, the atlas-support-nearest candidate at least 0.5 mm worse, a high-prior wrong candidate at least 1.0 mm worse, and a remaining random/hard candidate; document fallbacks and deduplicate. Never insert a true plane as an inference candidate. For the first 2,000 sections train only the new spatial correct-match and image-validity heads while the 092 parent remains frozen. For the remaining 18,000 continue the same checkpoint lineage and jointly train direct pose, atlas matcher, constrained fitter, local mapper and the shared image encoder at conservative learning rates. Supervise correct-match logits on synthetic surviving tissue at 2.0 mm coarse/1.5 mm fine tolerance, with missing/out-of-window/wrong matches as negatives; supervise image-only validity separately; retain valid-bin correspondence CE. Combine full-bank direct pose supervision, mapped physical error and plausible warp penalty on the best branch, and pairwise physical-error ranking among the four candidates. Ranking/fit loss must backpropagate through atlas rendering and fitting to old and anchor pose heads and direct prior logits. Save and hash checkpoints at 0/2,000/8,000/20,000, full draw/provenance records and train curves; retain the best DEV checkpoint afterward. All files, caches, temp and weights stay on I:.

After TRAIN exits, independently evaluate all checkpoints on the same exact 086 fixed 14 branch IDs and 246 eligible 061 synthetic DEV sections/eight deformation plans, plus the model's natural updated beam separately. Use identical 1,024 surviving pixels and mapped96 physical error for the matched historical comparison; assess selected mapped256 error, best14, score/error and score/support association, raw/black/imperfect-brush and plan strata, fit correction magnitude and warp. Evaluate per-site correct-match AUROC and candidate-level correct-match association on the 182 DEV sections *not* inspected in 093, using truth solely as a readout. The prespecified synthetic advancement gate is fixed-beam selected mapped96 ≤2.192 mm, score/lower-error Spearman ≥0.30, best14 mapped96 ≤1.011 mm, site correct-match AUROC ≥0.70, candidate-mean predicted-inlier/lower-error Spearman ≥0.30, no appearance stratum >0.15 mm worse than 089, and nonzero fit-only gradients to both old and anchor pose heads. Improvement in TRAIN loss or oracle availability alone is not a gate pass.

This is still an internal development experiment. If it passes, continue exposure and perform acquired donor-disjoint evaluation before calibration or GUI promotion. If it fails, distinguish feature-level discrimination, fit geometry, and pose capture using the saved raw candidates. No public DeepSlice benchmark, untouched final animals, calibrated region probabilities, or GUI replacement is claimed here. [SLIV-Reg](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18683) motivates 2-D/3-D spatial matching and robust pose fitting, while [DeepSlice](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-41645-4) illustrates the scale and animal-level validation needed for a narrower coronal task; neither proves this architecture will work on histology.
