# Development reset: spend compute only on a plausible path to blind accuracy

The goal is unchanged: one scratch-trained model for any brain-intersecting cutting plane, jointly producing a plane distribution and a usable local tissue-to-CCF map; optional brush and acquisition clues; eventually animal-calibrated electrode/region uncertainty and a GUI replacement. No current checkpoint qualifies. All development stays on `I:`. This note changes the *decision process*, not the frozen experiments.

## What the completed work actually established

- The stronger 22-million-parameter 309 research baseline still has **2.469-mm mean blind first-choice** error on its 256-case v10 TRAIN-inner synthetic panel, versus **0.931 mm for its unavailable best-of-16 choice**. Another 20,000 four-image batches improved first choice by only 0.126 mm; training loss and candidate capture improved more than selection. The 64 sparse cuts averaged 4.669 mm. These are synthetic, not physical-cut errors.
- The 6.23-million-parameter C4 direct joint model trained for 12,000 batches and remained worse than 309 on the matched inner, steep, and sparse panels. Its predicted local map changed mean error by less than 0.001 mm. Short 327/328 scratch runs taught us that balanced acquired coronal/sagittal exposure can fix *dominant-axis family* quickly, while lowering the weak-real loss moved the sparse mean mainly through a few outliers. Neither made C4 a useful mapper or reliable sparse selector.
- A single dense CCF-coordinate field plus differentiable plane fitting (258/260) learned validity and some position but collapsed toward one nearly constant normal: about 54° mean unsigned axis error. Appending a dense field to a pose head (075/076) also failed to choose better planes. Atlas-image fitting (305), direct privileged correlation (309), and several scalar candidate scores failed their blind all-candidate controls. Do not rename one of these mechanisms and run it again.
- The current post-artifact synthetic pose convention leaves only about **0.109 mm** mean exact-pose local residual on v10; its refit reduces the matched pre-refit residual from 0.146 mm by only 0.038 mm, so the refit is not the millimetre-scale blind-selection failure. This does not mean acquired local deformation is negligible. On one TRAIN Lupori animal, the human field improves an image-agreement assay, but 309/312/316 learned fields do not materially beat a zero field. More synthetic map epochs or artificially larger warps are not evidence-based remedies.
- Acquired coronal/sagittal *weak affines* and virtual oblique cuts are not independently measured physical arbitrary-plane pose truth. An independently acquired registered microCT volume caused a large 309 source-shift failure, confounded by modality and individual anatomy. There is no verified multi-animal physical steep-oblique calibration/final cohort. Consequently no model can yet earn a numerical all-angle or electrode-region probability claim.
- Two plausible shortcuts now have direct negative controls. Allowing map loss to train pose in matched 312 arms improved synthetic first choice by only 0.0286 mm, with a paired interval crossing zero; the acquired map signal is too weak to use as a trusted pose judge. Mixing one registered individual red-volume cut into each four-cut batch in 319 improved fresh atlas first choice by only 0.00085 mm, improved two DEV-volume donors by 0.103 mm, and worsened acquired AL1A mean by 0.220 mm; its predeclared gate failed. A single licensed AIND SmartSPIM specimen substituted into the same 256 virtual cuts raised 309 error from 2.469 to 4.416 mm (4.383 mm with ML mirroring) and reduced top-16 capture from 226 to 41 (40). These are different source contrasts and not a proof that donor-diverse scratch training cannot work, but another late source-mix continuation would repeat a failed question.
- A frozen 256-case branch audit rules out an easy ranking-loss repair: the unavailable top-16 minimizer of the training composite cost has 0.962-mm mean site error, versus 0.931 mm for the unavailable site-error minimizer. Their 0.031-mm difference is far smaller than the 1.538-mm first-choice regret. The raw 309 selector also differs from the inherited fitted-rescoring path in generic inference; do not treat an unevaluated inference path as the reported model.

## One critical path, with stop rules

1. **Freeze 309 as the research comparator; retire C4 321/327/328 as production candidates.** No further C4 loss-weight sweep, longer unchanged exposure, scalar atlas-score reranker, background-only tweak, or map-only continuation. Preserve all outputs and commit decisions; do not delete frozen runs.
2. **Reject a tempting new model before spending GPU.** A proposed 512-branch dense coordinate field failed an analytic control: every candidate can predict zero local correction, making its fitted-plane residual exactly zero even for an anatomically wrong plane. Its score then reduces to C4's failed image-only candidate score. The untracked prototype was removed without training. A dense field becomes a valid experiment only if the *observed slice versus that particular atlas plane* supplies nontrivial spatial evidence under a zero-warp and support-only control; calling a field “joint” does not supply that evidence. The existing 128/305/309/322 atlas-comparison failures mean another unchanged matcher is not the answer.
3. **Prioritize new discriminative evidence and target truth.** The immediate non-GPU work is to authenticate a signed specimen-volume-to-CCF coordinate chain and obtain independently referenced physical sections, especially steep obliques. The AIND registered asset is licensed but its exact signed grid is unresolved; it can supply an appearance stress test, not exact pose labels. No verified public multi-animal steep-oblique cohort with independent per-section truth emerged from the current source search. The existing Allen average, another copy of its appearance, weak Allen affines, and virtual cuts are not new independent anatomy. Do not download a donor cohort or run another 2,000-batch source-mix pilot until the label chain and a mechanistically different training question are written down. If candidate-specific anatomy can be shown to improve *blind* choice beyond shuffled-intensity/support controls on fresh TRAIN-only cases, implement exactly one joint pose/map architecture from scratch; otherwise do not buy a long run.
4. **Gate any new architecture early and casewise.** Compare against frozen 309 on the same fresh full-sphere synthetic cases: first-choice physical mapped-site mean and >5-mm tail must both improve, including steep cuts; sparse top-16 capture must not regress. Require a supplied-true-plane local gain over zero map, not just falling map loss. Retain donor-disjoint acquired coronal **and** sagittal behavior. Use a predicted tissue/reliability mask at inference; generator masks are training truth only. Report the paired median and per-case winners as well as means, so three rescued outliers cannot trigger scale-up. Also report error and >5-mm risk among cases accepted by a fixed image-observable rule, because 309's branch masses barely flag its own mistakes; do not choose the rule on the cases used to judge it. Stop a failed mechanism at the first meaningful readout, not after another exposure sweep.
5. **Scale only a gated winner** with fresh independently sampled planes, staged training/validation curves, and guarded best-checkpoint retention. Roughly a million presentations is conditional on animal-separated improvement, not a quota. Qualify on independent physically cut animals with 3-D plane/landmark and electrode references before calibration or GUI replacement; if those data remain unavailable, explicitly withhold all-angle accuracy and 90%-region claims. Integrate the eventual qualified model into the existing GUI with optional clues, then perform the fair coronal DeepSlice comparison only after strong internal real-animal gates. No benchmark tuning.

The immediate result of step 2 is an *analytic kill*, not another failed training curve. Report only a changed scientific conclusion, completion, or an error; no frequent polling/status messages.
