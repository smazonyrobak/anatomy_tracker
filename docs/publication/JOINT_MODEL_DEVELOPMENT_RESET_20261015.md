# Development reset: spend compute only on a plausible path to blind accuracy

The goal is unchanged: one scratch-trained model for any brain-intersecting cutting plane, jointly producing a plane distribution and a usable local tissue-to-CCF map; optional brush and acquisition clues; eventually animal-calibrated electrode/region uncertainty and a GUI replacement. No current checkpoint qualifies. All development stays on `I:`. This note changes the *decision process*, not the frozen experiments.

## What the completed work actually established

- The stronger 22-million-parameter 309 research baseline still has **2.469-mm mean blind first-choice** error on its 256-case v10 TRAIN-inner synthetic panel, versus **0.931 mm for its unavailable best-of-16 choice**. Another 20,000 four-image batches improved first choice by only 0.126 mm; training loss and candidate capture improved more than selection. The 64 sparse cuts averaged 4.669 mm. These are synthetic, not physical-cut errors.
- The 6.23-million-parameter C4 direct joint model trained for 12,000 batches and remained worse than 309 on the matched inner, steep, and sparse panels. Its predicted local map changed mean error by less than 0.001 mm. Short 327/328 scratch runs taught us that balanced acquired coronal/sagittal exposure can fix *dominant-axis family* quickly, while lowering the weak-real loss moved the sparse mean mainly through a few outliers. Neither made C4 a useful mapper or reliable sparse selector.
- A single dense CCF-coordinate field plus differentiable plane fitting (258/260) learned validity and some position but collapsed toward one nearly constant normal: about 54° mean unsigned axis error. Appending a dense field to a pose head (075/076) also failed to choose better planes. Atlas-image fitting (305), direct privileged correlation (309), and several scalar candidate scores failed their blind all-candidate controls. Do not rename one of these mechanisms and run it again.
- The current post-artifact synthetic pose convention leaves only about **0.109 mm** mean exact-pose local residual on v10; this does not mean acquired local deformation is negligible. On one TRAIN Lupori animal, the human field improves an image-agreement assay, but 309/312/316 learned fields do not materially beat a zero field. More synthetic map epochs are not an evidence-based remedy.
- Acquired coronal/sagittal *weak affines* and virtual oblique cuts are not independently measured physical arbitrary-plane pose truth. An independently acquired registered microCT volume caused a large 309 source-shift failure, confounded by modality and individual anatomy. There is no verified multi-animal physical steep-oblique calibration/final cohort. Consequently no model can yet earn a numerical all-angle or electrode-region probability claim.

## One critical path, with stop rules

1. **Freeze 309 as the research comparator; retire C4 321/327/328 as production candidates.** No further C4 loss-weight sweep, longer unchanged exposure, scalar atlas-score reranker, background-only tweak, or map-only continuation. Preserve all outputs and commit decisions; do not delete frozen runs.
2. **Make one bounded representation test, not a new full training campaign.** The testable idea is a *multi-hypothesis spatial CCF-coordinate vote*: retain full-sphere competing plane hypotheses; each hypothesis predicts a tissue-reliability mask and observed-pixel CCF field; a robust fit yields that hypothesis's global plane and its non-affine local residual. Train joint plane, field and hypothesis-selection losses from independently drawn v10 synthetic truth, with matched weak acquired exposure. This differs from the failed *single averaged field* of 258 and the image-only map of C4. It is only a hypothesis, not a promised solution; predicted masks, never generator masks, must drive inference. Do not shrink the candidate bank enough to hide a capture failure. A spatial field may still average/overfit or learn the atlas template rather than transferable anatomy.
3. **Falsify it early on frozen TRAIN-inner panels**, compared case by case with 309 on the *same* images. First-choice physical mapped-site mean and >5-mm tail must both improve, including steep cuts; the sparse top-16 capture must not regress. Require useful local gain at supplied true pose over zero map, not merely lower map training loss. Check acquired coronal **and** sagittal donor-disjoint family retention, input dependence, predicted-mask ablation, and support-only/shuffled-image controls. Stop at the first scheduled readout if the field again collapses to a nearly constant normal, the selected plane does not improve, or the map remains negligible. Do not turn a relative win driven by three outliers into a scale decision.
4. **Only after a convincing blind synthetic gain**, increase fresh *independent plane* presentations in stages while monitoring the same held-out cases, source/angle/visibility/artifact strata and acquired-donor gaps. Save the best guarded checkpoint and stop when validation plateaus. The target of roughly a million presentations is conditional on improvement, not a quota. Try source-diverse, rights-cleared 3-D training material only in a matched ablation; do not assume another atlas-derived appearance proves biological generalization.
5. **Qualify on new physical animals before deployment.** Obtain independent 3-D plane/landmark references for physically cut oblique slices and local map/electrode marks, prospectively reserve whole animals for calibration and final testing, and measure ambiguous/low-tissue abstention. If such truth remains unavailable, continue research but explicitly withhold all-angle accuracy, calibrated 90%-region, and GUI-replacement claims. Integrate the eventual guarded checkpoint into the existing GUI with optional clues, then perform the fair coronal DeepSlice comparison only after internal real-animal gates are strong. No benchmark tuning.

The immediate deliverable of step 2 is a short matched falsification result—not another proliferation of architectures. Report only a changed scientific conclusion, completion, or an error; no frequent polling/status messages.
