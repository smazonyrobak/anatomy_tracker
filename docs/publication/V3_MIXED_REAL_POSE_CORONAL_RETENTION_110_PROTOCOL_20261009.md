# Coronal-teacher retention continuation 110 — frozen protocol

The 108 shared-feature continuation improved arbitrary-plane synthetic proposal coverage and weak sagittal alignment, but harmed the separate weak coronal DEV donors. The matched heads-only 109 control tests whether freezing the image features avoids that regression; 110 instead tests whether those features can adapt while retaining the pre-unfreeze coronal behavior. Neither weak Allen affine is expert truth, and a teacher prediction is not a target anatomical location.

Branch from the exact 108 step-653 model, five-group AdamW optimizer and CPU/CUDA/subject random-number states. Replay updates 654–1,959 with the same saved coronal and sagittal TRAIN schedules, synthetic subjects and per-row geometry/appearance/artifact seeds, retry order, sampled surviving pixels, base synthetic and weak-real losses, and cosine learning-rate phase as 108. Train pose, anchor-pose, anchor-global, encoder and lateral modules at 108's stage-two rates; all other modules stay frozen. The only additional objective uses the one acquired coronal **TRAIN** image in each batch and a frozen copy of the exact step-653 model. Compute teacher-to-student KL over all 160 joint pose-mode/reflection probabilities, and mean 3-D displacement (mm) of the corresponding five physical candidate points, averaged with the teacher's branch probabilities. Add **2.0 × (KL + weighted five-point displacement in mm)** to the unchanged 108 base loss. The teacher never sees DEV, sealed test, expert truth or a public benchmark; it only preserves a previous model's outputs on TRAIN images, so it cannot certify anatomical correctness. Log the two retention components and base loss separately.

Save inherited step 653 and terminal step 1,959, the optimizer/RNG states and exact source/data/checkpoint/schedule hashes. Fail if any stage-two train draw differs from frozen 108, and verify that shared image weights did change. Keep all model development, outputs and caches on I:. After training exits, evaluate terminal 110 on the same **reused** 64-section/eight-plan full-angle synthetic DEV panel and six coronal plus eight sagittal weak-real DEV donors as 108/109, with their identical physical metrics. The preregistered advancement gate relative to inherited step 653 requires both plan-equal synthetic best-of-14 and prior-selected cutting-plane errors to fall by **at least 0.20 mm**, coronal weak-affine donor-equal five-point discrepancy to rise by **no more than 0.20 mm**, and sagittal discrepancy to fall by **at least 0.50 mm** with improvement in **at least five of eight donors**. Report every component and 108/109 paired comparisons, including failures; a passing reused-DEV gate is not independent confirmation.
