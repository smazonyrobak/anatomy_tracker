# 090: learn whole-plane anatomical compatibility, not another frozen score

Freeze this protocol before the first 090 training draw. This is a synthetic-development mechanism experiment; it does not select a deployable checkpoint or calibrate probabilities.

## Why this representation

On 089's unchanged 14-branch DEV beam, the physically best mapped candidate is 0.961 mm but the selected candidate is 2.592 mm, with within-section score/error Spearman only 0.141. The learned joint pose feedback shifted selected centres by only ~92 µm. The 083 local descriptor has correspondence signal yet its raw confidence selected 3.065 mm rigid error (084); the 085 all-branch scalar fitted-score regression and 088 no-match score also failed. Repeating that fixed-feature scalar recipe or expanding the warp is not justified. The next test must give the ranker a *new, globally discriminative image–atlas representation*, trained against within-section hard alternatives.

This direction is supported, with limits, by [Antanavicius et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.06662), who used contrastive embeddings to retrieve atlas plates for partial mouse sections on a very small coronal test set; [Mok et al.](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Mok_Modality-Agnostic_Structural_Image_Representation_Learning_for_Deformable_Multi-Modality_Medical_Image_CVPR_2024_paper.html), who used anatomy-aware contrastive structural features for multimodal registration; and a [2026 MICCAI workshop study](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026-sat/MLMI_039.html) arguing that a learned cross-modal metric can generalize better than an amortized warp. None proves arbitrary-plane single-mouse-slice retrieval or the chosen architecture. DeepSlice's [large coronal training study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-41645-4) used roughly 0.9 million synthetic plus large real collections, but that is a later scale reference, not a reason to prematurely benchmark or train a million presentations now.

## Frozen method and data

Initialize the plane/map model from 089 batch 6,000 (all its ancestry is project-trained from random initialization). Freeze its pose, map and 083 feedback path. Add a randomly initialized *dual image–atlas plane encoder*: a query branch sees the 5-channel observed slice once; a separate atlas branch sees finite-thickness CCF intensity and support rendered at each candidate's full arbitrary-plane pose/reflection with the acquisition PSF. Both branches preserve coarse spatial layout through pooled multi-resolution cells before projecting into a normalized embedding. Their cosine similarity with one learned, bounded temperature is a candidate compatibility score. No external weights, features, pseudolabels, segmentation model or automatic tissue selection.

For each newly and independently sampled eligible synthetic TRAIN physical section, keep **one** observed slice with one random appearance/artifact draw. Do not make paired black/non-black or deliberately repeated physical-section variants. From the frozen 089 pose prior construct its blind 8-old/6-anchor candidate beam, plus the *known physical plane as a training-only positive*. These 15 atlas planes are alternatives for the same query, not 15 duplicate image examples. Use the sampled surviving-tissue CCF coordinates to assign a soft within-section target proportional to exp(−rigid physical error / 0.5 mm), including the exact-plane positive and nearby candidates; preserve support rather than forcing a single positive when planes are nearly equivalent. Optimize listwise cross-entropy between that target and the learned score softmax. The exact plane must never be inserted into inference beams. Train only the two new encoders and temperature initially; this isolates whether global anatomy can discriminate candidates before further joint fine-tuning. Keep all dataset, output, source, atlas and checkpoint provenance on I:.

Use a modest 15,000 accepted-section pilot with batch size one, checkpoints at 0/3,000/8,000/15,000 and cosine learning-rate decay. Stop early only for technical failure; do not choose or tune on the frozen DEV panel mid-run. Record draw IDs, appearance, candidate geometry, support, physical-error targets, loss and gradient trend, exact hashes, and GPU-efficient candidate chunking. The current selected-plane baseline is 089 batch 6,000 at 2.592 mm mapped error on the fixed 086 beam. 

## Readout and gate

After the run exits, independently verify the receipt and evaluate all four prespecified checkpoints on the same frozen 061 DEV sections and **exact 086 candidate IDs**, joining the new score to the already verified 089 batch-6,000 mapped physical errors. Report top-1/top-3/top-5 best-available mapped error, score-vs-physical rank, selected error and regret, low-support and all three appearance strata, and overlap with the updated-prior on-policy beam. Hold the final scorer to at least a 0.4 mm reduction in selected mapped error from the exact 089 baseline of 2.5919 mm on this fixed beam, plan-equal score/error Spearman rho ≥0.30, and no more than 0.15 mm regression from the exact-black 089 baseline of 2.5624 mm; a lower contrastive training loss alone is insufficient. Because 090 initially freezes pose/map, any gain is specifically new global compatibility evidence. If it passes, integrate that score with the joint 089 pose/map path and then train/evaluate the entire corrected-candidate stack jointly. If it fails, examine positive/negative score separation by physical distance and tissue support before increasing exposure; do not simply rerun the 085 scalar score.

All probabilities remain uncalibrated. Do not access real final-test animals or public DeepSlice benchmark, and do not claim 90% region assignment until animal-separated calibration and point-accuracy noninferiority are demonstrated.
