# 078: real-data coverage decision after the failed pose-capture pilots

The 019–077 development sequence repeatedly reached about 1 mm with a **truth-chosen** synthetic pose candidate but about 2.6 mm for the blindly selected one. The 077 atlas-context correction changed the latter by only 0.016 mm and worsened the near-true candidate. This is not evidence that every arbitrary-plane slice is unidentifiable; it is evidence that the present model/data combination has not learned reliable blind capture and selection. Do not scale 077, rerun the same image review, calibrate its candidate weights, or promote it to the GUI.

The current biological real-image source is Allen Product 5, with exact `Donor.id` grouping and registered TSV/TVR pose affines. Its 263,754 reserved TRAIN images span 1,885 donors, but the registered plane normals are almost entirely near-coronal (median 4.91° off the AP axis; none above 30°). The six-donor DEV set is also near-coronal. These affines are useful weak references and the images supply real appearance variation; they are not independently adjudicated 3-D pose or dense-deformation ground truth. The historical AtlasPose Product-5 data use the same registration type, despite earlier descriptions calling it “trusted” relative to Product 8. See [the TRAIN inventory](RESERVED_ALLEN_TRAIN_IMAGES_20260930.md), [DEV geometry](ALLEN_RAW_PROPOSAL_GEOMETRY_20260928.md), and [data card](../../DATA_CARD.md). Synthetic held-out deformation identities all reuse one Allen atlas and are not biological animals.

A focused primary-source search did **not** identify a ready replacement satisfying all of: at least ten identifiable independent mouse volumes, reproducible subject-to-CCF transforms, manageable download, and clear image-data reuse rights. [mBrainAligner](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-021-01334-w) supplies [four small downsampled example volumes](https://github.com/Vaa3D/vaa3d_tools/tree/master/hackathon/mBrainAligner/examples/subject) across different modalities and [registered-result files](https://github.com/Vaa3D/vaa3d_tools/tree/master/hackathon/mBrainAligner/examples/registered_results), but not a verified uniform dense transform and license for a multi-animal training cohort; its [software license](https://github.com/Vaa3D/Vaa3D_Wiki/wiki/License_mBrainAligner) does not establish image-data rights. A [single registered X-ray brain](https://zenodo.org/records/10992465) packages volume and Elastix outputs, but is one animal, a different modality and has no stated license on its record. A [seven-mouse coronal microscopy collection](https://zenodo.org/records/7419283) includes QuickNII planes, VisuAlign maps, masks and displacement fields, but is near-coronal, packaged as a 48.7-GB archive, and cannot by itself validate arbitrary obliques. Do not silently convert any of these registrations into expert truth.

Near-term model work can continue on the held-out synthetic all-plane development track and real near-coronal weak-reference track, with the limitations above explicit. The next architecture decision should prioritize a **new source of whole-slice anatomical evidence for globally coupled pose**, not a third local reranker or greater deformation freedom. Any candidate must improve blindly selected physical tissue error and preserve near-true proposals on the same identity-disjoint panel before larger exposure. The four mBrainAligner samples are at most a bounded 3-D reslicing/coordinate-chain feasibility check, not broad training data.

For actual release and numerical probability claims, the independently collected, animal-split, cardinal-and-oblique expert-pose and landmark cohort in [the study protocol](STUDY_PROTOCOL.md) remains necessary. Untouched final-test animals and the public DeepSlice comparison stay closed until the model is credible on development data. This is a data-quality decision, not a claim of model superiority or a request to relax the any-plane requirement.
