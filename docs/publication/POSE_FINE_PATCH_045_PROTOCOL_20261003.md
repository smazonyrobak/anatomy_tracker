# 045 candidate-conditioned fine correspondence stage

044 established that 041's atlas-key short lists usually contain a key near synthetic tissue truth, whereas 043 showed that the highest learned key rarely belongs to a coherent correct group. The targeted change is an oriented, finite-thickness **high-resolution patch comparison at the keys proposed by the actual off-plane model**, not another pose solver. Coarse-to-fine cross-modal matching is supported as a design precedent by [2D3D-MATR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/papers/Li_2D3D-MATR_2D-3D_Matching_Transformer_for_Detection-Free_Registration_Between_Images_and_ICCV_2023_paper.pdf); its camera/point-cloud accuracy does not establish histology accuracy. [ContraReg](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10415941/) supports locally learned cross-modal features for deformable medical registration. Neither paper supplies weights or labels here.

Keep the scratch-lineage 019 probabilistic pose model and 041 3D coarse matcher frozen for this first curriculum stage. Continue the scratch-trained 025 observed/atlas patch encoder within the eventual joint model; no external pretrained features or legacy-model pseudolabels enter. Render each candidate atlas patch at its **own** 041 key location and proposal plane orientation with the actual finite section-thickness weights. Use top-32 041 coarse keys, including wrong-depth, wrong-plane and similar-looking negatives. Randomly generate two independent synthetic TRAIN sections per batch with arbitrary planes and one appearance each; do not generate paired background variants or deliberately reuse a physical section. Sample top-one, another proposal and the physically best proposal during TRAIN only. Known dense synthetic CCF targets supervise a soft physical-distance list loss; negative-only lists teach the matcher to reject absent matches. A 500-batch checkpoint is a stage boundary, not a completed model.

Before looking at the outcome, compare the inherited 025 initialization (batch zero) and the 045 batch-500 checkpoint on the unchanged 177 eligible 037 synthetic DEV sections (eight deformation identities). Query the same visible 16×16 cells and the original 019 top eight candidates. Report equal-identity exact-key top-one recall at 0.5 and 1.5 mm for the prior top-one and physically best-eight candidate, including raw/black/imperfect-brush strata, availability and no-match cases. The minimal promising correspondence criterion is ≥55% top-one recall at 1.5 mm on the physically best candidate and a ≥15-percentage-point improvement over batch zero, without losing >5 points on any appearance stratum. Independently report patch compute/runtime and worst-case GPU memory. If these are not met, inspect matching errors and train/curriculum behavior before adding a ranker.

This stage does **not** establish improved physical pose or candidate selection. After correspondence improves, fit full poses and train candidate quality from spatially coherent matches and deformation cost; then enable a documented fitting-loss gradient into the coordinate predictor. Require both matched synthetic pose improvement and no material donor-level weak-real regression before claiming the intended two-task feedback is useful. The 037 panel is repeatedly used for DEV design, not an independent final validation set; animal-separated expert truth and calibration remain necessary.
