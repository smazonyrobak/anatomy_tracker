# 228: model-free atlas contrast on imperfect blind pose candidates

The 227 anchor correction failed and every first-choice branch remained in the free-pose family. Earlier 172 fitted energy did not beat its atlas-intensity-null control on support-similar near/wrong pairs; it specifically called for a simple global contrast-normalized image-versus-rendered-atlas check. The 180 assay found a strong signal for the *exact* synthetic plane against large fixed perturbations, but that does not imply a useful signal among imperfect blind predictions. This read-only TRAIN-inner assay measures that missing link before further pose–deformation training.

Freeze the 226 step-1,200 pose model. Draw 24 independent support-intersecting sections per appearance (v4, v7) from each of procedural TRAIN bases 60–63: 192 accepted physical sections, with all rejected attempts recorded. There are no paired copies of a plane with changed artifacts or background. Infer all 160 pose/reflection branch priors from only the input. Take the blind top 16 and append the synthetic true-gauge state **for diagnostic scoring only**. Render every candidate through the unchanged atlas at the original 256-pixel chart with a fixed, normalized nine-sample 50-µm PSF. The true sample thickness is not supplied to scoring. Downsample source and atlas to 96 pixels and apply the 180 three-pixel smoothing. No perfect synthetic tissue mask, true pose, model-derived warp, or region label may enter any candidate score; physical truth is used only after scores are frozen to audit errors and define diagnostic pairs.

Compute polarity-invariant absolute Pearson correlation within each candidate's five-pixel-eroded atlas support. For every candidate, retain an independent deterministic null that permutes atlas intensity *inside the same support* before smoothing. The blind selection readout compares the unchanged pose prior, pure intact correlation, and the prespecified fused score `log_prior + 2 × correlation`; `log_prior + 4 × correlation` is descriptive only. Compare selected physical tissue error, top-16 oracle capture, and normal-axis error. No coefficient is tuned on the panel.
Candidates with fewer than 64 eroded support cells cannot win a contrast-based selection; if all 16 are ineligible, fall back to the unchanged prior and count the case. Null-fused selection is a descriptive intensity control under the same rule.

For a diagnostic near/wrong pair, choose the smallest-error member of the blind top 16 if it is <2 mm, then the highest-prior member at least 1 mm worse **among those** whose rigid atlas supports have intersection/union ≥0.85, support-area-fraction gap ≤0.08, and at least 64 cells in their common five-pixel-eroded support. Evaluate both candidates on the *same* common pixels. Also compare the appended true-gauge render with that same wrong candidate when it independently meets the support criteria. Record pair identities, physical errors, support, intact and null margins, and rank, including ineligible reasons. The oracle only defines evaluation pairs; it never selects an operational output.

The signal gate needs at least 24 eligible near/wrong pairs per appearance. On v7, intact near beats wrong in at least 75% of eligible pairs and by at least ten percentage points more than the null, and the fixed fused blind selector lowers median first-choice error at least 0.30 mm versus the pose prior, without >0.20-mm first-choice regression on v4. The exact-gauge comparison is diagnostic: exact-plane success with imperfect-candidate failure implicates pose precision or fit basin, while both failing implicates appearance/scoring. If support-matched pairs are too few, report the gate as inconclusive, not positive. If the signal gate fails, do not backpropagate this contrast to pose or scale its training unchanged; inspect contrast, support, and candidate precision before choosing another fitting architecture.

The 64 deformation bases are simulated descendants of one atlas, and bases 60–63 occur in older legacy lineages. This assay is case-independent synthetic TRAIN diagnosis, not unseen animal validation. Do not access DEV/CAL/FINAL animals, physically cut oblique material, or the public DeepSlice benchmark; do not calibrate uncertainty, promote a checkpoint, or change GUI inference.
