# Frozen protocol — v9 generator image/atlas signal audit 301

## Purpose

The 298 clean-image test and 299/300 atlas-intensity-versus-support null comparisons all failed to show an operational anatomy-ranking gain. Before altering the network again, measure whether the *actual v9 generator* preserves recognisable atlas intensity at its exact synthetic CCF point targets, and whether downsampling to 48×48 erases that signal. This is a diagnostic with no model update and no benchmark or biological validation. The frozen 275 model is used only to render an illustrative first-choice plane.

## Frozen sampling and measures

- Draw 32 distinct eligible v9 physical sections, four independently random planes/appearances per synthetic TRAIN base animal 48–55, using a new seed. No paired background variants, shared plane settings, or deliberately repeated sections. Apply the post-artifact affine gauge to the original single draw; retain full provenance, brush/inversion, artifact lineage and obliquity.
- Render (a) the observed v9 image; (b) the unranked true fitted-affine atlas plane; and (c) the frozen 275 first-choice atlas plane, each with the original nine-point finite-thickness PSF. Separately evaluate atlas intensity at every *exact dense observed-pixel CCF central target*, integrating the same nine offsets along the fitted post-artifact normal. This through-plane direction is approximate because the generator's locally curved slab director is not exported; image-space artifact resampling is also not reproduced by re-rendering. Replay the same underlying seeded source section once and geometrically resample its original atlas intensity using the recorded observed-to-source map. This warped-source oracle is an analysis-only control, not a new independent section or a model input.
- At native 192×192 and average-pooled 96, 48 and 24, calculate signed and absolute Pearson correlation of observed image with the warped-source oracle, approximate exact-target atlas intensity and true *rigid* atlas plane. Restrict to valid, non-displaced pixels with >95% generated tissue visibility, take tissue-masked block means, then retain cells with >75% tissue. Report usable counts and sign agreement with generated inversion, not just correlations. Compare native approximate exact-target correlation to a within-section spatial shuffle of atlas intensity that preserves its marginal histogram and tissue support. The absolute value handles deliberately inverted synthetic contrast.
- Separately reproduce the 299 ranker's *actual* 48×48 input sampling: average-pool the observation 4×4 without masking, render the true fitted-affine plane and frozen 275 first-choice plane at the centre of each 4×4 block using 299's renderer at zero extra depth, and compare correlations on fully intact tissue blocks. This true-versus-plausible-wrong-pose comparison is the candidate-discrimination control. The pooled-target correlations alone are not the 299/300 feature-lattice assay. These scalar correlations are diagnostics, not the learned ranker scores.
- Freeze and save a six-row visual grid directly from these actual generator draws: true affine atlas plane, actual synthetic observation, and frozen 275 guessed atlas plane. Select rows deterministically across the observed obliquity ordering; do not redraw to cherry-pick appearances. These examples are qualitative, not a performance sample.
- Preserve frozen source/input/output/figure hashes and all 32 physical-section identities on I:. Do not use DEV/CAL/FINAL animals, public benchmarks, pretrained weights/features/pseudolabels, or real-animal labels.

## Interpretation boundaries

If the warped-source oracle is poor already at 192, generated appearance or artifacts may obscure atlas signal even under known geometry. If it is strong but approximate exact-target correlation is poor, investigate dense CCF binding, image resampling and through-plane direction before scaling another ranker. A drop in pooled-target correlation at 48 indicates scale sensitivity but does not alone prove the 299/300 feature lattice discarded information; the centre-sampled true-versus-first-choice readout is needed as well. Weak distinction there may reflect sparse support, residual warping, photometry or insufficient pose signal rather than a proven impossibility of arbitrary planes. Strong distinction with a failed learned ranker shifts attention to objectives and candidate consensus. This audit cannot establish real histology realism: inversion/gamma/noise/background and deformation ranges still need comparison with acquired slides and independently verified physical-oblique animal data. None of these outcomes justifies restricting the model to cardinal planes or claiming calibrated electrode probabilities.
