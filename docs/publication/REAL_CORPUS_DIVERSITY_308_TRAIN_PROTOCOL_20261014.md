This is one bounded TRAIN-only data test, not a new architecture or a deployable checkpoint. The split is already frozen before any readout pixels: `I:/AnatomyTracker/data/coronal_train_split_308_001/split.json` (SHA-256 `b0e5ce4060c6192c2fc166494459c5134beda3caa55488d01a6581c374821bbf`). The 307 two-slot result failed; use the one-slot 305 acquired-rehearsal intervention batch-400 parent, descended only from project random initialization.

Run two arms from identical parent weights **and optimizer moments**, for 4,000 four-image batches per arm. Each batch has one independently drawn v9 arbitrary-plane synthetic section, one independently drawn v10 narrower-FOV synthetic section, one Lupori section from the same three TRAIN animals, and one Allen section alternating coronal and sagittal. Use identical synthetic/Lupori/sagittal draws and optimizer schedule between arms. The only difference is coronal donor diversity: control repeatedly samples 20 fresh gradient donors chosen by SHA-256 of `coronal-308-control-20261014:<animal_partition_key>`, then a random physical section from that donor; intervention walks all 1,727 gradient donors without replacement before reshuffling, then one random section each. Both arms therefore receive 2,000 physical coronal presentations but the broad arm sees most donors once; this tests breadth, not full-corpus-image exposure. No section is deliberately duplicated with alternate background or artifact settings. Synthetic draws remain independent physical plane draws.

Keep the encoder and pose heads trainable; freeze local mapping and atlas-fit modules for this isolated appearance/pose data test. The synthetic loss keeps exact arbitrary-plane pose targets. The Lupori loss/schedule stays as in the 305 rehearsal. For both Allen domains, use the same **tolerant weak-reference cost** in place of exact-affine cost: subtract 1,000 µm from five-site gap and 100 µm from the existing unsigned-normal penalty, clamp each at zero, then use their sum divided by 1,000 in the existing probabilistic pose loss at weight 0.3. Allen affines are registrations, not independent physical truth or deformation labels. Preserve source animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs and hashes for each draw.

Before gradients, freeze fresh independent TRAIN-inner synthetic panels from bases 56–63: 128 unconditioned v9, 128 unconditioned v10, and 32 v10 accepted with <15% visible tissue, with no paired same-plane appearance variants. Use the 100 pre-frozen new coronal donor sections, the 20 previously held-out sagittal TRAIN donors and the 52 AL1A Lupori sections as fixed acquired readouts. Read at batches 0, 1,000, 2,000 and 4,000; retain all four checkpoints and raw per-section rows. No DEV/CAL/FINAL image, public benchmark, or candidate oracle enters gradients. The prior 58-coronal readout may be reported descriptively but is not the new primary set.

At batch 4,000, the broad arm passes this **data-diversity mechanism gate** only if its median first-choice centre gap to the weak Allen affine on the new 100-donor coronal set is at least 0.15 mm below control, while its first-choice coronal axis count loses at most two donors. Its first-choice mean exact synthetic site error must be no more than 0.15 mm worse than control on each unconditioned v9 and v10 panel; each panel's top-16 ≤1.5-mm capture may fall by at most four of 128, and sparse v10 capture by at most two of 32. AL1A first-choice mean gap may worsen by at most 0.20 mm; first-choice sagittal correct-axis count may fall by at most one of 20. A pass authorizes broader acquired-data exposure and a separate direct joint-map study, **not** arbitrary physical-plane accuracy, electrode probabilities, DeepSlice benchmarking or GUI promotion. A fail ends this recipe rather than triggering an unbounded rerun. These weak-reference and synthetic inner measures cannot replace independent physical-oblique animal validation.
