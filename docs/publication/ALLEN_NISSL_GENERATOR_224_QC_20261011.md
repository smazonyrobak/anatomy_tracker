# 224: alternate Allen Nissl appearance is ready for a controlled test

The official Allen `ara_nissl_25.nrrd` is an **experimental intensity source**, not a new anatomical target or an accuracy result. Its SHA-256 is `16596c2c03935b7ff58f1fed41bac2aa21423f39d2c17d3fc400387f054b3bdf` (136,232,577 bytes). Its 528×320×456 float32 array has the same 25-µm AP/DV/ML grid, origin, and axis directions as the pinned average template and annotation. A read-only 50-µm support check found Dice 0.956 between annotation foreground and Nissl above its in-support fifth percentile; most disagreements were close to the brain boundary. This supports a small appearance experiment, not a claim of exact voxelwise correspondence.

`training/allen_nissl_appearance_224.py` verifies the raw file and header, linearly maps annotation-support Nissl q01/q99 (337.62844116/23347.95179688) to [0,1], zeros intensity outside the *unchanged* annotation support, and records the alternate source/normalization. It changes neither the inverse deformation maps nor plane/reflection/finite-thickness target calculations. The frozen v7 loader and prior 221–223 sources/results are untouched. Each 224 draw chooses **one** source volume independently from its seed; there is no same-plane Nissl/template training pair.

The [actual-generator preview](I:/AnatomyTracker/previews/nissl_generator_independent_224.png) (SHA-256 `27ac2826166efc22b041824a6cbcc6a80c3b21998750aa9c1a322f3fd36ab9ef`) contains six distinct first-eligible 166→217→v4 TRAIN sections, selected in six attempts. Three drew Nissl and three the average template. Nearest-cardinal normal angles are 19.4°, 44.0°, 44.9°, 37.3°, 40.0°, and 42.3°; the 13%-visible example shows the genuinely sparse-tissue case. The columns show the labeled atlas plane, pre-artifact virtual tissue, final image on the true [0,1] scale, and a display-stretched view of that same final image. The adjacent JSON pins seeds, physical-section IDs, source volume, tissue fraction, mask/background mode, events, and output hash.

Visual inspection: Nissl offers internal texture absent from the smoother template, and the new cuts cover steeply oblique planes. The preview also exposes a limitation: low-exposure final inputs can be nearly black on the true [0,1] scale, while brightening them for display makes structure visible. Nissl's contrast is not a measured model of fluorescence, staining, tears, or animal variation. Do **not** scale training or infer accuracy from this grid. Next compare source-vs-support anatomy discrimination and an explicitly matched TRAIN-only appearance continuation, followed by fresh animal-separated development/real-donor gates; only a measured improvement warrants larger exposure.
