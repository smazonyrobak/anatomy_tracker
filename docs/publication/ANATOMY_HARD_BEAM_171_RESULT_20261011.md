# 171: hard-beam training does not rescue anatomy feedback

The [frozen protocol](ANATOMY_HARD_BEAM_171_PROTOCOL_20261011.md) trained the 169 correspondence matcher for 1,600 further optimizer batches on freshly drawn synthetic TRAIN sections. Each batch mined the frozen pose model's blind 16-plane beam, including its anatomy scorer's highest-ranked wrong plane, then optimized four selected candidates. The 132 pose network remained frozen. The same 128-attempt TRAIN-inner panel was scored before training and at batches 400, 800 and 1,600; 94/96 unconditional and 28/32 deliberately steep-oblique attempts contained usable tissue. No physical animal holdout or public benchmark was touched.

Mean **initial-candidate rigid-gauge error**, in mm, on the 94 unconditional sections:

| Batch | Direct prior | Native 121 fitter | Anatomy score alone | Prior + anatomy score | Prior + intensity-null score |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 2.801 | 2.912 | 3.250 | 3.033 | 2.816 |
| 400 | 2.801 | 2.912 | 3.442 | 2.684 | 2.801 |
| 800 | 2.801 | 2.912 | 3.754 | 2.768 | 2.801 |
| 1,600 | **2.801** | 2.912 | 3.028 | **2.808** | **2.801** |

The predeclared **batch-1,600 gate fails**. Prior-plus-anatomy is 0.006 mm *worse* than the direct prior, improves only 0.104 mm versus the native fitter (required ≥0.25 mm versus each), and captures ≤1.5 mm in 34/94 cases—exactly the prior's count and only four more than the native fitter (required ≥10 percentage points versus each). Its selected fitted error, 2.802 mm, is lower than native fitted error, 2.912 mm, but that one criterion is insufficient. The matched score-stage intensity-null fusion has the same 2.801-mm initial error and 34/94 capture as the prior, so the anatomy fusion does not beat it either. Relative to the prior, the anatomy fusion changes just four selections: one improves and three worsen. Although the batch-400 mean is lower, that checkpoint was not the terminal gate and also fails the required gain and capture criteria; selecting it after seeing this panel would change the protocol.

The enriched 45–55° stratum must not be pooled into the population estimate. In its 28 usable sections, fused initial error was 2.638 mm versus 2.749 for the direct prior but **2.424 for the native fitter**; fused capture was 9/28 versus native 11/28. On the 18 pooled cases with under 10% valid target pixels, the fused score selected exactly the prior's 4.175-mm mean and 2/18 capture; the truth-chosen best-of-16 was still 1.903 mm. In the unconditional stratum the truth-chosen best-of-16 was 1.142 mm and captured 74/94 within 1.5 mm. A better selector could therefore help many sections, but this score is not that selector, and even the beam oracle is not yet a high-quality solution.

The bounded run exited successfully after 1,738 independently seeded attempts (1,600 nonblank optimizer batches, 138 blanks). All 1,738 recorded physical-section IDs were distinct by chance, not by a rejection rule; the generator did not deliberately reuse planes with changed backgrounds. The four raw-panel files each contain the same 128 section identities and blind candidate branches. Independently recomputed unconditional means and capture counts from the raw rows matched the summaries. All 15 frozen output-file SHA-256 values matched `completed.json`; its own SHA-256 is `246d1e6d6ea6f9f7a5f6ab97252474ce2fb2477dac3053623df3a36fa7a63086`. Source, protocol, previous matcher, parent checkpoint and panel hashes also matched. No nonfinite gradients or losses occurred. Frozen output is `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/anatomy_hard_beam_171_train_001`.

**Decision:** do not scale or deploy this independent local-peak matcher, connect it to pose-head gradients, claim calibrated uncertainty, or run DeepSlice benchmarking. Training loss improved on selected examples without producing reliable blind selection. The current score allows local correlation peaks without a spatially coherent tissue-to-atlas deformation and strain cost; this is a plausible failure mechanism, not a proved causal attribution. The next targeted experiment should compare a coherent joint fit energy—anatomical residual plus constrained deformation magnitude/smoothness, with explicit support and intensity-null controls—against the frozen direct/native baselines on a fresh TRAIN panel before training pose from fit feedback. In parallel, the single-atlas synthetic appearance needs a donor-stratified acquired-image realism audit; the present panel cannot establish physically acquired arbitrary-angle performance. True synthetic slab offsets/weights were supplied here, so unknown-thickness robustness also remains untested.
