# Candidate 301 — the v9 generator retains atlas signal at 48 pixels

The frozen [protocol](GENERATOR_SIGNAL_301_PROTOCOL_20261012.md) ran once on 32 independently sampled eligible v9 TRAIN sections from eight synthetic base animals (48–55). An independent read of the raw JSONL rechecked all source, protocol, parent-checkpoint, output and figure SHA-256 digests; 32 unique physical-section IDs, four per base; all reported medians; and the inversion signs. The runner exited successfully. Its `completed.json` SHA-256 is `71ff37b7076f94b2b2e7e282b0de3614fd80f1289235aa1e7a464e68d40e65de`. The fixed [six-case image grid](figures/generator_signal_301_examples.png) is qualitative; it was chosen by obliquity order before looking at appearances.

| Resolution | Median absolute correlation with warped generator source | Median absolute correlation with atlas at dense CCF targets | Median absolute correlation with true fitted-affine atlas plane |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 192×192 | 0.947 | 0.943 | 0.845 |
| 96×96 | 0.969 | 0.968 | 0.881 |
| 48×48 | 0.975 | 0.974 | 0.917 |
| 24×24 | 0.975 | 0.975 | 0.943 |

These are within-tissue, sign-invariant *image correlations*, not registration accuracy. All 32 cases were usable at each scale; their unpooled 192-pixel spatial-shuffle null had median absolute correlation 0.011. The signed native atlas correlation agreed with the generated contrast inversion in all 32. The replayed source-image oracle and CCF-target atlas render agree closely, so a gross image/coordinate-binding error is unlikely in these sampled synthetic sections. Signal did not disappear under 48-pixel pooling. At the 299 ranker's actual centre-sampled 48-pixel geometry, the true fitted-affine plane had median absolute correlation 0.863 versus 0.264 for frozen 275's first choice; true exceeded first choice in all 32. This does not demonstrate that a simple correlation can resolve the *hard support-matched top-16 candidates* from 299/300, but it moves that question ahead of further blind ranker scaling.

The sample included 15 deliberately contrast-inverted sections, six with brush input, and unbrushed exteriors of seven exact-black, ten near-black and nine gray cases. Obliquity from the nearest cardinal axis ranged 3.4°–42.5° (median 26.9°), with six ≥40° and **none ≥45°**. Consequently this audit cannot certify steep oblique performance. The displayed true plane is a fitted affine approximation after artifacts, not a pixel-perfect reconstruction of local deformation. The synthetic atlas supplies its own texture; high synthetic correlation says nothing by itself about acquired histology realism or cross-animal generalization.
