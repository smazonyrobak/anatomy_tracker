# 120: whole-plane atlas matcher did not improve blind pose

The paired head-only pilot and its frozen synthetic DEV gate completed. **Do not promote the matcher or extend this unchanged head-only training.** At the DEV-selected step 256, the atlas-aware arm changed prior-selected mean rigid pose error from 3.311 to **3.306 mm** (0.0048 mm improvement), while the otherwise identical support-only arm reached 3.306 mm. The atlas-specific paired advantage was **0.0003 mm**. The required gain against each was 0.20 mm; the gate failed. These are 64 synthetic sections balanced across eight deformation plans, not independent biological animals.

| Step / arm | Prior-selected rigid | Joint-selected rigid | Physically best original 14 | Physically best corrected 16 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 / atlas | 3.311 mm | 3.311 mm | 1.693 mm | 1.623 mm |
| 256 / atlas | 3.311 mm | **3.306 mm** | 1.693 mm | 1.629 mm |
| 256 / support only | 3.311 mm | 3.306 mm | 1.693 mm | 1.629 mm |
| 512 / atlas | 3.311 mm | 3.322 mm | 1.693 mm | 1.628 mm |

The truth-only best-corrected-16 metric gained just 0.064 mm over the original best 14 at step 256, not the required 0.50 mm. The two extra diverse anchors already gave a 1.623 mm best-of-16 at step zero, so the learned correction itself did not create that availability. Near-case and populated appearance/angle/support nonregression conditions passed, but they cannot offset the three failed improvement conditions. The best available branch remains much better than the selected branch (~1.63 versus ~3.31 mm), yet the model does not identify it. The learned head moved candidate centres only 0.052 mm on average at step 256 (maximum 0.116 mm), and its mean absolute extra match logit was 0.015. The support-only arm behaved almost identically. This suggests the frozen image features and short head-only training did not learn a useful atlas-anatomy correction; it does **not** show that arbitrary-plane recovery is impossible or that more joint training could not help.

TRAIN used 512 batches of two separately drawn eligible synthetic sections (1,024 unique physical-section IDs; 1,130 attempts). The parent 111 checkpoint and both newly attached heads belong to this project's random-initialization lineage. The DEV evaluator excluded every section from assay 118, selected eight other eligible sections from each of eight plans, reproduced the same blind 14+2 beam for every arm and checkpoint, and used truth only for measurement and diagnostic strata. After the first evaluator attempt stopped before writing results because some sections had no completely valid 16×16 block, the protocol and code were amended **before** outcome evaluation to use 256 SHA-seeded valid pixels per section. That amendment is recorded in the protocol and commit history; it is not a separate untouched confirmation.
