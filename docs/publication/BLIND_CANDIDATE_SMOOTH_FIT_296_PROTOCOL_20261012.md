# 296: does smooth fitting difficulty distinguish predicted atlas planes?

The exact-plane 295 image-only fit improved synthetic CCF site error, but the user needs the *difficulty of fitting each proposed plane* to improve blind pose choice. Test that proposition directly before wiring another scorer into a joint model. This is a bounded no-training TRAIN-inner diagnostic, not a benchmark or deployable predictor.

Freeze 32 new, independently drawn eligible v9 sections, four from each synthetic TRAIN base 56–63, with at least 512 distinct valid surviving sites each. Use one physical draw and one appearance per section, postartifact gauge v1 on the same draw, and no artificial plane/background pairing. Exclude all physical IDs in the 281/282/286/287/289/292/294/295 panels and training schedules. Save draw attempts, animal/specimen/experiment identities, image/state/target hashes, synthetic and atlas source provenance, and exact source/protocol hashes. Use only I: under `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/blind_candidate_smooth_fit_296_001`; do not read DEV/CAL/FINAL, sealed animals, or any public benchmark.

Hold the frozen 275 pose checkpoint fixed. For each observed image, take the prior's top 16 **blind** state/reflection branches by `log_mass + reflection log probability`; never inject the true plane into the ranked set. Render each candidate with the same finite-thickness PSF offsets/weights as the generated section, and record its rigid 512-site CCF error only for later evaluation. Render the true postartifact plane only as an unranked positive control. The exact generated PSF is privileged acquisition information here and must be treated as a limitation; this assay isolates the candidate-fit mechanism, not unknown-thickness robustness.

Before scoring, form **one fixed, candidate-independent, inference-observable mask**: the union of the 16 candidates' rigid atlas support >0.9, intersected with the optional supplied brush when present; without a brush use the union alone. The true plane and synthetic valid/visible/fragment masks must not affect this fit/scoring mask. Save the mask and every candidate's support fraction on it. This avoids choosing a different, conveniently small tissue subset for each candidate. Use 295's unchanged 16×16 control representation, affine removal, 0.35 physical-derivative bound, support-normalized atlas intensity, weighted absolute Pearson objective, support penalty, regularizers, Adam learning rate 0.1, 120 updates, and 0/40/80/120 readouts. Independently optimize each of the 16 branches and the unranked true-plane control from zero; select the minimum *image objective* readout for each. Preserve selected controls and all candidate/readout scores for replay. Rank the 16 predicted candidates by their fitted image objective alone, with no post-hoc weight search, truth-derived mask, or coordinate truth in selection. Also record no-fit image-objective ranking as a control.

Evaluate the prior's first branch, the no-fit-score winner, the fitted-score winner, the physically best available branch, and the unranked true control on the **same** 512 valid CCF target sites. Report both rigid and fitted mapped errors for selected branches, capture within 1.5 mm, support fractions, normal-axis error, image scores, field magnitude/derivative, best-step distribution, and per-base/obliquity/tissue-support/artifact/brush strata. Among cases with both a near candidate (rigid error ≤1.5 mm) and a far candidate (≥2.0 mm) with rigid-support fractions within 0.10 on the common mask, report whether the score prefers the near over the far candidate and the exact pair rule (nearest near, highest-prior eligible far). Do not tune a score fusion or threshold on these rows.

The predeclared **fit-feedback advance gate** requires fitted-score-selected mean rigid error at least 0.25 mm below the prior-first mean **and** at least 0.15 mm below the no-fit-image-score winner, positive mean improvement from the prior in at least six of eight synthetic bases, mapped error no worse than the prior-first branch's image-fitted map by more than 0.05 mm, and ≥70% near-over-support-matched-far wins when at least 16 such cases exist. If no ≥16 matched cases exist, that clause is inconclusive and the gate does not pass. If the fitted score does not beat the no-fit image score, extra fitting machinery has not earned its place even if image information itself helps. If the gate fails, do not simply train a larger scalar fit scorer or use this energy for pose feedback; diagnose score confounding and pursue an off-plane discriminative representation or stronger real acquisition evidence. If it passes, train a scratch-initialized direct joint pose/smooth-map model with this fit difficulty as a supervised training signal, then judge it on animal-separated data. Neither outcome licenses GUI promotion, calibrated electrode probabilities, or a DeepSlice comparison.
