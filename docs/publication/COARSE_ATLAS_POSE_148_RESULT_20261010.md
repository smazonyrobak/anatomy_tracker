At 6,000 updates, the full arm improved it by only **0.030 mm** (required ≥0.300), beat support-only by **0.075 mm** (required ≥0.150), and worsened 26/179 cases by >0.10 mm (14.5%; allowed ≤30%). Thus three of four criteria failed. v3 retention is also weak: 197 near cases improved by 0.018 mm; full beat support-only by 0.052 mm; exact-plane error was 0.321 mm. V4 direct top-prior branch, without post-correction reranking, moved only 3.278 → 3.218 mm; only 73/248 top-prior branches were within 1.5 mm initially. This does not repair blind candidate selection.

Training loss did favor atlas anatomy: in the last 1,000 updates, mean full versus support-only loss was 4.411 versus 5.574, and the mean corrected-plane training error was 0.762 versus 0.957 mm. That effect transferred only modestly to held-out synthetic plans and plateaued by checkpoint 3,000; more unchanged training is not justified. The v4 near-subset gain was small in every angle bin (<15° +0.016 mm, 15–30° +0.005, 30–45° +0.053, ≥45° +0.043), so this is not a steep-angle-only failure.
