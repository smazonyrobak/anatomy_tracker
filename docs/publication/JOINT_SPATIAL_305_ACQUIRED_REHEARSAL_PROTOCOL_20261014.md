# 305 acquired-axis rehearsal: frozen TRAIN-only continuation protocol

Start both matched arms from the *same* adapted 305 fluorescence batch-600 model **and AdamW moments**, not the original control or a fresh optimizer. Continue only the five previously trainable encoder/lateral and direct/anchor pose modules for 400 local batches at the batch-600 endpoint learning rates (pose 4e-6, shared features 1e-6); use a newly fixed matched seed because the checkpoint did not store RNG state. Each local batch has two independently accepted full-sphere synthetic TRAIN sections from bases 0–55, one `CC2B`/`CC4B`/`CC4A` Lupori section with the existing loss at weight 1, and one alternating Allen coronal/sagittal TRAIN section. Both arms receive identical draws, site samples, preprocessing and weak targets. The control gives the old Allen five-site-plus-unsigned-normal weak-affine loss weight 0; the intervention gives it weight 0.3. Allen labels are approximate registration, never physical truth or model input. No old-section mask is inferred.

Use one lower-median section per donor, fixed by the completed 98-case 305 acquired TRAIN diagnostic (58 coronal animals, 40 sagittal donors), for readouts. Hold out all sections of the 20 coronal donors `14543, 14599, 14601, 14642, 14643, 14644, 14711, 14745, 15060, 15111, 15112, 15158, 15162, 15304, 15305, 15346, 15347, 15396, 15653, 15979` and the 20 sagittal donors `10164, 10177, 10180, 10184, 10236, 10237, 10239, 10253, 10255, 10301, 10304, 10308, 10336, 10349, 10363, 10371, 10407, 10412, 10413, 10417` from every rehearsal gradient. The sagittal set deliberately includes all seven singleton donors, then 13 of the other 33 via fixed seed `20261014307`; the coronal set is a fixed seeded 20-donor sample. Cycle the remaining 38 coronal and 20 sagittal donors approximately equally within alternating domain, excluding their 58 lower-median readout sections from gradients too. The primary old-section readout uses the 20+20 entirely held-out donors; report all 58+40 as a secondary descriptive panel. Read only the first 1,280 TRAIN lines and selected TRAIN array rows from the mixed coronal source; the sagittal source is TRAIN-only. Use the identical 192-pixel image/zero-auxiliary input and sagittal area resize with `x256 = 4*x192/3 + 1/6`. Retain exact animal, donor, specimen, experiment, section, array-row and source-image hashes in draws/readout rows.

At local steps 0, 200 and 400, evaluate both arms on all 52 `AL1A` sections (guard, no gradients), the unchanged 64 synthetic inner-panel sections from bases 56–63, and the same 98 old Allen sections. Preserve the earlier first-prior-ranked and top-16 oracle AL1A/synthetic site-gap metrics, synthetic capture at 1.5 mm, and old coronal/sagittal first-axis, top-16 axis, unsigned weak-normal-angle and weak-centre-gap metrics, separately for fully held-out and all donors. The predeclared step-400 gate requires *all* of: **held-out** sagittal first-axis count at least control +3 **and** at least 15/20; AL1A mean first gap no more than control +0.20 mm; synthetic mean first gap no more than control +0.20 mm and top-16 capture no lower than control −5/64; **held-out** coronal first-axis count no lower than control −1/20. The +3/20, 15/20 and −1/20 count bounds are the conservative half-panel counterparts of +5/40, 30/40 and −2/58; all-donor old readouts cannot satisfy this gate. Local step 200 is diagnostic, not a selection point.

Hash the parent run/arm receipts and checkpoint, source/protocol/split, TRAIN manifests, fixed case list, matched draw logs and outputs on `I:`. This is one bounded TRAIN experiment, not hyperparameter search or checkpoint promotion. A passing weak-reference gate does not establish physical arbitrary-oblique or local-deformation accuracy. Do not access DEV, CAL, FINAL or public benchmark data.
