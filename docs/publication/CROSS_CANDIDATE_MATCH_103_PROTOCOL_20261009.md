# 103: cross-candidate match evidence on frozen 094 geometry

This is a **TRAIN-only descriptor pilot**, not a pose, fitting, mapping, or selector update. Fork the 099 trainer, initialize its matcher from the authenticated 099 step-4,000 checkpoint, and keep the 094 predictor, 8-old/6-anchor natural beam, four-branch TRAIN sampling rule, fitted candidate states, local mapper, atlas rendering/support policy, and inherited matcher projections frozen. Draw 4,000 accepted, distinct physical TRAIN sections from the established 64-base/4,096-virtual-subject v7 lineage through `sample_one_shot_slide_artifacts_v3` at side 256. Each attempt independently samples one plane and one appearance; there are no paired background copies. Its source cutting-plane state is not refitted from artifact-selected pixels. Retain every rejected draw, seed, lineage, geometry, appearance and artifact receipt. Exact CCF coordinates and validity are used for TRAIN targets and audit only, never as matcher or selector inputs.

Add an equal-weight **fine cross-candidate** loss at the *same* 32×32 visible site. An ordered pair (A, B) is eligible only when A has a supported bin within 1.5 mm of truth, B has at least one supported bin and **all** its supported bins are at least 2.0 mm from truth after frozen 094 fitting, and the two local supported-bin counts have ratio 0.8–1.25. Thus nominally wrong branches with a physically correct bin are never false negatives and the 1.5–2.0-mm ambiguity band is excluded. For each candidate use the *same inference-time* count-normalized raw-cosine evidence over all its supported bins, `logsumexp(20 × cosine)/20 − log(N_supported)/20`; truth determines pair eligibility only. Remove the fixed support penalty and mask unsupported bins. Apply the 099 softplus sharpness 10 and cosine margin 0.1 to B evidence minus A evidence, average over eligible ordered pairs, then give this term the same weight as the retained fine/coarse objective. The fixed count normalization and count-ratio restriction address search-set-size bias without DEV tuning. No new ranker or scalar correctness head is trained.

Require at least 16 distinct sites and 32 eligible ordered pairs for an accepted TRAIN draw, with a fixed cap of 40,000 total draw attempts. Log candidate IDs, paired-site/pair counts, support-count ratios/strata, margins, acceptance rate and coverage by appearance, angle family and beam branch; do not silently replace a sparse four-branch beam with truth-inserted candidates. Concentration in one branch or appearance family is a reported limitation, not an unregistered reason to move the gate.
