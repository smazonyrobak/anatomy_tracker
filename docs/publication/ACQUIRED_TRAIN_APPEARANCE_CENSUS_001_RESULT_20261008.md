# Acquired TRAIN-image appearance census

The two acquired TRAIN cohorts genuinely differ after their actual model preparation. Across **1,280 coronal images/58 donors**, the donor-equal full-canvas exact-zero fraction is **67.3%**; across **653 sagittal images/40 donors**, it is **16.3%**. Pixels below 0.03 account for 79.7% and 50.6% respectively. Both include substantial near-black areas, but the sagittal cohort cannot be made equivalent to the coronal cohort by simply attaching the same exterior style to every slice. The donor-equal median pixel is 0.0011 versus 0.0283; the 95th percentile is 0.4795 versus 0.4989. Saturated pixels (≥0.99) are rare, 0.012% versus 0.864%, so the fixed sagittal gain did not generally clip the images. These are **whole-canvas** measurements, not segmented background or tissue and not artifact counts.

The donor-equal adjacent-pixel difference is 0.0129 coronal versus 0.0238 sagittal, but their fields, stains and native model resolutions (192 versus 256) differ; it would be scientifically incorrect to interpret that ratio as a calibrated synthetic texture target. The data do justify sampling both black/near-black and gray/confusing exteriors *independently of whether a smart-brush outline is supplied*. They do not estimate fold, tear, bubble or tiling prevalence, which requires a small acquired-slide annotation census. The v2 simulator's high stress-event rates remain uncalibrated and out of main training.

This was a read-only census of TRAIN images; all development and sealed holdout donors were excluded by the frozen split. `training/census_acquired_train_appearance_001.py` records per-donor statistics and exact source-file hashes in `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/acquired_train_appearance_census_001/summary.json`, SHA-256 `9079e2a8a7d6aea660b15d30f8b6d9fdf1cf3fbb3e57892d080235ae10b77f0b`. An independent read confirmed the 1,280/58 and 653/40 counts, key donor-equal zero fractions, and output hash. These Allen acquisitions are not a substitute for the user's lab-specific histology or for physically acquired steep-oblique animal-separated validation.
