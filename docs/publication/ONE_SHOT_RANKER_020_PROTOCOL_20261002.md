# Frozen-geometry candidate-ranker probe 020

The 019 batch-18,000 model maps a correct plane with 0.129 mm identity-equal error, but its chosen candidate is 2.591 mm while the best of its eight candidates is approximately 1.15 mm. The fitted scorer improves over the image-only prior yet picks the physical best candidate in only 43.2% of frozen DEV cases. More exposure of the whole network did not close this gap.

Freeze the full 019 batch-18,000 pose encoder, probabilistic pose head, finite-thickness atlas renderer, shared pose correction, atlas encoder and local tissue mapper. Update **only** the existing fitted candidate scorer, on 6,000 batches of two newly sampled arbitrary-plane synthetic sections each. For each image, score the same prior top eight after their frozen pose correction and tissue fit; use the known surviving-pixel-to-CCF correspondences to train a soft physical-error ranking target and expected-error objective. Do not duplicate sections intentionally, use real weak labels as physical truth, change the candidate beam, or touch the public benchmark. Preserve every draw's provenance and source hashes. All files stay on `I:`.

Evaluate batches 0/2,000/4,000/6,000 on the unchanged 185-case synthetic and 64-section/six-donor real DEV panels. A result counts as useful only if it lowers identity-equal selected synthetic mapping error by at least 0.25 mm relative to 019 batch 18,000 (2.591 mm), does not increase the real weak five-point error by more than 0.2 mm, and leaves correct-pose mapping unchanged. A failed isolated scorer test means another whole-model extension with the same scorer is poorly justified; strengthen the correspondence evidence and/or proposal distribution instead. This is a diagnostic development gate, not a deployment claim.
