# Joint pose/deformation decision after the 268 capture readout

This is an implementation design, not a trained result. The 268 scratch pose model is running; no successor should be selected from its partial logs. Its held-out blind top-16 coverage, selected physical error, visibility and angle strata determine whether atlas-conditioned fitting has a viable starting beam.

The evidence is specific. In 154, a near synthetic pose existed in the blind top 16 for 47/64 TRAIN-inner sections, but the first choice was still wrong by 4.290 mm on average. In 236, a near candidate's residual was mainly a wrong 3-D plane, not tissue warp. In 257 and 267, atlas intensity discriminated wrong local pose factors and distant orientations **when the other pose factors were supplied**, but this was not blind localization. In 244 and 265, learned atlas-conditioned scoring/updating failed to beat direct selection meaningfully or defeated the support-only control. Thus another scalar "warp difficulty" reranker or a narrow 2-D warp is not justified. [DeepSlice](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10514056/) is primarily coronal and uses series information; its result does not settle single-image full-sphere inference. [SVoRT](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10129054/) supports the re-render/update principle in slice-to-volume MRI, not this histology domain.

If 268 supplies enough blind candidates, preserve its **single** shared image encoder and propose full-frame pose/reflection hypotheses with the joint model. Rank all 160 branches by their image-only mass before any atlas fit or synthetic truth; process blind top 16 in chunks of two on the 11-GiB GPU. For each candidate, render the two-channel atlas intensity and support at the exact finite-thickness PSF and original 192-pixel chart. A shared image/atlas correlation block should update centre, plane normal, in-plane frame, span and shear, then re-render. The same weights act on all candidates and iterations. Only after pose capture should a three-component local surface displacement be predicted. Remove its affine component so it cannot silently replace an incorrect global pose, bound its derivative, and re-predict it from scratch after each pose update. A removed-affine **direction** and regularized warp cost may inform the next pose update and candidate score, but the score must also use actual atlas intensity and coverage. A single affine matrix cannot repair displaced fragments; report them separately rather than claiming a smooth field recovered them.

The matching experiment must keep an equally trained direct one-pass branch, the identical candidate panel, and intact-atlas, shifted/shuffled-intensity and support-only arms. Include both true and blindly predicted starting planes to distinguish fitter ability from proposal failure. Train on on-policy blind candidates plus TRAIN-only near-truth jitter, supervising physical pose, valid-tissue mapped coordinates and a bounded field; wrong distant modes should be rejected, not forced to deform into the truth. Preserve a direct-pose loss while unfreezing the shared encoder. Check that atlas-fit loss actually sends a nonzero gradient to pose, that an exact supplied plane is not moved materially, and that a deformation-enriched exact-pose slice benefits from the local map. Calibrate no numerical probabilities from synthetic cases.

Advance beyond the small matched development experiment only if blind selected mapped-site error falls by at least 0.4 mm in at least six of eight held-out synthetic plans, intact atlas beats support-only and shifted-intensity controls by at least 0.25 mm, exact-pose drift is at most 0.25 mm, and the local map improves deformation-enriched true-pose cases by at least 10% over zero warp. These are proposed gates to freeze **before** that experiment, not a post-hoc interpretation of 268. Do not run DeepSlice or claim arbitrary-oblique animal accuracy from these synthetic plans. For inference, optional brush and approximate acquisition constraints may alter the posterior, but must never be required to make a prediction; low-information tangential fragments require broad uncertainty or abstention rather than forced confidence.

The current 268 v8 generator has a brush/exterior mismatch with the GUI; v9 fixes only that contract for future experiments. Neither version reproduces individual real 3-D anatomy or supplies physically oblique animal-level ground truth. Those remain separate validation requirements even if this architecture succeeds synthetically.
