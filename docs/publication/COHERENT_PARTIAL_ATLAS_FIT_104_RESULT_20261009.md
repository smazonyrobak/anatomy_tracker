# Coherent partial atlas fit 104: frozen negative result

The 104 TRAIN run completed 4,000 updates in 1,547 s. Its 4,406 attempted draws yielded **4,000 distinct physical sections**; no plane was deliberately repeated with altered appearance. The parent v3 pose checkpoint and 094 matcher/fitter are from the project's own random-initialization lineage. The new recurrent field had no external features or weights. The exact frozen 64-section DEV subset contains eight independent synthetic deformation plans (eight sections each), with 45 raw, 12 exact-black and seven imperfect-brush inputs; six are in the 45–54.7° nearest-axis bin. This is still one Allen atlas with simulated subjects, **not** biological animal validation.

The predeclared blind-candidate gate **failed**. On the best development checkpoint, step 1,000, plan-equal 3-D CCF error of the selected candidate, measured with the *same frozen 094 map for every selector*, was 4.088 mm for 104 versus 4.165 mm for the old 094 score and **3.744 mm for atlas-support-only selection**. The best-of-14 oracle on this common measure was 1.836 mm; it uses truth and is not operational. Mean within-section rank correlation with lower physical error was 0.0045 for 104, −0.0036 for old 094, and 0.1089 for support-only. Only four of eight plans improved over old selection, one was unchanged, and three worsened. The 104-selected candidate mapped by 104 itself was 4.089 mm.

The local map also did not learn a useful correction: among the three lowest-error candidates per section under the frozen 094 map, the 104 map was 2.3534 mm versus 2.3541 mm for its own rigid corrected plane and 2.3362 mm for the frozen 094 map. Mean predicted shift-gate activation was 0.0106 at step 0, 0.0074 at step 1,000, and 0.0360 at step 4,000; even step 4,000 changed top-three map error by only about 0.003 mm. TRAIN match-class/dustbin CE fell from a 3.779 mean over updates 1–500 to 2.671 over 3,001–4,000, but TRAIN map Huber stayed near 1.7 mm and ranking loss stayed near 1.2–1.3. Better classification loss was not evidence of a better registration.

The exact receipts, all 2,688 candidate rows, all 192 section rows, plan-equal summaries, checkpoint hashes, selected slots, and gate conditions passed the independent read-only verifier. The count-normalized dustbin and affine gauge corrected real defects in 094-derived scoring, but they did not create anatomical discrimination. The tiny gated *expectation* of a diffuse match distribution likely suppressed the coordinate-learning path; this is a testable mechanism, not a proven sole cause. Extending this same 104 training is not justified.

Next, make one targeted architecture change: predict a bounded **continuous three-component local displacement directly from the spatial correlation field**, zero-initialized at no shift but with a nonzero gradient at zero, rather than multiplying a diffuse-bin expectation by a low sigmoid gate. Keep explicit unmatched-site supervision, affine pose/local-warp gauge, fragment-aware smoothness, support-matched negative candidates, and the same blind selection controls. Require a substantial held-out improvement of the actual coordinate map before attempting joint pose-feedback training; then separately require selection to beat both 094 and atlas-support-only on fresh identities. No real accuracy, numerical uncertainty, GUI readiness, or DeepSlice claim follows from 104.
