# Million-plane direct-pose readout (2026-10-01)

The standalone v8 lineage completed 125,000 direct-pose updates on 1,000,000 eligible arbitrary-plane synthetic TRAIN examples. The original runner saved its last checkpoint at 960,000 examples; its unsaved final 5,000 updates were replayed from that checkpoint into `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/joint_v8_million_direct_tail_001`. Every replayed step matched the frozen original schedule, accepted-draw count, loss and physical error exactly (maximum recorded discrepancy zero). The recovered final checkpoint SHA-256 is `175fc93446d45bb0e3f3af0b0dc3ed2cb1c09e4a834210624c4a15f207e9b52b`.

Independent synthetic development readout: 64 sections from four held-out synthetic maps, each in raw, exact-black and imperfect-brush appearances. Subject-equal five-point physical errors (selected / best-of-16 oracle) were 6.89 / 0.88 mm, 6.42 / 0.85 mm and 5.81 / 1.15 mm, respectively. Selected antipodal plane-normal errors were 29.5°, 29.1° and 28.4°. The oracle uses reference coordinates and is **not** an inference result. These maps are not independent biological animals. Raw predictions, eligibility and identities are in `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/joint_v8_million_direct_dev192_001`.

On the same 64 real development images from six disjoint Allen donors, agreement with the original weak Allen affines deteriorated sharply: donor-equal selected five-point error was 1.29 mm before the synthetic-only continuation and 12.60 mm afterward; best-of-16 error was 1.25 versus 3.06 mm, and selected plane-normal error 5.48° versus 16.96°. Every donor worsened. These affines are **not** blinded expert anatomical truth, but the matched result is strong evidence of real-domain forgetting. Raw predictions and donor-level results are in `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/joint_v8_million_direct_real_dev_001`.

Decision: do not treat the million-plane checkpoint as deployable and do not launch a synthetic-only fitter continuation. Continue the same whole-model lineage with donor-uniform real TRAIN pose supervision alongside fresh arbitrary-plane synthetic examples, then enable joint fitting-to-pose feedback with real retention. Check both synthetic and real development domains after each substantial frozen stage. No public benchmark or uncertainty-calibration claim is warranted.
