This is a severe **current-model transfer failure**, not a theorem that arbitrary-angle histology cannot be localized. The source uses automated, not blinded expert, atlas affines; the checkpoint was developed on atlas-derived synthetic sections and near-coronal real training images, not real sagittal ISH. Its prior candidates themselves miss the weak sagittal reference, so fitting/selection alone cannot repair this probe. The test intentionally did not run the local warp/fitting stage. Acquired brightfield texture, whole-image scale, stain-specific patterns, and missing real sagittal training examples are plausible contributors, but this readout does not isolate which one caused the failure. Do not promote 019 to the GUI, infer a calibrated 98.3% failure probability from the mode mass, or use this weak cohort as a final test.

The v2 synthetic artifact generator now models independently moving tear fragments, occluding fold overlap, blurred bubble interiors and varied synthetic backgrounds, but its event frequencies are uncalibrated and its atlas-derived tissue still lacks acquired ISH appearance. Actual mouse histology registration papers document missing tissue, bubbles, folds, displaced pieces and uneven staining; digital slide work also documents scanner blur, lighting and knife-chatter/thickness variation. These are reasons to compare simulator statistics and failure modes with acquired TRAIN images, not to arbitrarily set all artifacts common.
