# 091: train anatomical ordering among blind candidate planes

Freeze before any 091 draw. This is one targeted continuation of 090, not a new architecture or a deployable model. The [090 result](GLOBAL_ATLAS_CONTRAST_090_RESULT_20261004.md) shows that positive-vs-beam contrast learned atlas-support extent: final within-section score/support Spearman −0.845 but score/lower-error only 0.044, with selected mapped error 2.828 mm versus 089's 2.592 mm. The training-only observed-affine reference had lower support than the blind beam, so its 0.585 average soft target mass offered an easy shortcut. More of that loss is not justified.

Continue the **same** 090 scorer from batch 15,000 and the **same** frozen 089 pose/map/083 lineage. For each newly and independently sampled eligible arbitrary-plane synthetic TRAIN section, generate one image with one independent appearance/artifact draw. No paired black/raw copies, intentional plane reuse, or exact-truth inference candidate. Form 14 blind 8-old/6-anchor prior branches and apply the two frozen 083 correction passes exactly as in 090. Render their finite-thickness atlas pairs. Compute each candidate's mean rigid 3-D CCF error at up to 1,024 surviving observed pixels and atlas-support fraction.

Train the scorer on **blind-to-blind ordering only**. For every ordered pair whose physical errors differ by at least 0.5 mm, apply `softplus(1 − (score_better − score_worse))`. Weight every eligible pair by 1 and add 3 if the two atlas-support fractions differ by at most 0.05; normalize by the sum of weights per section. This deliberately emphasizes anatomical discrimination when support cannot identify the winner, while retaining all other informative physical-error comparisons. The frozen 090 TRAIN draws show an average 32.52 support-matched ordered pairs per section, at least one pair for 99.993% of sections, and a matched worse comparator for the best blind candidate in 96.99%. No truth-plane reference or 090 positive-vs-beam loss enters 091. This matched-pair method is motivated by [debiased hard-negative contrast](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Jang_Difficulty-Based_Sampling_for_Debiased_Contrastive_Representation_Learning_CVPR_2023_paper.html) and [anatomy-aware structural contrast](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Mok_Modality-Agnostic_Structural_Image_Representation_Learning_for_Deformable_Multi-Modality_Medical_Image_CVPR_2024_paper.html), neither of which validates this exact histology loss.

Run 30,000 additional accepted sections, batch size one, AdamW 1e-4 with cosine decay, checkpoints at 0/5,000/15,000/30,000.
