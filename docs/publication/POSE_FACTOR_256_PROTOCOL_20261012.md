# 256: explicitly supervise physical pose factors without shrinking the plane

The completed 253–255 DEV audits show that 247 predicts a minor in-plane field of view ≤1.2 mm in 161/227 cases, and that 254's added nearest-anchor rigid loss reduces this to 33/227 but leaves image-selected coordinate error at 4.410 mm. Even an oracle choosing the best of all 128 of 254's branches reaches only 2.290 mm; zero cases are within 1 mm. These are observed failures of this model, not a proof that arbitrary-plane localization is impossible.

Train a **new, fresh-random** direct 64-anchor one-shot joint model for 3,000 batches of four independently sampled eligible 192-pixel v7 TRAIN sections (12,000 presentations), using exactly the 249–251/254 seed and draw stream. No pretrained features, prior model weights, pseudolabels, CAL/FINAL animals, or public benchmark. Record every accepted/rejected draw identity, all loss terms, gradient norms, optimizer/RNG states, source/protocol hashes, and checkpoints at batches 0/500/1500/3000 on I:.

Retain 254's 15° antipodal normal cross-entropy, reflection BCE and nearest-three-anchor better-reflection rigid tissue error, with coefficients 1, 0.1 and 0.25 per millimetre, respectively. Add **two direct physical-factor losses** on the same three nearest normal anchors: `0.5 ×` centre Euclidean error in millimetres, and `0.5 ×` mean absolute log-ratio of the two raster-axis physical spans. Compute spans from the norms of the two columns of `frame[:,:2] @ inplane_basis`, so they are invariant to the arbitrary internal rotation/basis factorization and to raster reflection. The true span target is from the synthetic pre-artifact state, not from surviving-fragment bounding boxes. This loss separates known physical size and centre from uncertain in-plane orientation; coordinate point loss alone can otherwise favor a too-short basis when a predicted roll is wrong. This is a mechanism hypothesis, not a claim of proven causality.

Use the same AdamW learning rate `1e-4`, weight decay `1e-4`, 256 valid tissue sites per presentation and global gradient clip 5 as 254. The atlas-fit scorer, refiner and local map remain random in this stage; their training starts only after pose capture passes. Evaluate the frozen 248 synthetic DEV panel after the run, subject-equal and case-level, against 254 and 247, including selected and all-branch-oracle coordinate errors, centre, span, axis, reflection and visible-tissue strata. Continue this loss to a larger independent-draw exposure only if the batch-3,000 selected coordinate error is ≤4.0 mm, axis error ≤51°, and predicted minor-span ≤1.2 mm in no more than 10/227 cases. These are **development continuation thresholds**, far from a shippable accuracy claim. If they fail, do not simply increase exposure under the same loss; investigate a different pose representation or supervision. Regardless of outcome, later animal-separated biological validation, uncertainty calibration, and GUI deployment remain separate gates.
