# 182 TRAIN protocol: direct mapped-surface mixture

This protocol is fixed before launch. The only parent is frozen 132 step 2,000. Run one bounded, 300-update matched continuation in a previously nonexistent output tree; do not open DEV, calibration, final animals, or a public benchmark, and do not promote a GUI model from this assay. The run-local `development_used` and `final_animals_used` flags refer to **182 access**, not to whether parent 132 had previously been evaluated. All data, caches, checkpoints, logs and receipts stay on `I:/AnatomyTracker`. The 64 synthetic TRAIN deformation bases are not 64 biological animals; bases 56–63 are an inner panel held out only from **182 continuation** gradients, not from parent 132. The eight bases each supply eight independently sampled physical full-sphere sections in each of v3 and v4 (64 per appearance). Bases 0–55 supply fresh training sections; every optimizer update takes one eligible independently drawn section, cycling v4/v4/v3, and one weak Allen-affine TRAIN coronal and sagittal image. This is **300 synthetic presentations**, not 132's three-synthetic-per-update batch size or exposure; preserve the 132 per-sample formula and category weights, without multiplying this one-section loss by three. Every attempt, section ID, base, appearance, source-map receipt and image identity is logged before optimization. All three arms use the same ordered draws, physical section IDs, site seeds, weak-real images, parent weights, trainable parameters, optimizer schedule and direct losses.

The direct 132 model remains the architecture: 16 base modes, 64 normal anchors, two reflection representations, and its atlas-conditioned affine-free local mapper. The 181 truth-blind 16-branch beam is 8 base, 6 highest anchor and 2 normal-diverse anchor representations. It is selected solely from the current prediction and never from synthetic CCF truth. There is no 27-state grid, NCC selector, learned verifier, pose-refiner action, or truth-inserted inference candidate. Inference reports the direct posterior's top representation and its mapped tissue coordinates. Mixture probabilities and local covariance remain **uncalibrated**.

Retain 132's **per-sample** synthetic rigid mixture formula: 128 sampled valid tissue sites, five frame points, its normal penalty, soft target KL, base-mode and nearest-normal terms. Retain its 0.5-weight weak-real five-point mixture formula, with the weak Allen affine treated only as weak pose reference. The additional synthetic loss is `L_map = -0.7 logsumexp_k(log p_k - E_k/0.7)`, where `p_k` is the direct 160-way pose/reflection probability restricted *without renormalization* to the blind 16 and `E_k` is mean observed-valid-tissue CCF error in mm of the mapped surface. This is **supervised coordinate-risk feedback using synthetic CCF truth**, not a blind atlas-fit-difficulty loss. All three arms also receive 0.2 times the true-plane map error as a mapper-capacity anchor and 0.03 times the local-displacement magnitude/roughness penalty. The true plane exists only in synthetic **training**; it is never proposed or selected in inference or panel scoring. Map 16 branches at 96-pixel output with a 48-pixel atlas-feature grid, in chunks of **two** for the 11-GiB run GPU; evaluate 128 original-resolution tissue sites. Freeze the old atlas matcher, scalar ranking, refiner, fit-energy and uncertainty heads, plus `warp_shared`; train pose heads at 2e-5, shared encoder/laterals at 5e-6 and the same `warp_condition`, `atlas_encoder`, `pair`, `warp` mapper modules at 1e-5 in all arms. AdamW weight decay 1e-4 and the same 132 cosine-to-20% schedule apply.

The **full versus detached** contrast changes only the gradient route of `L_map` and its anchor/warp terms. In full, mapped error reaches direct pose states, their 160-way mass, and shared image features. In detached, detach `state`, `feature` and `global_feature` before `map`, and detach the prior in `L_map`; do **not** detach map costs or mapper parameters. Because `warp_shared` is frozen in all arms, detaching its output removes all mapped-error gradients to the pose/shared representation while all arms still train the same mapper modules. The unchanged rigid and weak-real objectives continue to train pose/shared. The **full versus support_only** contrast keeps the full gradient route and every draw/seed/weight fixed, but zeroes atlas intensity channel 0 passed to the mapper throughout training while preserving support channel 1. Synthetic images and CCF targets do not change. This tests whether atlas **intensity during this continuation** contributes beyond support and supervised coordinate risk; it cannot erase atlas information already learned by frozen parent 132.

To limit activation memory without changing the derivative, first compute all 16 current map costs without grad and set fixed responsibilities `r = softmax(log p - E/0.7)`. With unchanged parameters, backpropagate each two-branch `sum(r_detached * E)` and once `-0.7 sum(r_detached * log p)`; this is the exact first-order gradient of `L_map` at the current weights. The detached arm omits only that final mass gradient and detaches pose/shared inputs to every map call. Report each arm's mapper gradient norm at the first update; all must be nonzero, while full and support_only, but not detached, must have mapped-loss pose/shared gradients. Abort if this preflight identity fails.

Use no checkpoint selection: save 0, 150 and 300 in each arm, score only parent 0 and arm 300 on the fixed inner panel, and gate the step-300 models. Count one decision per unique physical section, report each appearance and each of the eight held-out continuation bases, with exact draw/source/parent/protocol hashes. Evaluate every arm under both intact and support-only atlases at inference, reusing the *same top-one direct state*; atlas condition cannot alter direct posterior selection. Report mapped-tissue mean and ≤1.5-mm fraction in both conditions, direct rigid mean and capture, best-blind-16 rigid capture, exact-supplied-plane mapper mean under intact atlas, atlas support coverage of the intact selected map, and p95 intact direct prediction+map latency. Intensity-zero evaluation alone is an out-of-distribution perturbation, not evidence that intensity improved pose learning; the trained support_only arm supplies the matched continuation control.

Progress beyond this TRAIN pilot only if, in **both** v3 and v4 independently: full top-one mapped-tissue mean under intact atlas beats detached and frozen 132 by at least 0.25 mm; its ≤1.5-mm fraction exceeds detached by at least 5 percentage points and is no worse than frozen 132; at least six of eight bases have non-worse mean error versus detached. Full direct rigid mean must be within 0.10 mm of the better of detached and parent, and direct rigid ≤1.5-mm capture within 5 points of their better capture. **Atlas-intensity training evidence requires** full direct rigid mean to beat support_only by at least 0.15 mm and capture by at least 5 points; full intact mapped mean must beat support_only mapped mean under both fixed intact inference and each arm's matched training atlas by at least 0.15 mm. Full best-16 rigid ≤1.5-mm capture may be no more than 5 points below detached or parent; exact-plane mapper mean may be no more than 0.05 mm worse than parent and must remain ≤0.25 mm; selected-map atlas coverage may be no more than 0.02 below parent; full intact mapping must be at least 0.05 mm better than its own intensity-zeroed inference; p95 intact direct prediction+map must stay ≤2.0 s on the run GPU. Any failed condition stops this route; a lower training loss or mapped gain without direct rigid gain versus support_only is not a pass. Even a pass allows only a separately prespecified animal-disjoint oblique/DV study with independent physical landmarks/tissue references and held-out uncertainty calibration, not a GUI claim.
