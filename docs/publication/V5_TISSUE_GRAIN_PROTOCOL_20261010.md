# v5: independently sampled tissue grain for arbitrary-plane sections

The TRAIN-only 119 audit compared 512 distinct acquired donors with independent synthetic sections at 192². Interior one-pixel high-pass RMS divided by tissue contrast was 0.0935 acquired versus 0.0751 synthetic; the three-pixel values were 0.2024 versus 0.1866. The acquired source is `I:/AnatomyTracker/data/joint_v7_reserved_train_images_192_001`, restricted by `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/reserved_real_train_appearance_census_001/rows.jsonl` to one section per TRAIN donor. Its completed manifest reports 1,885 TRAIN donors/263,754 TRAIN images, and the 119 receipt reports 512 distinct acquired donors. No DEV or final donor is a texture source. This descriptive gap does not establish texture as the cause of pose error or determine artifact prevalence.

`sample_one_shot_slide_artifacts_v5` draws **one new v3 physical plane and one new v3 appearance per example**, then adds independently seeded Gaussian-filtered, locally high-passed grain to its visible tissue. Gaussian scale is 0.55–1.25 source pixels and pre-exposure tissue RMS is 0.008–0.055. The broad range is a small TRAIN-motivated perturbation, not a fitted estimate of a real stain process. It does not copy acquired sections, donor-specific anatomy, or labels. Existing v3 tears, fragment displacement, folds, bubbles, seams, and optional brush then act on this textured source; v4 exposure/read noise follows. The grain is exactly zero where source visible tissue is zero, so raw exact-black exteriors stay zero. All pose, finite-thickness, CCF coordinate, local-map, validity and artifact targets remain from the original physical section; texture never changes geometry or labels. Provenance records the independent noise seed, kernel scale, amplitude and source-code hash. There are no paired black/gray or recolored versions of one section and no rejection of coincidentally similar random planes.

Before using v5 in training, run a bounded fresh TRAIN-only image QC at 192²: 256 independent v5 planes, compared descriptively with 256 **different** v4 planes and the frozen 119 acquired summary. Check interior one- and three-pixel high-pass RMS/contrast, full-image and tissue-interior quantiles, exact-black exterior fraction, and a small actual atlas/input image grid across orientations and defects. Inspect over-noisy or boundary-corrupted cases. Do not select a checkpoint or infer biological realism from the scalar gap. If acceptable, compare matched training exposure on v4 and v5 with the same model initialization but independent section streams; retain v5 only if fresh synthetic DEV and animal-separated real DEV improve without a coronal regression. Do not use this generator on DEV/final anatomy or tune it to final animals. No training, GUI promotion, calibrated uncertainty, or public benchmark is implied by this generator change.
