# 068: matched score discrimination before retraining

The frozen 067b raw-intensity high-pass score preferred a wrong blind maximum over the truth-fitted good transform in 25/32 synthetic sections. Before training another network, compare three **fixed, literature-motivated** image scores on the same frozen transform pairs from 067/067a: direct-render high-pass NCC (control), local normalized cross-correlation (robust to local intensity gain), and a compact neighbourhood self-similarity descriptor (MIND-style, robust to contrast polarity/shape). All use the same finite-thickness atlas renderer, 48×48 input, candidate support weighting, and no synthetic truth as a score input. The unavailable truth is used solely to designate the good transform for discrimination statistics. Count, for each score, how often the good transform out-ranks the wrong 067 maximum within its branch and across all branches; stratify by raw/black/brush appearance. An advance signal is at least 16/32 cross-branch wins and no raw-background collapse. This is a score diagnostic, not blind registration, animal validation, calibration, or a benchmark. No images are to be opened.
