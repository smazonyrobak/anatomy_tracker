# 316: matched real-field supervision of the current joint model

The [314](ACQUIRED_MAP_314_RESULT_AND_DECISION_20261015.md) current-lineage mapper failed against zero displacement, but the checkpoint-free [315](ACQUIRED_FIELD_IMAGE_SIGNAL_315_RESULT_20261015.md) assay found that the human affine-free field improves both atlas-image scores on the same acquired sections. The earlier 193 adaptation froze the image encoder and only narrowly beat zero in its older lineage. Test one information-pathway change on the current randomly initialized 309 lineage: whether real-field supervision needs to update the shared image encoder as well as the map modules. This is not yet map-to-pose feedback or a deployable model.

Start both arms from the **same** frozen 309 step-20,000 tensors. In both arms train `warp_shared`, `warp_condition`, `warp`, `pair`, and `atlas_encoder`; in the treatment alone also train `encoder` and `lateral`. Pose and probability output heads remain frozen in both. The treatment's pose predictions can still change indirectly through its shared encoder, so pose retention is mandatory. Use 600 matched batches per arm, each containing one of the 188 valid `CC2B/CC4B/CC4A` TRAIN real sections, traversed in seeded shuffled epochs, and one freshly sampled eligible full-sphere v10 synthetic section from TRAIN bases 0–55. Reset real and synthetic draw RNGs before each arm and verify exact matched draw identities and image hashes. One physical synthetic section is sampled independently per presentation; no plane is deliberately reused with changed artifacts. No outside model weights, pseudolabels, DEV/CAL/FINAL animal or public benchmark enter gradients.

For each acquired section, give the mapper its approximate human QuickNII pose only as a *map-supervision condition*. Use the 314 field sign, 192-pixel p99 image input, 96-pixel map grid and unweighted full-canvas affine-free reference. Apply mean tissue-site vector distance in mm. For the synthetic sample, give the exact post-artifact pose and reflection; supervise 96-pixel surviving-site CCF mapping error in mm at a fixed relative weight 0.25. Keep the same normalized five-offset ±50-µm atlas PSF for real maps; use each synthetic sample's own offsets/weights. Optimizer: AdamW, warp `3e-5`, other map modules `1e-5`, shared encoder/lateral `1e-6` only in the treatment, weight decay `1e-4`, gradient-norm cap 5. Keep both models in evaluation mode so normalization/dropout behavior is unchanged. Save step 0, 300 and 600, but inspect the guard only at the final endpoint.

The prespecified readout is the entire 52-section `AL1A` TRAIN-inner guard supplied-plane local-vector RMS, compared against the *same-grid* zero field and unchanged 309 parent. Also read the first 64 frozen v10 TRAIN-inner panel cases (eight per held-gradient synthetic base 56–63) for exact-pose mapped-site and blind first-choice pose retention; those reused cases are regression guards, not fresh improvement evidence. Record AL1A blind first-choice five-site gap to the approximate human plane, per-section and per-case rows, source hashes and paired draw receipts. A candidate is promising only if its AL1A mean map RMS is ≤0.9× both zero and parent, no more than four sections worsen against zero by >0.1 mm, synthetic exact-pose mapping worsens by ≤10% relative to parent, synthetic blind first-choice mean worsens by ≤0.15 mm with at most two extra >5-mm cases, and AL1A blind pose gap worsens by ≤0.20 mm. For a candidate passing those guards, repeat the AL1A map readout with the 52 acquired input images cyclically permuted one section while keeping each original supplied plane and target field: original input must lower mean RMS by ≥0.005 mm and win on at least 35/52 sections before calling its map image-dependent. To attribute a benefit to shared-encoder adaptation, its AL1A map RMS must additionally beat the map-only arm by ≥0.005 mm while satisfying the same guards. Failure stops this lineage extension; passing only licenses a fresh donor/animal-separated confirmation before map-to-pose feedback, larger-scale training, calibration or GUI promotion.

`AL1A` and the synthetic panel have been inspected repeatedly, and the weak human planes/fields are not independent biological truth. Any result here is a mechanism decision, not a generalization, physical arbitrary-oblique, electrode-region probability, or DeepSlice claim. All code, input, cache and outputs stay on `I:`.
