# Observed-coordinate continuation 015: limited mechanism test

Model 014 fixed the spatial head's gross real-domain mismatch but left 2.79 mm mapped error on the fixed arbitrary-plane synthetic DEV panel. The frozen 013 oracle diagnostic showed that an exact **observed tissue-to-CCF** coordinate field, including the imposed nonrigid warp, can recover the global plane near 0.12 mm when fitted over known tissue. That is the physically relevant correspondence target; the previous coordinate head was trained to predict the undistorted rigid plane at each *observed* image cell, an intentional simplification that may waste the visible local anatomical displacement signal.

Continue the same randomly initialized model lineage from 014 batch 12,000 for 12,000 batches, with three fresh independent arbitrary-plane synthetic sections and one previously unused real TRAIN section per batch. Unfreeze the shared image encoder, direct probabilistic pose head and coordinate head; keep the atlas-conditioned local mapper and fitted ranker weights fixed. Supervise synthetic coordinate cells against the **observed** CCF tissue field weighted by visible occupancy; retain a one-tenth-weight rigid-plane target outside visible tissue so the field can extrapolate. Supervise the real head against inherited weak Allen affine fields and preserve the existing direct all-branch physical-pose loss on both domains. The real labels remain weak, not expert truth. Keep strict provenance and no external model weights, old pseudolabels, DEV training, public benchmark or final-test animals.

After exit, score batch zero and every 2,000-batch checkpoint on exactly the same 185 eligible synthetic DEV sections from eight synthetic deformation plans and 64 real DEV images from six donors. Compare direct pose, coordinate-fitted pose, physical mapped tissue error and the known-correct-pose mapper; separately report predicted field error against both the observed tissue and rigid-plane fields. The direct and dense real five-point readouts are comparisons with weak affines. Require a meaningful synthetic mapped improvement (at least 10% from the 014 dense 2.793 mm) **without** a >0.2 mm real weak-affine regression or material correct-pose mapper regression before allocating a longer scaling run. This is a continuation gate, not deployment qualification or an anatomical probability claim.
