# Short matched v10 framing continuation: frozen protocol

The v10 framing preflight passed its limited coverage gate, but did not show that pose accuracy improves. Compare two short, otherwise matched pose continuations from the same frozen randomly initialized-project 305 rehearsal intervention checkpoint (`I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/joint_spatial_305_acquired_rehearsal_001/intervention/pose_local_00400.pt`, SHA-256 `56d10febb1a6e6592adf18b9b4c7fe1fe0beae2a9179d9ddaffb78eefb82e692`) and its AdamW moments. This tests the **v10 recipe**, not visible-tissue fraction in isolation: v10 also changes pixel scale and the box-offset distribution.

Freeze the atlas, local deformation, uncertainty, fitted matcher and reliability modules. Continue the encoder/lateral and direct/anchor probabilistic pose modules with the parent endpoint learning rates (pose 4e-6; shared 1e-6), existing 305 losses and **400 four-image batches per arm**, with readouts at batches 0, 200 and 400. Each batch has two independently sampled synthetic TRAIN sections from bases 0–55, one Lupori TRAIN section from CC2B/CC4B/CC4A, and one alternating Allen coronal/sagittal TRAIN section from a donor eligible for gradients. The old weak-affine loss weight remains 0.3. The control's two synthetic sections use v9; the intervention has one v9 and one v10. Use the same *base/donor schedule* but disjoint synthetic physical seeds and IDs across arms and panels. Never make paired background/artifact versions of a physical plane. Keep the existing 20+20 fully held-out older donor exclusions, readout-section exclusions and AL1A animal guard. Keep v9 and v10 sampler contexts separate: v10 mutates bounds and acquisition spans in memory.

Before gradients, freeze fresh independent **v9 and v10 64-section panels**, each with eight sections from every TRAIN-inner base 56–63. Use both same panels for parent/control/intervention readouts, never for gradients; no preflight case is reused. Log every attempted/accepted seed, physical identity, loss, first-choice rigid-site error, top-16 oracle capture within 1.5 mm, per-base error, acquisition guard and the older held-out donor-axis readouts. Save step-0/200/400 checkpoints and hashes on `I:`. Also report panel strata for sparse (<15% visible), oblique (≥45° from nearest cardinal), conservative-box versus TRAIN-tuple framing, and support-point versus box-offset plane placement. If the new v10 panel contains fewer than four sparse or four oblique sections, declare the primary result inconclusive without filtering or resampling after inspection.

The step-400 intervention passes only if its v10 mean first-choice gap beats control by **≥0.20 mm**, at least six/eight v10 base means improve, and v10 top-16 capture loses no more than four/64. It must also keep v9 first-choice mean within **0.10 mm** of control and v9 top-16 capture within four/64; AL1A weak Visualign five-site mean must be no worse than both the parent +0.20 mm and control +0.20 mm. Among the fully held-out older donors, sagittal first-axis accuracy must be ≥15/20 and no worse than control −1, and coronal accuracy ≥19/20 and no worse than control −1. A pass supports expanding this recipe carefully; it does not validate physical arbitrary-oblique accuracy, local warp, uncertainty, electrode regions or GUI use. A failure stops the framing intervention rather than resetting thresholds. No acquired DEV/CAL/FINAL or DeepSlice data, external trained weights/features/pseudolabels, or public benchmark may be used.
