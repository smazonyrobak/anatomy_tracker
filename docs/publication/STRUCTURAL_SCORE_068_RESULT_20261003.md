# 068: structural scores recover information the raw-intensity score missed

On the frozen 32-section synthetic development subset, 067b showed that the original broad-plane high-pass NCC objective gives the truth-fitted best-geometry transform mean score **0.289**, while its wrong blind maximum averages **0.447**. The good transform beat the blind maximum in only **7/32** sections. That rejects using this image score as a pose/deformation feedback signal.

In a matched follow-up, the exact same 067/067a transform pairs were rendered directly at 48×48 through the same finite-thickness atlas PSF and scored by three fixed metrics. The truth-fitted best-geometry transform beat **every frozen blind candidate** in 12/32 sections with direct high-pass NCC, 18/32 with local NCC, and **25/32 with a compact MIND-style neighbourhood self-similarity score**. MIND-style wins were 12/12 exact-black, 6/11 imperfect-brush, and 7/9 raw-background sections. It passed the predeclared 16/32 and raw ≥4/9 discrimination gate. The direct-render NCC improvement over 067b's broad-plane score also suggests interpolation/feature construction contributes to failure, but the paired comparison does not isolate that cause.

This is *not* blind registration success. Re-ranking only the 14 **already incorrect** 067 blind solutions by MIND still yields 4,948.6 µm identity-equal selected error, worse than the unmodified 059 beam (2,310.9 µm). A MIND-guided search must actually discover new, low-error transforms; the truth-fitted probes cannot be used at inference. The score is only a compact approximation of [MIND's self-similarity descriptor](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22722056/), not its exact original implementation. The comparison was motivated also by [multimodal histology registration using learned structural similarity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12271352/) and [histology reconstruction using contrastive registration](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09708-2); their results do not establish performance on our arbitrary-plane mouse task. [Image similarity alone is an unreliable surrogate for registration accuracy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3274625/), so subsequent decisions must use physical ground-truth errors, not score improvement alone.
