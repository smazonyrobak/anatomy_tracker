# 099 contextual matching: positive descriptor signal, no pose or release claim

The frozen 099 pilot completed 4,000 accepted, distinct synthetic TRAIN sections in 4,433 draws. It used the internally initialized 094 lineage, frozen 094 image/atlas projections and pose/fitting geometry, and trained only the new multiscale contextual residuals. No legacy application model, external learned weights, real labels, calibration animals or public benchmark entered this experiment. The audited first/last 500-batch mean loss changed 0.1354→0.1208; fine and coarse hard-match margins changed 0.0016→0.0372 and −0.0061→0.0321. These are TRAIN diagnostics, not anatomical accuracy.

The independent frozen DEV panel contains 256 separately drawn planes from eight new synthetic deformation identities; 242 met the predeclared visibility rule. Fourteen were retained in the record but excluded from the match readout. The panel and TRAIN are disjoint in animal/specimen/experiment/plan identity, though every synthetic identity still derives from one Allen atlas. The panel's box-slab sampling produces more peripheral sections than TRAIN's predominantly support-point sampling. It is a useful stress test, not biological animal separation. All 256 panel files, 13 source snapshots, and eight plan packages passed independent hash/identity audits. The evaluation verified **exact** step-0 equality to 094 logits, support and decoded coordinates, then used identical frozen candidate states at step 0 and 4,000.

Thus the predeclared **descriptor scale-up signal passed**: all-candidate raw top-1 rose 0.0606, physically best raw top-1 rose 0.2190, all eight deformation plans improved, and raw/exact-black/imperfect-brush modes each improved. A separate aggregation of all 6,776 candidate rows reproduced the two primary means. However, the operational match remains **below the unchanged-plane null** by 0.026 across all candidates and 0.109 for the physically best candidate. The 099 descriptor was not fed into 094 pose fitting or candidate selection, so its improvement cannot be called a joint-registration improvement.

The predeclared 100 exploratory angle/visibility readout on the same frozen panel confirms the larger global gap. The best candidate among 14 has plan-equal mapped-96-point error **0.832 mm** and normal error **13.2°**; the unchanged 094 score selects **2.580 mm** and **31.3°**. For the 24 most oblique eligible sections (45–54.7° from every cardinal axis), best-vs-selected mapped error is 0.930 vs 3.454 mm; selected normal error is 53.1°. These are synthetic diagnostics, not evidence that such cuts are intrinsically unrecoverable: the candidate beam often contains a much better answer than the current selector picks. Conversely, the best candidate's 0.832-mm average remains too coarse to infer small electrode nuclei. The panel is often partial: 57 eligible sections have <10% valid pixels, 170 have 10–30%, and only 15 have ≥30%. Per-angle cells are exploratory and share one atlas template.

The deterministic six-section visual grid at `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/contextual_match_099_examples/actual_generator_true_and_guessed_planes.png` shows true affine atlas plane, actual artifacted generator input, and unchanged 094 selected atlas plane for cuts ≥25° from the nearest cardinal axis. Selection was by family/artifact count/visible fraction, never model score. Its frozen selection receipt records all section IDs and hashes. The images show real geometric variety, but also stylized damage and examples where a plausible-looking selected plane is millimetres wrong. See [the prespecified domain/feasibility protocol](DOMAIN_AND_ARBITRARY_PLANE_100_PROTOCOL_20261008.md) for the active generator's missing effects and the real-data limitation.

Decision: retain 099 as a promising **descriptor branch**, not a shippable model. Do not claim calibrated confidence, launch a DeepSlice comparison, or promote it to the GUI. Before a large exposure increase, visually and statistically compare actual generator artifacts/framing with available acquired TRAIN material, recognizing that Allen Product-5 images may differ from the lab's staining and processing; revise only demonstrated gaps and keep every new training section's geometry and appearance independently sampled. Then continue the same lineage with best-DEV retention, require the *operational* matcher to beat the unchanged-plane control, and connect fitting evidence back into the direct probabilistic pose predictor while measuring selected physical error. In parallel, test full-plane versus partial-section feasibility across all angle families and obtain expert-labeled oblique animals; synthetic angular coverage alone cannot validate real arbitrary-plane use. The Allen CCF's nominal 10-µm voxel grid was interpolated from 100-µm through-plane STPT sampling, so off-axis atlas fidelity is an additional unresolved domain question.
