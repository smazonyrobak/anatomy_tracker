# TRAIN-only observable atlas-fit evidence assay

The inherited fitted selector has a large truth-best-eight gap, and a v2-artifact stress readout found no mean normal-angle improvement. Before changing the model, use a fixed batch-653 checkpoint and 96 fresh independently drawn eligible v2 TRAIN cases (seed `2026100903`) to measure whether a deliberately simple anatomical descriptor supplies missing candidate-ranking evidence. Each case has one new plane, appearance and artifact draw; no paired variants or saved pseudolabels. Atlas planes, warp fields and candidate scores may be observed at inference; synthetic true CCF coordinates and validity masks are used **only** to measure candidate errors afterward.

For the same eight top image-prior branches, render the rigid atlas and the atlas after the inherited predicted warp. Compare each with the source image using unsigned eight-bin gradient-orientation histograms in 8×8-pixel cells, normalized per cell and weighted by atlas tissue support. Record separately: unwarped HOG distance, post-warp HOG distance, atlas support, predicted warp magnitude/roughness, image prior and inherited fitted score. No true tissue mask enters these candidate features. The unsigned gradient descriptor follows the staining/illumination-tolerant motivation of [Xiong et al.](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroinformatics/articles/10.3389/fninf.2018.00093/full), but its implementation and applicability here must be assessed, not assumed.

Predeclare two summaries: (1) within-case pairwise concordance with synthetic mapped error when candidates differ by at least 0.25 mm, and (2) mean mapped error if each individual feature alone selects the best of eight. A feature with pairwise concordance above 0.60 and no collapse on raw-background or heavy-oblique strata is worth testing as an input to a new TRAIN-only selector; less is not sufficient to justify joint pose-gradient training. This is not a release gate or final validation. No real DEV, animal holdout, DeepSlice benchmark, calibration, GUI integration or weights are changed.
