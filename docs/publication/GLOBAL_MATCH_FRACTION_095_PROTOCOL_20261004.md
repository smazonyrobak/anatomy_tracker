# 095: can whole-slice match evidence select the right atlas plane?

Freeze this before 095 TRAIN draws or DEV scoring. The completed 094 model contains a genuinely accurate candidate in its 14-plane beam (best mapped96 0.864 mm) but picks a 2.537-mm candidate. On 182 complementary synthetic DEV sections, actual correct-match fraction is 0.605 for the best and 0.114 for the worst candidate, whereas 094 predicts 0.858 and 0.842. Its balanced per-batch site BCE produces useful local supervision but is not a prevalence-preserving candidate-fraction objective; the local verifier also lacks explicit whole-slice context. These observations motivate a small isolated readout experiment, not a claim that either choice caused the failure.

Use the frozen randomly initialized/scratch-trained 094 step-20,000 checkpoint as parent. Freeze its entire image/pose model, 3-D matcher, bounded fit, and local mapper. Add only one trainable whole-field candidate-quality head that sees the full 32×32 fine correspondence distribution/support, 094 spatial evidence and its image-only tissue-validity prediction. It must have a whole-field context path and output a single logit per candidate estimating the *fraction of surviving tissue sites* whose 3-D match is within 1.5 mm of the synthetic true physical location. No mask, true plane, specimen ID, experimental label, or parent-network checkpoint from outside this scratch lineage enters inference. Avoid paired copies of the same plane with only appearance changed.

Train this head on 12,000 independently randomized eligible TRAIN sections, one independently sampled geometry/appearance per draw, batch one. Form the unchanged blind 8-old/6-anchor beam and select four existing candidates as in 094 (physically best, support-nearest ≥0.5 mm wrong, highest-prior ≥1 mm wrong, one remaining random); truth is used solely for TRAIN candidate/target/loss construction. Optimize prevalence-preserving binary cross-entropy with the true *fraction* as a soft target, plus a small pairwise ranking loss for candidates whose physical error differs by ≥0.5 mm. Do not class-balance positives and negatives within each example. Keep the parent pose and warp fixed to isolate the failure mode. Save the initial, 4,000 and 12,000 checkpoints, exact physical-section/virtual-subject/provenance draw log, train losses, source/protocol hashes and completion receipt on I: only.

After TRAIN exits, independently evaluate all checkpoints on the exact 086 fixed 14 branches for the same 246 eligible 061 synthetic DEV sections/eight plans. Recompute whole-field head scores from the frozen parent and independently join to the verified 094 raw candidate mappings, checking section, branch, source hashes and parent-state equality. For every candidate use the same score form, `direct log prior + log(predicted correct-match fraction)`, with no DEV-fitted temperature; report the candidate fraction's mean absolute error and within-section Spearman with actual fraction/lower mapped error, fixed-beam selected mapped96 error, score/error ordering, appearance strata and natural updated beam separately. Choose the best of the three checkpoints by minimum plan-equal fixed-beam selected mapped96, noting DEV selection bias. The focused advancement gate is candidate predicted-fraction/lower-error ρ≥0.30, selected mapped96≤2.192 mm, fraction MAE≤0.15 on the 182 sections not inspected in 093, and no raw/black/brush stratum >0.15 mm worse than 089. Also require intact parent best14 mapped96 and exact source/receipt audits. A head-only pass establishes only that the available match field supports better *selection*, not that joint pose/deformation or real-animal generalization is solved; if it passes, jointly train the head with pose/fit/map and perform donor-disjoint acquired validation. If it fails, further exposure alone is not presumed helpful; inspect geometric descriptor discrimination and whole-slice compatibility before redesigning the matcher.

This isolates the evidence readout with minimal extra machinery. [SLIV-Reg](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18683v1) uses many 2-D/3-D correspondences and robust consensus to determine slice pose; [PointDSC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.05465) uses nonlocal spatial consistency for outlier rejection. These are architectural analogies, not imported weights or evidence of histology performance. No public DeepSlice benchmark, untouched animal test, probability calibration, electrode-region certainty claim, or GUI promotion is authorized by this diagnostic.
