# 134 independent frozen-descriptor evaluation

Freeze this evaluator and protocol before inspecting the completed 134 descriptor run. The only primary checkpoint is **step 4,000**. Steps 0, 1,000, 2,000 and 3,000 describe learning trajectories and cannot replace the final checkpoint on the basis of DEV results. If step 4,000 fails, report failure; a new TRAIN-only run/protocol is required to test a different stopping rule.

Evaluate the unchanged frozen 132 step-2,000 pose/map model and its exact blind 16-branch beam. For every eligible section of the fresh-v4-132 DEV panel and the existing older `joint_in_path_correspondence_128_dev_panel` v3 DEV panel, compute the beam and fitted 96-grid maps **once**. The separate `v3_pose_capture_confirmation_panel_001` remains untouched for later qualification. At each candidate's actual fitted coordinates, render the finite-thickness atlas intensity and support on a 64-grid with the section PSF. Use the same candidate-independent image-derived reliability and identical coverage, local-displacement and strain costs as the 134 TRAIN objective. Score each frozen descriptor milestone with both identically trained arms, full atlas intensity and support-only, in two prespecified ways: fit-only `-3E` and prior-plus-fit `frozen direct pose log prior - 3E`. The direct prior is the common no-fit control. Reuse the same beam, fitted surfaces, support, true synthetic targets and pair definitions for all arms and milestones.

Scoring may use input images and sampled PSF, but must not use target state, valid mask, true observed-to-atlas coordinates, deformation plan or synthetic section metadata. Open truth fields only after **all** candidate scores are frozen. Then compute mean observed-valid-pixel 3D fitted-surface error in millimetres at native 256, rigid-surface error, first-choice ≤1.5-mm capture, and the best-in-beam bound. Preserve and report the frozen eligibility exclusions rather than filtering by candidate quality. No DEV truth enters gradients or checkpoint selection.

The primary comparison is **step-4,000 prior-plus-fit** on fresh v4. Plan-equal selected fitted error for the full-intensity arm must improve by at least 0.15 mm against **both** the same-checkpoint support-only arm and the direct pose prior. Its plan-equal ≤1.5-mm first-choice rate must improve by at least 0.05 against both. For conditional anatomy discrimination, consider a section only when its best fitted beam candidate is ≤1.5 mm and another candidate is >1 mm worse, has atlas-support Dice ≥0.85, support-area-fraction gap ≤0.08 and ≥32 common supported 64-grid pixels. Choose the least-wrong such candidate using synthetic truth only after scoring; compare score margins best minus wrong. Require ≥64 such pairs, full arm winning ≥60%, and its win fraction exceeding support-only by ≥10 percentage points. All four requirements must hold. Report pair count and all non-scored reasons. These thresholds are evidence for a subsequent pose-feedback experiment, not model promotion.

The separate v3 retention guard for that same primary score is: full arm plan-equal selected fitted error no more than 0.15 mm worse than direct, and plan-equal ≤1.5-mm rate no more than 0.05 lower. A v4 signal pass with v3 retention failure does not justify feeding this descriptor back into pose. Report all other checkpoint/score-mode results without changing the primary decision. Include per-plan, raw/exact-black/imperfect-brush, v4 low-exposure and non-low-exposure strata; report section-equal and plan-equal estimates separately. Every case retains animal/specimen/experiment and physical-section provenance, but both panels are synthetic deformations of one Allen atlas, not independent biological animals.

The completed 134 run, its 132 parent, panel records/files/source snapshots, atlas receipt, evaluator code and this protocol must be SHA-256 bound in an `I:`-only completed receipt. Evaluation must not access public benchmarks, final or expert-labeled animals, or claim calibrated probabilities. A passing synthetic descriptor signal still requires genuine joint coordinate-gradient feedback, then blinded real-animal validation and uncertainty calibration before GUI replacement.
