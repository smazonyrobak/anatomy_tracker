# 266: whole-slice context to whole-atlas correspondences

## Why this experiment

The frozen 265 spatial scorer failed: its entire finite-raster bank was already 4.391 mm from truth on average, its ranked top 32 was farther away, and intact atlas intensity beat support-only by only 0.147 mm. It cannot be rescued by making the same fixed bank scorer larger. Earlier 030/031 placed some correct patch matches in global lists, but position-only consensus promoted coherent false matches; 032/033 then collapsed toward no-match. The new mechanism must generate a plane from image-to-atlas correspondences without a parent pose or discrete plane bank, and use *whole-slice context* during feature extraction rather than isolated patches. This is motivated by single-slice 2-D/3-D feature matching and robust pose fitting in [SLIV-Reg](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18683v1), and cross-modal contrastive representations in [CoMIR](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2020/hash/d6428eecbe0f7dff83fc607c5044b2b9-Abstract.html). Neither paper demonstrates mouse-histology performance. Do not interpret a local match metric as pose success.

## Frozen, bounded TRAIN-inner stage

Initialize both arms from the same fresh random weights. A 2-D full-image CNN plus two attention layers encodes 16×16 query positions, including global context. A trainable multiscale 3-D CNN encodes the *entire* 200-µm Allen atlas grid, not a truth-centred crop. One arm sees atlas intensity/support/coordinates; the matched control sees support/coordinates with intensity zeroed. Their input images and optimizer draws are identical. Neither arm loads 260, 265, any other prior project weight, external feature/pseudolabel, or acquired weak pose label. A matchability output learns whether a query site survives tissue damage/background. Full-volume and 400-µm coarse-grid correspondence losses use synthetic tissue-to-CCF coordinates **only as training targets**. Coordinates, valid-tissue masks, true pose, and synthetic artifact parameters are never image/model inputs at inference.

Reserve independent synthetic TRAIN deformation bases 56–63 for a fixed 64-section TRAIN-inner panel, eight fresh sections per base. Only bases 0–55 supply optimizer examples. Every generated section receives a new physical-section seed and one independently sampled appearance; there are no deliberately paired backgrounds or repeated planes. Use 1,500 batches of two independently sampled eligible sections (3,000 presentations), with checkpoint/readout at batches 500 and 1,500. Extend only if the frozen TRAIN-inner metrics improve; do not open synthetic DEV or biological CAL/FINAL merely to tune this pilot. Run on `I:` only, preserve source, seed, subject, draw, checkpoint and result hashes. Do not touch public DeepSlice evaluation.

## Decision measurements

On the frozen 64 cases, report plan-equal top-one and top-eight 3-D match recall within 0.5 mm at actually valid query sites, and the same metric using only automatically selected query sites. Report matchable precision/recall, the fraction with three spatially separated correct top-eight matches, exact physical-coordinate error, appearance/angle strata, and paired intact-minus-support differences at both checkpoints. `≥3` separated matches is a *necessary* condition for plane fitting, not a deployable plane prediction; no oracle-selected pose can be called model accuracy.

Proceed to a blind robust correspondence-to-plane fitter only if the intact arm's top-eight recall rises on the fixed panel and is meaningfully above support-only, with at least 25% of sections containing three separated correct automatically selected matches by batch 1,500. If recall improves but has not reached that level, extend the same architecture at most one predeclared scale step before changing it. If it collapses or support-only matches it, diagnose atlas orientation information, source appearance and learning objective rather than making a full joint-warp model around a broken correspondence field. A fitter must later beat 260 on blind held-out tissue-site error, then add bounded local deformation and prove its fit cost improves pose. Animal-separated acquired validation and numerical probability calibration remain mandatory before any GUI replacement or electrode-region claim.

The 200-µm 3-D codebook loses local plane orientation, whereas SLIV-Reg explicitly renders oriented 2-D atlas patches; failure of this particular codebook cannot establish arbitrary-plane impossibility. Sections containing too little or near-symmetric tissue may be genuinely multimodal; the eventual model must retain alternatives or abstain, and optional acquisition constraints may narrow them without serving as hidden truth labels.
