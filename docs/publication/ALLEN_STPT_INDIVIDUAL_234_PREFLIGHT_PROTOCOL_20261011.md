# 234: individual STPT volume source preflight, no model training

The matched [233 source ablation](KIMLAB_LSFM_SOURCE_ABLATION_233_RESULT_20261011.md) did not improve blind original-source localization. Do not extend that average-template contrast mix. A different question is whether actual individual 3-D anatomical images can supply the diversity absent from the current single population-average intensity plus procedural deformations. The official [Allen MapMySections data page](https://alleninstitute.github.io/MapMySections/) describes CCF-registered red/green STPT volumes at 25-µm output resolution, and its [official tutorial](https://github.com/AllenInstitute/MapMySections/blob/main/ccf_and_registered_stpt_tutorial.ipynb) names the red NIfTI channel. This is a **source feasibility audit**, not an accuracy experiment or a new ground-truth claim.

1. Read the official challenge sheet to identify image-series IDs in its **training** partition, and check whether specimen/animal and experiment identifiers can be recovered. Keep its test partition unused. Pin the sheet and selected S3 object URLs, byte counts, hashes, license/terms and retrieval date. Select two distinct TRAIN image series before looking at image appearance; fetch only their red registered volumes and the minimal CCF metadata needed for orientation checks, all under `I:/AnatomyTracker/data/allen_stpt_individual_234/`. Do not fetch a large cohort at this stage.
2. Verify each NIfTI affine, axis order, shape, voxel spacing and atlas support against the pinned CCF; test the eight axis flips and physical landmarks rather than assume filenames imply voxel alignment. Inspect whether the two volumes differ from each other and from the Allen population average beyond scale/contrast. Registered-to-CCF intensities are *not* independent precise physical slice labels and are *not* undeformed animal geometry.
3. Render a fixed, small set of independently sampled full-sphere, finite-thickness TRAIN brain-intersecting planes from each volume. Show each source plane beside its matched CCF plane with absolute and display-stretched intensity. Quantify section visibility, through-plane blur/anisotropy and seam artifacts, especially at steep obliquity. STPT was physically acquired as coronal sections about 100 µm apart, so a 25-µm resampled volume must not be described as isotropic physical histology.
4. Advance only if the individual volumes have coherent anatomy, genuinely distinct visible texture, trustworthy coordinate/identity provenance, and usable arbitrary-oblique reslices at the model's 256-pixel scale. Even a pass licenses only a bounded TRAIN-source study with animal/series grouping and unchanged external validation; it does not establish an all-angle physical-slice model. A failure means do not train on this family merely because it is large.

No external model weights, features or pseudolabels may enter training.
