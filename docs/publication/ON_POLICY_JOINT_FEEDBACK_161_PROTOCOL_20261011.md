# 161: on-policy anatomy-to-pose feedback, frozen before training

## Why this experiment

The 160 continuation preserved weak-real pose performance but missed its first-choice synthetic gain gate (0.120 mm versus 0.250 mm required). The older fitted score in 159 actively worsened blind selection, so more of the same pose-only or score-only training is unjustified. Start from the scratch-trained 132 step-2000 checkpoint, not the failed 160 checkpoint. The scientific question is whether a candidate's *anatomical* atlas fit and the amount of tissue deformation can teach the direct plane predictor, beyond silhouette/area or ordinary pose supervision.

The completed 161 TRAIN-only preflight, `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/joint_feedback_161_train_preflight_001`, used 128 independent v4 physical-section draws from TRAIN bases only (no deliberate same-plane appearance variants). Of these, 95 met the generator's original eligibility criterion, 64 had a truth-near (≤1.5-mm mean physical displacement) branch in the truth-blind top 16, and 56 had at least one near/wrong pair with comparable atlas support (922 pairs). The pair rule is wrong at least 1 mm worse, rendered-support Dice ≥0.85, area difference ≤0.08 of the 24×24 grid, and ≥32 common cells. Pair sections occur across all tested nearest-cardinal angle bins, including 45–55° (6/14 draws). Yet among sections with <8% valid tissue, only 1/59 draws has a pair. Pair availability does **not** establish an anatomy-sensitive score or accuracy. Sparse intersecting planes remain in the intended input domain; training/evaluation must separately report them and never turn them into confident forced guesses.

## Frozen staged comparison

1. Freeze 132's direct pose predictor while fitting its existing atlas-conditioned local tissue mapper on *predicted* TRAIN top-16 branches near the truth; preserve supplied-exact and supplied-near map quality. Train a branch-difficulty score on on-policy near/wrong pairs with comparable support. The negative branch must not receive a supervised warp-to-truth target that encourages an implausibly large deformation. A second, identically seeded arm trains from the same parent and same sections with atlas *intensity zeroed during training*, keeping support/pose/feature inputs; this is the silhouette control, not an after-the-fact ablation.
2. Hold back disjoint TRAIN bases/plans for early selection. Before any DEV read, require ≥64 support-matched pair decisions; the full-intensity arm must rank the near branch above the wrong branch at least 10 percentage points more often than the support-only arm. Check atlas-intensity swaps and feature removal on these TRAIN-inner cases. If the ranking advantage disappears, stop: do not call it anatomical evidence or continue to expensive pose training.
3. Freeze the selected fitter and run a no-update gradient preflight. Both pose-training arms must have identical forward mapped coordinates and direct loss on the same sample. The treatment passes spatially supervised post-fit physical-coordinate error (and constrained deformation difficulty) through finite-thickness rendering, local fitting, and candidate state back into the *same direct pose predictor*. The control detaches the candidate-state feedback path while retaining the same ordinary direct-pose loss. Show nonzero finite pose-coordinate gradient in treatment and zero feedback-path gradient in control; log their magnitudes before choosing a bounded coefficient.
4. Train the matched treatment/control on the same independently drawn physical sections, background/artifact distribution, TRAIN-only weak-real retention mixture, optimizer state, and checkpoint schedule. Do not deliberately keep a plane fixed and vary just its background. Reusing the **identical** draws across the two experimental arms is a paired scientific control, not a paired-image training augmentation. Selection of a checkpoint uses TRAIN-inner outcomes only. Track per-appearance, angle, and tissue-fraction losses and candidate capture. Do not increase presentation count by a preset million if animal-separated validation stops improving.
5. Only if TRAIN-inner gates pass, open a newly frozen synthetic DEV panel and weak-real coronal/sagittal DEV identities once. Primary metric is the *unfitted first-choice direct pose* mean five-point/valid-tissue physical error, plan-equal, against the detached arm. Require at least 0.30-mm mean gain, ≥10-percentage-point gain in direct top-1 ≤1.5 mm, and non-worsening in at least six of eight deformation plans. Blind top-16 near capture may fall by no more than five points. Supplied-exact and supplied-near local map error may regress by no more than 0.10 mm; each real donor's weak-reference five-point error may regress by no more than 0.50 mm and donor-equal mean by no more than 0.20 mm. Report all cases, including low-tissue failures, per angle and tissue fraction. This is an internal development gate, not final animal validation or public benchmarking.

If stage 1 or 2 fails, retain 132 as a research baseline and diagnose the measured failure rather than scaling training. If the DEV gate passes, increase distinct physical-plane presentations only while genuinely independent animal-level metrics improve. No external pretrained weights, pretrained features, or pseudo-label teacher may enter the lineage. The current pose distribution and local-map variance are **uncalibrated**; no 90% electrode-region probability or GUI replacement claim is permitted until expert-labelled, unseen-animal calibration and release checks exist. Any future public DeepSlice comparison remains sealed until internal validation is convincingly promising.
