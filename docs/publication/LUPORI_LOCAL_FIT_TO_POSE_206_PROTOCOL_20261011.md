# 206: matched local rigid MIND fit-to-pose pilot (pre-run)

This is a bounded TRAIN-only learning experiment, not a model-selection or deployment run. Both arms restore the identical scratch-lineage `lupori_fluorescence_pose_187_inner_AL1A/joint_step_00800.pt` model **and AdamW moments**. They replay the same 400 updates: one valid Lupori section drawn without replacement per epoch from `CC2B`, `CC4B`, `CC4A`, plus three independent arbitrary-plane v7-stream TRAIN synthetic sections (fluorescence-compatible artifact v7/v7 and v3 appearances). Each draw uses its returned artifact-aware `valid_mask` for synthetic tissue sites. `AL1A` is strictly excluded from loading and gradients. The official DEV `CC1B`, CAL `CC3A`, FINAL `CC1A`, and any public benchmark are not read. The exact image and weak human VisuAlign plane are prepared as in 187; the human mask weights supervised tissue sites but is not an input channel.

Both arms use 187's direct weak-human real mixture loss and exact-pose synthetic mixture loss. Only the direct pose heads and shared encoder/lateral features are trainable; map, atlas matcher, refiner, and uncertainty heads stay frozen. The full arm alone receives a rigid finite-thickness MIND descriptor loss from the **currently predicted prior top-1 pose/reflection**, never a truth-best candidate or oracle-supplied pose. That term is permitted only when this top-1 has mean five-frame-point error ≤1.5 mm against the TRAIN human plane and the predicted atlas support has at least 400 pixels after 7×7 erosion. The threshold is a training-safety gate, not fit supervision. The atlas-support mask and intensity-normalization support are detached. No local warp participates. No MIND score enters global branch masses or reflection probabilities.

The fit gradient is computed separately and injected only into the **final geometry-output rows** of the three pose heads, not their shared hidden layers or probability rows. Its norm is capped at 20% of the same-update supervised geometry-output gradient norm before the common 5.0 global clip. The detached arm computes and logs the same fit diagnostic when eligible but injects no fit gradient. Every eligible step records the actual autograd fit-state gradient's direction relative to the weak-human selected-pose cost gradient in a stated mixed state scale (1 mm for centre coordinates, unit scale for rotation-6D/log-scale/shear); positive dot product predicts that infinitesimal fit descent reduces the weak-reference cost. This is an audit, **not** a guarantee that MIND improves pose or an eligibility filter. Nonfinite losses/gradients abort rather than being silently skipped.

The runner writes `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/lupori_local_fit_to_pose_206_matched_001`: hashed config and source/checkpoint/animal provenance, every draw attempt and per-step raw metrics/gradient audit, checkpoints at 0/200/400 for each arm, and completion receipts. No checkpoint is selected by a held-out set in this script. After exit, a separate frozen readout should compare paired `AL1A` selected and all-candidate five-point error, synthetic arbitrary-plane retention, and old weak-real retention at the declared milestones. The pre-run advancement rule is for **step 400**: fit must lower paired `AL1A` first-choice mean five-point error by at least 0.10 mm versus detached, create no additional `AL1A` first-choice >5-mm failures, regress by no more than 10% on each frozen v3/v7 synthetic selected-pose panel, and raise each old weak-real donor-equal discrepancy by no more than 0.20 mm. Otherwise do not scale fit feedback. Report the raw paired effects and descriptive section-level confidence intervals regardless of this gate; one TRAIN animal does not support a population-level interval. Fit-gradient direction, not merely a favorable ±250 µm discrete MIND ordering, must be examined. Existing 204 evidence was only locally suggestive (AL1A 24/33, CC1B 40/49 local direction wins); MIND did not reliably beat the direct prior globally. These are near-coronal acquired slides with approximate human planes, so this pilot cannot establish steep-oblique biological accuracy, calibrated probabilities, safe electrode-region assignment, or GUI readiness.
