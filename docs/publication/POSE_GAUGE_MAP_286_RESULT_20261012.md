The fitted pose alone lowered the unwarped error by **0.074372 mm (36.3%)**. This is an oracle-label/geometry effect: the fitting procedure had access to synthetic dense coordinates that an inference model does not. It must not be described as a 0.076-mm learned-warp improvement. The post-artifact arm's learned map shifted sampled tissue by 0.014306 mm on average but reduced error by only 0.001525 mm (1.17% of its no-warp error). Its gain advantage over the old-label arm was 0.001927 mm, not the preregistered 0.010 mm. Fixed post-artifact gains at 0/750/1,500/3,000 optimizer batches were +0.000004/+0.000171/+0.000226/+0.001525 mm; the late increase does not turn the fixed gate into a pass.

The prespecified **gate fails**: post-artifact gain ≥0.020 mm and advantage ≥0.010 mm both fail. Six of eight nonnegative base means and derivative ≤0.35001 pass. The mean fitted-label centre moved 0.132725 mm and the unsigned plane normal 0.463° from the inherited state on the panel. The wrapper retains the original finite-thickness PSF offsets/weights, so the refitted normal is a central-surface label, not a separately refitted slab director. As [audit 285](V9_GENERATOR_AUDIT_285_RESULT_20261012.md) already cautioned, the affine-free field has no demonstrated capacity floor on observed tissue; [oracle 281](EXACT_MAP_CAPACITY_281_RESULT_20261012.md) can fit individual coordinate targets. This result instead shows that correcting the gauge alone did not make the **frozen-feature image mapper** learn a useful transferable field in 3,000 unique TRAIN sections.

Do not promote either 286 checkpoint. The next bounded change is learnable, map-relevant source/atlas correspondence at sufficient spatial resolution, with a gauge-consistent pose objective and fresh sections for readout; do not simply multiply the same frozen-feature schedule. Pose selection remains much worse than conditional mapping ([275](POSE_CAPTURE_275_RESULT_20261012.md)), and an inference model must learn the fitted pose from pixels rather than receive it from dense truth. Independent physical arbitrary-oblique animal validation, calibrated electrode-region uncertainty, GUI replacement, and a fair later DeepSlice comparison are still outstanding. No DEV/CAL/FINAL animal or public benchmark was used in 286.
