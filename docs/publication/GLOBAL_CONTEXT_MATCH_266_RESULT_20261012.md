# 266: full-image 2-D to whole-atlas 3-D matching did not capture pose

The frozen 266 pilot completed 1,500 optimizer batches (3,000 independently drawn, eligible synthetic TRAIN sections). Its independent post-exit verifier rehashed the source, protocol, 64 stored TRAIN-inner sections, draw and readout logs, and both checkpoints; reproduced every per-section checkpoint-0/500/1500 readout from the saved images and weights; confirmed 3,000 distinct accepted physical-section IDs and no overlap between optimizer bases 0–55 and inner bases 56–63. No external or earlier project weights/features/pseudolabels, biological DEV/CAL/FINAL animals, GUI route, or public benchmark entered this test. The image-feature/target-centre alignment was corrected **before** the source hash was frozen and training launched.

Recall is averaged first within each section and then equally across the eight bases; there were 4,035 actually valid query sites. Intact-atlas fine-grid validation cross-entropy fell 11.70→10.03, but that loss decrease did **not** produce useful location matches. At batch 1,500, the intact-minus-support top-8 gain was only **0.23 percentage points**, with four of eight bases favoring each arm. The predeclared necessary three-match gate was 25%; it failed. Most automatically selected sites were valid tissue (91.5% precision), but only 3.15% of those sites had a correct location among their eight highest-scoring atlas points. No blind plane, local deformation, uncertainty calibration, or electrode-site probability was evaluated. In particular, falling loss is not a claim that a slice can be registered.

**Decision:** do not promote this checkpoint or extend the unchanged codebook. The support/coordinate-only control nearly matches the intact atlas, so the learned field is not extracting enough location-specific anatomy to justify scale-up. The atlas CNN produces one orientation-agnostic descriptor per 200-µm voxel; unlike the explicitly orientation-sampled descriptors in [SLIV-Reg](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18683v1), it has no representation of how a local structure looks when sectioned at a particular angle. Earlier 030 used oriented patches and placed a correct point among 16 candidates more often, but its blind geometric selection still failed; those numbers were obtained with different query selection, panels and banks and are **not** a matched improvement claim. The next mechanism must learn globally coherent full-slice pose and make anatomy-specific atlas evidence change its decision, rather than merely lower independent point-classification loss. It should test a direct probabilistic full-frame pose proposal with a sufficiently capable scratch image encoder against a matched atlas-conditioned joint pose/deformation update; the update is retained only if blind mapped-site error improves and a correct supplied plane does not drift. Current synthetic-to-acquired appearance gaps (especially unbrushed black exteriors and fine tissue texture) also need correction from TRAIN-only evidence, but they cannot explain this synthetic-only miss. Failure here does **not** establish that arbitrary-plane localization is impossible.
