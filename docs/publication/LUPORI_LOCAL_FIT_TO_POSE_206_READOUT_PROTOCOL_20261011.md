# 206 matched local-fit-to-pose post-exit readout (preregistered before exit)

This document freezes the readout plan for the still-running `lupori_local_fit_to_pose_206_matched_001` experiment. The evaluator source is `training/evaluate_lupori_local_fit_to_pose_206.py`. Neither it nor any manual readout may inspect or load the active run tree before both arms have exited and written completion receipts. Run the evaluator once after exit; it writes a new `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/lupori_local_fit_to_pose_206_readout_001` tree and never changes training artifacts. A missing receipt, hash mismatch, incomplete count, different draw manifest, or missing input panel aborts the fixed readout rather than being repaired by substituting data or checkpoints.

## Locked inputs and scopes

- Compare `detached` and `fit` at every declared step `0`, `200`, `400`, with identical architecture and direct `predict()` prior. Top-1 is the argmax of the joint mode/reflection prior *before* atlas matching, MIND fitting, map fitting, candidate reranking, or calibration. No checkpoint or threshold is chosen from a held-out result.
- Verify both arm receipts, exactly 400 training-log steps and 400 Lupori presentations per arm, identical draw manifests, all checkpoint/config/parent/protocol/source hashes, split, 188 distinct eligible TRAIN source sections, and the absence of official DEV/CAL/FINAL/public-benchmark use. Verify the fit gradient row mask and finite-gradient training audit from the recorded step logs. The pre-run training protocol and its hash remain unchanged.
- `AL1A` is the one reserved TRAIN animal (52 valid sections), excluded from both arms' gradients. Reproduce the 187 thumbnail preprocessing exactly: 256-square bilinear RGB, `max(red,green)/255`, image-wide p99 normalization, four zero input channels. Its human VisuAlign plane is an approximate weak reference, not expert truth. The direct five-point comparison uses the four 256-frame corners and centre; report top-1 and oracle-best among all 160 direct candidates (plus top-16 in raw rows). The oracle is diagnostic and never used to select a candidate in deployment.
- Frozen synthetic v3 and v4 panels are `joint_in_path_correspondence_128_dev_panel` and `fresh_v4_pose_dev_panel_132`. Verify their receipts, records, source copies, and every section file hash. These are synthetic deformation-plan DEV panels, not official Lupori `CC1B` DEV and not independent animals. Report each eligible section's direct top-1 mean 3D physical error over its observed `valid_mask`, plus section-weighted and eight-plan-equal means.
- Separately generate a fresh v7 fluorescence-artifact panel from the exact 64-TRAIN-base context/source pinned by the training config: one first eligible physical draw for virtual index `64 * base_index`, for each base `0..63`, using seeds `206700000000 + 16 * base_index + attempt` for attempts `0..15`. Every generated physical ID must be absent from **all** training draw attempts. Abort if a base has no eligible draw in 16 attempts. Generate each chosen input/target once, hold it fixed across all six checkpoint reads, and record all draw attempts plus chosen seeds, attempt numbers, identities, and array hashes. These are new planes from *trained* synthetic deformation bases; their scope is out-of-draw only, neither out-of-base nor biological generalization.
- Frozen old weak-real coronal/sagittal panels retain the 187 evaluator's source, image resizing, five-point coordinates, and inherited affine references. Coronal: 64 development sections, six animals; sagittal: 158 source-available weak-dev2 sections, eight donors disjoint from their TRAIN donors. The metric is direct top-1 five-point disagreement from those inherited weak Allen affines, **not** anatomical truth. Report section means, per-donor means, and donor-equal means separately for each family.

The 206 *training* protocol described a “frozen v3/v7” synthetic retention check, but the existing frozen historical panels are v3 and v4; there is no frozen v7 panel. This readout resolves that wording explicitly: v3 and v4 are frozen historical synthetic DEV, while v7 is the above predeclared fresh TRAIN-base out-of-draw panel. Do not relabel v4 as v7 or v7 as held-out DEV.

## Paired effects and fixed gate

For each step, pair by AL1A section ID, synthetic physical ID, or weak-real family/donor/section ID, respectively. Fit-minus-detached is the signed effect; negative error/discrepancy is favorable. At AL1A report each arm's top-1 mean/median and >5-mm count; paired mean top-1 and all-160-oracle effects; newly created and resolved >5-mm top-1 failures. Use exactly 10,000 paired section bootstrap resamples with NumPy seed `20671101` for descriptive percentile 95% intervals on the two AL1A mean effects. Resampling the 52 sections of one animal is **not** an animal-population confidence interval; no significance test is planned. Report direct fit-gradient/weak-reference direction audits from the training log descriptively, not as a proxy for held-out improvement.

The predeclared step-400 advancement gate is **all** of: fit-minus-detached AL1A mean top-1 ≤ −0.10 mm; no newly created AL1A top-1 >5-mm section failure; fit/detached section-weighted direct top-1 mean ratio ≤1.10 on each v3 and fresh v7 panel; fit-minus-detached donor-equal weak-real discrepancy ≤+0.20 mm in **each** old coronal and sagittal family. v4 is reported as an additional frozen stress panel, not substituted for v7 or silently added to this original gate. A passing descriptive gate would justify a next experiment, not claim biological oblique accuracy, calibration, safe region assignment, or GUI readiness. A failing gate means do not scale this fit feedback. No official Lupori DEV (`CC1B`), CAL (`CC3A`), FINAL (`CC1A`), or public benchmark is read.

The evaluator writes raw AL1A, synthetic, old weak-real, and fresh-v7-manifest/draw-attempt JSONL files; a summary with every milestone and the gate; a config binding every source/input/checkpoint hash; and a final completion receipt with output SHA-256 hashes. This is a readout of fixed checkpoints, not a model-selection run.
