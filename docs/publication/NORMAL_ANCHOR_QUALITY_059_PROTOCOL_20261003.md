# 059 full-pose anchor-quality continuation: predeclared development protocol

058 failed: normal-only probability targets selected a spatially wrong new anchor on all 64 weak-real DEV sections. The 019 batch-18,000 checkpoint remains the reference. This run starts from that checkpoint, adds 64 fixed antipodal normal anchors, and freezes the inherited encoder, 16-mode pose head, atlas-conditioned mapper, and fit scorer. Only the new spatial/global anchor heads train. The added global head has a zero-initialized output, so the inherited 019 pose states and their relative ordering are initially unchanged; adding anchors can still change the selected overall candidate. This is a continuation of the same randomly initialized lineage, not a pretrained external feature model or pseudo-label transfer.

Run 50,000 batches of two freshly drawn eligible synthetic arbitrary-plane slices and one real TRAIN section: 100,000 synthetic and 50,000 real presentations. Real sections are unique within 059 and sampled donor-balanced from the reserved TRAIN pool; reuse of some sections trained in earlier runs is disclosed. Each synthetic draw independently samples a physical plane, deformation and image appearance; no paired-background construction or rejection of chance plane similarities is used. Keep exact animal/specimen/experiment/section and source provenance. No public benchmark, final-test animals, calibration, or GUI promotion is permitted during this run.

The correction is to supervise new-anchor probability mass and old-versus-new gating by complete physical plane quality, including position, scale, in-plane frame and normal, rather than normal alone. Gate targets compare the *prior-selected* old and new branches, not lucky oracle minima that inference cannot retrieve. TRAIN-only true-normal neighboring anchors receive direct pose and fitted-surface supervision; inference has no access to this oracle neighborhood. Separately, the predicted top new anchors receive differentiable fitted-surface error and ranking feedback through the finite-thickness atlas render and frozen local mapper. The fitting objective includes the mixed old/new beam, and inference reserves at least one old branch when returning multiple candidates. The inherited 16 modes remain unmodified. A nonzero fitting gradient at batch 1 is a mechanical check only, not evidence of scientific success.

After the process exits, compare checkpoints 0/10,000/30,000/50,000 on the same identity-disjoint 185 synthetic DEV sections from eight synthetic deformation identities and the 64 weak-real DEV sections from six donors. Report identity-equal selected visible-tissue error, truth-scored candidate capture for old and new branches, anchor selection rate, and weak-real five-point disagreement. The real affine is a weak label, not expert location truth. The model may advance only if the selected synthetic result improves over 019's 2.591 mm **and** the new branches cease damaging real retention relative to 019's 0.595 mm weak-reference disagreement; an oracle candidate improvement alone is insufficient. A result near 11 mm would be anatomically unacceptable even if a training loss falls. If the new branch fails to improve these gates, retain 019 and investigate the source of global ambiguity before spending on larger training. Neither checkpoint confers calibrated electrode-region probabilities or a deployable GUI replacement without later animal-separated validation.
