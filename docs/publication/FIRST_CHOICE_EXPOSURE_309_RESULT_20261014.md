# 309: more exposure did not solve blind plane selection

**Decision: the predeclared advancement gate failed.** The single-arm continuation completed 20,000 four-image batches from the verified 308 control checkpoint, with one independent full-sphere v9 synthetic cut, two independent narrower-field v10 cuts, and one cyclic acquired TRAIN image per batch. The independent post-exit verifier passed the frozen source, protocol, split and parent bindings; all draw, donor-separation, output-hash and row-level checks; the restored optimizer and frozen modules; a replay of the input-visible sparse and steep panel criteria; and the gate recomputed from raw rows. No DEV, calibration, final-test or public benchmark data entered this study.

The primary gate required a **≥0.35-mm** v10 first-choice improvement at batch 20,000. It achieved **0.126 mm**. Every prespecified nonregression guard passed; none can substitute for the failed primary gate. The selected, guarded checkpoint is batch 20,000 for research follow-up only, not qualification. A paired case bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 309; exploratory, not the gate) gives a 95% interval of −0.249 to −0.005 mm for the v10 mean change. Thus there is a modest improvement, not the needed improvement.

The distinction between *having* a good candidate and *choosing* it is now clearer. On the same 256 v10 cuts, the top-16 oracle mean improved from 1.115 to 0.931 mm, while the first-choice mean moved only 2.595 to 2.469 mm. Mean first-minus-oracle regret **increased** from 1.480 to 1.538 mm. At the last readout, 226/256 cases have a ≤1.5-mm candidate in the top 16, but only 109/256 choose one first. The final first-choice median is 1.877 mm, 90th percentile 4.404 mm, and 17/256 exceed 5 mm. The sparse panel remains especially unreliable: 4.669-mm mean first choice, 1.928-mm mean top-16 oracle and 25/64 first choices over 5 mm. Some tiny tissue intersections may be intrinsically ambiguous; this panel tests whether the system recognizes that ambiguity, not proof of an exact recoverable plane for every sliver.

Optimization did continue: mean total loss fell from 9.155 in the first 1,000 batches to 8.116 in the last 1,000, and synthetic loss from 7.331 to 6.811. The optimizer phase took 1.982 hours on the RTX 2080 Ti. Improving training loss and candidate availability without a comparable first-choice gain points to candidate ranking, objective alignment and/or synthetic-to-acquired mismatch as higher-value targets than another longer run with unchanged weights and loss. This is an inference, not a proven single cause. Acquired numbers are distances to weak Allen or approximate human registrations on TRAIN-inner animals, **not** independent physical pose truth; steep synthetic cuts likewise do not validate physically cut oblique brains.

**Post-run process correction:** the initially proposed next learned atlas-conditioned ranker would substantially repeat failed 125, 128, 299, 300, 304 and 305 experiments. Some distinguished a near plane from a support-matched wrong one on synthetic pairs, but none converted that cue into the required all-candidate first-choice gain over the prior and support controls. The 306 logit-only intervention also failed. The 309 top-16 oracle gap alone does **not** establish that another selector can extract the missing information. Do not start that ranker or another long continuation from this result.

A read-only decomposition of the frozen 309 panels using their recorded true reflection and post-artifact pose found mean surviving-site rigid residuals of **0.130 mm** (v9, 256), **0.109 mm** (v10, 256), **0.074 mm** (v10 sparse, 64) and **0.105 mm** (v10 steep, 64). The corresponding 90th percentiles were 0.237, 0.182, 0.139 and 0.177 mm. These are not model errors or measurements of physical histology: the robust post-artifact pose gauge already absorbs global affine deformation, and missing tissue is not a correspondence target. They show why the current synthetic score is dominated by plane selection rather than local residual correction. On the v10 panel, the 226 cases with a ≤1.5-mm top-16 candidate still have a 2.29-mm mean first-choice error. Visibility matters: 15 v10 cases with <15% visible tissue average 5.53 mm first choice, versus 1.97 mm for 85 cases with ≥50% visible tissue; the separate sparse panel remains worse.

**Matched v10 gauge check, 15 October:** Replaying the same 256 saved section IDs, reflection flags and surviving-site CCF targets with their saved pre- and post-artifact affine states gives **0.1464→0.1088 mm** mean exact-pose site error. The refit removes 0.0376 mm, or 25.7% of the pre-refit mean—not most of the v10 local residual, and nowhere near the 1.538-mm first-choice-versus-top-16 gap. The separate v9 affine-projection percentage is a different calculation and must not be transferred to this panel. `training/diagnose_309_local_residual_axes.py` now prints this matched readout while preserving its in-plane/through-plane decomposition. Replacing the atlas pose target with the raw generating plane, or enlarging synthetic warps solely to make fit difficulty salient, is not supported by this comparison. The [AL1A human-field guard](ACQUIRED_MAP_314_RESULT_AND_DECISION_20261015.md) has 0.1055-mm mean tissue-vector RMS after its affine-free gauge, similar in scale but not an identical error statistic or independent physical truth; the cross-animal deformation distribution remains unestablished.

**Rank-target audit, 15 October:** A frozen read-only replay of all 256 v10 cases tested whether the training loss's composite candidate cost (75% surviving-site error, 25% five-point rigid error, plus unsigned-normal cost) points away from the reported surviving-site metric. Within the checkpoint's same top 16, choosing the unavailable *minimum composite-cost* branch would yield **0.962-mm** mean site error, versus **0.931 mm** for the unavailable minimum-site-error branch; their choices agree on 205/256 cases. Across all branches the corresponding means are **0.863 versus 0.826 mm**. Thus the composite target costs only about **0.031 mm** of the top-16 oracle gap, not the observed 1.538-mm first-choice regret. Training and evaluation flatten mode/reflection in the same order; no indexing or reversed-gradient bug was found. `training/diagnose_309_objective_alignment.py` reproduces this check without writing run files. Do not start another loss-weight sweep on this evidence. Generic `infer_one_shot` still invokes inherited fitted rescoring, whereas the published 309 result chooses raw model mass; those paths must be reconciled before any future deployment claim, but this mismatch does not explain the frozen reported result.

**Revised next decision:** spend no further training budget on unchanged pose exposure, scalar-logit tuning, or the previously tested atlas-fit score. First use frozen TRAIN-inner evidence to determine whether the practically observable image and atlas carry an all-16, tail-safe ranking signal beyond support, position and the direct prior; an oracle tissue mask or pairwise wins alone do not qualify. In parallel, audit the synthetic-versus-acquired image/label gap and whether the current residual-deformation distribution adequately represents the intended use. Only a mechanistically different, predeclared intervention with a meaningful fresh full-sphere first-choice gain, no harmful-tail regression and acquired TRAIN-donor guards should receive a longer run. Direct local mapping, truly joint training, animal-level validation and calibrated electrode-region uncertainty remain required. Neither this pose checkpoint nor its uncalibrated branch masses is a GUI replacement; the DeepSlice benchmark remains sealed.

**Post-run framing preflight (descriptive, not a new model result):** A read-only replay of all 256 frozen v10 images compared their generator-visible tissue shapes with 188 actual masks from three TRAIN Lupori animals; every synthetic input matched its saved panel image bitwise. All Lupori references were AP-family, so this says nothing about acquired physical obliques. Of 77 synthetic AP-family cuts, 46 lay outside the real masks' joint 95th-percentile centroid/bounding-box envelope. Yet first-choice-minus-top-16-oracle regret was **1.392 mm outside versus 1.588 mm inside**; among the 32 cuts within 30° of AP it was **1.142 versus 1.844 mm**. Visibility-stratified comparisons likewise did not show consistent excess regret outside the envelope. The crop distribution is descriptively mismatched, but this result does not implicate that mismatch in the selection failure. Do **not** spend training time on a framing-only v10 tweak on this evidence. Reproduce the bounded analysis with `training/preflight_framing_309_train.py`; it reads only TRAIN images and frozen 309 outputs, and writes nothing.

**Post-run atlas-correlation ceiling check:** On the first 64 frozen v10 cases, the blind top-16 bank and selected 309 checkpoint were unchanged; inputs were regenerated bitwise. A diagnostic given the generator's *true intact-tissue mask*, the exact section PSF and direct sign-invariant atlas intensity correlation selected **2.458-mm** mean rigid-site error and **six** >5-mm cases, versus the image prior's **2.322 mm** and **four**. A privileged support-only score selected 2.391 mm/four; support-preserving shuffled atlas intensity selected 2.619 mm/five. The same bank's unavailable physical-error oracle was 0.964 mm. Thus direct raw-intensity correlation did not produce safe all-16 selection even with an unavailable perfect tissue mask; its predeclared ≥0.25-mm improvement over every control without a harmful-tail increase failed. Close this *specific* atlas-correlation selector direction for now, not every possible learned anatomical representation. The read-only `training/preflight_privileged_anatomy_309.py` reproduces this TRAIN-inner check; no reserved animal, new model training or benchmark was used.

**Post-run pose-factor audit (descriptive):** Re-evaluating the same frozen 256 v10 cases and step-20,000 checkpoint gives 99 cases where the chosen pose is >2 mm wrong although a top-16 candidate is ≤1.5 mm wrong. In those 99, the chosen candidate has median **59.1° unsigned plane-normal error** and **2.03-mm centre error**, versus **8.6°** and **0.50 mm** for the best top-16 candidate. Its median minor field-of-view span is 10.60 mm against 10.84 mm in truth; none is below half the true span. Across all 256, only one chosen span is below half truth. This rules out the earlier severe span-collapse pattern as the main explanation for *these* 309 wrong-choice cases. Angular and centre errors co-occur, so this is not a causal attribution between them. The next experiment must target blind plane *selection* with a mechanism genuinely different from the failed scalar, atlas-correlation and recurrent-fit selectors, and should remain bounded until an all-candidate selected-error gate improves. Do not buy more batches or warp capacity to solve this measured ranking failure. Reproduce the factor readout with `training/diagnose_first_choice_309_pose_factors.py`; it writes no files and uses TRAIN-inner cases only.

**Input-dependence null (descriptive):** With the same checkpoint and 256 case truths, permuting complete input images among independently drawn cuts *within each of the eight TRAIN-inner bases* raises mean chosen rigid-site error from **2.469 to 5.379 mm**, median unsigned normal error from **21.9° to 60.1°**, and >5-mm cases from **17 to 157**. Every base worsens (mean-error increases 2.39–3.34 mm). This rules out the narrow hypothesis that the current pose head ignores the target image or chooses normals solely from a fixed atlas prior. It does **not** show that it uses genuine internal anatomy rather than synthetic appearance, contour or background cues, nor that any one damaged slice is uniquely identifiable. The image-only head already has all-candidate physical ranking supervision; a further temperature, scalar-logit or longer-exposure retrain is not evidence-led. The next short experiment should change the *information or spatial representation* available for candidate comparison and require a fresh blind selected-error gain before scaling. Reproduce this null with `training/preflight_image_dependence_309.py`; the permutation and source cases are TRAIN-inner only, and no model or saved run output is changed.

For the same frozen 256 cases, forcing first choice to the global-only or normal-anchor-only branch yields median errors of **2.268** and **2.199 mm**, respectively, versus **1.877 mm** with both branches available. Their 90th percentiles are 4.426 and 4.544 mm. Thus merely disabling either proposal family is not an obvious rescue; the near-correct candidates need image-grounded selection across both families. These descriptive branch readouts are included in `training/diagnose_first_choice_309_pose_factors.py`.

**Tissue-versus-exterior perturbation (descriptive):** On all 256 frozen v10 cuts, exact replay of the generator allowed a diagnostic *oracle* tissue mask to be used only for perturbing the input. Permuting the intensities spatially within surviving tissue while preserving the exterior and the separate brush/outline channels raised first-choice mean error from **2.469 to 4.940 mm**, median unsigned normal error from **21.9° to 56.6°**, and >5-mm cases from **17 to 104**. Permuting exterior pixels instead yielded **2.534 mm**, **23.8°**, and **18**. This shows the model depends strongly on the within-tissue spatial image arrangement, rather than mainly on the exterior/background. It does *not* prove that the useful cues are biologically valid anatomy or that these out-of-distribution scrambled images resemble histology. The short read-only `training/preflight_tissue_vs_exterior_309.py` reproduces this test; do not respond with another background-only augmentation or a repeated 309 exposure run.

**Optional cutting-direction hint sensitivity (descriptive, 15 October):** A read-only replay of the same 256 v10 cases tested whether a researcher-supplied *section cutting direction* could narrow the existing 160 branches without retraining. The true plane normal supplied either a three-family label (nearest AP/DV/ML axis) or an artificially noisy precise normal; these are truth-derived simulations, not actual user-provided hints. With a soft 0.8/0.1 family likelihood, first-choice mean error changed only **2.469→2.428 mm**, with **17→17** cases over 5 mm. An AP-centre hint with approximately 0.6-mm median noise changed mean error to **2.441 mm** in this replay. A normal hint perturbed by about 9.6° median changed it to **1.615 mm**, with **17→6** over 5 mm; adding the AP hint gave **1.528 mm**. When 10% of family labels were deliberately wrong, the wrong-hint subset worsened from **1.984→2.138 mm**. The script `training/diagnose_first_choice_309_acquisition_hints.py` prints the full reproducible sensitivity readout without saving a run. A *probe insertion angle* is not a *section cutting normal*: the current GUI's insertion constraint cannot be credited with the precise-normal gain. Nor does this oracle-derived synthetic test establish that researchers can supply a 10°-accurate normal, real-image accuracy, or calibrated probabilities. A vague three-family input is not a high-value next training direction; optional trustworthy orientation evidence remains worth using later, with fallible-hint handling.

**Process priority after these diagnostics:** further unchanged pose exposure, scalar fit-score variants, or background-only augmentation are not justified. The current roughly 2.5-mm error is dominated by blind plane choice; exact-pose local residuals are about 0.1 mm. The [independent-mouse microCT source-shift diagnostic](REAL_ARBITRARY_PLANE_VALIDATION_SOURCE_AUDIT_20261008.md) also cautions against interpreting one-atlas synthetic gains as specimen transfer; that CT image is a different modality and cannot by itself fix training. Before another large joint run, require a short, matched intervention that actually improves all-candidate first choice and its >5-mm tail on fresh full-sphere cuts, while retaining donor-separated acquired-image guards. Then require a nontrivial exact-pose local-map gain over zero warp and a jointly trained mapped-site gain before scaling. The 188 TRAIN Lupori fields and 52 `AL1A` guard sections offer a near-coronal real-map check, but not physical steep-oblique truth; do not use the sealed animals or DeepSlice to select the intervention.
