# 053: Is the local patch control using unavailable warp information?

025 and 052 use an atlas positive rendered at each synthetic pixel's exact tissue-to-CCF coordinate. The global 030/052 search bank instead contains only rigid oriented atlas patches. The former is an oracle-warped surface, not merely the known plane. This geometry difference may make its 96% local recall an optimistic estimate of what a deployable rigid atlas comparison can achieve. It has not yet been measured on the same queries.

Freeze the 025 batch-3,000 encoder. On the existing 185 eligible synthetic DEV sections from eight held-out synthetic deformation identities, take the exact same seeded 32 tissue-valid query pixels and 256 regular atlas candidates as 025. Compare two atlas renders with identical through-plane offsets and weights: (A) the exact pixelwise warped CCF surface already used by 025 and (B) the rigid full-frame plane encoded by the known synthetic `target_state`, including the known image reflection. The latter is an **oracle true pose**, not an inference estimate; it removes only the unavailable local warp. For each arm report identity-equal top-one/top-16 point recall within 0.5 mm, exact-pixel candidate rank and cosine score, and actual rigid-versus-warped displacement at the queries. Keep raw/black/imperfect-brush identities and results separate.

A loss of at least 20 percentage points in rigid top-one recall would make the train/search geometry mismatch a major target and justify changing positives to rigid atlas renders with displacement-tolerant supervision. A smaller loss would redirect attention to orientation/position bank discretization and many-way anatomical lookalikes. This control cannot validate unknown-plane pose capture, real-animal accuracy, probabilities, or GUI readiness. Do not inspect images, retrain, or use public/final-test data for this diagnostic.
