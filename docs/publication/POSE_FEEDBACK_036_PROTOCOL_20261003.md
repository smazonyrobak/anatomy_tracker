# Per-candidate atlas-fit pose feedback 036 — frozen development protocol

The 017–019 recurrent refiner received atlas-render/warp evidence but its physical refiner loss took the **minimum across eight candidates**. It therefore gave direct pose-correction supervision to roughly one candidate per section, while the selected candidate remained ~2.6 mm off. The 035 diagnostic also showed that simply ascending the unchanged 025 patch cosine is not a useful off-plane direction. This stage changes **the refiner's training target**, not the generator, atlas or direct pose model.

Start at the scratch-lineage 019 batch-18,000 checkpoint. Freeze its direct pose CNN, atlas-conditioned mapper and fitted ranker. Train only the existing shared 9-DOF local pose updater and its scalar score residual for 3,000 batches of two fresh, independently randomized TRAIN synthetic sections, with provenance written per accepted/rejected draw. For every section, include the actual prior top-eight pose/reflection branches, three randomly perturbed known planes spanning local translational/rotational error scales, and the exact plane as a training-only zero-update anchor. No known plane or validity mask enters inference candidate construction or scoring. The first finite-thickness atlas render and frozen mapper at 64×64 supply structured fit evidence. Supervise **each** candidate's post-update physical tissue-coordinate error on sampled observed pixels plus normal-angle error, weighting extremely distant predicted branches down smoothly, not discarding all but the best. A score-residual ranking loss is anchored to each predicted branch's post-update physical error; penalize unnecessary updates. Do not allow the local warp to replace pose supervision.

Keep source, checkpoints, training draws and optimizer/RNG on `I:`. Evaluate checkpoints 0, 1,000, 2,000 and 3,000 on the same historical 185-section synthetic DEV panel and 64 weak-affine real DEV images, reporting prior top-one, corrected top-one, best-eight rigid and corrected-score selection separately. The synthetic gate is ≥0.25 mm improvement in corrected top-one rigid error and ≥0.20 mm improvement in best-eight rigid error from checkpoint zero, with no >0.20 mm donor-mean real weak-affine regression. This gate only establishes whether stronger per-candidate supervision can move the frozen direct proposals. Because this historic DEV panel has been repeatedly consulted, it cannot qualify a final model or select a deployable checkpoint; the next joint continuation needs a new independent synthetic DEV identity cohort and real-animal labels.

If the gate fails, do not continue this same refiner longer: develop an off-plane-trained structured correspondence/energy model or substantially stronger global proposal mechanism. If it passes, unfreeze direct pose and constrained mapper for a matched atlas-connected versus atlas-detached joint training comparison. Preserve optional raw/black/brush backgrounds and full brain-intersecting-plane coverage; no final-test animals, numerical probability claims or public benchmark here.
