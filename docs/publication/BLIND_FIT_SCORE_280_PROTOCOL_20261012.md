# 280: blind-candidate anatomical-fit and local-map pilot

This is a bounded mechanism test, not a checkpoint-promotion or DeepSlice test. The 279 v11 continuation failed its preregistered gate and is not a parent. Freeze the verified 275 step-8,000 scratch-lineage pose checkpoint as the proposal source. Its blind top 16 contains a ≤1.5-mm plane on 60/64 newly drawn synthetic sections, but first-choice error is 2.329 mm, and previous fit scores failed to improve blind choice (159, 245). The question is whether a *trained* candidate-conditioned local fit can order those already proposed planes using atlas anatomy, rather than merely atlas support or the image-only pose prior.

Use the existing `OneShotJointSliceModel` atlas-conditioned map and fitted-candidate scorer. Load the frozen 275 pose weights strictly into the common submodules, initialize the atlas/map/score modules from scratch with the same seed in two arms, and freeze the proposal encoder/pose heads. This is a diagnostic continuation of this project's scratch lineage, not the eventual independently retrained release. Arm A sees the true atlas intensity plus support; arm B is trained and evaluated with atlas intensity zero but identical support and everything else. Both see the same independently drawn physical v9 sections, the same blind top-16 poses, and the same optimizer budget. Do not call an inference-only atlas-zero perturbation a trained control.

Training may use the synthetic true plane to supervise a **separate** exact-plane local tissue map. The candidate-ranking loss must use only planes in the pose model's *blind* top 16: no injected true or perturbed-true candidate in its softmax or pairwise comparisons, and no deliberate second rendering of a physical slice with a new appearance. Select a small fixed candidate subset per update that includes the prior top one, the closest available branch, and support-similar errors where possible. Pairwise rank supervision uses physical surviving-tissue CCF error, ignoring pairs separated by <0.5 mm; avoid an injected easy branch absorbing the signal, as happened in 244/245. Limit the local field to the existing constrained residual surface and keep its magnitude/roughness cost in the final score. Train the exact-plane map on known synthetic correspondences, with a validity/reliability target, but never let a wrong plane earn a low score by an implausibly large warp. Record what fraction of updates actually supplied near-versus-wrong and support-matched ranking pairs, including sparse sections.

Run 1,500 optimizer batches per arm at one new eligible physical section per batch, checkpoint/read out at 0, 500 and 1,500, with only step 1,500 eligible for the gate. The 64-case synthetic readout is drawn **before** updates: eight fresh eligible sections per held-gradient TRAIN deformation base 56–63, selected without seeing model errors. Pose 275 used these bases for its earlier checkpoint-selection panel, so this is an inner TRAIN mechanism test, not an independent biological or synthetic-base validation. The physical planes in 280 must be new independent draws. At each readout, fit all *blind* top-16 branches in small chunks for both arms. Record original rigid error, mapped CCF error, rank-selected original/mapped error, physically best-of-16 diagnostic, valid/visible fraction, obliquity, per-branch direct prior, fit score, displacement norm/roughness, and full provenance. The physically best branch is diagnostic only and must not be selected at inference. Separately supply the true plane to measure exact-plane no-warp versus local-map error, without mixing it into ranking. Read the same 98 already inspected acquired TRAIN donor median images as a weak orientation retention guard, not position truth. No DEV/CAL/FINAL animal or public benchmark is touched.

On the fixed step-1,500 panel, a positive signal requires **all** of:

1. Full-atlas blind rank-selected *original rigid* first-choice mean error falls at least 0.25 mm below the same frozen pose's direct top-one and at least 0.15 mm below the trained support-only arm; at least six of eight synthetic bases have nonnegative full-versus-direct gain. Report the mapped result separately; a warp that hides a wrong plane cannot satisfy this clause.
2. Among cases with a blind ≤1.5-mm candidate and a ≥1-mm-worse alternative whose atlas supports have Dice ≥0.85, area fraction gap ≤0.08 and ≥32 common 24×24 cells, at least 24 distinct sections contribute one prespecified nearest-versus-wrong decision. Full-atlas near wins ≥70% and exceeds the matched trained support-only arm by ≥10 percentage points. Fewer eligible sections means *inconclusive/fail*, not a lowered denominator.
3. The exact-plane local map improves mean surviving-tissue CCF error at least 0.05 mm versus its own no-warp map without violating the model's derivative bound. For blind candidates already within 0.5 mm, report map gain and require no mean degradation >0.05 mm. The local map must not collapse to near-zero displacement merely to game a deformation penalty.
4. On the 98 acquired TRAIN orientation readouts, full-atlas first-choice family agreement is at least 54/58 coronal and 18/40 sagittal—the 275 step-zero values replayed in 279—and sagittal median weak normal discrepancy does not rise >5° over the frozen direct prior. These are weak-label guards, not a claim of real localization.

If any clause fails, do not scale this scorer or feed it back to pose. A pass licenses a separately preregistered full model training comparison from common random initialization with live versus detached fit gradients, not GUI promotion. Verify that live fit-loss gradients reach pose state, branch mass and shared encoder while the detached control's do not. Later animal-disjoint physical-oblique validation, local-deformation accuracy, calibrated electrode-site/region probabilities, optional acquisition constraints/smart brush, and GUI qualification remain mandatory. A sparse or severely damaged single section may have multiple plausible planes and must eventually be allowed broad uncertainty or abstention.

The design follows the general joint-registration insight of [Iglesias et al.'s histology/MRI synthesis-registration model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05284) and the shared-anatomy/stain-residual distinction in [Athalye et al. (MICCAI 2026)](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/0518-Paper6039.html). Neither paper establishes this model's arbitrary-plane accuracy; 280 tests one necessary directional claim against our own prior failure.
