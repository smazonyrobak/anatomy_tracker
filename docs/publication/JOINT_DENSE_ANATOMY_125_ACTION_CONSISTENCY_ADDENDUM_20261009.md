# 125 action-consistency addendum (before any 125 evaluation)

The frozen 125 protocol and its primary gate remain unchanged. A static review found that its inherited 124 selection rule adds evidence from the **corrected** fitted map to both the original and corrected action scores. When the original action wins, that is candidate-level transfer, not evidence that the original map itself fits. The running 256-section panel is untouched.

Record a prespecified corrected-only sensitivity on the same blind 16 candidates: choose the baseline by its corrected matcher score, and each ranker arm by that corrected score plus evidence computed from that candidate's corrected map. Measure mapped-tissue and rigid errors on exactly the selected corrected map; report plan-equal means for frozen 122, full intensity, support only, and the three input-ablation controls. This readout cannot rescue a failed primary gate. Even if the original stage-1 gate passes, do not start feedback-to-pose continuation 126 unless corrected-only full intensity also improves plan-equal mapped error by at least 0.30 mm versus corrected-only frozen 122 and at least 0.15 mm versus corrected-only support only, with no rigid-gauge regression versus corrected-only frozen 122. No threshold or model is chosen from this panel.

This remains synthetic development evidence on eight reused deformation plans, not independent animal validation, uncertainty calibration, a public benchmark, or GUI qualification.

Also report descriptive selected/best-candidate error and near-candidate counts by nearest-cardinal angle (0–15°, 15–30°, 30–45°, 45–55°) and by raw/exact-black/imperfect-brush input. These unequal groups diagnose all-angle and no-brush failure; they do not change either advancement gate or establish causal effects of the input mode.
