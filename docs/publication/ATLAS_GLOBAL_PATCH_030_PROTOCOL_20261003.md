# Global oriented-patch capture 030: frozen development protocol

The direct one-shot model's 32 pose branches miss a 250 µm rigid plane on every one of 185 fixed synthetic DEV sections (023). The scratch-trained cross-contrast patch model 025 matches a known plane, but that does not establish unknown-plane capture. This experiment asks whether its frozen features can retrieve atlas point and orientation **without using the correct plane to build the bank**. It is a diagnostic, not a proposal to replace the direct probabilistic pose head with a permanent catalogue.

Build one atlas-only bank from the pinned 25 µm Allen atlas and frozen 025 batch-3000 weights: 4,027 regular support locations approximately 500 µm apart; 64 deterministic antipodal plane normals; eight in-plane rolls; a 64×64, 67 µm/pixel patch per location and orientation; nine normalized through-plane samples from −50 to +50 µm. The bank has 2,061,824 patches. Store their 128-component normalized descriptors as float16 on I:. The bank depends on neither TRAIN nor DEV images or coordinates.

Evaluate once on the unchanged 185 eligible synthetic DEV sections from eight disjoint deformation identities. Sample 32 fixed, ground-truth-valid query pixels per section exactly as in 025–029. **This is an oracle tissue-pixel selection for diagnosis, not a deployable query selector.** Encode each observed patch and its horizontal flip; search all atlas location×orientation entries by cosine score. For each query report whether any top-16 entry maps within 500 µm of its true CCF point. Also report whether each section has at least three such query correspondences separated by at least 1 mm in true CCF space, an approximate necessary condition for robust plane fitting. Weight the eight synthetic identities equally. Preserve appearance-mode results. Bank location coverage and frame quantization are recorded separately as ceilings; they do not count as learned retrieval.

The necessary gate is ≥10% top-16 point recall within 500 µm, ≥20% of sections with three separated correspondences, and no appearance mode below half overall point recall. A pass justifies a small robust-geometry pose-proposal experiment. A failure means the existing descriptor is not a viable global search key at this bank scale; do not treat 025's truth-derived-bank success as global localization. Neither result qualifies real anatomy, probability calibration, GUI deployment, or DeepSlice superiority. No weights update, public benchmark, calibration animals, or final-test animals are used.
