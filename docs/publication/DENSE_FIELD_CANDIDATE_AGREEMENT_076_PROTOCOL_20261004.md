# 076: frozen dense-field agreement as a pose-selection screen

Freeze the completed 075 batch-10,000 checkpoint. Before training another ranker, test whether its predicted dense CCF field can select among its **actual 80 pose modes × two reflections**. No pose is inserted from synthetic truth, no weights change, and no development result is used to tune the score.

For each section, use the predicted 128×128 CCF field and predicted validity only at the fixed regular indices `0, 4, 8, …, 124` on each axis (32×32 points, identical for every candidate and section). Render each candidate's rigid CCF coordinates at those same chart positions. At each point calculate 3D Euclidean candidate–field distance in µm and Huber loss with a 1,000-µm bend. Sort each candidate's point losses, retain exactly the lowest 80% of its **predicted-validity probability mass** (fractionally including the boundary point), and take the retained weighted mean. The lowest score selects one plane; the eight lowest scores form the proposed beam. No synthetic tissue mask, coordinate truth, Allen affine or DEV-tuned prior mixture enters scoring. There is no secondary affine-fit score in this experiment.

Evaluate once on the existing 061 eligible synthetic DEV panel (246 sections, eight deformation identities) and weak-Allen real DEV panel (64 sections, six held-out donors). Match the 075 evaluator's physical readout: mean Euclidean CCF error at its fixed 1,024 surviving synthetic tissue pixels, and five-point disagreement with inherited weak Allen affines on real images. Report identity-equal means, each synthetic appearance and low-/higher-support stratum, each real donor, the frozen 075 direct-prior selection, and the 059-derived step-zero baseline from the already frozen 075 evaluation rows. The best physical error among the score-ranked top eight is a **truth-scored availability oracle**, not inference accuracy; only top-one is blindly selected.

Advance to a separately preregistered selector-training pilot only if all conditions hold: identity-equal synthetic selected error ≤2.35 mm, truth-best of the proposed top eight ≤0.95 mm, and selected error ≤the matched 059 baseline +0.20 mm for **every** weak-real donor, every raw/exact-black/imperfect-brush synthetic appearance, and both low-/higher-support strata. Otherwise stop this field-selection branch. Passing would show only useful frozen development discrimination; it would not satisfy the earlier 073 full advancement gate, establish biological accuracy or calibration, justify GUI promotion, or authorize public benchmark or final-test use.
