# 086 frozen candidate-order diagnostic: result (4 October 2026)

The 085 selector's failure is primarily an **anatomical ranking failure conditional on its existing 14-plane beam**. Across 246 held-out synthetic sections in eight deformation plans, the fitted score's within-section rank correlation with *lower* physical mapping error was only **Spearman 0.140** (Kendall 0.106). The image prior alone scored 0.101 and the fitted matcher's learned output alone 0.092. None reliably orders nearby and distant anatomical matches.

| Selected visible-tissue error at the 96-grid map | 2.587 mm | 2.635 mm |
| Regret versus best of the same 14, top-scored one | 1.595 mm | 1.643 mm |
| Best available among top three, excess error | 0.420 mm | 0.403 mm |
| Best available among top five, excess error | 0.180 mm | 0.166 mm |

The physically best of the 14 branches averaged **0.992 mm**. It ranked first under the fitted score for only 49/246 sections, within the top three for 130/246, and within the top five for 173/246. Thus many good proposals exist, but selecting one remains unreliable. The fitted score slightly improves top-one selection over the prior yet worsens top-three/top-five coverage; the matched full-resolution 085 development evaluation remains **0.055 mm worse** than parent 059 (2.591 versus 2.535 mm). The diagnostic did not modify or retrain either model.

The weakness is not confined to a background condition: fitted-score rank correlations were 0.129 for exact black, 0.164 for raw backgrounds, and 0.146 for imperfect brush input. An exploratory score decomposition (not a preregistered success gate) found that the 083 spatial-quality term had correlation **−0.013** with lower error and median within-section population standard deviation only **0.037**, versus **1.066** for the prior and **0.358** for the fitted learned score. The residual warp penalty had correlation 0.049 and median deviation 0.0003. These are associations, not proof of causality, but extending training or reweighting the same nearly constant fitting-quality term is not a well-supported next move. The next change must expose spatially consistent anatomy to the coordinate/plane updater and prevent flexible deformation from disguising a wrong plane.

The frozen 086 protocol used the same 246 eligible arbitrary-plane sections, same 14 blind branches, same 1024 deterministic surviving-tissue pixels, and same two-pass 083 map as 085. It never inserted true pose into the beam. The diagnostic wrote 3,444 raw candidate rows and 246 section rows. An independent read recomputed all 14 branch identities per section, selected and truth-best errors, each section's fitted Spearman coefficient, and eight-plan-equal aggregate values; all agreed with the frozen summary. Output config, candidate, section, and summary SHA-256 hashes were separately recomputed and matched the completion receipt. The script verified the training/checkpoint, panel, evaluator, and 085 development-row hashes before inference; its maximum mapped96 parity difference from 085 was **0 µm**.
