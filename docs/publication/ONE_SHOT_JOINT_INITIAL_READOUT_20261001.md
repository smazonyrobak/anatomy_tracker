# One-shot joint-model initial readout (2026-10-01)

The new randomly initialized, single-pass pose-and-warp model completed 2,500 batches of four fresh arbitrary-plane synthetic sections (10,000 presentations) from 64 TRAIN synthetic deformation maps. No section was deliberately re-styled into a second optimizer example. The first 700 batches trained the local map at the known plane; the remainder used predicted planes and a differentiable atlas-fit loss. The fit-to-pose gradient connection was separately checked, but that check is not an accuracy result.

On 64 fixed sections from four held-out synthetic maps, with one appearance per physical section, the selected five-point physical error was 12.89 mm at random initialization, 13.06 mm at batch 1,500 and 13.48 mm at batch 2,500. The corresponding best-of-16 reference oracles were 8.30, 7.71 and 7.44 mm. On 64 real development images from six disjoint Allen donors, agreement with weak existing Allen affines was 11.10, 10.40 and 13.14 mm; reference oracles were 8.55, 5.87 and 6.23 mm. Oracle selection uses reference coordinates and is not an inference result. Real affine labels are not blinded expert anatomical truth.

At the known correct plane, 44 held-out synthetic sections had visible tissue. Their no-warp dense coordinate error was 56.74 µm. The model's local map error was 56.71 µm at initialization, 61.87 µm after 500 known-plane batches, 122.03 µm at batch 1,500 and 96.36 µm at completion. Mean atlas-fit mismatch rose from 0.343 to 0.416. The learned warp therefore did not improve the held-out clean domain, and turning on predicted-plane fitting at batch 701 was premature. Selected reflection accuracy on those 44 sections fell from 61% to 36%; scores are uncalibrated.

This pilot **failed** the accuracy requirement and is not deployable. The saved early checkpoint at batch 500 is the parent for an evidence-led curriculum: sample fresh planes with both near-clean and strong distortion, improve probabilistic pose ranking and reflection supervision while the warp sees the known plane, then restore fit-to-pose training only when predicted planes are close enough for atlas fit to be informative. The expanded TRAIN-only Allen collection on I: has 263,754 distinct images from 1,885 donors; donor-balanced real supervision is needed to avoid the severe real-domain forgetting seen in the previous synthetic-only lineage. Development, calibration, final-test and public benchmark images must remain separate.
