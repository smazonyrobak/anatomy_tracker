# 192: the inherited map is worse than no local warp on acquired fields

The [frozen 64-section TRAIN-only assay](LUPORI_ACQUIRED_LOCAL_MAP_192_PROTOCOL_20261011.md) supplied each valid Lupori section's approximate human atlas plane to the unchanged 188 atlas-conditioned mapper. It compared the mapper's 96² local field with the matching VisuAlign image→reference field **after removing the same unweighted full-canvas affine component** that the model cannot represent as local deformation. The original tissue mask was used only for scoring, never as a model input or affine-fit weight. No optimizer update, DEV/CAL/FINAL animal or public benchmark was involved.

The vector RMS difference from the human affine-free field, averaged equally over four TRAIN animals, was **0.118 mm for a zero local field** and **0.172 mm for the inherited model field**. The inherited field was worse than zero on **all four animals** (AL1A 0.114→0.155, CC2B 0.125→0.145, CC4B 0.120→0.181, CC4A 0.112→0.208 mm). The predeclared screen failed. This result does not say that no tissue warp is needed: the human registrations themselves contain nonzero local displacements, but the frozen synthetic-trained field does not predict them reliably on this fluorescence modality.

The raw field sign had previously been checked against VisuAlign markers; the CSV displacements were sampled in original thumbnail pixel units, converted through each section's physical O/U/V edges, and projected into the same local pose gauge as the model. The independent verifier rechecked all 64 exact section/image/field/mask hashes, checkpoint/source/protocol identities, finite values, per-animal means and the gate. Human VisuAlign fields are approximate registration labels and can contain local folds; they are **not** independent tissue correspondence ground truth. These near-coronal TRAIN sections cannot validate arbitrary cuts or electrode sites.

**Decision:** the inherited deformation effort is not a credible measure of how well a candidate plane fits real tissue. Do not send its cost to the pose head, claim joint fitting success, or deploy it. Next, train the same model's local mapper on gauge-corrected acquired TRAIN fields with one entire TRAIN animal withheld, while preserving full-angle synthetic mapping. Require held-animal improvement over both the zero-field and unchanged-parent baselines before connecting map evidence to pose learning.
