# 099: contextual anatomical descriptors trained against hard false matches

The audited 098 diagnostic found the physically best 094 candidate's local search contains a correct supported fine bin at 0.970 of available tissue sites (plan-equal), yet 097 raw descriptor top-1 reaches only 0.536 and its operational coordinate 0.609, both below the unshifted plane's 0.892. The atlas-support penalty changes top-1 correctness by less than 0.001 and the local-softmax decoder improves it. Therefore the next intervention is feature discrimination, not an untested decoder tweak or another correct-match fraction head. [Mok et al.](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Mok_Modality-Agnostic_Structural_Image_Representation_Learning_for_Deformable_Multi-Modality_Medical_Image_CVPR_2024_paper.html), [SAM](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35442886/) and [LoFTR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Sun_LoFTR_Detector-Free_Local_Feature_Matching_With_Transformers_CVPR_2021_paper.html) motivate multiscale/contextual representations, but do not establish efficacy for mouse histology.

Continue the internally random-initialized 094 lineage, without importing 097's failed descriptor or any external weights/features/pseudolabels. Replace only the 083 matcher's 16-channel image and atlas descriptor projections with residual multiscale 2-D/3-D context modules. The old 094 projection is frozen inside each module; zero-initialized residual outputs make step 0 match 094 exactly while giving the new branch room to learn. Keep 094's pose generator, frozen fitted candidate geometry, finite-thickness atlas rendering, atlas-support policy, verifier, map and score unchanged at this isolated stage. Retain full-depth context in the atlas module and broader-than-five-cell spatial context in both modules; use spatial pyramid/downsample processing that fits the 11-GB GPU.

TRAIN on exactly 4,000 accepted independently sampled arbitrary-plane synthetic sections from the established 64-base TRAIN lineage, one randomized geometry and one appearance per physical section, with raw/acquired-like, exact-black and imperfect-brush modes treated as ordinary variability, not deliberate paired copies. Use the unchanged 094 8-old/6-anchor beam and its four-branch selection rule from 097 (rigid best, support-matched worse, high-prior worse, distinct random). The 094 teacher supplies frozen fitted states. Synthetic exact observed-tissue CCF coordinates are TRAIN targets only. At each visible site in the four branches, label supported fine bins ≤1.5 mm positive and ≥2.0 mm negative; for coarse bins use ≤2.0 and ≥3.0 mm. Optimize a smooth hard-ranking loss whose positive term is the *best* supported positive and negative term the *hardest* supported negative under raw normalized descriptor similarity, with a positive separation margin; average only sites having both. The fine and coarse losses both update the same new context modules. Save initial and 4,000-batch weights, full ordered draw/acceptance provenance and training curves/hard margins on I:. No GUI promotion or calibration from TRAIN loss.

The 4,000-batch **scale-up signal**, not a deployment gate, is: plan-equal raw top-1 correctness improves ≥0.05 across all candidates and ≥0.10 on the physically best candidate versus step 0, with positive all-candidate change in at least six of eight subject plans and no appearance mode dropping by more than 0.05 absolute. If not, stop this architecture's scaling and inspect the source of failure. If the signal holds, continue in larger distinct-section batches with best-DEV-checkpoint retention and separately evaluate whether the operational matcher can exceed the unchanged-plane control. Only then feed its signal back into the same joint pose/deformation model, correct the 094 pre-fit-rank-versus-post-fit-mapped-error target mismatch, and measure natural-beam selected pose/mapped tissue error on a new synthetic panel and donor-disjoint real development data.
