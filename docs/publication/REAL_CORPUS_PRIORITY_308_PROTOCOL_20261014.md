# 308: use the real TRAIN corpus before another pose-head experiment

The 307 two-pose-per-anchor test failed its frozen gate. The retained 305 lineage had 104,000 synthetic pose presentations and 8,000 synthetic joint presentations, but its acquired stages used only 188 distinct Lupori TRAIN images and 200 coronal plus 200 sagittal Allen optimizer presentations. The prepared TRAIN-only coronal corpus contains 263,754 physical sections from 1,885 donors. Downloaded is not trained: most of that corpus has never entered the model. The next development cycle therefore tests real-image diversity, not another synthetic-only candidate-head variation.

First freeze a new TRAIN-inner coronal readout **before** looking at its images. Read the completed TRAIN-corpus manifest and donor metadata only. Exclude all 58 donors in the earlier 1,280-row coronal pool from eligibility for this new readout. Order the remaining `animal_partition_key` values by SHA-256 of `coronal-308-inner-20261014:<key>`; hold out the first 100 entire donors, including every section and experiment from each. Select one median `(section_number, section_id)` section per held-out donor for fixed readout. Keep those 100 and all 58 earlier-pool donors out of 308 coronal gradients; the remaining donors form the broad-corpus gradient pool. Save the exact split, section IDs, source hashes, weak-affine label role and source-manifest hash on I:. No DEV/CAL/FINAL, benchmark, or image pixels enter split construction.

In the next matched training study, replace the old first-1,280-row coronal loader with lazy donor-shard reads from this split. Sample donors evenly, then a section within the selected donor; do not present deliberate paired background/artifact versions of one section. Retain independently drawn arbitrary-plane synthetic examples for exact pose and local-map supervision and retain fluorescence/sagittal exposure. Allen 2D/3D registration affines are **weak references**: use only broad/tolerant pose supervision, never exact physical plane or deformation truth. The control and broad-corpus arms must start from identical scratch-lineage weights and optimizer state, use equal optimizer batches and noncoronal draws, and differ only in the coronal source/sampling. The specific loss weights, batch count, readout times, and stopping gates must be frozen in a separate training protocol before gradients.

Read out blind first-choice pose and local-map error on fresh independent full-sphere synthetic sections, including steep and sparse strata; donor-separated acquired coronal/sagittal weak-reference diagnostics; and the withheld Lupori TRAIN animal. Report both gains and regressions. A large real-image corpus can teach appearance but cannot by itself validate steep physically cut planes: these Allen sections are near-coronal with registration-derived coordinates. Do not promote a checkpoint, claim calibrated electrode-region probabilities, run DeepSlice benchmarking, or replace the GUI without independent physical-oblique animal evidence and later sealed-animal qualification. If broad physical exposure does not help blind held-out performance, diagnose label/appearance signal before increasing batches or model size.
