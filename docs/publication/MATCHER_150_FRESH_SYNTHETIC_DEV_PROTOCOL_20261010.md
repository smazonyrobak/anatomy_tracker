# Matcher 150: fresh synthetic development panel (frozen design)

## Purpose and boundary

This panel is a one-time mechanism readout after the 148/149 correspondence-and-weighting failure. It tests whether a changed matcher improves plane correction on *new synthetic deformation plans*. It is not a set of new biological animals, not expert-labelled histology, not uncertainty calibration, and not a public DeepSlice benchmark. Do not use it to tune architecture, checkpoint, fit ridge, confidence threshold, or training hyperparameters after seeing results; make those decisions on TRAIN-only data first.

## Frozen split and draw

- Source: pinned Allen 25 μm atlas context `c3bd31cc81af2788437cfd064f4cbf44d1d9f111919031c4c691552e796d94a8` and `sample_animal_subject_deformation_plan_v2` defaults. Eight independent nonidentity synthetic `development` plans use root seed `2026101015001` and synthetic indices `15000…15007`; their exact animal, subject, specimen, experiment, plan, and realization IDs are saved in each plan receipt. All 64 independent TRAIN base deformation plans and their 4,096 virtual affine descendants, and every previous synthetic DEV plan, are excluded by identity and receipt. The plan output is `I:/AnatomyTracker/data/matcher_150_fresh_dev_plans`.
- Panel: 32 independent random brain-intersecting physical planes per plan, 256 target sections after empty-tissue retries, native `256×256`, panel seed `2026101015002`, artifact seed prefix `2026101015003`. Proposed normals are uniform on antipodal plane space, with independent roll, offset, 25–100 μm thickness, appearance, and artifact draw per section; conditioning on tissue intersection can change the accepted normal distribution. Empty-tissue geometry is redrawn; marginal/ineligible nonempty sections are retained and counted. Exact chance repeats are allowed. No plane is deliberately reused with different background/artifacts, and no geometry de-duplication is imposed. The panel output is `I:/AnatomyTracker/data/coarse_atlas_pose_150_fresh_dev_panel`.
- Each plane is rendered through nine normalized finite-thickness samples on its accepted synthetic subject deformation. The observed appearance is one independently sampled raw, exact-black-exterior, or imperfect-brush mode (mode probabilities `.70/.15/.15`), followed by independently sampled v4 exposure/read noise and v3 local warp, fragment/fold/bubble/tile-seam events. The generator's limitations remain: atlas-derived/stylized tissue texture and simplified artifact shapes; this panel cannot establish real-slide accuracy.

## Frozen eligibility, truth, and metrics

Eligibility is decided without reading predictions: source visible support mass and post-artifact valid observed pixels must each be at least `256²/144`; all failures remain in the receipt. The ground-truth rigid plane is the accepted subject-to-CCF canonical plane fit. Local observed-to-CCF correspondence uses the same exact synthetic deformation map and excludes occluded/ambiguous pixels. The `.npz` contains the image input, rigid state, centre map, valid mask, reflection, slab offsets/weights, and source physical OUV; the JSONL record includes all four provenance IDs, deformation-plan receipt SHA256, random-seed branches, normal/roll/offset/thickness, appearance mode, eligibility, and image-file SHA256.

Compare preregistered matcher variants on the same eligible sections. Report per-plan-equal and per-section means in millimetres for exact-plane and near-candidate (`≤1.5 mm` initial rigid error) correction, degradation rate `>0.1 mm`, and angle-bin counts/effects (`<15°, 15–30°, 30–45°, ≥45°` from the nearest anatomical axis). Report full-atlas versus support-only contrast if both are trained. Keep query correspondence and reliability diagnostics separate from the rigid-pose endpoint. No claim about biological generalization, electrode-region probabilities, or GUI replacement follows from this panel.

## Freeze and audit

Commit the two preparation scripts and this protocol before generation. Plan and panel `protocol.json` files record source Git commit, exact archived source SHA256 hashes, upstream plan/atlas receipts, random seeds, and split. Each plan directory records the raw artifact files and SHA256, plan receipt, and lineage; `completed.json` hashes every frozen file. Each section file and the panel `records.jsonl` are hashed; panel `completed.json` binds its protocol, plan completion, and records. Verify those hashes, finite arrays, exact section count, 8 disjoint plan receipts, and source IDs before any evaluation. Do not alter the frozen data after generation.
