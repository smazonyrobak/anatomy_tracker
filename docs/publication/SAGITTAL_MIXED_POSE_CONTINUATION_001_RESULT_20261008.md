# Mixed-pose continuation 001: frozen internal result

The 653-batch pose continuation of the **same scratch-lineage joint model** completed on `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/sagittal_mixed_pose_continuation_001_retry1`. It used 653 distinct physical-sagittal TRAIN sections from 40 donors, 653 different reserved coronal TRAIN sections from 653 donors (none repeated from the immediate 019 parent schedule), and 1,306 independently sampled eligible full-sphere synthetics. Every physical section was seen once in this stage; 158 additional synthetic attempts were ineligible and logged. Only the image encoder and direct pose head were updated; the atlas encoder, local mapper, recurrent fitter and fitted scorer remained frozen. No public benchmark, prior external model, pseudolabel, heldout animal or probability calibration was used. The source-available sagittal weak-DEV2 denominator was frozen at 158/159 before model readout because the official server does not return a JPEG for SectionImage `101345593`; all eight reserved DEV2 donors remained represented.

The one predeclared evaluation compared batches 0, 326 and 653 on the same 185 eligible identity-separated synthetic DEV sections, 64 coronal real DEV sections from six donors, and 158 physical-sagittal weak-DEV2 sections from eight other donors. Means below weight animals/identities equally; distances are physical millimetres. Real references are **weak automated Allen affines**, not expert anatomical truth.

Both nonzero checkpoints passed the frozen mechanism gate; batch 653 wins the predeclared tie-break. Against batch 0 it improves sagittal normal by 42.95° and five-point by 4.015 mm, while coronal worsens only 0.068 mm and synthetic mapped-tissue improves 0.028 mm. All eight sagittal donors improved on both selected metrics, but one still has 29.19° normal and 3.990 mm five-point disagreement. Independent aggregation of all 1,221 raw evaluation rows reproduced the summary and donor-equal means.

**Not ready to ship.** Even on this cardinal orientation, 2.425 mm mean five-point error is anatomically substantial. Separate oracle minima over the *same predicted branches* are 1.255 mm sagittal five-point disagreement and 4.55° normal disagreement; on synthetic DEV, the best predicted branch's mapped-tissue error is 0.980 mm versus 2.925 mm for the selected branch. Those are diagnostic oracles, not deployable results, and the minima need not come from the same branch. Candidate selection and residual position/scale remain major bottlenecks in addition to synthetic/acquired appearance mismatch. This result supports continued all-angle work rather than declaring arbitrary cuts impossible, but it does not validate physical steep-oblique cuts, dense local deformation, electrode-site accuracy, calibrated probability, or the GUI replacement. The new v2 artifact simulator remains out of training pending acquisition-realism calibration.

A frozen top-k audit (`I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/sagittal_mixed_pose_candidate_analysis_001/summary.json`, SHA-256 `fcaa627738a0fe5042e08d3c30035472063b6be6e7197297e9c38484a55d1b8b`) localizes the bottleneck. At batch 653, the best branch *among the eight highest-prior branches* averages 1.118 mm synthetic mapped error, versus 0.980 mm among all 32; for sagittal weak-DEV2 five-point disagreement it is 1.281 versus 1.255 mm. The selected top-one branch is 2.925 and 2.425 mm respectively. On synthetic sections nearest AP, DV and ML normals, top-eight oracle mapped errors are 1.141, 1.126 and 1.085 mm. Thus useful candidates are usually in a small shortlist across the whole normal sphere; the current image-only prior fails to choose them. This is an oracle diagnostic, not a usable inference method, but it argues for a targeted *candidate-selection/fit-evidence* experiment before adding more orientation modes or narrowing allowable cutting angles.
