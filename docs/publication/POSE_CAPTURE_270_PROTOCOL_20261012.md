# 270: bounded continuation of blind arbitrary-plane pose capture

268 stopped at 32,000 independent synthetic presentations with a failed 45/64 top-16 capture gate. Its loss and top-16 capture were still improving at the final checkpoint (20/64 at batch 4,000, 31/64 at batch 8,000); the post-exit cost-ranking audit ruled out the proposed composite-versus-site loss mismatch. This is a new, bounded learning-curve experiment, not a retrospective pass for 268 or permission to promote its checkpoint. It tests whether more independent physical draws help before spending on a joint fitter. V9 corrects the optional-brush exterior contract and was preflighted on 24 independently drawn examples (finite inputs, 24 unique physical IDs, zero image outside both sampled brush masks).

Initialize from the project's own fresh-random 268 batch-8,000 model and optimizer only. Train the same pose modules for 8,000 further batches of four independently sampled v9 sections from synthetic TRAIN bases 0–55. Each presentation samples its own full-sphere cutting plane, subject deformation, image appearance and artifacts. No deliberate appearance pairing or plane deduplication; no prior/external trained model, feature or pseudolabel. Use AdamW with inherited moments, learning rate 5e-5 then 3e-5 after 4,000 batches, weight decay 1e-4 and gradient-norm clipping at 2. Keep the physical-site/five-point/normal objective and 80-mode × two-reflection proposal. The v9 data change and fresh panel make this distinct from merely reinterpreting 268; neither change asserts improved biology.

Before the first update, freeze 64 newly drawn v9 TRAIN-inner cases (eight from each synthetic base 56–63) and preserve the old 268 v8 panel as a retention readout. Read at added batches 0, 2,000, 4,000 and 8,000. The main diagnostic is blind top-16 capture within 1.5 mm of sampled valid tissue, with top-two capture, first-choice physical site error, all-160 oracle, normal error, visible-tissue and angle strata, and minor-FOV ratio also reported. Select a continuation checkpoint by the fresh-panel top-16 count, then lower fresh-panel equal-base first-choice error; do not use synthetic truth to rank branches at inference.

The selected checkpoint may enter the subsequent *research* atlas-feedback stage only if, on the fresh panel, top-16 capture reaches at least 45/64, top-two at least 16/64, first-choice mean site error is at most 3.0 mm and median minor-FOV ratio at least 0.5; on the old panel, top-16 must remain at least 40/64. No more than four fresh selected planes may have minor FOV at or below 1.2 mm. This is a capture/retention gate, **not** a shippability or calibrated-probability gate. The next atlas stage must still outperform intact-atlas controls and lead to a genuinely joint pose/local-map improvement on identity-disjoint synthetic development and real animal-level data. If this gate fails or the curve plateaus, stop this recipe and diagnose rather than simply enlarging the run.

The panels are derived from one Allen atlas with eight held-out synthetic deformation bases, not eight independent animals. No acquired animal labels, CAL/FINAL data or public DeepSlice benchmark may select this checkpoint. This experiment does not establish physical oblique accuracy, effective nonrigid alignment, trajectory uncertainty or GUI readiness.
