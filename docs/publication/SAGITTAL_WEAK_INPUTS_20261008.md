# Frozen physically sagittal development inputs

`training/prepare_allen_sagittal_weak_inputs_001.py` implemented the acquisition-centred preprocessing declared before a model readout in `SAGITTAL_WEAK_COHORT_SPLIT_20261008.md`. It prepared all 240 acquired sagittal ISH sections, retaining the donor-frozen 160 TRAIN/80 weak-DEV assignment, all exact animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs and source JPEG hashes. The arrays and per-image CCF affines are on `I:/AnatomyTracker/data/allen_sagittal_ish_weak_inputs_001_20261008`, not in Git. `model_input.npy` SHA-256 is `fb2aca6e162cdba2e58ae6a46df40ae670b9f524867260f45f8cb16406e81ed2`, `geometry.jsonl` is `714beb3e195cc4831bdc80d14bbdf6c97aa20a6fbcc440fb75d68da49842baa7`, and `summary.json` is `811106150f042a5b98b59e51754747192d1731450f014bc9e56343d478a78050`.

Each section has **one** 256² red-channel input with brightfield polarity inverted, no paired background copy and no brush mask. The square field covers the complete acquired image plus 5% margin around its larger physical dimension; its span is 9.08–16.73 mm (median 14.51 mm), so the prior fixed 12-mm coronal window would not be valid here. Gaussian antialiasing and bilinear sampling use the saved exact pixel-coordinate transform. Acquired-border median padding precedes inversion. A tiny numerical interpolation overshoot gives a minimum input value of −2.4×10⁻⁷ rather than exactly zero; it is not a negative-intensity acquisition artifact.

The per-row 3-D affine composes official SectionImage Alignment2d, experiment Alignment3d, the official reference-space-10→9 flip and the established +12.5-µm voxel-centre convention. An independent external point check for full-resolution pixel `(7032,3648)` of section 69750516 differs from the official Allen `image_to_reference` endpoint by `9.1×10⁻¹³` µm after that transform. All 240 array rows are finite and have unique section IDs; output and script hashes match the saved summary. The absolute median affine-normal components in AP/DV/ML are `(0.0150,0.00284,0.9997)`, confirming this cohort probes a physically sagittal orientation. Median acquired-image support is 55.2% of the square field.

This is an **appearance and gross-orientation development cohort**, not dense-warp truth, independent expert plane truth, physical steep-oblique data, or a probability-calibration/final-test set. The Allen alignments may be imperfect; no model accuracy has been measured on these sections. The four weak-DEV donors were not visually inspected or used to choose preprocessing. The raw images have Allen noncommercial-use/citation conditions; rights must be reviewed before commercial distribution or integration. Do not confuse this sagittal check with evidence that arbitrary-angle single-slice inference works.
