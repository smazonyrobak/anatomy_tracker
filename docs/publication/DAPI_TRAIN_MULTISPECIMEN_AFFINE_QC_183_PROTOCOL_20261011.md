Individual NIfTI identity sforms and unknown spatial units do not establish native anatomical orientation. The in-memory native convention assumes header pixdim in millimetres, origin zero, positive voxel axes, and axis 1 as the reconstructed coronal stack. Left/right, AP sign, physical origin, and between-animal conventions remain unverified. A high global intensity correlation or tissue Dice is not a landmark-accuracy gate. Orthogonal overlays expose gross failures, not local correspondence; no resulting transform is approved as CCF or pose/deformation truth. The DAPI population average includes held-out animals, and any oblique reslice of these native reconstructed volumes would be interpolated from 100-µm acquired coronal sections rather than an independent physically oblique validation sample.
