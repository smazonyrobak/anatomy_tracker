# 074: can existing fit difficulty rescue 073's ranking?

No. On the **same eight blindly proposed coarse planes** per section from checkpoint 2500, the frozen 059 mapper was applied to each plane and its learned fit energy, inferred-mask image agreement and squared local displacement were recorded without changing the model. The exact pose represented by every proposal was reconstructed before mapping (maximum landmark mismatch below 1 µm). The 310-row diagnostic used all 246 synthetic DEV sections/eight identities and 64 weak-real DEV sections/six donors; source, checkpoint, row and summary hashes were verified after completion. Raw results: `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/whole_plane_correlation_073_fit_ranking_diagnostic_002`.

Identity-equal selected synthetic rigid error was 2.988 mm by the correlation score alone. Ranking these same eight candidates by the existing learned fit energy gave **3.008 mm**, by displacement cost **2.980 mm**, by inferred-mask image mismatch **2.784 mm**, and by the old pose prior **2.758 mm**. Even a diagnostic ranking with the **true synthetic tissue mask**, unavailable to the GUI, gave 2.650 mm. An in-sample four-feature weight search reached 2.675 mm, while leave-one-synthetic-identity-out weight selection gave **2.827 mm**; both missed the old prior's 2.656 mm selected result. Chosen feature weights varied widely across the held-out identities. The best of these eight coarse candidates remained 1.250 mm, so neither simple reweighting nor the current bank can satisfy the target 0.75-mm best-eight gate.

On weak-real DEV, fit energy selected 0.983 mm and inferred-mask image mismatch 1.056 mm, versus 1.276 mm for correlation-only and 0.694 mm for the old prior. In-sample weights tuned on synthetic identities produced 0.776 mm real error and still worsened individual donors. The frozen mapper changed selected synthetic physical error only from 2.995 to 2.986 mm section mean; mapping a wrong plane does not correct its global location. These are research diagnostics against weak labels, not calibrated or biological-ground-truth results.

Interpretation: the user's fit→pose feedback idea remains sensible, but the **existing** fit signals are not sufficiently discriminative on this coarse, on-policy candidate bank. The next controlled step is blind fine-grid refinement of its complete plane proposals, then—only if a good pose enters the retained set—train a candidate ranker on those actual inferred planes with fitting evidence, rather than adding a fixed warp penalty or training on an inserted exact-truth plane. No GUI promotion or public benchmark is justified.
