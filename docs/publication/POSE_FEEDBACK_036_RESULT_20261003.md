# Per-candidate pose feedback 036 — frozen development result

The preregistered **pose-movement gate failed**. Starting from the scratch-lineage 019 checkpoint, the unchanged shared atlas-conditioned updater was trained on all eight actual predicted branches rather than only the best branch, plus three varied known-plane perturbations and one exact training-only anchor. It saw 6,000 fresh accepted arbitrary-plane synthetic TRAIN sections over 3,000 batches; each generated physical section had its own seed, appearance and logged animal/specimen/experiment lineage. The rest of the model was frozen. The public benchmark, final-test animals and calibration sets were untouched. An independent read-only verifier passed the frozen source/checkpoint/output hashes, all 6,000 accepted unique section IDs, TRAIN split bindings, 996 raw DEV rows and every reported group-weighted metric/gate.

At batch 3,000 the score-selected synthetic result improved by 0.236 mm from the frozen parent, but correcting the original top-one moved physical accuracy by only **0.016 mm**, and best-eight coverage by only **0.025 mm**, far short of the frozen 0.250/0.200 mm movement gates. The real weak-affine top-one discrepancy worsened by 0.050 mm; one donor's mean regressed by 0.112 mm, below the 0.200 mm guardrail. These real affines are inherited near-coronal labels, not expert-oblique truth. Single training batches occasionally showed larger gains on injected near-true perturbations, but the fixed DEV panel demonstrates that this does **not** transfer into useful movement of actual off-plane proposals.

The cause is not fixed by giving every candidate the same physical loss for longer. The first-pass 019 atlas/image feature comparison was trained around known/candidate planes and uses a narrow local correlation window; 026 and 035 showed that its companion 025 exact-match descriptor does not reliably order or descend from wrong planes. The next architecture must learn **spatially structured off-plane anatomical evidence** with a wide capture range, such as global/multiscale correspondence and a pose-gradient or residual-update target on actual wrong proposals. [ProST](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10879149/) supplies a peer-reviewed precedent for training a differentiable image similarity's pose-gradient shape rather than only its scalar score, although X-ray/CT transfer to histology is unproven. The concrete next gate is that fit-driven updates materially reduce physical error on new, identity-disjoint synthetic DEV subjects without worsening real donor DEV, before unfreezing direct pose and local warp into one joint feedback model. Do not promote 036 to the GUI, scale the unchanged refiner, call its scores probabilities, or run DeepSlice benchmarking yet.
