# Mixed acquired/synthetic global-pose continuation 108 — audited result

The 1,959-update continuation of the randomly initialized v3 model lineage completed. It saw 3,918 independently drawn synthetic full-angle sections, 1,959 distinct acquired coronal TRAIN sections, and 653 acquired sagittal TRAIN sections presented in three shuffled epochs. Its parent was the frozen v3 step-2,000 checkpoint. Updates 1–653 trained only pose heads; updates 654–1,959 also trained the shared image encoder and lateral features. The atlas fitter, deformation mapper, fitted scorer, and uncertainty heads were frozen. This experiment did **not** train the required fitting-to-coordinate feedback.

The independent read-only audit confirmed checkpoint, schedule, source, protocol, raw-row, and summary hashes, split separation, row counts, and all preregistered gate calculations. The reused synthetic panel contains 64 sections in eight plans, with 14 natural blind pose candidates per section. Real development measurements compare to inherited weak Allen affine alignments, **not** blinded expert anatomical truth.

At step 653, sagittal weak-reference agreement improved markedly and coronal agreement was preserved, but both synthetic gains were only about 0.037 mm, below the preregistered 0.20-mm minimum. At step 1959, synthetic best-candidate and selected errors improved by 0.703 and 1.000 mm, and sagittal disagreement improved by 5.080 mm with all eight donors improving. However, coronal disagreement rose by 1.953 mm over the parent, far above the allowed 0.20 mm. **Neither checkpoint passed the predeclared advancement gate.**

The coronal regression is not one bad section or a simple summary error: relative to step 653, all six donors and 51/64 sections worsened. Two donors dominate its size, but four others also worsened. The same sections, weak references, and evaluator formula were used for all three checkpoints; 13/64 chosen pose branches changed from step 653 to 1959, so branch selection alone does not explain the result. Because additional updates and two further sagittal presentations occurred at the same time as shared-feature unfreezing, this staged run does **not** establish that unfreezing alone caused the regression. A continuation from the saved step-653 optimizer/RNG state with the same draws and schedules but shared features still frozen is the next matched causal control.

The improvement on steep synthetic angles shows full-plane geometry remains trainable; it does not establish reliable recovery for every brain-intersecting plane. Low-information or damaged sections may remain genuinely ambiguous and require uncertainty, constraints, or neighboring sections. The prior-selected 2.949-mm synthetic error remains too large for electrode-site claims. The six coronal and eight sagittal donor sets are repeatedly used DEV material, and their affines are weak labels. There is no calibrated probability, independently proven real anatomical accuracy, deployable GUI replacement, or public comparator result here.
