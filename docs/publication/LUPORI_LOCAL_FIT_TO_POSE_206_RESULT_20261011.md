# 206: local atlas fit gradient does not materially improve pose

The [pre-run training protocol](LUPORI_LOCAL_FIT_TO_POSE_206_PROTOCOL_20261011.md) completed both matched 400-batch arms from the same scratch-lineage 187 step-800 checkpoint. The [pre-exit readout protocol](LUPORI_LOCAL_FIT_TO_POSE_206_READOUT_PROTOCOL_20261011.md) then evaluated fixed 0/200/400 checkpoints. Each arm saw exactly the same 1,200 eligible synthetic presentations and 400 acquired Lupori TRAIN presentations from `CC2B`, `CC4B`, and `CC4A`. All 188 allowed acquired sections were presented. The `fit` arm alone injected a capped rigid atlas-versus-image MIND gradient into final pose-geometry output rows on 248/400 batches where its current top-one plane was within 1.5 mm of the approximate TRAIN human reference and had enough atlas support. Neither arm trained on reserved `AL1A`, official DEV `CC1B`, CAL `CC3A`, FINAL `CC1A`, or a public benchmark.

The fixed step-400 gate **failed**. On all 52 `AL1A` sections, first-choice five-frame-point mean disagreement with the approximate human plane was 1.83520 mm for the detached arm and 1.83490 mm for fit: a **0.00030-mm (0.30-µm) gain**, versus the preregistered ≥0.10-mm requirement. A descriptive paired-section bootstrap interval for fit-minus-detached was −0.00127 to +0.00062 mm; it is not an animal-population interval. Both arms had two first-choice failures >5 mm. At step 200, the fit effect was −0.00113 mm. The best-of-160 candidate mean at step 400 slightly worsened under fit, 1.23484 to 1.23598 mm.

The local MIND fit-state gradient pointed in the weak-reference-improving direction in 142/248 eligible fit-arm presentations (57.3%; median scaled-state cosine 0.105), not a reliable directional signal. The 248 fit gradients were applied with the declared ≤20%-of-supervised-geometry norm cap and zero probability-output-row gradient. The matched detached arm generated the same draw manifest, so the near-zero difference is not due to different training sections.

The fit arm met the no-additional-catastrophe and *relative-to-detached* retention ceilings, but not the improvement gate: at step 400 its direct first-choice error changed by +0.0114 mm on frozen v3 synthetic DEV, −0.0077 mm on frozen v4 synthetic DEV, and −0.00007 mm on 64 new v7 planes from previously trained synthetic bases. The v7 panel is out-of-draw, **not** out-of-base or a biological validation set. Donor-equal inherited-affine disagreement changed by −0.0013 mm on older coronal acquired DEV and +0.00034 mm on older sagittal acquired DEV. These tiny arm differences do not establish useful anatomical feedback.

Both continued arms also drifted relative to their own step-zero checkpoint without old-real rehearsal: the detached arm's older coronal donor-equal disagreement rose 0.985→1.333 mm, sagittal rose 1.260→1.411 mm, and frozen v3 synthetic section mean rose 3.188→3.244 mm. The matched fit-versus-detached gate isolates the added fit term but **neither continued checkpoint is a promotion candidate**. The direct AL1A mean improved only 1.904→1.835 mm over those same 400 batches. The historical synthetic DEV panels are v3/v4; the training protocol's “frozen v3/v7” wording was explicitly reconciled in the pre-exit readout protocol by adding a separate fresh v7 TRAIN-base panel, not mislabeling it as frozen DEV.

The first readout invocation stopped before any readout output was created because PyTorch's `weights_only=True` loader rejected the locally saved NumPy RNG arrays. After confirming the checkpoint hashes, the evaluator explicitly loaded these locally produced checkpoints with `weights_only=False` and wrote the sole completed readout. No training output was changed. Independent post-exit rehashing matched both arm receipts/checkpoints and every readout output against its completion receipt; a separate raw-row calculation reproduced the 52-section paired mean and both >5-mm counts.

**Decision:** do not scale this MIND-to-pose feedback, rerank global branches with its fit score, calibrate it, or replace GUI models. This failure does not prove joint pose/deformation inference impossible; it shows this particular atlas-image fitting signal supplies essentially no useful held-animal pose improvement at the safe local gradient strength, consistent with the preceding [204 ranking assay](LUPORI_STRUCTURAL_FIT_204_RESULT_20261011.md). The binding next work is stronger independently supervised acquired anatomy and specimen variation, plus a matched all-angle validation source. Additional epochs on the same three weakly labelled acquired animals or more synthetic copies of one atlas would not resolve that evidence gap. The model remains uncalibrated; no electrode-region probability or DeepSlice-superiority claim is justified.
