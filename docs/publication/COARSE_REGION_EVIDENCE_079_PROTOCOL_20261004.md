# 079: can coarse anatomy distinguish the existing blind planes?

The 019–077 attempts have not reliably selected or corrected a whole plane. Before adding a segmentation branch to the **same joint pose/deformation model**, test whether perfectly known broad Allen regions would distinguish the frozen 059 model's actual blind proposals. This is an **unavailable-at-inference oracle** and a necessary-condition screen, not a trained model or accuracy claim. [SynthSeg](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10154424/) motivates contrast-randomized semantic learning and [single-section mouse-atlas localization](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10406728/) motivates regional anatomy as pose evidence; neither establishes this proposal.

Freeze the 059 checkpoint, its normal anchors, and the 246 eligible sections/eight synthetic deformation identities in the 061 DEV panel. For each section, retain exactly its image-prior top eight legacy branches and top six normal-anchor branches, including their original reflections and order; never insert a truth pose. At 1,024 fixed surviving-tissue pixels (the same deterministic selection as 077), obtain the **oracle** broad-region target by nearest-neighbour lookup of the known synthetic CCF correspondence in the pinned Allen annotation. Obtain each candidate's broad-region labels at its own unwarped physical finite-frame coordinates. Neither tissue truth nor target region IDs may enter candidate generation or the frozen model.

Map annotation IDs through the pinned Allen ontology to 14 coarse, potentially recognizable classes: isocortex, olfactory areas, hippocampal formation, cortical subplate, striatum, pallidum, thalamus, hypothalamus, midbrain, pons, medulla, cerebellum, fiber tracts, and ventricles. Exterior/unassigned is class zero. Score each candidate by mean foreground-class Dice among classes occupying at least 16 of the 1,024 oracle target pixels; ties retain the frozen image-prior order. Whole-canvas Dice and exact Allen-ID agreement are diagnostics only and cannot replace the primary score. The mask and labels are privileged; a deployable model would have to infer them from the input and retain performance on real acquired images.

Report identity-equal physical visible-tissue error for the unchanged prior-selected candidate, the semantic-oracle-selected candidate, and the truth-best candidate among the same 14. Also report the fraction of sections on which semantic selection picks the physically best branch, exact-black/raw/imperfect-brush strata, low/higher support strata, and the score–physical-error relation. All synthetic identities and source/checkpoint/panel/annotation/ontology/result hashes remain bound in an `I:`-only output receipt. Do not open images, retrain, access public/final-test data, or use real weak affines to choose the score.

**Advance to training a coarse-region auxiliary head only if** semantic-oracle-selected identity-equal physical error is ≤1.80 mm, improves the prior by ≥0.50 mm, and no appearance/support stratum worsens by >0.20 mm. A failure means that even perfect broad-region identification does not make this specific 14-proposal bank sufficiently selectable; do not train a semantic head for this purpose. A pass is only a premise for one joint-model architecture experiment, not validation of real region segmentation, candidate probability calibration, arbitrary-plane biological accuracy, or GUI readiness.
