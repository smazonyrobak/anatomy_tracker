# 041 global 2D/3D matching and gated correction: development result

The 041 pilot is **not promoted**. Its frozen 2,000-batch run completed with 4,000 fresh accepted arbitrary-plane synthetic TRAIN sections and 2,000 distinct acquired weak-affine real TRAIN sections. It did not use public or final-test images. The independent read-only verifier passed all source, parent, schedule, draw, checkpoint, result, identity, and donor checks (`training/verify_pose_feedback_global_041.py`). The corrected TRAIN schedule samples without replacement within 041, but permits the same unchanged acquired image to have appeared in a past *separate* experiment; the pre-output startup correction is recorded in the 041 protocol.

The frozen development panel contains 177 eligible physical sections from eight disjoint synthetic deformation identities, plus 64 weak-real Allen sections from six development donors. The real reference is an inherited Allen affine, **not expert arbitrary-plane truth**. All numbers below are identity- or donor-equal means in millimetres; the original raw rows remain frozen at `I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/pose_feedback_global_041_development_eval/rows.jsonl`.

The strongest selected gain is 0.066 mm at batch 1,500, far below the predeclared 0.25 mm threshold; its best-eight gain is −0.001 mm, below the 0.20 mm threshold. The worst-donor weak-real regression at that checkpoint is 0.108 mm, within the 0.20 mm safety limit but not an accuracy gain. Other checkpoints also fail. At batch 1,500, 112 near-true cases with a prior best-eight error ≤1 mm worsen from 0.681 to 0.700 mm. Raw (ungated) fits are substantially less safe on real images: at batch 1,500, 0.768 mm versus the 0.584 mm prior, while gating limits the result to 0.618 mm. Gating is therefore useful as damage control, not as successful refinement.

Global context raises the fraction of learned soft 3D matches within 1.5 mm of synthetic truth from the 039 pilot's 30%/48% top-one/prior-best-eight to 44%/75% at batch 1,500. Yet neither the physical best-eight fit nor score-selected pose meaningfully improves. Across all 1,205 per-checkpoint evaluation rows, the score-selected branch is **always** the parent's top-ranked branch. The predeclared matched-top-four sensitivity exactly agrees with the top-eight selected result; the extra four candidates are not causing the selection failure. The geometric opportunity remains: the 040 unavailable-truth correspondence experiment reduced the same parent best-eight mean from 1.001 to 0.422 mm using snapped exact atlas matches. The most direct interpretation is that approximate, entropy-weighted correspondences and their fit are still inadequate; the new context branch has not solved precise matching or candidate selection. This is an inference from development data, not proof of a single causal bug.

The next targeted diagnostic is to hold the 041 network fixed and compare its soft expected 3D matches with hard/high-confidence and robust-consensus alternatives under the *same* full-frame fit, on a frozen synthetic development panel. This distinguishes an appearance-descriptor limitation from bias introduced by averaging ambiguous 3D matches or trusting outliers. If hard/consensus matches make the oracle-best candidate improve without increasing real-reference regression, use that evidence to design the next trainable joint pose/deformation architecture; otherwise revisit the representation, potentially rotation-equivariant local descriptors. The latter is supported as a 2D/3D registration strategy by [SLIV-Reg](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18683), but its CT/MRI results do not transfer automatically to histology. [2D3D-MATR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/papers/Li_2D3D-MATR_2D-3D_Matching_Transformer_for_Detection-Free_Registration_Between_Images_and_ICCV_2023_paper.pdf) also supports geometric matching with robust pose estimation; [Osechinskiy and Kruggel](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3335496/) shows why deformation energy must be explicit in histology slice-to-volume alignment.

The 041 pilot only trained a matching/correction head around a **frozen** 019 pose-and-local-warp predictor. It does not yet constitute the user's requested native joint pose/deformation model, calibrated electrode-region probabilities, qualified GUI replacement, or evidence of superiority to DeepSlice. Keep the untouched final animals and public comparison closed.
