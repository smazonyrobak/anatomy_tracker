# 072 joint pose-correction continuation: development protocol

The 071 score-only experiment failed on blind atlas-search candidates despite recognizing exact supplied poses. Experiment 072 therefore targets **moving actual predicted poses**, not another fixed reranker. Continue the randomly initialized standalone 059 model; retain its image pose mixture and bounded local tissue mapper, but reset and train its shared full-frame residual updater, and allow gradients into the image encoder, probabilistic pose heads, atlas-pair representation and (later) mapper. No legacy-app weights, external features or pseudolabels enter.

Each batch draws fresh arbitrary-plane synthetic TRAIN sections and distinct real TRAIN sections, retaining their IDs and source bindings. At inference-equivalent training time, take the 059 quota of two old and six normal-anchor pose/reflection candidates. Render each finite-thickness atlas plane, obtain the mapper's fit evidence, and update its full 3D pose and in-plane scale/shear once. Supervise physical correction and selection on **these predicted candidates**; retain direct pose supervision so the updater cannot excuse a missing or wrong global proposal. A first stage establishes correction without image-fit gradients. A second stage enables a small, validity-weighted anatomical fitting and bounded-deformation loss through the corrected pose and mapper into the coordinate predictor. Record a nonzero fit-to-pose gradient, but do not equate this alone with improved prediction.

Freeze checkpoints at 0, 3,000, 6,000 and 10,000 batches. After the run exits, evaluate all four on the existing fixed 185 eligible synthetic-development sections from eight held-out synthetic identities and 64 weak-Allen-reference images from six held-out real donors. Report identity-equal selected visible-tissue error, reference-best corrected candidate, correct-pose mapped error, weak-real five-point disagreement, raw/brush/black and low-support tails. The decisive gate is substantial improvement over the 059/019 reference on **selected** synthetic accuracy without worsening the six real donors or the known-pose mapper. The synthetic identities are not biological animals; weak Allen affines are not expert truth. No public benchmark, final-test animal, probability calibration or GUI promotion in this experiment.

This is a staged continuation of one model, not a separate trained component spliced in later. If the correction cannot reduce blind pose error, stop this branch and diagnose global representation/observability rather than tuning the same score again. Published slice-to-volume iterative transform updates ([SVoRT](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10129054/)) and jointly optimized histology pose/deformation ([Casamitjana et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09708-2)) are design precedents, not guarantees for an isolated arbitrary-plane mouse section.
