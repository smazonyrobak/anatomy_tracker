# 236: near blind candidates are wrong in 3-D, not merely locally warped

The [frozen 236 replay](TRUE_POSE_DEFORMATION_FLOOR_236_PROTOCOL_20261011.md) reproduced all 64 physical-section IDs and image SHA-256 hashes from [235](BLIND_CANDIDATE_REACHABILITY_235_RESULT_20261011.md). No model prediction or training was used. At each section's **true synthetic pose**, the median 75th-percentile displacement of the exact tissue→CCF target normal to the plane was only **0.041 mm** (case range 0.019–0.072 mm). Median true-pose rigid tissue-site error was 0.146 mm and 75th-percentile in-plane shift was 3.14 pixels. Every true pose had 100% of its 128 tissue sites within the 235 window of 0.5 mm through plane and 32 pixels in plane.

For the 51 cases with a `<2 mm` blind top-16 candidate, the median paired excess 75th-percentile through-plane residual of the best-reachable candidate over its true pose was **1.053 mm** (quartiles 0.786–1.478 mm). The predeclared ≤0.25-mm true-pose-floor interpretation applies: the near candidates are predominantly **incorrect planes in 3-D**, not victims of a large synthetic through-plane deformation floor. A local 2-D warp around a fixed proposal cannot correct this mismatch. The first-choice synthetic pose is still poor; this finding does not imply the image lacks information, nor does it demonstrate an improved model.

The next architecture must allow candidate-conditioned updates to the full plane normal/offset/in-plane frame while examining atlas appearance in 3-D or across nearby re-rendered planes, before or jointly with a constrained local tissue warp. The 13/64 cases without a `<2 mm` top-16 proposal also need broader or improved global retrieval. Deformation effort may then inform pose probability, but only after both wrong-pose and true-pose fit costs are measured on support-matched proposals and compared with an anatomical-intensity null. Do **not** train the narrow 32-pixel 2-D-only updater, promote 235's stage-A result as registration success, calibrate probabilities, use reserved animals, run DeepSlice, or replace the GUI.
