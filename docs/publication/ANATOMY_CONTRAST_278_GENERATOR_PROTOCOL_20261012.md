# 278: anatomy-conditioned appearance, qualitative gate first

275 supplied a near plane among its top 16 for 60/64 new synthetic TRAIN-inner sections but still chose a 2.329-mm mean first plane and transferred poorly to acquired sagittal images. The 276 family decision gained on its development donors but failed its separate 277 donor gate. The current v9 source is one smooth average-template volume with global brightness/texture perturbations; acquired TRAIN tissue had stronger fine-scale texture in 119. Simple blur, Fourier low-frequency style, and swapping the whole volume to Nissl did not establish a robust remedy (273/274/225). Before another pose continuation, test one generator mechanism rather than more v9 exposure.

For each **independently drawn** full-sphere, brain-intersecting synthetic plane, take its existing warped CCF central coordinate raster, nearest-sample the pinned Allen annotation, and draw fresh per-label contrast codes and correlated fine tissue texture from that plane's own seed. Blend these into its source tissue before the existing local strain, tear fragment, fold, bubble, tile seam, exposure, PSF-derived pose targets, and optional brush/exterior handling. No physical plane, style code, background or artifact is intentionally reused for another training example. The annotation is source anatomy, not a learned feature or pseudolabel. Central-plane label sampling approximates a 25–100-µm slab and may produce unrealistic boundaries; this is a limitation to inspect, not a claim of histological realism. Keep v9 immutable for exact historical replay.

First freeze a six-row actual-generator preview: independently sampled eligible sections chosen only to span observed obliquity, with finite-thickness fitted atlas plane, pre-damage styled source, and final damaged model input on the same display scale. Record every draw and output hash. Visually inspect for mosaic label artifacts, implausible texture, support/exterior leakage, and preserved local damage. If clearly implausible, revise *before* any pilot and make a new candidate rather than declaring a result. Also measure at least the predeclared 119 one- and three-pixel high-pass tissue metrics on a modest fresh sample and compare descriptively with acquired TRAIN ranges; no single scalar alone determines plausibility.

Only if QC is acceptable, predeclare a separate short v9-versus-v11 pose training comparison from identical 275 weights and optimizer state, with equal batches, independent physical-plane draws per arm (no paired duplicate section in training), the same frozen synthetic and acquired readouts, and an explicit promotion gate. This protocol itself authorizes no long run, GUI integration, calibration or DeepSlice benchmark. Acquired Allen affines are weak orientation readouts, not expert pose truth. Untouched CAL/FINAL animals remain reserved; physical steep-oblique accuracy cannot be claimed from simulation.

The design hypothesis is informed by [SynthMorph's label-map contrast randomization in MRI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34587005/) and [Xiong et al.'s uneven-staining limitation for mouse histology-to-atlas reconstruction](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroinformatics/articles/10.3389/fninf.2018.00093/pdf). Neither primary study demonstrates that this generator will improve our single-slice arbitrary-plane task.
