# Six fresh 221 generator examples: qualitative check, not a benchmark

The I:-only preview script drew six independent random TRAIN bases, support-intersecting planes, artifact seeds, and v3/v4 appearance choices from the actual 166→217→v3/v4 generator. All six were eligible on the first draw, had distinct physical-section IDs, and were displayed without selecting for model success. Each row shows (1) the atlas rendered at the known affine plane, (2) the generated section before extra slide artifacts, (3) the **single** final input image seen by the model, and (4) the atlas at the 221 framed-400 blind first-choice plane. Columns 2 and 3 are a *diagnostic before/after view*, not paired copies used as separate training examples.

The six affine plane normals were 27–46° from the nearest cardinal axis, with 9–33% visible tissue. The final inputs include raw variable/black exterior, an imperfect brush, low exposure, a tile seam, a displaced fragment, a fold, and a bubble; some rows had no extra slide event. The model's dense CCF error was 0.43–4.91 mm. It got one visibly convincing plane and several clearly wrong ones. The low-tissue/low-exposure rows make the ambiguity evident, but other visibly informative rows are also wrong. Six images cannot establish any-angle reliability or isolate the causal artifact.

Qualitatively, these remain *atlas-derived* textures with synthetic noise, smooth warp fields, and a small set of hand-coded damage events. The images demonstrate variation but do **not** establish that cellular staining, genuine section borders, real folds/tears, focus changes, or scanner artifacts match physical histology. A side-by-side audit against provenance-controlled acquired TRAIN sections is required before large-scale synthetic exposure. No generator or model parameters were changed based on this six-image view.
