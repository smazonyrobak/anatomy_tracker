# 160: bounded mixed-real pose recovery from the 132 research checkpoint

The 154→158 synthetic-only pose has a large acquired-image domain failure, while 159 shows that its frozen atlas-fit energy does not reliably select a better blind branch. Do not scale that branch. The earlier scratch-trained 132 model retains substantially better weak-real alignment and v4 synthetic first-choice accuracy, though its own coronal no-harm gate failed. The narrow 160 question is whether continued pose training with **both** acquired TRAIN images and full-box peripheral synthetic planes improves direct candidate quality without further real transfer loss or breaking its already trained local map. This is a foundation for a later genuinely fit-informed joint model, not a claim that direct pose alone completes the goal.

Freeze the 132 update-2,000 checkpoint and its exact AdamW moments as parent. Run 653 updates (one pass through all 653 distinct sagittal TRAIN images). Each update sees three independently drawn synthetic physical sections: one v7 virtual/acquisition-framed v3, one v7 virtual/acquisition-framed v4, and one 151 full-box-offset v4, plus one distinct coronal TRAIN donor/section and one distinct sagittal TRAIN section. Do not deliberately duplicate a physical section with another background or artifact treatment; record every accepted and rejected draw with seed, specimen/animal/experiment/section, base or virtual index, framing, artifact settings and source hashes. The synthetic atlas is the same Allen atlas, and virtual affine descendants are not independent biological animals. The two real sources have inherited **weak** Allen affines, not expert truth. Preserve all-angle plane normals, raw no-brush/exact-black/imperfect-brush inputs, finite thickness, and all artifacts from the current versioned source. The current v3 artifact code differs from the 132-frozen version; bind its new hash rather than mutating historical receipts.

Optimize only the shared image encoder/lateral and 132 direct probabilistic pose heads. Retain the 132 synthetic pose-mixture and 0.5-weight weak-real pose losses, model constructor, parameter-group order and initial optimizer moments, with a monotone learning-rate taper no higher than the parent's terminal rates. Freeze the fitter, local mapper, ranker, uncertainty and other heads. Save immutable step 0, 326 and 653 checkpoints and raw TRAIN curves. The output tree and all caches/temp files must be on `I:`. No prior trained external weights, final animals, public benchmark or confidence calibration may enter.

After process exit, an independent evaluator must compare the frozen checkpoints on the existing 150 fresh-plan synthetic DEV panel (all 256 recorded sections, reporting eligibility), the existing v3 DEV panel as retention, and the six coronal/eight sagittal weak-real DEV donors. Synthetic metrics are blind first-choice and truth-best top-16 rigid physical error over surviving tissue pixels, by plan, tissue fraction, appearance and angle; the truth-best is diagnostic only. Real five-point/normal disagreement is donor-equal and labelled weak reference. On supplied exact/near synthetic planes also check the frozen mapper with the changed shared features against step 0; a pose gain that breaks mapping is not a useful joint-model gain. The checkpoint is not promoted unless final step 653 improves plan-equal v4 blind first-choice error by at least 0.25 mm versus step 0, loses no more than five percentage points of v4 top-16 near-pose capture, worsens neither real family by more than 0.20 mm donor-equal or any donor by more than 0.50 mm, and worsens supplied-pose mapped error by no more than 0.10 mm. These are development criteria, not shippable accuracy thresholds. If the gate fails, do not extend the unchanged training recipe; use its targeted failure to choose the next change.

The next required architectural step after a passing foundation is anatomy-sensitive blind candidate selection and pose correction coupled to local deformation, with fit evidence that beats atlas-intensity-ablated and support-matched controls. Neither 159 nor earlier scalar fit scorers established that. Acquired physically oblique animal-level truth, calibrated electrode-site/region probabilities, GUI integration and a locked DeepSlice comparison remain later gates.
