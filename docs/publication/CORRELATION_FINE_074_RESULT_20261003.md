# 074 blind fine-grid readout: no useful capture gain

The frozen 059 direct-pose model and 073 correlation head (batch 2,500) were evaluated on 32 fixed, eligible 061 synthetic DEV sections: four distinct sections from each of eight synthetic deformation identities, with 11 raw, 11 exact-black and 10 imperfect-brush inputs. The same eight spatially distinct, blindly selected coarse planes per section were refined over normal offset (±250 µm in 125-µm steps), roll (±15° in 5° steps), isotropic scale (×0.8–1.2) and translation (±0.15 chart units in 0.025 steps). Synthetic truth was used only to measure physical error, never to choose a candidate. The zero-change fine score exactly matched the coarse score in the parity check.

Identity-equal rigid error was **3.572 mm selected / 2.096 mm truth-best-of-eight** before refinement, and **3.422 / 2.086 mm** after refinement. The matched 059 prior selected 3.372 mm on this deliberately balanced but small subset. Thus the local search added only **0.010 mm** to available best-eight accuracy and did not beat the direct prior's selected result. Neither of the 256 refined descendants selected a translation outside the original coarse ±0.8125 chart grid. The subset's prior error is higher than the full 246-section panel's 2.656 mm; these figures must not be presented as full-panel estimates.

**Decision:** do not scale this 073/074 correlation search or treat the truth-assisted 073 lattice witness as model performance. Prioritize genuinely better direct pose capture and anatomically informed training on the model's own proposals; simple local grid refinement does not close the gap.
