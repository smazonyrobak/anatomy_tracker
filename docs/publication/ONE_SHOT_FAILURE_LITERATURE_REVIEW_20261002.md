# One-shot arbitrary-plane model: failure analysis and literature-led decision (2 October 2026)

## What has actually failed

The frozen 010 synthetic-development panel has 185 scorable sections from eight *synthetic deformation plans of one atlas*, not eight biological animals. Its identity-equal visible-tissue error is 2.903 mm after local mapping for the image-prior choice and 2.998 mm for the fitted-score choice. At the same checkpoint, the reference-best rigid choice among the prior's top eight is 1.285 mm; among all 32 pose/reflection branches it is 1.072 mm. Thus **selection regret and proposal coverage are separate, substantial bottlenecks**. These are physical 3D coordinate errors on visible synthetic tissue, not an AP-only metric or a mm/µm conversion mistake. At the known correct pose, rigid error is 0.139 mm. The 011 continuation improved the known-pose learned mapper from 0.186 to 0.124 mm, yet its deployed fitted choice remained 2.921 mm versus a 2.916 mm image-prior choice. More deformation capacity cannot plausibly rescue a missing global pose candidate.

On individual 010 cases, the fitted error is 5.24 mm when tissue occupies <5% of the canvas (24 cases), 3.17 mm at 5–15% (65), 2.49 mm at 15–30% (85), and 1.62 mm at ≥30% (11). These support groups are observational, not matched manipulations, but severe partialness concentrates the catastrophic tail. A known synthetic valid-tissue mask did **not** make the old atlas-correlation selector useful: the frozen 009 diagnostic still selected 3.052 mm versus its 2.938 mm image prior and 1.287 mm top-eight oracle. Missing-tissue handling matters, but a mask alone is not the answer. The 010 fitted ranker was worse than its prior within raw, exact-black, and imperfect-brush appearance strata; those strata also differ in section geometry and cannot establish causal effects of backgrounds.

Real development evidence is weak: 010 moved from 0.571 to 0.619 mm against inherited Allen five-point affines on 64 images from six held-out donors. Those affines are not blinded expert truth and the material is mostly near-coronal. They neither verify arbitrary oblique accuracy nor support biological regional probabilities. The original 010 training monitor's apparent ~9.8 mm real error was a row-indexing bug in its *reporting only*; the frozen independent evaluation is the meaningful result. The later 012 positive-fit continuation and its independent matched readout are documented separately in `ONE_SHOT_POSITIVE_FIT_012_RESULT_20261002.md`.

## Relevant primary literature and transfer limits

| Evidence | Useful lesson | Limit here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [DeepSlice, Carey et al. 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-41645-4) | A direct image-to-plane CNN trained with large real and synthetic atlas sections; shared angle and section order improve coronal predictions. | Trained/recommended for whole coronal sections; not proof for isolated partial arbitrary planes. Its 0.9M synthetic images are a scale reference, not a required exposure count. |
| [Xiong et al. 2018](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroinformatics/articles/10.3389/fninf.2018.00093/full) and [giRAff, Piluso et al. 2023](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2023.1230814/full) | Search and compare atlas planes before nonrigid fit; robust structural / independently scored registration can localize partial mouse sections. giRAff reports 120/164 µm mean AP disagreement for 2,135 half-sections/636 whole sections against experts. | giRAff deliberately neglected <5° tilts, so its AP-only number cannot be compared directly to unrestricted 3D tissue error. |
| [Partial mouse atlas retrieval, Antanavicius et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-11203-4_18) | Preserve multiple plausible atlas candidates and measure top-*k* recall. | Its top-five result involved only 12 held-out coronal images; not an arbitrary-plane benchmark. |
| [TIRL, Huszar et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10933796/) and [histology–µCT registration, Chen et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12271352/) | Multistart/coarse-to-fine global pose search precedes increasingly flexible in-plane and through-plane correction; cross-modal local features can refine pose. | Different specimen/imaging modalities; use as design precedent, not a numerical target. |
| [Probabilistic histological atlas, Casamitjana et al. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09708-2) | Joint affine/nonlinear registration with contrastive cross-modal features improved manually marked 3D landmark error on five human hemispheres; its robust Bayesian refinement handles nonparallel blocks and outliers. | A much richer multi-section human-MRI reconstruction with 250 landmark pairs, not evidence that a single mouse slice can achieve the same accuracy. |
| [MIND, Heinrich et al. 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22722056/) | Local self-similarity can be more stable than raw intensity across modalities and contrast changes. | A MIND/HOG-like scorer needs a matched ablation on our candidate pool; superiority here is not established. |
| [Tward et al. 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7027169/) and [Wang et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12157374/) | Missing tissue and flexible nonlinear fitting can cause attractive-looking but geometrically false registrations. | Supports validity-aware scoring and deformation bounds, not a claim that the current mask is the sole failure. |
| [Rohlfing 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21827972/) | Whole-image similarity and tissue overlap can rate anatomically incorrect registrations highly; localized anatomical labels better distinguish them. | Directly cautions against promoting our fitted image score as an accuracy surrogate without held-out physical landmarks. |
| [Registration by Regression, Gopinath et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12380105/) | Predict dense atlas coordinates, then fit global/nonlinear transformations from spatial correspondences; the method also predicts a tissue mask. | Demonstrated on 3D MRI, not isolated arbitrary-plane histology. A 2D coordinate head is a hypothesis to test on our fixed panel. |
| [Hou et al., MICCAI 2017](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/predicting-slice-to-volume-transformation-in-presence-of-arbitrar) | Direct slice-to-volume CNN regression can supply an initialization over arbitrary orientations when conventional intensity registration has limited capture range. | Its roughly 7 mm simulated fetal-MRI error is on a different scale and task; it does not establish accuracy for mouse histology and cautions against expecting one-pass regression alone to solve ambiguous fragments. |
| [GridNet, 2021](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/37/22/4216/6299384) | Combining local histology features with wider spatial context improves regional assignment over local-only or coarse-only approaches. | Regional classification is not a full 3D plane estimate; it motivates retaining both spatial and global image evidence, not a proven architecture here. |

The direct architectural implication is **pose proposals → atlas comparison → constrained tissue map → fit-informed pose revision**, all trainable within the same model, while maintaining direct physical-pose supervision. A low fit energy is not a pose label: the deformation must not be free to hide a wrong atlas plane. Serial-section angle/order priors should be used only when provenance shows that sections share a block. Uncertainty scores remain uncalibrated; apparent score gaps cannot yet be called probabilities.

## Targeted decision after the frozen 012 readout

1. Compare every saved 012 checkpoint on the same fixed synthetic DEV identities and real DEV donors. Report prior/fitted mapped error, best-of-eight/all-32 rigid error, true-pose mapped error, normal error, >5 mm tail, and performance by visible-tissue fraction. Select a checkpoint by the physical end-to-end error and donor behaviour, not lower training loss or a nonzero fit-to-pose gradient.
2. If top-eight/all-32 coverage remains around a millimetre, prioritize atlas-conditioned **global pose proposal/refinement** before another free-form warp or score-only reranker. Include both reflections, explicit finite-thickness rendering, physically bounded pose updates, and a geometry-supervised target on actual predicted candidates. The old frozen-feature top-four updater failed; repeating it unchanged is not justified.
3. On an identical proposal set, test whether observed/learned tissue-validity weighting plus a coarse anatomical feature comparison ranks physically good poses better than the present fitted score. The earlier oracle-mask correlation failure makes this an experiment, not an assumed cure. Keep local warp bounded and compare rigid versus fitted ranking to expose false-fitting.
4. Preserve the untouched animal-level final test and public DeepSlice benchmark for a *later* qualification gate. The synthetic plans and weak Allen affines are useful development diagnostics only. Numerical pose, trajectory, or region probabilities require separate calibration on unseen animals, with point accuracy not traded away for apparent coverage.

The current GUI's experimental one-shot path does not yet use surgical/trajectory constraints or provide calibrated electrode-region probabilities, and the desktop bundle does not include this model. It must not replace the existing model until both scientific and packaging gates are satisfied.

## Updated decision after the 012 local-direction and coverage probes

The local fitted score improves a 19-way pose choice by only 0.025 mm, while even a 1,120-way *oracle* proposal expansion leaves 0.778 mm error. A learned 16×16 CCF coordinate field, regressed from the shared encoder with global context and explicit image position, is therefore a targeted alternative to repeating the same local fit-score loop. For synthetic images, supervise the global rigid-plane coordinate field at every image cell (more strongly on visible tissue), fit a full 3D plane analytically, and keep the existing atlas-conditioned local mapper distinct. Train the head with the same randomly initialized checkpoint lineage and independent fresh synthetic draws; keep direct multimodal pose supervision and real TRAIN adaptation. On fixed DEV cases, compare its resulting rigid and mapped errors against the current direct prior and fitted selector, including partial-tissue groups and all six weak-real donors. Also check that updating the shared encoder has not damaged the true-pose mapper. Reject the addition if it fails those gates. This is a project-specific hypothesis inspired by the coordinate-regression literature, not an established histology result.
