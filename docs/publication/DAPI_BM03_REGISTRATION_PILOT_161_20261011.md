# BM03-to-DAPI registration pilot (161, 2026-10-11)

The exploratory, same-modality low-resolution registration in `training/register_dapi_bm03_pilot_161.py` used the source-frozen BM03 native volume and DAPI population average. It did **not** create CCF labels or training examples. Source SHA-256 values, coordinate assumptions, optimization settings, transform matrices, and results are recorded in `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/dapi_bm03_registration_pilot_161/report.json`; the pull transform and before/after orthogonal overlays are in the same directory.

The geometry-centre initialization, rigid fit, and selected affine fit had fixed-grid tissue-mask Dice 0.913, 0.952, and 0.976, respectively. Within-fixed-tissue intensity Pearson correlation rose from 0.140 to 0.456 to 0.565. The affine singular values were 1.072, 1.030, and 1.019. Visual overlays show substantially better gross alignment, while local differences remain, notably along some edges and internal boundaries. These metrics were measured on the images used to fit and select the transform, so they are not independent anatomical accuracy estimates.

The pilot sampled every 16th in-plane voxel without antialiasing and used approximately 0.138 × 0.100 × 0.138 mm voxels. BM03 has unknown header spatial units and an identity selected sform inconsistent with its acquisition spacing; the script explicitly assumes its pixdim is millimetres, positive native axes, and zero origin **in memory only**. The DAPI average uses its metric NIfTI affine. The saved transform maps DAPI-average fixed physical coordinates to BM03 moving physical coordinates for resampling; neither its inverse nor the published DAPI-to-CCF pull affine has been composed or anatomically validated here. SimpleITK physical-number agreement is not by itself proof of the RAS/LPS convention needed for that composition.

Before any BM volume is used for supervised labels: verify the physical-axis/left-right convention against source documentation and landmarks; perform higher-resolution, antialiased per-animal registration; inspect three-plane overlays and anatomical landmarks independently of the fitting metric; record transform version and uncertainty; and reject specimens or regions whose mapping is poor. Preserve `BM17` and `BM18` as sealed secondary animal holdout. This pilot establishes feasibility of gross alignment, not verified atlas correspondence or real oblique-section truth.
