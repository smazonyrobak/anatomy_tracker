The predeclared confirmation gate **fails**: sagittal gain was only two of 18, below the required four, although coronal loss was zero and the median angle fell by 5.74° (required ≥5°). Three sagittal donors changed from wrong to correct family and one changed in the opposite direction. The 277 independent verifier passed hashes of the frozen output, source, protocol, checkpoint, transfer inventory and acquired arrays; checked all 96 rows as 48 paired, distinct donors with their animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs; and recomputed the summary and gate. A separate read-only audit found no arithmetic, pairing, provenance or direct affine-input leakage error. The acquired affine was used solely after inference for this weak orientation readout.

The earlier 276 improvement from 9/22 to 17/22 was real on its development donors but not reliably reproduced at the predeclared effect size. Do not replace the default inference rule with family grouping or interpret its mass as calibrated probability. Even the grouped sagittal normal differs from the automated affine by a median 44.43° here; none of 18 is within 15° and only two are within 30°. Neither arm supplies an acceptable real sagittal pose, let alone an arbitrary physical oblique one. More unchanged v9 pose exposure is not supported by 275, and this zero-training shortcut does not repair that failure. The next experiment must address the anatomical evidence/domain mismatch and/or candidate-selection mechanism itself, with matched training and independent gates before any GUI promotion or DeepSlice comparison.
