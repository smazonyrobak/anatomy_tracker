# 244: fresh shared-anchor joint pose/warp training

This is a development experiment, not a qualified model or a public benchmark. Initialize **every learned parameter from the run seed**. Do not load the 226/240 or any other checkpoint, feature, pseudolabel or external pretrained model. The only atlas is the project's already bound Allen CCF image. Keep code, inputs, outputs and temporary files on I:.

## Reason for the change

The 017 and 058 lineages could map an exact plane to about 0.13 mm but could not select it blindly. In 058 the new anchor probability was taught by plane-normal similarity, even when the centre and scale were wrong. The 240/243 learned matcher also failed to distinguish nearby anatomy; its site-support score and source feature lattice were flawed. Another frozen-head ranker or longer exposure to the same objective is not justified.

## One model and data

Use a single image encoder, a shared image-plus-normal-anchor pose query for 64 antipodal normal anchors, 16 free-frame modes, one atlas-conditioned local-map head with predicted tissue reliability, one shared atlas-evidence pose updater, and a fitted-candidate scorer. Render the atlas through the specified finite-thickness PSF for each candidate. Source and atlas feature sites must use the same pixel-centre chart. The nonrigid field has its affine component projected out; penalize magnitude and valid-neighbour gradients, and supervise pose independently so a flexible warp cannot conceal a wrong plane. Inference must take only an image, atlas, PSF and optional evidence, never synthetic truth.

Draw independently randomized full-sphere physical planes and independently randomized appearances/defects from the TRAIN-only v7/64-base generator. A single draw is one section, not a paired background variant. Mix donor-uniform acquired Allen TRAIN images (weak affine labels, mostly coronal) as an appearance/pose-retention guard; their weak labels must not supervise fine deformation or certify true accuracy. Persist all animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs and source bindings. The 64 synthetic bases are variations of one atlas, **not** 64 biological animals.

## Training and decision

Train the whole model from random initialization. First establish full-frame proposal and true-plane map supervision within this same checkpoint; retain both losses when adding blind top-K candidate renders. For feedback, mix actual blind top-K proposals with independently perturbed true-plane candidates, then render, predict a bounded pose residual, re-render, predict the local map and score candidates. This is a training curriculum, not a separate model or test-time oracle. Probability mass and fitted ranking must be supervised by full physical surviving-tissue location error (centre, angle, in-plane scale and reflection together), never by normal similarity alone. The scored objective also penalizes required local deformation, but physical rigid error remains a separate term. No truth mask is allowed at inference; train a source-side reliability head and use support-preserving coverage in ranking.

Use an unchanged synthetic-development panel with separate synthetic deformation bases, plus donor-separated acquired weak-label development images. At saved milestones inspect blind selected and best-of-K rigid and mapped errors, exact-pose mapping, real weak-label retention, orientation/visibility strata, and intact-versus-atlas-null discrimination. Require a meaningful fall in blind physical error and no real regression before more exposure; a good teacher-guided train loss or gradient alone is not a pass. Retain the best checkpoint by a predeclared combined development score, not the last update. If candidate fit has no atlas-specific ranking advantage, stop this objective rather than continuing it longer. Do not use held-out animal calibration/final sets or the public DeepSlice benchmark here.

The all-angle interface remains required, but any claim of physically acquired heavy-oblique accuracy needs independent animal/pose truth that this synthetic/weak-affine training line does not supply. Numerical electrode-region probabilities require later animal-level calibration. Approximate slice range, orientation, insertion site/angle and smart-brush information remain optional evidence, not mandatory labels; this run does not yet qualify them.
