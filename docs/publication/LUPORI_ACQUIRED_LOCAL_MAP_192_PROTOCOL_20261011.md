# 192: gauge-corrected acquired deformation readout before training the map

The 189–191 atlas scoring experiments did not produce a blind fit selector. Before changing the joint architecture, measure whether the frozen 188 model's inherited **local deformation** describes real Lupori tissue variation at all. Use the same 64 TRAIN section identities as 189, 16 per animal; no DEV/CAL/FINAL, public benchmark or weight updates. The human VisuAlign fields are weak registration labels, not biological ground truth.

For each section, use the exact 188 full-thumbnail fluorescence input, insert its human QuickNII plane as a diagnostic supplied pose, and run the existing atlas-conditioned mapper at 96² with a normalized five-sample ±50-µm axial PSF. Read the matching VisuAlign `dispFieldX/Y.csv` and original-size tissue mask. The CSV displacement is in original thumbnail pixels, with the marker-checked sign **reference image coordinate minus observed image coordinate**; sample it at the model's 96² full-frame pixel centres. Convert it through the section's physical O/U/V edges to a CCF displacement and then into the supplied plane's local frame.

The mapper removes every full-canvas affine component from its local field. Apply the **same unweighted full-canvas affine projection** to the human field before comparison; do not use a tissue-weighted projection, which would change the model's pose/deformation gauge. The tissue mask is used only to report error after prediction, never as a model input or to choose the affine gauge. On tissue pixels, report vector RMS in mm for (a) a zero local field and (b) the inherited model field against the human affine-free target, plus target and predicted displacement magnitudes and source QC. The model's optional topology limiter is off, matching the frozen 188 checkpoint.

The inherited mapper passes the screening gate only if its equal-animal mean RMS is at least 10% below the zero-field baseline and it improves at least three of four animals. If it fails, do not treat deformation effort from that map as credible pose feedback; real-field supervision with a constrained pose gauge must precede such a feedback experiment. This diagnostic cannot by itself certify tear-fragment mapping, arbitrary physical angles or electrode-site probabilities. Freeze source, checkpoint and prior-selection receipts and audit raw rows after exit.
