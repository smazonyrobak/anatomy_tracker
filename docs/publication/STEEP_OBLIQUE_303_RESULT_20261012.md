# Candidate 303 — actual-generator steep-oblique examples

The frozen [protocol](STEEP_OBLIQUE_303_PROTOCOL_20261012.md) accepted one independently seeded v9 section from each of six synthetic TRAIN base animals (48–53), conditional on an eligible section and post-artifact fitted-plane obliquity ≥45° from the nearest cardinal axis. It took 61 draws. Every draw and rejection was recorded; no appearance or model prediction was used to select a case. The [six-row image grid](figures/steep_oblique_303_examples.png) shows the true fitted atlas plane, actual generator output and frozen 275 first-choice atlas render at the original [0,1] intensity scale.

Post-artifact fitted obliquities were 45.1°–50.8°. Five underlying virtual cutting normals were themselves ≥45°; the sixth was 40.5° and reached 45.1° after deformation and the post-artifact affine fit. Thus the grid is accurately described as six steep *fitted* sections, not six original cuts ≥45°. None offered a smart brush; four exteriors were near-black and two gray. Several observations are intentionally very dim, with v4 exposure draws of 0.110–0.880. Earlier [TRAIN-only appearance comparison](APPEARANCE_GAP_129_RESULT_20261010.md) motivated a low-exposure branch for one acquired sagittal-like cohort; that does not prove these particular steep-oblique appearances match real acquisitions.

The frozen proposer's six rigid-site errors were 0.648, 4.168, 1.515, 2.153, 1.038 and 0.455 mm (mean 1.663 mm). They are descriptive selected-synthetic-case errors, not a benchmark. In particular the 4.168-mm miss is visibly consequential; this model is not yet accurate enough to ship. The grid confirms that the generator can emit highly oblique planes and varied backgrounds/damage, but does not validate physical oblique histology, probabilities or acquired-domain transfer.

An independent audit rechecked protocol/source/checkpoint/output/figure SHA-256 digests, all 61 attempts, six distinct TRAIN identities and the reported angles/errors. The runner exited successfully; `completed.json` SHA-256 is `3758294e56afe4d7432f787ae28bb9f492b810892521d818e2d1762c172328c2`. No DEV/CAL/FINAL animals, public benchmark, external trained weights/features/pseudolabels, model weights or GUI were changed. Continue full-sphere development; no cardinal-plane restriction is justified by this six-case grid.
