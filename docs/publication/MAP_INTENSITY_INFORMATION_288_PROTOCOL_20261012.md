# 288: is local synthetic warp observable from atlas intensity? (frozen before readout)

After 287, the atlas-conditioned map gains only 0.005 mm on fresh TRAIN-inner sections while a 0.134-mm exact-pose residual remains. Test the narrow information question **without training**: when the exact post-artifact plane is supplied, does the acquired-like synthetic image prefer atlas intensity rendered at its actual surviving-tissue coordinates over the unwarped plane and over scrambled displacements? This does not test whether a network can learn the mapping, whether a plane can be found blindly, or whether real histology has the same signal.

Replay the exact 64 frozen 287 TRAIN-inner images and 512 deterministic surviving sites/case, verifying image, target, physical ID and post-artifact pose hashes. At each site, render the pinned 25-µm Allen atlas with that case's nine-node PSF at (a) the known synthetic coordinate, (b) the robust post-artifact rigid plane, and (c) 16 rigid-plane-plus-spatially-permuted **true** displacement vectors. Keep the same source pixel values and intersection of atlas-supported sites (support >0.9 for every candidate, ≥64 sites) for all 18 comparisons. Score absolute Pearson correlation, permitting the generator's polarity inversion; do not use silhouette/support as the score. The permutations retain the displacement magnitudes and approximate location distribution but break spatial correspondence. Use fixed RNG seed 20261012288 plus case index. Record correlations, target–rigid physical shift, source/exterior/brush/artifact strata, site counts and exact source/output hashes.

Predeclared descriptive signal gate: at least 48/64 cases and six of eight deformation-base means prefer true over rigid by a positive correlation margin; mean true-minus-rigid absolute-correlation margin ≥0.010; and at least 38/64 true correlations exceed their own 95th percentile of scrambled-field correlations. If support/variance exclusion leaves fewer than 48 cases, call the gate inconclusive. A failure of this **particular global intensity statistic** is not proof of absent anatomical information; it points to local/contrast-invariant matching or generator-consistency analysis before another large map training run. A pass justifies a spatially resolved atlas-pair representation and pose-preserving joint loss, not GUI promotion or more unchanged 287 training.

This uses TRAIN-inner synthetic sections only, with true coordinates supplied for diagnosis. No acquired DEV/CAL/FINAL animal, external model, numerical uncertainty claim, or public DeepSlice benchmark is involved.
