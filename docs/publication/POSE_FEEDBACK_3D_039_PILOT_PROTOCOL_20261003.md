# 039 image-to-3D-atlas pilot — before development evaluation

The 036/037 local-render pose-correction gates failed. The frozen 038 geometric diagnosis on new synthetic development identities found only 62% of top-ranked candidate tissue coordinates within ±2 mm normal **and** tangent, but 93% within ±6 mm on both axes. This motivates one targeted, coarse 3D-context pilot; coverage alone does not establish matchability.

Freeze the scratch-lineage 019 direct predictor. On 1,000 independently sampled, accepted arbitrary-plane synthetic TRAIN sections (500 batches of two), train a new cross-modal image/atlas descriptor from random initialization. For each section use the prior's actual top four reflection/pose branches, two independently perturbed known poses and one exact known pose used **only in training**. Render 13 finite-thickness atlas slabs at 1 mm spacing over ±6 mm around each candidate, with a 24×24 in-plane grid spanning twice its field of view. Match 16×16 observed-image tokens to 3D atlas keys. Supervise in-range token correspondences from the generator's dense physical truth, visible tissue reliability, and the physical error of a robust weighted least-squares full-frame fit. Retain a learned candidate score but report best-of-four/eight separately so ranking does not masquerade as pose correction. Training image appearances are sampled once per fresh physical section (raw, exact black, or imperfect brush); no deliberate appearance-paired duplicates, external weights, or real image labels.

After the frozen pilot exits, evaluate checkpoint 0/250/500 on **all actual top eight** candidates of the already-frozen eight-identity, 177-eligible-section synthetic development panel and all 64 weak-reference real development images from six donors. Report equal-identity and equal-donor physical errors, top-one movement, best-eight, selected, correspondence in-range and match accuracy, appearance strata, and weak-real donor regressions. A continuation beyond 500 batches is justified only by convincing development movement, particularly on near-true candidates. A meaningful development promotion still requires at least 0.25 mm improvement in selected synthetic error and 0.20 mm in best-eight over the parent, without more than 0.20 mm worst-donor weak-real selected regression. Real labels are inherited Allen affine points, not expert arbitrary-plane truth; this pilot cannot qualify probability calibration, GUI replacement, or DeepSlice benchmarking.
