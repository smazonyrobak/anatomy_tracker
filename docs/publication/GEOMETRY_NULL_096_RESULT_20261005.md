# 096: the apparent match signal is mostly plane geometry

The prespecified read-only geometry null **outperformed the learned match coordinates** on the same frozen 094 candidates. This corrects an important interpretation of 093–095: their truth-scored “actual correct-match fraction” ranked good atlas planes, but that ordering did not establish useful *observable correspondence evidence*. Most of it was already present when every fine-grid query was left at the candidate's unshifted fitted-plane coordinate.

The diagnostic loaded the 094 step-20,000 model and the exact 182 complementary 061 synthetic DEV sections, with the unchanged fixed 086 14-branch beam (2,548 candidate rows). It reproduced each 094 candidate's saved actual-match fraction to less than 0.0001, the saved atlas-support fraction to less than 0.00001, and the frozen 094 plan-equal actual-fraction/error correlation exactly. It used the same 32×32 visible-site denominator, atlas-available condition and 1.5-mm correctness tolerance for learned versus unshifted coordinates. The numeric source image tensors were processed but **no image was displayed or saved**. These are synthetic development sections, not independent animals.

The learned-minus-unshifted fraction averaged −0.072 across candidates (mean absolute difference 0.128); its within-section association with lower placement error was **negative** (ρ=−0.489). Across the eight plans, the learned fraction beat the null in rank association for only three, one by just 0.001 and none by more than 0.035. The 094 result's ρ≈0.802 was an unweighted mean over all 182 sections; the matched plan-equal version here and in its frozen evaluator is 0.791. Neither is an inference-time quality measure because both use synthetic truth.

**Decision:** stop training another head to estimate the current match fraction. For already-good poses, the present local matcher often moves coordinates *away* from truth, and its oracle fraction is dominated by the pose itself. The 094–095 head failures cannot be explained just by the balanced-BCE objective or lack of whole-slice context. A next targeted intervention must improve the image–atlas descriptor and its ability to reject anatomically wrong matches; use known synthetic CCF correspondences as positive sites and physically separated, atlas-support-matched negatives, then measure whether matched coordinates improve over the unshifted plane on frozen candidates before allowing match feedback to drive pose or warping. The negative result does not prove that every individual match is wrong, nor that a better descriptor is impossible. It does show that the current match field is not adequate evidence for the planned joint feedback.

This direction is consistent with [Mok et al.'s anatomy-aware contrastive cross-modal descriptors](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Mok_Modality-Agnostic_Structural_Image_Representation_Learning_for_Deformable_Multi-Modality_Medical_Image_CVPR_2024_paper.html) and [SLIV-Reg's robust 2-D/3-D feature matching](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18683), but their tasks and data differ from this arbitrary-plane histology problem. No DeepSlice benchmark, final-animal assessment, probability calibration or GUI promotion follows.
