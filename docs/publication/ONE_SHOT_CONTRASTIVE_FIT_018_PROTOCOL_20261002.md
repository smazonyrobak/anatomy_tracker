017 proved that fitted-loss gradient reaches the global pose head but failed its held-out accuracy gate. On its frozen 185-case panel, the eight proposed locations include a 1.19 mm average physical best; the learned fitted score chooses 2.80 mm, with only 0.156 mean within-case score/error correlation. The weak **cross-image anatomical discrimination** is now the measured target. A further identical rerender pass is not justified.

[CoMIR](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2020/hash/d6428eecbe0f7dff83fc607c5044b2b9-Abstract.html) and [anatomy-aware structural representation learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Mok_Modality-Agnostic_Structural_Image_Representation_Learning_for_Deformable_Multi-Modality_Medical_Image_CVPR_2024_paper.html) support contrastive training of dense corresponding image features across modalities; neither proves this specific mouse-histology model will improve. The paired synthetic data here provide stronger point correspondence than those general settings. DeepSlice's [published training scale](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-41645-4) also cautions against declaring saturation after tens of thousands of distinct synthetic presentations.

Continue the same standalone 017 batch-14,000 whole checkpoint, not another pretrained or separate registration model. Preserve the probabilistic 16-mode pose head, finite-thickness atlas renderer, local fitter, fitted scorer, and explicit second-render fitting gradient to global pose. For each fresh synthetic training section, render atlas intensity at known surviving tissue-to-CCF coordinates, encode it with the **existing** atlas encoder, and contrast corresponding foreground image features against spatially distinct/cross-section features from the **existing** image encoder. Use bidirectional InfoNCE on 16 sampled tissue locations per synthetic section. This loss teaches the shared feature maps to recognize anatomical correspondence despite synthetic staining, background and damage; it is auxiliary to the same joint fitting/pose objective. No exact-pose data enter inference.

For the measured ranking deficit, double the predicted-branch physical ranking term and reduce the exact-pose-positive ranking term to one quarter of its 017 weight, so training cannot rely mainly on spotting the unusually easy teacher branch. Retain direct spatial supervision, weak real Allen supervision, local tissue mapping, deformation plausibility and fitted-score-to-pose feedback. Run 8,000 batches with two independently sampled arbitrary-plane synthetic sections and one unused real TRAIN section each, keeping all data and caches on `I:`. This is an evidence check within the deployment-target lineage; increase exposure only if held-out performance warrants it.

After process exit, assess the same 185 eligible synthetic development cases and 64 real development sections at all saved checkpoints. The main decision is whether selected physical mapping and per-branch ranking improve relative to 017's 2.784 mm best refined selection and 1.19 mm best-eight opportunity, without >0.2 mm weak-real degradation. A ≥0.25 mm improvement is a meaningful development target, not a substitute for final animal-level validation. Do not touch the public DeepSlice benchmark, calibration animals or final-test animals.
