# 307: test whether a second pose per plane-normal anchor recovers missing proposals

This is a short **TRAIN-only mechanism test**, not a new deployable model. The frozen 305 parent has 16 free poses plus one full pose for each of 64 antipodal normal anchors, with both image reflections. A one-pass read-only decomposition of the already frozen v9/v10 panels found 58/64 and 51/64 cases, respectively, with an all-160-branch rigid-site error ≤1.5 mm. All 19 misses still had some predicted plane normal within 15° of the true antipodal normal. Thus fixed normal tiling is not the immediate absence: the model fails to combine normal with the right position, roll and field of view on these cases. This observation motivates—but does not prove—a second independent *full pose* per normal anchor. Multiple-hypothesis prediction has precedent in [Rupprecht et al., ICCV 2017](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_iccv_2017/html/Rupprecht_Learning_in_an_ICCV_2017_paper.html). Its value here must be earned experimentally.

Freeze the image encoder, local mapper, 16 free pose modes, and all atlas-fit/refinement modules. Start both arms from the exact 305 acquired-rehearsal parent and its frozen normal frames. Control continues the original one-slot anchor head. Intervention duplicates that head into two independently trainable full-pose slots per anchor, preserving the original slot exactly and giving the second slot a small fixed-seed center/roll perturbation; divide their initial probability mass equally, so the new arm does not gain probability merely by having more outputs. No external weights, pseudolabels, or reuse of the same synthetic plane with altered artifacts/backgrounds. Draw each training section independently from the v9/v10 generators; the two arms receive the same number of presentations and deformation-base schedule but independent physical draws. Both use the same nearest-four-normal, best-slot geometric supervision and optimizer schedule. Do not train an atlas-fit selector or change the generator in this test.

Before gradients, freeze two new identity-disjoint 96-case TRAIN-inner panels using bases 56–63: for each generator version, 64 unconditioned draws (eight per base), then 16 accepted cases with <15% visible tissue and 16 with ≥35%. All draws, rejects, seeds, animal-base lineage, images, physical section IDs and panel hashes are recorded. The panel is reused only for checkpoints 0, 400 and 800; it is not an untouched DEV/CAL/FINAL set. Gradient cases use bases 0–55 and exclude all panel IDs. Train for at most 800 two-image batches per arm, one v9 and one v10, retaining checkpoints 0/400/800. Compare both arms on identical frozen panels. Read out all-branch ≤1.5-mm capture, first-choice and top-16 rigid-site error, slot use/separation, visibility and obliquity. The v9/v10 panels are different physical sections, so compare *arms within a panel*, not v9 against v10.

Predeclared mechanism gate at batch 800: on the 64-case unconditioned v10 panel, the two-slot arm must gain ≥6 all-branch captures over the one-slot control **and** ≥4 over its own batch-0 initialization; on the v9 unconditioned panel it may lose no more than two captures versus control. On each 16-case v10 visibility tail it must gain at least one capture versus control, so the overall result cannot be driven solely by fuller images. Its v9 and v10 unconditioned first-choice mean error must not exceed control by >0.20 mm. If any clause fails, do not promote or extend this two-slot recipe unchanged. These thresholds are mechanistic filters, not an accuracy claim. All 160-branch control and 288-branch intervention oracles use synthetic truth only *after* blind inference; neither is available to a user.

Even a pass authorizes only the next bounded step: train/assess blind ranking and joint atlas-fit feedback with acquired-animal regression guards, then independent physical steep-oblique animal validation and probability calibration. A pass does not justify a DeepSlice benchmark, GUI replacement, or claim of reliable electrode-region probabilities. A fail redirects attention to image-conditioned placement evidence or the data/label domain gap, not another long unchanged continuation.
