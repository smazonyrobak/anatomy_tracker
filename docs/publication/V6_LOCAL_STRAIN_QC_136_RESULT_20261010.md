# Exploratory localized tissue strain, not yet a training source

The v6 generator adds one independently sampled, Gaussian-localized stretch or compression field **before** the existing v3 tear/fold/bubble/tile artifacts and v4 exposure. Each invocation still draws a fresh arbitrary atlas-intersecting plane; it does not manufacture paired background variants. The observed-pixel-to-CCF target follows the deformation, and the affine pose gauge remains the pre-artifact source gauge.

A bounded TRAIN-only QC used 64 eligible v4 and 64 eligible v6 sections from separate, unpaired random streams (152 distinct attempts). The mean valid-tissue distance between the exact CCF target and its rigid gauge was **0.142 mm** in v4 and **0.224 mm** in v6; the 90th percentile of section means was **0.256 vs 0.397 mm**. Two v6 sections and zero v4 sections had mean displacement above 0.5 mm. Local strain was active in 56/64 v6 sections. In a read-only analytic Jacobian calculation over those 64 fields, the minimum determinant was 0.127; none folded, although five had a minimum below 0.2. These are *unpaired descriptive* comparisons, not an estimated treatment effect or a calibrated physical deformation distribution.

The [actual-generator atlas/input grid](I:/AnatomyTracker/runs/v6_local_strain_qc_136/atlas_input_grid.png) shows three different v6 physical planes and modes. The sections retain conspicuously synthetic atlas-derived morphology and stylized artifacts. The broader warp tail is useful coverage for development, but **do not promote v6 into large-scale training on this QC alone**. We still need real-slide deformation/landmark evidence and independent real-donor guardrails; neither this visual nor a larger deformation target addresses the current 3-mm blind pose-selection error. Physical steep-oblique validation, calibration, GUI qualification and DeepSlice comparison remain undone.
