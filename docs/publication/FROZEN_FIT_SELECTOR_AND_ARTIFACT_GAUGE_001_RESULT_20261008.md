# Fresh TRAIN-only fit-selector and artifact-gauge assay

The frozen batch-653 model's inherited atlas-fit selector **does contain useful ranking information**, but does not yet select reliably enough to promote. On 64 independent eligible TRAIN synthetic sections drawn from 67 attempts, the image-prior top-one mapped visible tissue with **2.510 mm** mean error; the same model's fitted top-eight selector gave **2.149 mm**. The physically best of these eight branches was 0.961 mm before and 0.952 mm after its tiny refiner update. The refiner applied to the *unchanged prior branch* changed 2.510→2.507 mm, and evaluating the fitted-selected branch *before* refinement gave 2.152 mm. The 0.361-mm gain is thus almost entirely a different branch choice, not improved pose or warp fitting. The selector changed branch in 37/64 and improved 41/64 sections.

This benefit was uneven. Raw images improved 2.454→1.927 mm (22 cases) and imperfect-brush images 2.536→1.689 mm (17), but **exact-black images worsened 2.541→2.657 mm** (25), despite 13/25 individually improving. By nearest normal axis, AP improved 2.318→1.624 mm, DV 2.746→2.292 mm, and ML only 2.483→2.460 mm. These are small descriptive TRAIN strata, not a pass on real or independent development sections. A predeclared frozen development readout must require no material appearance or cut-family regression before the fitted score is allowed to inform pose prediction.

The old artifact-layer affine refit does *not* appear to be the leading source of millimetric pose error in this sample: the source physical state versus post-artifact label differed by 0.066 mm at five points on average and 0.0012° in normal. Tear-present sections averaged 0.096 mm versus 0.053 mm without a tear, but events co-occur and this is not a causal artifact estimate. The assay says nothing about whether event rates or visual effects resemble acquired histology. The latest 653-batch continuation itself used the base synthetic stream, not this artifact layer; v2 remains outside training pending acquired-slide calibration.
