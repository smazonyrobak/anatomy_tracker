# 073: normal-conditioned whole-plane correlation — development protocol

072 could alter candidate poses but did not improve blind selected physical accuracy. The accurate known-pose tissue map and the 066b normal-fixed similarity oracle (~0.338 mm on the 246-case panel) leave a specific gap: the model has plausible plane normals yet fails to recover the **coupled offset, in-plane position, roll and scale**. Local single-render correction, fixed intensity/MIND search, patch-key voting, score-only training, and truth-injected sparse candidate recognition have already failed. Do not repeat them under a new name.

Add a learned whole-plane retrieval head to the existing randomly initialized joint-model lineage. For each of the model's actual antipodal normal proposals, render a broad finite-thickness atlas plane at a small signed-offset lattice. Encode the observed image and atlas plane into spatial feature maps trained together from scratch. Compare the *entire visible spatial pattern* over roll and scale hypotheses, using correlation to score all in-plane translations coherently rather than voting from isolated patches or feeding one wrong render to a residual updater. Retain several distinct peaks as **complete physical plane** candidates; pass them through the existing bounded tissue mapper. This is an atlas-conditioned proposal mechanism inside one model, not a separate pretrained registration product. The atlas image, raw/black/imperfect-brush variability, and optional brush-outline channel retain their existing input contract; neither a perfect foreground mask nor an automatic external segmentation is required at inference.

First calculate a truth-only, read-only geometric ceiling for the *discretized* search family on the frozen 246 eligible synthetic sections/eight development deformation identities, using the 059 candidate normals but no truth in inference. The initial lattice is normal offset −1.5…+1.5 mm at 0.5-mm coarse steps and ±0.25 mm at 0.125-mm fine steps; full-circle roll at 30° coarse steps and ±15° at 5° fine steps; relative scale coarse {0.65, 1.0, 1.5} with fine multiplier {0.8, 0.9, 1.0, 1.1, 1.2}; and in-plane x/y shift −0.8…+0.8 candidate-edge units at 0.025 steps (the translation search is a correlation grid, not explicit repeated renders). The ceiling asks whether at least one plane in this coarse-to-fine family can map visible tissue to within 0.75 mm identity-equal mean. If not, adjust lattice resolution/range **before** fitting a learned scorer and record the change. This is a feasibility gate, not a performance result.

In the first learning pilot, train only the new query/atlas feature and correlation-proposal path for approximately 2,000–3,000 batches on fresh, independent arbitrary-plane synthetic TRAIN sections from the 64 existing TRAIN deformation identities; retain exact section and source lineage. One section gets one independently sampled plane, warp, artifact and background realization—no deliberately paired appearance copies and no duplicate-exclusion trick. Search the same blind lattice used at inference. Supervise a soft physical-error distribution over its cells and mine high-scoring false peaks; **never insert the exact ground-truth plane as a candidate**. Use modest resolution and chunked atlas rendering/correlation to fit the 11-GB GPU, then refine only the retained peaks. Keep the existing mapper and prior available as matched controls. Real TRAIN weak affines may support low-weight domain adaptation only if donor-disjoint provenance is maintained; they are not expert truth.

Freeze a fixed training exposure and compare the new path with the unmodified 059 proposal/019 research references on exactly the same 246 synthetic DEV sections/eight identities and 64 weak-real DEV sections/six held-out donors. Evaluate best-eight **blind** full-plane physical error and selected visible-tissue CCF error, not merely contrast loss or a truth-injected top-one result. A credible advancement gate is best-eight ≤0.75 mm (from the ~1.02-mm 059 best-of-14) **and** selected ≤2.20 mm (from ~2.66 mm on this panel), with no real DEV donor worsening by >0.20 mm from its matched control and correct-pose mapper error ≤0.15 mm. Report raw/black/brush and low-support tails. If it only improves the oracle or fails real transfer, reject rather than scaling exposure. If it passes, continue **the same model** with selected-candidate re-rendering and joint bounded-map training, then test whether fitting feedback improves pose over a matched no-feedback arm. Only after robust animal-separated validation and calibration should it replace the GUI model or face a fair, untouched DeepSlice comparison.

The design draws on [single-slice 2D/3D rotation-equivariant feature matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18683) and [differentiable-rendering pose refinement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Gopalakrishnan_Intraoperative_2D3D_Image_Registration_via_Differentiable_X-ray_Rendering_CVPR_2024_paper.html). Their CT/MRI and X-ray results motivate architecture, not mouse-histology accuracy claims. Synthetic-development identities are not biological animals, and candidate scores remain uncalibrated.
