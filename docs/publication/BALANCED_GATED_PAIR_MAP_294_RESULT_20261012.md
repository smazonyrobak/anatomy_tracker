# 294: balanced multiscale matching and a move gate did not recover a useful map

The frozen 3,000-section TRAIN-inner exact-pose pilot **failed** its predeclared advance gate. Do not scale this matcher, use its fit score to revise pose, or expose it in the GUI. This is a mechanism result under the *true synthetic plane*, not blind localization or biological validation. The protocol and runner were committed and pushed before the run at `1834dff`.

The 294 whole-panel gain was only **0.000679 mm**, versus the required 0.030 mm; its fragment gain was 0.033175 mm, versus 0.070 mm required. It was **0.018004 mm worse in overall gain than frozen 289** on these same cases, where a ≥0.015-mm advantage was required. Six of eight base means were positive and intact tissue was not harmed at the selected readout, but those gains were tiny. The later batch-3,000 readout increased fragment gain to 0.049077 mm while making whole-panel gain negative (−0.000475 mm), so selecting more batches would not rescue the prespecified mechanism gate. The independent raw-row audit reproduced the selection and all gate arithmetic.

Balanced offset supervision did learn something, but not accurate fine correspondence. Panel mean three-scale offset cross-entropy fell from 4.5475 at batch zero to 4.3009 at selected batch 1,800. At the 192-pixel scale it was 3.8747 nats over 49 candidates, only slightly below the 3.8918-nat uniform reference; the 48-pixel scale improved more (5.1371→4.7002). The learned gate moved fragment sites more than intact sites (mean 0.0535 versus 0.0120), which limited the severe intact-tissue damage seen in 292, but also suppressed most useful fragment motion. This is evidence that the present local descriptor/cost-volume directions remain weak, not that a move gate or all-angle geometry is impossible.

The panel had only nine fragment-bearing sections, though their 985 sampled fragment sites exceeded the predeclared 100-site minimum. Training deliberately required a fragment-positive **new independent plane** on every second accepted batch: all 1,500 such batches met that rule, and 1,832/3,000 accepted batches contained fragments. This corrected the 292 imbalance without repeating planes or manufacturing background-paired copies. The 275 first-choice rigid-pose readout remained 2.347952 mm at every 294 readout; this map experiment did not train pose and makes no claim to solve that much larger blind-pose error.

The next targeted question is whether a **spatially coherent smooth deformation**, optimized from the observed slice and rendered atlas at the known plane, can use the image cue that the 288 true-coordinate intensity assay detected. The 281 coordinate oracle gained 0.137 mm with smooth 16×16 controls, while 294's fine local peak remains nearly uniform. A bounded image-only smooth-control fit, with an explicit comparison between privileged tissue-validity and inference-observable support, would distinguish an image-objective/masking failure from a local-descriptor/decoder failure before another large model run. This is consistent with smooth control-point and intensity-matching methods used in histological registration ([Casamitjana et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09708-2), [Osechinskiy and Kruggel](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3335496/)). It is a proposed diagnostic, not yet an established improvement or a deployment path.
