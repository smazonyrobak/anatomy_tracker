# 229: four real TRAIN brains align grossly, not at site-label precision

The frozen [protocol](DAPI_TRAIN_LANDMARK_GRID_229_PROTOCOL_20261011.md) ran to completion on BM03, BM04, BM06, and BM10 only. The DAPI population-average review grid was 331×70×236 voxels after antialiased 4×3×4 decimation. The same four coronal indices 13, 26, 38, and 51 (DAPI NIfTI world-axis values −2.40, −1.10, +0.10, +1.40 mm) were used for every specimen. [Overlay grid](I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/dapi_train_landmark_grid_229_001/train_anatomy_grid.png) and [separate grayscale grid](I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/dapi_train_landmark_grid_229_001/train_anatomy_grayscale.png) are saved on I:.

The overlays show the expected broad AP progression and generally aligned brain outlines across four genuinely distinct animals. They also show non-coincident internal boundaries, notably around ventricles and hippocampal/deep structures, and some specimen-dependent edge/shape differences. This is a qualitative review of **registered specimen versus population average**, not an independent CCF landmark measurement. The overlays do not resolve left/right anatomical sign, AP sign from native metadata, local electrode-site correspondence, or the accuracy of the published average-to-CCF transform. No specimen is promoted to CCF pose or deformation truth. The source volumes were reconstructed from physical coronal sections; an arbitrary-oblique reslice remains virtual.

The completion receipt records all four specimen source hashes, four transform hashes, both image hashes, script/protocol hashes, native shapes, the fixed AP positions, and the label-ineligible decision. An independent post-exit check matched both images, script, protocol, fixed-average and specimen hashes against the source manifest, and all four transform hashes against the immutable 162/183 inputs. The completed receipt is `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/dapi_train_landmark_grid_229_001/complete.json`, SHA-256 `af52d188fa4540cadd66ec4e36a199353cf454405db07547936b82312fc1e1e2`. No BM15/BM16 development or BM17/BM18 sealed individual image was opened, and no GPU training, CCF label, calibration, GUI change, or public benchmark was made.

The next data gate is independent anatomical axis/landmark review on TRAIN specimens before using them even as weak CCF-labelled virtual views. The eventual all-angle claim needs physically cut steep-oblique sections with animal-disjoint expert/blockface 3-D truth; the existing DAPI reconstructions cannot substitute for that. The [STAM methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09211-8) offer a relevant precedent—different-angle training reslices from registered whole-brain PI/DAPI data followed by nonlinear registration—but their success does not validate our current coarse specimen transforms or license their images for this proprietary training line.
