# 162 pre-run protocol: on-policy fitter gradients into direct coordinates

Status: **design/code only; long training is not authorized by this protocol**. The script requires an explicit `JOINT162_TRAIN_APPROVED=1` environment flag for training. A separate one-section TRAIN gradient preflight may be run before approval. No DEV, final, public benchmark, GUI, external weights, or failed-161 selected weights are inputs.

## Question and paired contrast

Can *measured atlas-fit and plausible-deformation feedback*, beyond synthetic coordinate targets, teach the 132-lineage direct pose coordinates and branch probabilities? Both arms load the **same** `v4_pose_adaptation_132/joint_step_02000.pt` model weights; optimizer state is freshly and identically initialized. No new module is added or randomly reinitialized. This is not a clean-from-random architecture test. **Both arms backpropagate the same direct 160-branch objective and the same synthetic corrected near-map/rigid objective through pose and shared encoder.** Only the measured fit/plausibility feedback differs: the full arm permits it through `prediction['state']` into coordinate pose heads, through `prediction['log_mass']`/reflection logits into branch probabilities, and through `prediction['feature']` into `lateral`/`encoder`; the detached arm computes that feedback from a copy with **every prediction tensor detached**. Frozen `warp_shared` is identical in both arms, so the trainable parameter set is matched. Both arms train the same native global matcher, atlas encoder, pair mapper, warp, and fit-quality head with the same coefficients, optimizer, update order, and fresh physical sections.

## Data and on-policy branches

Use only 64 documented TRAIN inverse-map bases and the `sample_panel_geometry_train_151` full-box normal/offset generator followed by v4 artifacts. Reserve bases 0–59 for 2,000 independent one-section **attempts**; bases 60–63 supply 256 fixed independent inner attempts. Freeze `(base, draw_seed, site_seed)` order and complete generator provenance (including animal ID, plane, appearance and exposure) before either arm. No repeated seed or physical-section ID; assert unique plane/appearance keys. No eligibility or tissue retries. **Zero-valid-pixel sections are recorded but receive no optimizer step or pose score:** a blank image cannot identify its latent plane. Every nonzero-tissue section, including ineligible/no-near and <8% tissue, receives 132-style direct 160-branch pose supervision. Scale direct loss by `min(1, valid_fraction/0.02)` below 2% valid tissue to limit near-empty label leverage; above 2% direct weight is 1. Log no-near and sparse rates by physical section, and the equal actual optimizer-update count in both arms. Fitter loss is absent when the blind predicted beam has no ≤1.5 mm branch, never replaced with a truth-derived near pose.

Re-select blind top16 on each arm's **current** prediction: top8 base, top6 anchor, two diverse anchors. Truth coordinates label rigid cost and the near/wrong branch only; they never enter fitter input. When a ≤1.5 mm predicted branch exists, fit that predicted state. Add a wrong branch only if it is ≥1 mm worse and its 24×24 atlas support matches the near branch (Dice ≥0.85, area-gap ≤0.08, ≥32 common cells). Branch choice is discrete/no-grad; selected state values remain differentiable in the full arm. An exact truth-state fit is permitted only in the fixed inner retention diagnostic, not in training or ordinary inference.

## Native losses and tensor path

All draws: original 132 synthetic physical-pose mixture objective on 160 reflected states, with tissue/five-point CCF error, normal penalty, mass KL, and nearest-anchor neighborhood. Near branches: `joint_forward_121(model, prediction, ..., candidate_state=predicted_states, match_side=24, map_side=96)` gives a bounded global correction and native atlas-conditioned local map. The **common spatial term** is `0.5 × corrected_near_map_mm + 0.25 × corrected_near_rigid_mm` against synthetic targets at 128 valid sites; it reaches pose in both arms. Separately, `joint_fit_loss_121` measures polarity-invariant source/atlas LNCC mismatch and atlas coverage for **each predicted branch** (the synthetic valid-pixel mask weights TRAIN measurement, never supplies coordinates or inference input). Measured difficulty is `LNCC_mismatch + 0.5 × coverage_penalty + 0.05 × plausible_warp + 0.05 × global_correction_cost`, with plausible warp = displacement energy plus 0.3 × each spatial roughness. The **feedback term** is `0.1 × measured_near_difficulty`; on support-matched pairs add `0.25 × softplus(0.1 + measured_near_difficulty − measured_wrong_difficulty)` and `0.1 × softplus(0.1 + (prior_wrong − measured_wrong_difficulty) − (prior_near − measured_near_difficulty))`. Thus both branches' measured fit and coverage ground ranking, and the latter term reaches direct mass/reflection logits only in full. No wrong warp-to-truth supervision.

To preserve inference without a truth mask, train the existing native `fit_energy` head in **both** arms against detached measured `LNCC_mismatch + 0.5 × coverage_penalty` via `0.2 × smooth-L1`, with a detached prediction so this calibration never supplies pose feedback in either arm. The inference score remains `fit_energy + 0.05 × warp + 0.05 × correction`; report its pair wins separately from measured-difficulty wins. Each arm makes a full prediction/fitter pass for the common spatial term and a detached-prediction fitter pass for calibration; full alone sends measured feedback through the full pass, while detached sends the same feedback through the detached pass. Fitter parameter gradients therefore receive the same numerical objectives. Multiply spatial, feedback, and calibration by `min(1, valid_fraction/0.08)` so almost-empty sections cannot dominate gradients; direct has its separate 2% reliability rule.

The preflight must show nonzero *isolated measured-feedback* coordinate-state, direct mixture log-mass, selected pose-head, and shared-encoder/lateral gradients in full; exactly zero/unused measured-feedback gradients into pose heads and encoder from the detached pass; nonzero native matcher/mapper gradients in both; nonzero common synthetic-spatial and direct-pose gradients in both. Select a ≥8% tissue pair from distinct predicted modes for this gradient test. Record per-component norms, full/direct ratio, peak allocated GPU bytes and elapsed time. Abort before training if any assertion fails. Do not silently substitute a different loss or branch.

## Frozen selection and independent gates

Save both arms at attempt indices 0/500/1000/1500/2000. Select within each arm on the same 256 inner TRAIN attempts by lowest direct-top1 mean CCF rigid error over **all nonzero-tissue sections**, subject to exact-pose map retention ≤step-0+0.10 mm and no >0.25 mm deterioration in nonzero <8% tissue direct-top1 mean. Report every attempt, including zero-valid omissions, <8% nonzero tissue, ≥30° nearest-cardinal angle, and exposure <0.15 / 0.15–0.30 / ≥0.30, not only fitter-eligible cases. Compare selected arms by **paired physical section**: full must improve nonzero-section direct-top1 mean by ≥0.15 mm and ≥5% relative, improve common-near corrected-map mean by ≥0.10 mm, preserve nonzero sparse direct-top1 within +0.25 mm, and not reduce blind16 ≤1.5 mm capture by >5 percentage points. Pair-difficulty wins are secondary and only on a common eligible-pair intersection. Failure of any criterion stops progression; no DEV read.

If the TRAIN-only gate passes and checkpoints/manifest are frozen and independently audited, a separately authorized DEV evaluation must use an independent predeclared physical-section panel, with no tuning or reruns: paired **nonzero-tissue** direct-top1 mean improvement ≥0.15 mm and a section-cluster bootstrap 95% interval for detached-minus-full entirely >0; common-near corrected-map improvement ≥0.10 mm; nonzero sparse <8% direct-top1 noninferiority ≤+0.25 mm; exact-pose map ≤parent+0.10 mm; and atlas-intensity AP-shift/source-feature-zero anatomy perturbations must worsen the full native fit on support-matched branches more than the detached arm. Zero-valid counts must be reported as unscorable, not converted into precise-pose claims. These are hard gates, not a license to choose a new panel after seeing scores.

Compute expectation before approval: one 256² section per update; two gradient-enabled `predict` passes per update (direct then fitter), at most two fitted branches (24² matcher, 96² map) and a second detached fitter pass for matched calibration/control, 2,000 updates × two arms, plus 256-section inner reads at five checkpoints. On the local 11 GiB RTX 2080 Ti, separate direct/common-spatial and detached-feedback backpropagation is intended to cap peak memory; the preflight must measure actual peak plus estimated Adam states and abort if >9.5 GiB. Stage-1's fitter-only 192 updates took ~46 s per arm, but this experiment adds encoder/pose backprop, a second fit pass, and inner reads, so extrapolation is unreliable; budget tens of minutes to hours, not seconds. No second correction iteration is included initially because it doubles atlas rendering/activation cost and would confound this gradient-boundary contrast.
