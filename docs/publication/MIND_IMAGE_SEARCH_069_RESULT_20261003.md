# 069: MIND-guided blind search still fails the physical gate

The frozen MIND-guided search on the identical 32-section/eight-synthetic-identity 067 subset **failed** its predeclared gate. It reduced the catastrophic 067 raw-NCC selected error (6,763.0 µm) to **3,272.3 µm**, but remained worse than the untouched 059 selected baseline **2,310.9 µm**. The truth-best among its 14 blind-found solutions was **1,296.1 µm**, also worse than the untouched beam's 1,065.1 µm. Selected error by appearance was exact-black 1,354.6→1,881.6 µm, imperfect-brush 2,917.1→3,144.3 µm, and raw background 2,845.0→5,282.8 µm. The raw-background regression is especially unacceptable. This is not a replacement for 059 and was not promoted.

The outcome separates *fixed-pair discrimination* from *search*. In 068, MIND-style structure preferred a truth-fitted transform to 067's wrong hypotheses in 25/32 sections, but when optimized over a much larger pose grid, 069 found new false matches. A weak appearance score can be overoptimized even when it looks promising on a fixed negative bank. The next change must either improve the learned/fixed image–atlas evidence and its capture basin or combine it with a principled pose prior; merely searching this same score harder is not justified. The 067a geometric fit (320 µm) remains an unavailable-truth ceiling, not a blind result.
