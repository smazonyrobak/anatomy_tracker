# Independent v2-artifact stress: unrestricted angles remain viable to test, not solved

The predeclared [no-update TRAIN-only stress](SLIDE_ARTIFACT_V2_FROZEN_STRESS_001_PROTOCOL_20261009.md) finished: 64 eligible, **independently drawn physical planes** from 76 attempts, with 64 distinct plane IDs. The inherited batch-653 image-prior branch averaged **2.590 mm** valid-tissue mapped error on the 96-pixel diagnostic grid; the inherited atlas-fit selector averaged **2.423 mm**, changing branch in 31/64. The truth-best of those *same* eight direct branches averaged **1.507 mm**. Antipodal plane-normal error was **31.21° → 31.30°** after fitted selection, against **12.12°** for the truth-best normal among eight. This is not electrode-site-level accuracy. The eight-plane oracle remains much better than the selected branch, while the selector does not improve mean orientation.

The 36 heavy-oblique cases (`max(abs(normal)) <= 0.85`, at least 31.8° from every cardinal normal) were **not worse** than the 28 less-oblique cases in this small synthetic sample: mapped error **2.373→2.242 mm** versus **2.868→2.655 mm** for prior→fit. That is no proof of real-world robustness: support, position, artifact and appearance vary independently, the sample is small, and all cases derive from one Allen atlas. It does, however, argue against declaring unrestricted cutting intrinsically impossible or imposing a mandatory plane-family restriction based on current results. The lowest visible-tissue quartile averaged **3.470→2.961 mm** versus **1.738→1.597 mm** in the highest; one low-support case exposed only about 3% valid tissue. Such sparse or damaged sections may be underdetermined from a single image and should eventually trigger broad uncertainty or a request for a neighboring section/rough cutting prior, not a forced confident label.

This interpretation is consistent with existing methods, but goes beyond what they establish. [DeepSlice's current implementation](https://github.com/PolarBean/DeepSlice) accepts tilted **coronal** sections and uses multiple sections to stabilize angle; it does not demonstrate all-plane single-image recovery. [AMBIA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10406728/) handles tilted atlas-plane localization but notes major-artifact failures. [Xiong et al.](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroinformatics/articles/10.3389/fninf.2018.00093/full) improve nonstandard-angle matching by aggregating a section sequence and explicitly warn that excessive deformation can make a wrong atlas plane look plausible. The physical ambiguity of a feature-poor single section is our inference from missing image information, not a proven universal angle limit.

The v2 simulator includes independently sampled smooth/nonrigid distortion, displaced fragments, occluding folds, local bubble lens/blur, a shifted and defocused tile seam, and exact-black/near-black/gray exteriors. It was **not** in the inherited model's training. Its event rates are deliberately high and uncalibrated against acquired slides, so this independent v1/v2 comparison is not a causal artifact effect estimate. Fragment-present cases had a small fitted-selection regression in this panel, but events co-occur and case anatomy differs; do not infer that fragment motion alone caused it. The generator covers several major defects, not a measured reproduction of real microscopy or every possible lab artifact.

A separate fixed-seed [montage of actual preprocessed TRAIN images](I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/acquired_train_input_montage_001/montage.png) displays eight donor-distinct coronal and eight donor-distinct sagittal sections. The coronal examples have predominantly black surroundings; the sagittal examples span much darker or brighter tissue, partial fields and some visibly non-black/structured surroundings. This is a qualitative visual audit, not an artifact-frequency estimate, and these are Allen acquisitions rather than independently labeled steep-oblique lab sections. The [whole-canvas TRAIN census](ACQUIRED_TRAIN_APPEARANCE_CENSUS_001_RESULT_20261008.md) quantifies their background and intensity variation. Before v2 becomes a large-scale training source, sample and label artifact types/severity on acquired TRAIN slides and tune synthetic frequencies and morphology accordingly. Do not manufacture paired background variants of the same plane.

The next model step remains observable candidate comparison: separate unwarped anatomical agreement, post-warp agreement and deformation cost; learn when atlas fit is reliable on TRAIN-only examples, then test once on a new identity-disjoint panel before allowing that signal to update plane prediction. Physical steep-oblique, animal-separated truth, calibrated uncertainty and GUI replacement are still missing. DeepSlice was not benchmarked and no holdout was opened.
