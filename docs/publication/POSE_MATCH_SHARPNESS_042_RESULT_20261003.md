# 042 frozen-match sharpness diagnostic: averaging is not the whole problem

The 042 mechanism check completed and its independent read-only verifier passed. It reproduced the original 041 soft fit within **0.015 µm** at the fixed five-point geometry check, confirming that the same physical fitter was used. No weights, 041 gate, selected checkpoint, data, or fit hyperparameters were changed. The frozen 177 synthetic development sections (eight disjoint deformation identities) and 64 weak-real donor sections (six development donors) were reused only for diagnosis; no public or untouched final set was opened.

At 041 batch 1,500, identity-equal synthetic best-eight physical error was 1.001 mm for the 019 prior, 1.002 mm for the 041 soft gated fit, **0.958 mm for normalized top-four matching**, and **0.962 mm for hard top-one matching** with the same frozen gate. Thus sharpening recovered only 0.044/0.040 mm—well short of the predeclared 0.20-mm mechanism threshold. Top-one errors were 2.499, 2.433, 2.436, and 2.439 mm respectively. Among the 112 prior-near-true sections, top-four/hard gated best-eight errors were 0.648/0.646 mm, versus a 0.681-mm prior. The inherited-affine weak-real top-one mean was 0.584 mm prior, 0.618 mm soft gated, 0.623 mm top-four gated, and 0.621 mm hard gated; none is a real-data improvement. The worst weak-real donor regression was 0.108 mm soft, 0.076 mm top-four, and 0.068 mm hard. Raw ungated hard matches were worse on real images (0.784 mm top one).

Every predeclared promising-direction flag is false. Simple replacement of a diffuse expectation by the highest-scoring keys does not explain or solve the large gap to the unavailable-truth snapped-key fit in 040 (best eight 0.422 mm). Inference: there are still enough incorrect or imprecise learned matches that the one-pass weighted fit is biased, or the appearance descriptors have not learned the required fine discrimination. Sharpening modestly helps near-true cases and reduces worst-donor *gated* regression but is insufficient to promote a model.

The next single mechanism test should use a robust geometric consensus estimator on the same frozen matches, without any new learning or broad hyperparameter sweep. This is directly motivated by the match-then-robust-pose structure in [SLIV-Reg](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18683) and [2D3D-MATR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/papers/Li_2D3D-MATR_2D-3D_Matching_Transformer_for_Detection-Free_Registration_Between_Images_and_ICCV_2023_paper.pdf). If consensus cannot materially recover physical best-eight error, more fitting tweaks are unlikely to help; the next intervention should be a better learned matching representation and candidate-quality model. This exploratory development panel cannot qualify probabilities, GUI deployment, or DeepSlice superiority.
