# 125: dense anatomy ranker learned image content, but did not improve plane selection

The predeclared 125 gate **failed**. The full-atlas-intensity ranker distinguished correct from support-matched wrong synthetic planes better than its support-only and input-ablation controls, but this did not translate into a useful reduction in selected spatial error. Do not promote this ranker or start the conditional 126 fitting-to-pose continuation. This is a negative result for this *formulation of fitting feedback*, not evidence that arbitrary-plane localization is impossible.

## Frozen experiment and independent audit

The frozen 122 step-2000 pose, mapper, 16-candidate beam and atlas PSF were unchanged. Newly initialized full-intensity and support-only rankers received the same 10,000 independently drawn synthetic TRAIN physical sections (11,020 unique attempts, 6,357 scored hard pairs, 9,172 nonempty dense labels, 9,242 optimized presentations). The final checkpoint, not a DEV-selected checkpoint, was evaluated. The new 256-section panel reused eight synthetic DEV deformation plans but drew new physical planes and appearances; 241 sections were eligible and 15 were retained as ineligible. The 64 coronal and 158 sagittal acquired DEV sections carried inherited weak Allen-affine references, not expert truth.

An independent read-only audit rehashed the 256 panel files, all five checkpoints, TRAIN inputs, sources and four evaluation outputs against their frozen receipts; recomputed unique physical/plane IDs, split and prior-panel separation, raw pair counts, metrics and gate decisions. All checked bindings and reaggregations agreed. The panel's scope sentence says “125 sections”; this is a frozen metadata typo: its records, receipt and raw evaluation consistently contain 256. No frozen output was changed.

## Result

Of the 241 eligible synthetic sections, 116 yielded a scored support-matched blind pair. Another 71 lacked a mapped candidate within 1.5 mm, 47 lacked a support-matched wrong candidate, and seven lacked enough common intact interior. Full intensity won **86/116 (74.1%)** pairs, versus support-only **80/116 (69.0%)**, frozen 122 **84/116 (72.4%)**, zero atlas intensity **72/116 (62.1%)**, shuffled atlas intensity **58/116 (50.0%)**, and swapped source image/features **61/116 (52.6%)**. Thus the full ranker uses spatial atlas and source-image information in this synthetic conditional test, unlike the failed 124 ranker; it still missed the ≥75% primary threshold and exceeded support-only by only **5.2**, not ≥10, percentage points. The source-swap holds the original fitted map fixed and does not test a complete rerun on another image.

Plan-equal selected mapped-tissue error was **3.364 mm** for frozen 122, **3.353 mm** for full intensity and **3.355 mm** for support-only. Full gained only **0.011 mm** versus 122 and **0.002 mm** versus support-only, far short of the predeclared 0.30/0.15 mm. Selected rigid-gauge error did not regress (3.375 to 3.370 mm), and six of eight plans were non-worse. Full changed the selected candidate slot in 95/241 sections and original/corrected action in 26/241, but helpful and harmful changes largely cancelled; some individual errors worsened by several millimetres. The corrected-action-only sensitivity, in which the scored fitted map is always the reported map, also failed: mapped error was 3.364 mm frozen, 3.352 mm full and 3.354 mm support-only. Both its required mapped gains failed, despite no rigid regression. Therefore the inherited action-scoring inconsistency does not explain away the primary failure.

The weak-real donor-equal five-point discrepancy improved from 0.922 to 0.871 mm in six coronal donors and from 2.004 to 1.728 mm in eight sagittal donors (support-only: 0.949 and 1.896 mm). These guardrails passed, but affine-reference agreement cannot establish anatomical accuracy or override the synthetic selection failure. No independent physical steep-oblique truth, calibration animals, final-test animals or public benchmark were used.

Descriptively, on this unequal/unpaired panel the raw/no-brush subset had 99/149 near candidates and a 3.512 mm full selected error; exact-black had 41/50 and 2.768 mm; imperfect-brush had 30/42 and 3.456 mm. These are associations, not a controlled background experiment. Across nearest-cardinal-angle strata the 45–55° group still had 20/24 near candidates and a 1.311 mm mean best-candidate error, but 3.572 mm selected error. This does not show that steeply oblique planes are intrinsically unresolvable; it shows that proposal/selection and raw-input robustness remain poor. A ranker cannot recover the 71 eligible sections whose 16-candidate beam contained no near mapped plane.

**Decision:** retain 125 as a documented development experiment; do not connect its scalar score to the direct pose head, promote it to the GUI, calibrate it, or run the conditional 126 continuation. Prioritize stronger direct candidate recall and raw/no-brush robustness within the same intended deployment architecture, while designing a more spatially coherent and safely coupled fitting signal. A next experiment must assess one-pass direct plane/map accuracy and wrong-candidate risk on fresh sections and weak-real donor guardrails before further scaling. Keep strict animal-level physical validation and uncertainty calibration as separate requirements.
