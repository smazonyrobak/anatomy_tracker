# 305 joint feedback: fit signal exists, blind selection fails

The corrected 305 joint run `_joint_004` completed its frozen 4,000 two-section optimizer batches (8,000 accepted independently drawn synthetic sections; 8,803 attempts) without a numerical failure. The independent post-exit verifier passed all run-file hashes, parent pose/panel lineage, source/protocol/amendment hashes, all draw and training rows, all four 64-section TRAIN-inner readouts, and the predeclared checkpoint/gate arithmetic. No acquired DEV/CAL/FINAL animal or public benchmark was read. The selected checkpoint is batch 2,000; **the joint gate failed at every readout**.

These are mean mapped-site errors on the same 64 frozen synthetic TRAIN-inner sections and each arm's blind choice among the same 16 proposals. At batch 2,000, full fitting improved on the direct prior by only **0.058 mm**, instead of the required 0.40 mm, and was **worse** than both controls, rather than beating each by 0.25 mm. Only **3/8** deformation-base means beat both controls (required 6/8). Full intensity evidence did prefer the near plane in **47/58** support-matched near/far pairs versus **29/58** for support alone. Thus the pairwise cue is present, but it does not yield reliable selection across all proposals: full selection changed 19/64 direct choices, improving nine and worsening ten. One gray-background case with 19% visible tissue changed from 0.51 to 7.18 mm even though the near and far proposals had similar atlas support; this is an observed failure, not a claimed universal cause. The 16-proposal mapped-error oracle mean was 0.884 mm, unavailable at inference.

The exact-pose mapper and pose restraint passed their narrow clauses: mean exact-pose drift 0.080 mm, and on the prespecified strong-warp quartile the exact map reduced mean error from 0.185 to 0.165 mm (10.55%). That absolute mapping gain is small beside the roughly 2-mm blind plane-selection error. At the selected checkpoint, the nine ≥45° synthetic-oblique cases averaged 1.604-mm full mapped error and 30.6° unsigned normal error; five <10%-visible cases averaged 3.379 mm. None of this validates physically cut steep-oblique brains, calibrated probabilities, or electrode-region claims. The existing GUI worker must continue refusing this gate-failed checkpoint.

The fixed post-run acquired TRAIN-only image-prior diagnostic evaluated one section each from 58 Allen coronal and 40 Allen sagittal donors, with four zero auxiliary channels and no weak affine at inference. Relative to approximate source affines, the 305 pose checkpoint selected the right cardinal-axis family in **55/58 coronal** and **30/40 sagittal** cases; the median unsigned normal gaps were **9.2°** and **32.1°**, and median centre gaps **2.316** and **2.019 mm**. The earlier 270 checkpoint on these same TRAIN donors selected 56/58 and 19/40, with 7.6° and 47.1° median normal gaps. This is a meaningful sagittal-family improvement but still poor orientation precision and is not independent physical ground truth. It does not rescue the failed joint gate.

A separate fixed zero-shot check on **all 240 valid Lupori fluorescence sections from four TRAIN animals** is substantially worse. The equal-animal first-choice five-site gap to approximate human VisuAlign references is **7.989 mm**; per-animal means range 7.721–8.443 mm, median unsigned normal gaps range 60.1–82.0°, and only **9/240** sections have a top-16 candidate within 1.5 mm of the human reference. The 192-pixel evaluation retains the full source QuickNII O/U/V physical extent, uses exactly the historical `max(red, green)`/p99 preprocessing with four zero auxiliary channels, and reads human coordinates only after inference. These are weak-reference TRAIN diagnostics, not adjudicated physical errors. Nevertheless, the gap is too large to treat synthetic selector tuning as the sole missing step; acquired appearance transfer and candidate capture require direct attention.

**Decision:** do not extend this synthetic-only training recipe, calibrate, benchmark against DeepSlice, or promote to the GUI. The next work should first identify what the acquired fluorescence input lacks relative to the synthetic training distribution and test a small matched, scratch-lineage acquired-adaptation intervention on TRAIN animals without contaminating reserved animals. Candidate-ranking work must separately check heavy-tail wrong selections and atlas-intensity value beyond support/background effects, not just pairwise average accuracy.
