# Allen individual STPT native-image source: TRAIN preflight

This is a **source and geometry preflight**, not a model result or a validation of arbitrary-angle physical histology. The frozen TRAIN reservation identified Allen donor `14383`, specimen `710918`, experiment `100140756` before any image fit was measured. Its 140 red-background-fluorescence sections (`section_number` 2–141) were acquired coronally at 100-µm inter-section spacing. Allen's original SectionImage tiles, per-section Alignment2d, per-series Alignment3d, and authored CCF-to-native displacement field are available through its [Mouse Brain Connectivity Atlas](https://brain-map.org/support/documentation/api-allen-brain-connectivity-atlas) and [SDK](https://allensdk.readthedocs.io/en/latest/allensdk.core.mouse_connectivity_cache.html). The source is an independently imaged specimen, not another rendering of the average atlas.

The native-to-CCF chain is `native -> tvr affine -> inverse of Allen's CCF-to-affine-aligned displacement field`. Six fixed-point inverse iterations were used on the 25-µm vector field. On four **TRAIN** native sections (40, 60, 80, 100), pulling Allen's separately distributed CCF-resampled red volume back through that chain reproduced the downloaded native red image with tissue-supported Pearson correlations `0.9901, 0.9934, 0.9865, 0.9846`. The same native pixels correlated with the CCF template at `0.7682, 0.9024, 0.8445, 0.6591`. The former checks the transform implementation; neither statistic is a landmark accuracy or a held-animal model score. The inverse numerical residual p95 was below 0.41 µm on those four sections.

For the first TRAIN specimen only, `training/build_allen_stpt_native_map_209.py` materialized an exact-grid native-to-CCF map at 280×210 XY pixels, 140 acquired section positions, 49.96×49.9733 µm XY pixel-center spacing, and 100 µm Z spacing. The map is float32 `[AP posterior, DV inferior, ML right]` in micrometres, has no NaNs, and its worst per-section p95 inverse-equation residual is 1.81 µm. Map SHA-256: `74cee3d10f4fa768fc90c2635a0b2c2d8585f88c5488cfd8522c959810b8d646`. Frozen source and output stay on I: under `data/allen_stpt_registered_volume_207_train_001`, `data/allen_stpt_native_map_209_train_001`, and `reports/allen_stpt_native_207_train_001`; they are not committed or redistributed. The CCF-resampled volume was used only to test the mapping, not substituted for native-shape training input.

**Limits:** Allen's displacement/affine alignment is an automated estimated reference, not an expert-certified local coordinate for every tissue point. Virtual arbitrary-angle views through this stack will interpolate between independent **coronal** sections 100 µm apart; they are not independently acquired oblique slices. This source increases real specimen anatomy and appearance in training, but cannot by itself establish physical-oblique accuracy or calibrated probabilities. The eight-donor TRAIN acquisition is frozen independently; held-animal DEV/CAL/FINAL and public DeepSlice benchmarks remain untouched. Allen data use must follow [Allen Institute terms](https://alleninstitute.org/legal/terms-of-use), including attribution and any required permission for commercial use or redistribution.
