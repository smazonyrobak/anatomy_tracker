# 134: all-beam learned fitting descriptor, frozen-pose stage

This is a TRAIN-only, stage-1 test of whether anatomical agreement after fitting can distinguish plausible from wrong atlas planes. It does **not** yet send fitting gradients to the pose predictor. Freeze the randomly initialized project-lineage 132 step-2,000 pose/map checkpoint, its exact blind 16-branch beam, the finite-thickness Allen atlas, and the 96-pixel fitted maps. No 125 ranker weights, legacy weights, external features, pseudolabels, GUI output, final animals or public benchmark are used. The 132 weak-real coronal guard already failed; this experiment cannot promote that checkpoint.

For each independent synthetic TRAIN section, draw a new virtual subject, physical plane and appearance seed from the 64 TRAIN deformation bases. Use v4 appearance on two of every three accepted updates and v3 on the third, with no deliberate plane reuse, paired black/nonblack version, or plane-uniqueness rejection. Retain all attempted draw IDs and complete provenance. If the generator declares a section ineligible, or if no member of its blind 16-beam maps observed valid tissue within 1.5 mm, record the attempt and draw a new section. These exclusions are for the descriptor's informative stage-1 training only; no DEV or real-animal case is excluded from subsequent assessment.

With the frozen model, fit all 16 branches at 96 × 96 using the section's sampled PSF. At each branch's **actual fitted 3D coordinates**, render atlas intensity/support on a 64 × 64 grid using the same PSF. Candidate-specific maps and atlas support are identical across comparison arms. Compute each branch's target as mean 3D fitted-surface error against the synthetic observed-to-atlas coordinates over valid observed tissue, at the original 256-pixel grid. Form the all-16 soft target as `softmax(-(mapped_error_mm - beam_min_mm) / 0.5)`; the truth is never supplied to the descriptor as input.

Train two independently optimized but identically initialized small source/atlas convolutional-descriptor pairs on the **same draws**. Both source heads receive only the frozen 64-grid image feature and input intensity channel 0. The full atlas head receives the fitted atlas intensity and support; the matched support-only atlas head receives a zero intensity channel and the same support. No descriptor head receives xy, CCF coordinates, candidate ID, beam position, target geometry or prior logits. New heads start from random initialization within this lineage. The fixed pose/model remains frozen, and its existing fitted-score head is not used.

For both arms, compare L2-normalized source/atlas descriptor vectors pointwise by `(1 - cosine)/2`. Reliability is one image-derived, candidate-independent 64-grid map: `0.2 + 0.8 clamp(local_5x5_std(input_intensity)/0.12, 0, 1)`. The same reliability sum is the denominator for supported appearance mismatch and unsupported atlas coverage. Add identical fixed map penalties `0.25 × reliability-weighted local-displacement magnitude in mm` and `0.25 × reliability-weighted dimensionless nearest-neighbour local-displacement gradient`. Thus unsupported image pixels are penalized rather than disappearing from the loss. Fitting energy is appearance + coverage + map penalties. Train the two heads with equal-weight all-16 cross-entropies of (a) fit-only logits `-3 × energy` and (b) pose-prior-plus-fit logits `frozen_direct_log_prior - 3 × energy` against the mapped-error soft target. Use AdamW, 4,000 accepted updates, identical learning-rate schedule and update budget, 64-grid descriptors, and save initial, intermediate and final checkpoints. Report per-arm losses and selected mapped error during TRAIN, but choose no model from TRAIN curves alone.

After this run freezes, evaluate every saved milestone on the unchanged fresh-v4-132 DEV panel and older v3 DEV panel with exact same beam/maps/target definitions. The final step-4,000 checkpoint is the sole primary gate; intermediate checkpoints are descriptive and cannot be selected by DEV score. On fresh v4, require the full arm to beat **both** its matched support-only arm and the original direct pose prior by at least 0.15 mm in synthetic-plan-equal selected mapped error and at least five percentage points in plan-equal ≤1.5-mm first-choice rate. The full arm must also win at least 60% of at least 64 predeclared support-matched near/wrong pairs and exceed support-only by at least ten percentage points; report all pairs and missing-pair cases. Report fit-only and prior-plus-fit separately, per-plan and exposure strata, best-in-beam bound, and v3 retention. The independent DEV comparison may reject the learned descriptor even if TRAIN losses fall. A passing signal merely justifies a subsequent experiment in which fitting loss updates the coordinate predictor; it does not establish that feedback, real-animal validity, calibrated uncertainty or GUI readiness.

All scripts, caches, source snapshots, draws, checkpoints, metrics and receipts remain on `I:`. Bind the 132 checkpoint, atlas, TRAIN source, code, protocol, random seeds and outputs by SHA-256. Do not inspect incomplete run artifacts while the runner is live; monitor its stdout/process/GPU telemetry and audit only after exit.
