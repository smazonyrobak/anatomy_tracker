# 067: blind raw-intensity atlas search rejected

The predeclared 32-section/eight-synthetic-identity pilot **failed**. The frozen 059 prior-selected error was **2,310.9 µm** on this subset; blind high-pass normalized-correlation search selected **6,763.0 µm**. Even the truth-best among the 14 solutions that the blind search returned was **3,477.3 µm**, versus 1,065.1 µm in the unmodified 059 beam. Selected error worsened on exact-black (1,354.6→6,192.1 µm), imperfect-brush (2,917.1→7,244.7 µm), and raw inputs (2,845.0→6,935.2 µm). The method is rejected; no model or GUI was changed. This is exploratory synthetic data, not biological validation or a public benchmark.

Follow-up 067a isolates geometry from image scoring. With synthetic truth, the **precise candidate-edge rotation/scale/translation family searched by 067** attains **320.5 µm** best-of-14 error. A truth-fitted solution lay within 067's parameter ranges in **31 of 32** sections, with 311.2 µm mean error among those sections. Thus the old candidate normals and transform family can represent good matches; the failed blind selections reflect the image objective and/or coarse-to-fine optimization, not a missing geometric solution. It remains to determine whether the correct transform scores poorly or whether the optimizer misses a high-scoring correct transform. No truth-fitted transform was substituted into blind outputs.
