# 052: train atlas descriptors against global anatomical lookalikes

The 025 scratch-trained patch encoder matches a true-plane local bank well (96% top-one at 0.5 mm), but its frozen 2-million-entry whole-brain, multi-orientation atlas bank retrieves a correct point in only 15% of top-16 lists. The 031 geometry-only solver then selects coherent false planes. The next necessary representation change is to expose the descriptor to the **globally most similar wrong atlas patches** during training, not to add another scalar candidate score.

Continue the complete 025 weights and optimizer, with a lower learning rate, for 5,000 batches of freshly and independently sampled arbitrary-plane synthetic TRAIN sections. Each batch chooses 16 surviving tissue points and their known finite-thickness atlas-surface positives. A frozen copy of 025 and its atlas-only 030 bank mines eight visually similar atlas keys per point at least 1 mm from the known coordinate; the trainable image/atlas encoder contrasts the positive against these hard negatives and the other points in the section. The miner is frozen and uses no DEV/final/public images. No source plane is repeated deliberately with altered artifacts. The bank itself was rendered from the atlas only, using no image/annotation labels. Smart-brush availability and backgrounds remain generator variability, not mandatory segmentation.

After training, rebuild the same atlas-only bank using the new checkpoint and evaluate unknown-plane lookup on the frozen synthetic DEV panel, including both image parities. The **necessary** representation gate is top-16 point recall within 0.5 mm ≥30% (versus 030's 15.4%), top-one ≥5% (versus 1.9%), local true-plane top-one ≥80% (versus 025's 96.2%), and no raw/black/imperfect-brush mode below half the aggregate top-16 recall. A later truth-blind plane fit must still beat 031's 3.668 mm before this can help the joint model. Known synthetic tissue coordinates are used to train and measure point recall; they must not be used to choose inference query points or select a pose. This component gate alone does not establish real-animal accuracy, calibrated uncertainty or GUI readiness.
