# 097: anatomical contrast did not rescue local matching

The frozen 097 gate **failed**. Updating only the existing 094 image and atlas descriptor projections on 4,000 independently drawn synthetic TRAIN sections increased average positive-versus-negative similarity, but it did not make the operational correspondence field more accurate than leaving a tissue site at the candidate's fitted atlas-plane coordinate. Do not merge this descriptor into joint pose/warp training or promote it to the GUI.

TRAIN completed 4,000 accepted physical sections in 4,485 ordered attempts (469 generator-ineligible and 16 without an eligible contrastive site). Accepted physical-section IDs and geometries were distinct; appearance modes were 1,352 raw, 1,309 exact-black and 1,339 imperfect-brush. Mean contrastive loss was 1.486 in the first 500 batches and 1.423 in the last 500; mean hard-negative margin remained negative (−0.249 to −0.236). These are atlas-derived synthetic lineages, not 64 independent biological animals. The parent 094 weights were trained in this scratch-only lineage; no externally pretrained weights, real labels or public benchmark were used.

The independent frozen evaluator reproduced the 096 step-0 matched and centre fractions exactly on the same 182 complementary synthetic DEV sections, eight subject-deformation plans and 14 fitted candidate states per section. All eight plans failed the matched-versus-centre requirement (0/8 positive, versus the frozen requirement of at least six). The step-4,000 deficits were −0.06125 raw, −0.06967 black and −0.08764 imperfect-brush; low/middle/high atlas-support deficits were −0.11768/−0.05512/−0.01012. For the physically best branch, 30,741 of 30,938 visible sites (99.36%) had both supported positive and negative fine bins, so missing a local positive is not the main explanation for its large deficit. The positive/negative *mean* gap improved while the best-positive/hardest-negative gap worsened; this supports investigating hard-bin discrimination and the argmax/local-softmax decoding policy, rather than treating falling TRAIN loss as proof of useful matches. This is a diagnostic inference, not a claim that either component has yet been isolated.

**Decision:** retain 094 as the last baseline and keep 097 as a negative experiment. Next run one frozen-logit diagnostic that separates descriptor ranking from the operational score penalty and decoder: supported-positive ceiling, top-1 positive rate, best-positive versus hardest-negative margin, raw-cosine argmax versus support-penalized argmax and local-softmax physical correctness, stratified by best/other candidate, appearance and atlas support. Choose a targeted objective or readout change only after that result; then require improved selected pose and mapped-point error on a new synthetic panel before animal-separated real development. No probability calibration, DeepSlice benchmark or GUI promotion follows from 097.
