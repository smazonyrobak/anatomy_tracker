# High-resolution dense-coordinate auxiliary: frozen 075 result

The 075 pilot continued the project's randomly initialized 059 direct probabilistic pose lineage. A new 128×128 head predicted each surviving pixel's Allen CCF coordinate and tissue validity from the same image features as the pose heads. The image encoder, pose heads, and new head trained together; the atlas mapper/fitter stayed frozen. **This stage did not feed fitting difficulty back into pose prediction.** It was a bounded test of whether denser physical supervision improves global pose capture, not the requested completed joint model.

Training finished normally after 10,000 batches: 20,000 independently sampled, eligible synthetic physical sections and 10,000 distinct within-run real TRAIN sections. Another 2,089 synthetic draws failed the existing eligibility rule. Every accepted synthetic physical-section ID was unique in this run; draws retained virtual/base subject, source, seed, and section provenance. Real sections retained animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs. Reuse of real TRAIN sections across historical runs was random and disclosed in the frozen config, not a paired-background duplication scheme. All development files and temporary paths were on `I:`. The parent is the project's own checkpoint, not AtlasPose, AtlasWarp, DeepSlice, or external pretrained features.

The fixed development readout used 246 eligible synthetic sections from eight identity-disjoint synthetic deformation plans and 64 weak-Allen-labelled real sections from six separate development donors. Errors below are identity-equal means in **millimetres**. “Direct” means the model's selected pose; “field fit” is the plane fitted to its predicted pixel coordinates, with no truth at inference. Neither is an atlas-registered nonrigid mapping result.

The field and tissue-validity predictions learned, but the deployable selected pose did **not** improve: synthetic direct error increased by 0.035 mm relative to its parent. The final field-derived pose was only 0.057 mm better than the final direct choice on synthetic development data and was 0.395 mm worse on weak-real data. One real development donor (15447) regressed by 0.336 mm in the direct readout, exceeding the previously used 0.20-mm donor guardrail. At the final checkpoint, low-support synthetic sections had 3.323-mm field-fit error. The raw, exact-black, and imperfect-brush synthetic field-fit errors were 2.726, 2.568, and 2.603 mm respectively. The final **truth-selected oracle** best-of-eight and best-of-160 candidate errors were 1.157 and 0.790 mm; these are availability ceilings, not inference performance.

**Decision:** do not promote 075, continue the same training blindly, calibrate its scores, install it in the GUI, or spend the untouched public/final-test benchmark. The monotonically improving field is worth one cheap, predeclared frozen diagnostic of whether field–candidate physical agreement can select among the model's *actual blind* candidate planes; any learned selector must wait for that signal. A flexible unpenalized warp residual is not an acceptable selector because it can disguise a wrong global plane. If the frozen diagnostic fails, stop this branch rather than adding another arbitrary scorer.
