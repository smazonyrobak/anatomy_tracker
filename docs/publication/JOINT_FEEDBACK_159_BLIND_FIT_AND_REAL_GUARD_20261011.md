# 159: blind fit ranking fails; 158 synthetic-only pose does not transfer to weak real references

The 159 assay froze the 158 baseline and both preselected update-500 arms. It evaluated the same 64 independently sampled v4 synthetic DEV sections (eight per each of eight reused deformation plans). The direct pose supplied its **blind** top 16; the frozen 156 atlas fitter corrected and scored each branch without inserting a true pose. Physical errors were calculated only after ranking, on up to 1,024 surviving source pixels. An independent read-only verifier checked all 192 rows, output hashes, checkpoint identities, plan balance and summary arithmetic. These eight plans share one Allen atlas and are **not** independent animals.

The untuned score `direct log mass − fitted energy` did **not** improve mean physical selection for either trained arm. The matched detached control was better than feedback on direct first-choice error on this separate panel (3.790 versus 3.876 mm), reversing the small advantage seen on the 158 checkpoint-selection panel. Setting atlas intensity to zero while retaining support yielded selected-original errors of 3.722/3.623/3.388 mm for baseline/feedback/control, each lower than its full-intensity fit score. This perturbation is out of distribution and is **not** a clean support-only ablation, but it rules out claiming that the current fitted score has demonstrated useful image-anatomy discrimination. Near-candidate availability is much better than blind selection; arbitrary-plane representation alone is not the immediate measured bottleneck. Do not train a larger feedback loop on this score unchanged.

The independent 158 weak-real guard used 64 coronal images from six donor-disjoint development animals and 158 sagittal-ish images from eight other development donors. The inherited Allen affines are weak automated references, not adjudicated physical truth. At step 0, donor-equal direct first-choice five-point discrepancy was **14.189 mm / 62.58°** coronal and **8.458 mm / 50.79°** sagittal. The selected feedback arm changed these to **14.664 mm / 62.84°** and **8.530 mm / 54.25°**; the detached control to **14.549 mm / 62.68°** and **8.400 mm / 52.52°**. Both trained arms thus failed any plausible real-reference retention gate. The weak-real verifier checked the 1,110 rows, donor/section identities, source and checkpoint hashes, and donor-equal arithmetic.

The large real discrepancy prompted a separate coordinate sanity check, not a post-hoc change to the gate. On the 64 coronal DEV images, the 158 step-0 first-choice plane centres had median AP/DV/ML **[7230, 4799, 4018] μm** versus inherited-affine reference **[7280, 5321, 7074] μm**; median centre distance was **4.012 mm**. Reference centres lie in a plausible atlas range and all six coronal donors show 13.7–14.7 mm mean five-point discrepancy. The old 132 mixed-real lineage was previously measured against these same weak-real families at roughly **0.904 mm coronal / 1.242 mm sagittal**, though that is not an exposure-matched comparison. The coordinate check supports a severe **synthetic-to-acquired transfer failure** of the 154→158 pose lineage rather than a mere unit conversion in the new evaluator. It does not certify the weak affines as true anatomy.

Decision: retain 154→158 as an experimental source of arbitrary-plane proposals and near-pose fitting, **not** the model to promote, calibrate, deploy or benchmark. The next model-development stage must address two measured failures together: learn anatomy-sensitive ranking/correction on actual predicted branches, and retain acquired-image pose performance using only TRAIN donors and weak labels as such. The earlier 132 mixed-real checkpoint is the current stronger research parent for real retention; any future continuation must use a fresh development panel for confirmation, protect the six/eight DEV donors, and not mistake virtual atlas deformations for new biological animals. The synthetic generator must also diversify framing, offsets and specimen-like appearance using TRAIN data, while keeping each sampled physical plane and its artifacts independent and preserving full-angle support. Missing physical steep-oblique animal truth and measured artifact prevalence remain explicit data limitations, not reasons to silently narrow the supported angles.
