# Spatial-coordinate pose head 013: frozen development result

The 012 batch-6,000 model was continued for 6,000 batches with an added 16×16 CCF coordinate/validity head sharing its encoder. Its original direct pose head and atlas-conditioned local mapper remained in the same checkpoint. Each batch used two newly sampled arbitrary-plane synthetic sections and one distinct Allen TRAIN section: 12,000 accepted synthetic sections plus 6,000 real sections from 1,885 TRAIN donors. The real sections are disjoint from 151,159 previously scheduled real TRAIN identities. The coordinate field was supervised against the synthetic **rigid plane**, not the locally distorted tissue; the real sections supplied direct weak-affine pose supervision but no coordinate-field supervision. No pretrained weights, DEV labels, public benchmark labels or final-test animals entered training.

The independent fixed-panel readout scored all six post-initial checkpoints on 185 eligible synthetic DEV sections from eight synthetic deformation plans and 64 weak-affine real DEV images from six donors. Identity-equal means in millimetres:

| Batch | Synthetic direct mapped | Synthetic coordinate mapped | Synthetic correct-pose mapped | Real direct five-point | Real coordinate five-point |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1,000 | 3.067 | 3.364 | 0.129 | 0.623 | 9.599 |
| 3,000 | 3.119 | 3.084 | 0.130 | 0.590 | 9.368 |
| 5,000 | **2.984** | **2.922** | 0.130 | **0.564** | 9.957 |
| 6,000 | 3.015 | 2.926 | 0.130 | 0.568 | 9.945 |

At batch 5,000 the coordinate head modestly outperforms its own direct pose head on synthetic sections but remains around 2.9 mm, far from the ~0.13 mm correct-pose result. On all six real DEV donors, the coordinate candidate is around 9.7–10.2 mm from inherited Allen affines, while direct prediction is 0.24–0.75 mm away. These real affines are weak references, not blinded expert truth, but the gap is too large to ignore. The correct-pose learned mapper stayed stable, so this was not a mapper regression.

A targeted batch-5,000 ablation separated coordinate-field failure from analytic plane fitting. The predicted field itself is 2.963 mm from the synthetic rigid-plane target over tissue and 5.602 mm over the full image grid. The real full-grid field is 7.531 mm from the weak affine. Changing the plane fit from predicted validity weights to uniform weights moved synthetic error **2.920→3.152 mm** and real five-point disagreement **9.957→9.938 mm**; even an unavailable oracle synthetic tissue mask gave 2.912 mm. Thus a weighting bug is not the explanation. The earlier oracle-label diagnostic showed that exact 16×16 coordinate labels could fit a plane near 0.12–0.14 mm, but the network has not learned those labels accurately enough. Severe partialness remains difficult: at batch 5,000, synthetic coordinate mapped error is 5.33 mm for <5% tissue, 3.12 mm at 5–15%, 2.25 mm at 15–30%, and 2.10 mm at ≥30%.

**Decision:** do not use the 013 coordinate candidate in GUI inference or call it a calibrated posterior. The existing direct/fit candidate remains the safer experimental path. The next discriminating experiment is a limited continuation that trains the coordinate head on **distinct real TRAIN weak affine fields as well as fresh arbitrary-plane synthetic fields**, while freezing the already learned direct pose and mapper. This directly tests whether the real-domain failure is lack of field supervision; it also allows more synthetic exposure without changing the central model. Reject the head if separate-donor real and fixed arbitrary-plane synthetic development errors do not both improve materially. Only then revisit fit-to-pose joint training; a lower field loss alone is not a reason to promote it.
