# 184 TRAIN-only atlas-intensity reach assay

The frozen 182 model chooses its top-one pose from the image-only mixture before atlas intensities enter its local tissue mapper. The supplied-correct-plane mapper is accurate, but blind top-one error remains about 2.6 mm. This assay asks whether atlas intensity contains a useful mapping signal **when the candidate plane is already correct**, separating a representation failure from a pose-selection/reach failure.

Use only the 128 unique 182 inner TRAIN physical sections, the frozen `full_step_00300.pt` checkpoint, and the same site selection and model inputs as 182. Recompute each supplied-correct-plane map with the intact atlas and with channel-0 atlas intensity set to zero while leaving support unchanged. Record mean tissue-to-CCF map error and the zero-minus-intact penalty by v3/v4 appearance. Join the previously frozen top-one intact/zero map errors and rigid errors; stratify top-one penalties by <0.5, 0.5–1.5, and ≥1.5 mm rigid error. Check exact-intact replay against the 182 row before interpreting any difference.

This is a diagnostic, not a checkpoint-selection or qualification gate. A sizable exact-plane penalty with little top-one penalty would support candidate-pose reach/selection as the bottleneck; little penalty even at the exact plane would indicate insufficient use of atlas intensity by the mapper. Zero intensity is out of distribution, so the matched 182 support-only continuation remains the stronger training control. Do not use DEV, sealed/final animals, a public benchmark, or these TRAIN rows for probability calibration. No frozen 182 artifact is modified.
