# 189: can atlas anatomy correct a captured fluorescent pose?

This is a frozen, TRAIN-only diagnostic, **not** model training or a deployment gate. Use the selected 188 step-1200 checkpoint unchanged. From each of the four Lupori TRAIN animals, select the 16 valid sections with the smallest SHA-256 of `animal_id/section_id`, before inference. The source is the verified Lupori archive (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7419283); the approximate human QuickNII/VisuAlign plane is used only to measure physical error and construct the diagnostic direction. Never read the Lupori DEV, CAL or FINAL animals. No public benchmark.

Preprocess each complete RGB thumbnail exactly as in 188: bilinear resize to 256², `max(red,green)/255`, divide by image-wide p99 and clip to [0,1], four other input channels zero. Do not use the provided tissue mask or deformation field in the fit score. Take the first 16 probabilistic pose/reflection proposals. For the diagnostic *captured-pose* condition, retrospectively choose the proposal closest to the human plane among candidates with 0.5–3.0 mm five-site error. Score the highest-prior proposal separately, with no truth-based selection.

For each eligible proposal, compute a five-sample finite-thickness Allen 2017 atlas rendering at -50,-25,0,25,50 µm with normalized weights 1,2,4,2,1. Fix an eroded scoring mask from the proposal's **initial** atlas support, requiring at least 100 pixels at 48². Move the full plane 20% of its local 9-parameter residual toward the human reference and 20% away, without retraining or warp changes. Compare absolute smooth image/atlas correlation over the same mask at all three poses. As an anatomy-null control, circularly permute atlas intensities among the same supported pixels while leaving support and image unchanged. Record atlas-support retention, physical error monotonicity, the toward-versus-away score ordering, and the exact score derivative at the original pose.

The captured-pose signal passes screening only if at least 32 of 64 sections are eligible, the real-intensity toward-versus-away win fraction is at least 65% and at least 10 percentage points above the permutation control, the derivative favors the correct direction in at least 60%, and at least three animals have a real-intensity win fraction above 50%. If any condition fails, do not connect this score to the pose model or scale training. Passing supports only a **local, oracle-selected** anatomical direction on near-coronal fluorescent sections; it does not establish blind candidate ranking, arbitrary oblique performance, map accuracy, or a benefit from gradients. Those require separate matched tests.
