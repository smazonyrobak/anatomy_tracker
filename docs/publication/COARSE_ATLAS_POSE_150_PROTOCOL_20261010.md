# 150: contextual atlas correspondence with separately supervised match reliability

The frozen 149 oracle found a 0.146-mm correspondence gap and a further 0.130-mm weighting gap on the v4 near-candidate subset; supported atlas keys were reachable at every valid sampled query. Experiment 150 changes those two mechanisms rather than enlarging the key lattice or continuing unchanged 148 training. This is a synthetic development mechanism test, not a deployable model, probability calibration, or biological validation.

Initialize the 150 source/atlas encoders randomly. Give both branches of a matched pair identical initial weights and independent, identical TRAIN-section draws. One branch sees finite-thickness atlas intensity; the control sees atlas support with intensity zeroed. Preserve independent random arbitrary-plane, thickness, artifact and background sampling per section; chance repeats are allowed, with no deliberate paired recoloring or geometry uniqueness filter. Use 60 of the 64 existing synthetic TRAIN deformation plans for gradient updates; reserve four plans for frozen inner checkpoint selection. The 132 direct pose checkpoint supplies no training features, labels or weights: it is loaded only to generate blind candidate beams in development evaluation.

The changed matcher adds wider-context 2D source and 3D atlas features, retaining a finite-thickness atlas neighborhood and explicit unmatched probability. Supervise local atlas assignments with spatially soft positives derived from the known synthetic CCF map, and separately supervise a match-reliability estimate from the predicted correspondence error. Source tissue visibility has its own label. During this warm-up, detach fit weights from the global plane loss, so suppressing match confidence cannot cheaply lower that loss; fit weights have an explicit low-evidence fallback. The target for global correction is the original rigid atlas plane; the warped per-pixel CCF map labels local correspondence. Neither truth enters inference. Do not claim a recurrent joint pose/warp model until the matching mechanism works and gradients are actually coupled to the pose predictor.

Freeze four checkpoints (initial, 1,000, 3,000, 6,000 batches). Each training batch draws three independently randomized synthetic sections: two v4 and one v3, with exact, nearby and wrong candidate states. Select the checkpoint using the prespecified sum of inner-plan mean exact and nearby corrected rigid errors; use the same selected batch number for both full and support-only arms. Log presentation counts, independent plan and section IDs, input modes, orientation bins, training/inner errors, effective fit mass, source/code/protocol hashes and GPU run provenance. Do not select a checkpoint using the external development panel.

Before evaluator access, freeze a fresh panel from eight identity- and plan-disjoint synthetic development deformation plans, each with independently drawn arbitrary brain-intersecting planes and one v4 appearance per section. Keep the existing 148/149 DEV sections out of 150 checkpoint selection and its primary gate. Score every valid native-256 tissue site on exact truth planes and fixed frozen-132 blind-beam branches; report the truth-best branch separately as an oracle diagnostic, and top-prior branch as the blind readout. Evaluate the same sections, branches and angle strata for both 150 arms and for the frozen 148 step-6,000 reference. The primary mechanism gate on the new panel's v4 near-branch subset (initial rigid error <=1.5 mm) is a plan-equal full-arm improvement >=0.30 mm, full-versus-support-only after-error advantage >=0.15 mm, <=30% worsened by >0.10 mm, and exact-plane full-arm error <=0.25 mm. Report denominators; do not silently drop difficult sections. Passing this gate authorizes joint training experiments, not deployment.

The prospective real/acquired donor guard, physical all-angle expert truth, uncertainty/electrode-region calibration and public DeepSlice comparison remain separate gates. No public benchmark, final animals, expert physical-oblique truth, pretrained external features/weights, or hidden pseudolabels are used here. The contextual matching and explicit reliability choices are informed by [CoMIR](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/file/d6428eecbe0f7dff83fc607c5044b2b9-Paper.pdf), [LoFTR](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.00680) and [Deep Global Registration](https://arxiv.org/html/2004.11540); transfer to arbitrary-plane mouse histology is a testable inference, not an established result.
