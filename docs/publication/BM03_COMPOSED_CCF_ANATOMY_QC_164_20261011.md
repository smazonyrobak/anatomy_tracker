# BM03-to-Allen composed mapping: grossly aligned, not valid region labels

The selected antialiased BM03→DAPI-average registration from pilot 162 was composed with the [published DAPI-average→Allen pull transform](DAPI_CCF_AFFINE_DIRECTION_QC_163_20261011.md). In the pilot's explicitly constructed BM03 millimetre frame, BM03 points map through the inverse of the DAPI-RAS-mm→BM03-mm affine, a RAS→LPS sign flip, and the published DAPI-LPS-mm→Allen-LPS-micrometre affine. BM03's identity NIfTI sform is not a usable physical-anatomical frame; the registration supplies this metric convention. The explicit chain and composed affine agreed to 1.3e-12 micrometres at the original-volume centre, which mapped to Allen index (234.48, 160.41, 230.22).

The CPU-only `training/qc_bm03_composed_ccf_164.py` used the BM03 native grid reduced only in the two high-resolution axes, resampled Allen average intensities and annotations **in memory** for QC, and produced three-plane intensity/annotation overlays plus two oblique *reslices* of the reconstructed volume. It did not save a label volume or train a model. BM03-versus-Allen thresholded tissue Dice was 0.968; BM03-versus-annotation-*union* Dice was 0.971. Orthogonal union Dice ranged from 0.919 at the central sagittal sample to 0.990 at one horizontal sample. Two oblique reslices had union Dice 0.975 and 0.976. These measurements chiefly show coarse envelope agreement; the oblique cuts are computational reslices, not independently acquired oblique sections.

Visual inspection finds offsets at dorsal cortical, ventricular, hippocampal, and central boundaries. Thus a high whole-brain Dice must not be read as correctness of individual Allen region labels. The asymmetric 95th-percentile surface distances (BM03→annotation 1.66 mm, reverse 0.22 mm) are likely contaminated by the 68-section BM03 slab's cut faces and are **not** an anatomical registration-error estimate. No expert landmarks, specimen-specific nonlinear refinement, blinded region validation, or held-out specimen was used. **Do not use these pulled annotations as BM03 training truth yet.**
