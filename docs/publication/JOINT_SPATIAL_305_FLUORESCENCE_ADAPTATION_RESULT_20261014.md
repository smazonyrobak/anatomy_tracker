At batch 600, the adaptation improved the withheld TRAIN animal by **5.905 mm (75.4%)** versus its matched control, clearing the predeclared ≥1.0-mm and ≥25% clauses. The synthetic mean regressed by 0.103 mm, below the 0.20-mm ceiling, and capture was unchanged. The adapted `AL1A` first choice was within 1.5 mm of its approximate human plane for 34/52 sections, with 90th-percentile gap 3.908 mm and four >5-mm failures (control 0/52, 9.571 mm, 46 failures). Median unsigned normal gap fell to 6.54° versus the control's 73.21°. This independently reproduces the *kind* of acquired-domain improvement previously seen in the separate 187 scratch lineage; no 187 weights, features or pseudolabels initialized 305.

The fixed difficult synthetic strata remain limited: on nine ≥45° cases, first-choice means were 1.936→1.995 mm (control→adapt); on five <10%-visible cases, 6.010→6.016 mm; on 11 gray-background cases, 2.113→2.264 mm. These tiny groups are diagnostic, not population performance. They show that fluorescence adaptation did not fix sparse-support or blind plane-selection failures. The earlier 305 joint-fit gate still failed against support-only and intensity-shuffled controls; the present run trains only pose/image-feature modules and does not repair or validate fit-to-pose feedback, the local deformation field, uncertainty calibration, or physical arbitrary-oblique animal accuracy. It must **not** replace the GUI model.

**Process decision:** use short matched, animal-separated tests to isolate one bottleneck at a time. This pilot establishes that acquired fluorescence supervision is needed for candidate capture and that 600 batches are enough to detect its effect; do not repeat a long synthetic-only pose schedule or scale to a million presentations now. Next, measure whether this shared-feature adaptation damaged older acquired coronal/sagittal pose and exact-pose local mapping. Then address the independent joint selector failure using a fixed synthetic/acquired candidate bank with explicit support/intensity-null and deformation-cost comparisons; scale only if the blind gate improves without losing full-sphere/sparse cases. Preserve official DEV/CAL/FINAL, calibrated electrode claims, and DeepSlice comparison until internal gates pass. The existing near-coronal Lupori animals cannot substitute for independently labeled physical steep-oblique cuts.
