# 089: train the atlas comparison to revise pose and tissue mapping

Frozen before the 089 run. This is a synthetic-development mechanism test, not a model-release or probability-calibration result.

## Question and prior evidence

The 085/088 correspondence descriptors can compare image and atlas patches, but the 085 checkpoint has exactly zero pose-update and spatial-map-output weights. Hence its 14-branch fitted selector yields 2.587 mm selected versus 0.992 mm physically best mapped CCF error on the frozen 061 DEV panel without having learned the proposed joint feedback. The fixed geometric correction in 087 worsened errors; that does not test a trained correction. Can a learned comparison update both the rigid plane and local tissue map, with anatomical fit information fed back into a second pass? This is motivated by [SynthMorph's joint affine/deformable learning](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11247402/) and [SVoRT's iterative slice-to-volume updates](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10129054/), neither of which establishes performance for damaged single-slice mouse histology.

## Frozen training

Initialize model and 083 correspondence head from the exact 085 batch-1,000 checkpoint. Retain the learned 083 image/atlas embeddings and temperature. Unfreeze the direct old/anchor pose outputs, bounded local-map modules, and the feedback head's spatial, summary, pose-update and map-evidence modules. Keep the atlas, image encoder, uncertainty and fitted ranking weights fixed; ranking can be retaught only after correcting the physical result. No external pretrained weights, pseudolabels, real-label fitting or public benchmark.

Use 6,000 fresh eligible synthetic TRAIN sections, batch size one, from the 64 TRAIN deformation bases; save at 0/1,000/3,000/6,000. Each draw independently samples its plane, contrast/background, cut artifacts and deformation; do not deliberately make paired copies of a physical section. Keep per-draw seed/provenance. Build the blind 8-old/6-anchor beam from the model prior. Train two *existing* beam branches per section: its prior top and the physically best branch, or prior runner-up if the two coincide. Truth may select training branches but never inserts or reranks a branch at inference.

Train the full probabilistic pose bank against 3-D physical-point/normal error, then perform two differentiable shared 083 atlas-feedback passes. Between them, fit the first predicted plane with a bounded, affine-projected-out 64-grid local map and pass its warp magnitude/roughness, atlas support and correspondence reliability into the next pose update. Fit the final 96-grid local map and backpropagate surviving-pixel 3-D CCF error through both updated pose and map. Penalize local displacement/roughness. Add native atlas-image fit only under a detached, soft plausibility gate near 2 mm first-pass physical error. At the first batch and every 100 batches thereafter, cap its weight monotonically by the measured 20%-of-base gradient ratio in each nonzero sampled old/anchor pose, warp, feedback-pose and feedback-map parameter group; no positive floor. The measured cap is held between audits, so it is a safeguard rather than a proof of a per-batch bound. This addresses the 008 fit-gradient imbalance. AdamW with cosine decay; record losses, fit-gradient norms and exact config/source/parent/checkpoint hashes. All inputs, computation and output remain on I:.

## Prespecified development decision

After the run exits, independently verify the complete frozen receipt, training draw count/provenance, finite gradients and saved checkpoints. Evaluate only the four prespecified checkpoints on the unchanged 061 synthetic DEV panel and the same 086 14-branch beam, with raw candidate rows and eight deformation-plan-equal aggregation. Report selected and best-of-14 96-grid mapped CCF error, five-point pose error, local warp magnitude, score-vs-physical-error rank and separate exact-black/raw/imperfect-brush strata. A promising physical-feedback result requires at least 0.3 mm improvement in selected mapped error from 085's 2.587 mm without worsening the 0.992 mm best-of-14 error, and no severe appearance-stratum regression. These are a mechanism gate, not biological/GUI qualification. If corrected planes improve but frozen fitted ranking fails, learn ranking on corrected candidates as the next targeted step; do not infer failure of the pose/map feedback from the frozen score alone. If physical correction fails, inspect candidate-specific correction and gradient evidence before adding capacity or training longer.

Hold out final-test animals, keep strict animal-level future validation and acquisition provenance, and do not make calibrated 90% electrode-region claims or compare to DeepSlice yet.
