# 254: direct-head full-pose signal on matched v7 synthetic TRAIN draws

The frozen 251 direct 64-anchor head distributes its image-only choices over 38 anchors, versus 4–5 for the shared-head comparisons, but its normal cross-entropy remains 4.076 nats against 4.159 nats for uniform mass, and all of its non-normal pose outputs are untrained. Four fixed-stratum 247 visual examples also expose narrow predicted atlas fields of view. This experiment tests whether giving the direct head a physical full-pose target repairs both failures. It is a mechanism study, not a deployable model.

Initialize the full 64-anchor one-shot joint architecture randomly with `shared_anchor_pose=False`; load no prior weights, features, labels, or pseudolabels. Use the exact 249/250/251 seed and independently drawn v7 synthetic TRAIN stream: 3,000 batches of four eligible 192-pixel sections, for 12,000 accepted presentations from 64 TRAIN 3D deformation bases. A presentation gets one independently sampled physical plane and appearance; never deliberately pair the same section with a different background. Retain all accepted and rejected draw identities.

Optimize the 15°-width antipodal 64-anchor normal cross-entropy plus `0.25 ×` the target-nearest-three-anchor mean surviving-tissue rigid error in millimetres, taking the better raster reflection for this geometric term, plus `0.1 ×` a normal-target-weighted binary cross-entropy for the true reflection. The latter corrects the 249 training/readout mismatch in which the reflection-selection logit received no supervision. The rigid target uses 256 sampled valid tissue sites per presentation. Keep AdamW learning rate `1e-4`, weight decay `1e-4`, global gradient clipping at 5, and the same 0/500/1500/3000-batch checkpoint schedule. Record component losses, selected-coordinate error, gradients, RNG/optimizer states, source/protocol hashes, and checkpoint hashes on I:. Only the pose and reflection pathways receive gradients; the map, atlas-fit scorer and refiner are not trained here.

After training completes, compare the batch-3,000 image-only prediction on exactly the 227 eligible cases of the already frozen 248 synthetic DEV panel with 247 and 251. Report subject-equal and case-level normal-axis error, image-selected rigid coordinate error, and reflection-oracle rigid error separately; report centre and in-plane field-of-view errors so a narrow-plane loophole cannot masquerade as success. A pass requires last-500 TRAIN normal CE `<=3.659`, subject-equal axis error `<=42.78°` (at least 10° below 251), and subject-equal image-selected rigid error `<=3.75 mm` (at least 0.5 mm below 247). Report each condition even if the combined gate fails. No CAL/FINAL animal data or public benchmark may enter training or checkpoint selection. Failure stops expansion of this pose stage and calls for a different evidence-led intervention; it does not prove arbitrary-plane recovery impossible.
