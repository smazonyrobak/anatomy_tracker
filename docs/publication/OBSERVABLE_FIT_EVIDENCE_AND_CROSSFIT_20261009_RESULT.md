# Observable atlas-fit evidence: real but inadequate scalar signal

The first frozen TRAIN-only HOG assay (`observable_fit_evidence_train_assay_001`) completed, but its gradient-magnitude epsilon assigned artificial orientation mass to perfectly flat pixels. Its output remains preserved and bound to Git commit `bfbb313`; it is **not** used for this decision. The [predeclared correction](OBSERVABLE_FIT_EVIDENCE_TRAIN_ASSAY_002_PROTOCOL_20261009.md) used 96 **new**, independently drawn eligible v2-artifact physical sections from 110 attempts. All 96 physical IDs were distinct, the frozen batch-653 model made no weight updates, and post-run hashes, counts and prior/oracle means were independently verified.

On the corrected 96-case 96-grid assay, eight-candidate pairwise concordance with known *synthetic* mapped error was **0.610** for rigid-plane unsigned HOG and **0.612** after the inherited predicted warp, versus **0.635** for the inherited learned fitted score and **0.617** for image-prior score. Both HOG variants exceeded the prespecified 0.60 all-case criterion and remained above chance on raw backgrounds and heavy-oblique cuts. Thus coarse atlas/image anatomy carries a measurable signal. But choosing a plane with either HOG score *alone* gave **2.840/2.827 mm** mapped error, compared with **2.661 mm** for the inherited fitted scorer and **1.712 mm** for the truth-best of eight. Predicted warp magnitude and roughness had only **0.533/0.541** pairwise concordance, so “the wrong atlas plane needs more warping” is **not** a reliable scalar rule in the current model. This does not refute the desired joint-feedback architecture; it shows that its present fit-difficulty proxy is weak.

The bounded [base-subject-disjoint five-fold cross-fit](OBSERVABLE_FIT_RANKER_CROSSFIT_001_PROTOCOL_20261009.md) combined image prior, inherited fitted score, rigid HOG and warped HOG with fixed ridge regularization. It **failed** the predeclared gate. Across 44 synthetic deformation-base subjects, equal-base selected error worsened **2.400→2.480 mm**; case-equal error worsened **2.661→2.785 mm**, including raw and heavy-oblique groups. No weights of the main model were changed. Do not add this small scalar ranker to the network, unfreeze joint pose updates on its signal, or spend a larger training budget tuning it on the same cases.

The remaining work is not a better hand-tuned “warp difficulty” number. A correct candidate must be supported by *spatially distributed anatomical correspondences* that remain coherent before and after constrained deformation, and the model must learn to reject absent/ambiguous landmarks. The prior 099 hard-match descriptor improved synthetic point matches but its operational soft correspondence was still worse than the unchanged-plane control, so that branch is not yet a solution either. The next experiment should fix the match aggregation/reliability at the point level on TRAIN data and demand a newly frozen, base-identity-disjoint selected-plane improvement before connecting the fit gradient to the pose head. In parallel, calibrate synthetic defect types and rates to acquired TRAIN slides; the current v2 is stress augmentation, not a measured lab simulator. Physical steep-oblique animals and expert truth remain necessary to decide any angle-family compromise. There is no current basis to abandon unrestricted-angle coverage merely because the present selector is poor.
