**Decision: do not use the 102A displaced atlas correspondences to update plane pose or tissue deformation.** Continue to develop the full-sphere model, but do not launch the proposed 102B joint-feedback continuation or scale the same matcher merely because its training loss fell.

The 4,000-presentation matcher improved correct-match fraction over 099 on **8/8** synthetic plans for both candidate subsets, and no nearest-axis family or appearance mode regressed by the predeclared 3-point tolerance. Yet it remained **5.70 percentage points below doing nothing** on the physically best candidate, and **0.71 points below** on all candidates. The prespecified gate required **3 points above** the no-shift control for best candidates and **2 points above** for all. Both required conditions failed at 4,000; 2,000 failed more strongly. Mean physical error agrees: the decoded displacement still harms good fitted planes, while most wrong candidates remain wrong. This is not a blind selected-plane result and is not a real-image validation.

This result limits a specific proposed feedback signal, not arbitrary-plane localization in principle. It is especially important because the current synthetic artifact generator already covers the same broad *types* of appearance as this DEV panel, so missing real artifacts alone cannot explain failure **on this synthetic panel**. Separately, the earlier million-synthetic-presentation v8 run showed severe deterioration on weakly aligned real-donor images, so more exposure to the present simulator is not a credible stand-alone remedy. The [domain/angle audit](DOMAIN_AND_ARBITRARY_PLANE_100_RESULT_20261008.md) identifies oversimplified folds, tears, tile seams, missing focus defects, single-template anatomy and near-coronal framing cues; the [real-donor virtual-reslice audit](REAL_DONOR_VOLUME_RESLICE_104_FEASIBILITY_20261008.md) supplies a promising but weak-label TRAIN-only diversity source. We should first diagnose why this matcher moves anatomically good planes away from truth and why the blind candidate ranking misses its better candidates; then revise only the relevant model/data mechanisms and retest on a new frozen identity-disjoint panel. No DeepSlice test, probability calibration, GUI promotion or physical all-angle claim is justified.
