# 050: whole-plane evidence against coherent wrong anatomy

The unchanged scratch-lineage 019 model supplies its eight highest-prior pose/reflection branches. A continuation of the scratch-trained 025 image/atlas feature encoder renders each entire finite-thickness candidate plane at 128 pixels and scores spatially ordered correspondence, not a mean of local inlier counts. The new head sees local feature correlation plus image/atlas context. Training uses one newly drawn arbitrary-plane synthetic TRAIN section per batch, one independent appearance/artifact realization, and no deliberately repeated physical section. Its known plane and two independently perturbed planes enter only during TRAIN; inference sees the eight 019 branches and no truth, segmentation, prior-model labels or final-test data.

The shared cross-plane loss ranks the actual eight branches by their known physical tissue error, rewards the known plane, and contrasts it against support-matched wrong branches at least 2 mm away. The 019 pose/warp weights stay frozen for this representation stage. This tests whether a learned whole-plane image/atlas comparison can distinguish mutually coherent false local matches. A good injected-plane classification alone is **not** sufficient: the frozen DEV endpoint is truth-blind selection among the eight actual branches.

Evaluate checkpoints 0, 1,000, 3,000 and 5,000 on all 177 eligible sections from eight fixed synthetic DEV deformation identities, then on 64 weak-reference sections from six disjoint real DEV donors. The primary endpoint is identity-equal selected rigid tissue-coordinate error. A useful capture gate requires selected synthetic error ≤2.0 mm and ≥0.4 mm below the matched 019 prior, while maintaining at least 80% injected near-true versus support-matched ≥2-mm wrong-plane discrimination across all three appearance modes. Do not promote if weak-real donor-mean five-point disagreement regresses by more than 0.2 mm. The best eight fixed proposals cannot improve in this scorer-only stage; report their physical oracle error separately. If these gates pass, repeat on new synthetic identities before unfreezing coordinate prediction and connecting fitting loss back through the renderer. Neither synthetic identity nor weak Allen affine is an independent expert biological alignment; no probabilities are calibrated here.
