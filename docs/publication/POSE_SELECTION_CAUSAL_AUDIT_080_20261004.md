# 080: correct the pose-selection diagnosis before another training run

The 059 two-old/six-new inference beam inadvertently removed six of the 019 fitted candidates before scoring. Recomputing the frozen 019 batch-18,000 candidate rows shows an identity-equal **2.59120 mm** selected error when its fitted scorer may choose among all eight old candidates, versus **2.84490 mm** when it may choose only the prior's top two. In 57/185 sections, the fitted winner lay below image-prior rank two. The latter exactly reproduces 059's step-zero synthetic error **without changing model weights**. Therefore the often cited 019→059 0.254-mm step-zero “regression” is a beam-policy artifact, not evidence that 059 training damaged the inherited old branch. The later 061/075/077/079 246-section comparisons use eight old plus six anchors and do **not** have this particular truncation.

There is a separate verified training/inference mismatch in [the 059 trainer](../../training/train_one_shot_anchor_quality_059.py): the gate target compares the *truth-best physical error* among its prior-top two old and top four anchor branches, while inference must choose one branch by predicted score and later evaluates eight old plus six anchor branches. Recomputing the two gate preferences on the 079 frozen 246-section rows gives opposite old/anchor preferences on 31.3% of cases. That mismatch may impair rank learning, but is **not proven** to be the sole cause of 2.656-mm selected error. A perfect oracle choosing between the *prior-top old and prior-top anchor* branches would reach 2.062 mm; the truth-best among the actual 14 is 1.022 mm. A gate-only fix cannot create the missing near-true planes.

The limitations of the current representation are broader: [079](COARSE_REGION_EVIDENCE_079_RESULT_20261004.md) shows that unavailable perfect coarse or fine atlas-region IDs still select above 2.3 mm, [075](DENSE_AUX_075_RESULT_20261004.md) and [076](DENSE_FIELD_CANDIDATE_AGREEMENT_076_RESULT_20261004.md) show a predicted dense coordinate field did not choose near-true branches, and [053](RIGID_VS_WARPED_ATLAS_PATCH_053_RESULT_20261003.md) and [057](ATLAS_KEY_GEOMETRY_FACTORS_057_RESULT_20261003.md) show that a local descriptor's correct-location match loses to global lookalikes when orientation/scale are discretized. The 075 trainer already applied a branch-level soft physical-error target across all 160 old/anchor/reflection branches while updating the shared pose encoder, yet its direct selected error did not improve. Thus simply replacing 059's gate with another branch-level ranking loss would largely repeat a failed mechanism. A further scalar reranker or gate-only long run has a low ceiling; do not spend another large exposure on it.

The next architecture intervention should be **spatially coupled, whole-slice 2D↔3D evidence** over arbitrary antipodal orientations, with candidate quality and pose updated jointly with the existing deformation map. In particular, it must train on the model's wrong proposals and hard anatomical lookalikes, preserve the correct-pose local mapper, and measure blind near-truth proposal recall *before* longer training. [SLIV-Reg](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18683v1) demonstrates rotation-equivariant patch matching plus robust global pose fitting for single CT/MRI slices, but its same-modality performance does not establish histology transfer. [DeepSlice](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10514056/) trained on roughly 0.9 million synthetic **coronal** sections and itself recommends multiple images from a brain rather than relying on one; neither its scale nor its coronal accuracy is evidence that our arbitrary-plane single-image problem is solved by more of the present 059 training.

No checkpoint is promoted by this audit. The untouched final animals, public comparison, probability calibration and GUI replacement remain closed. The 019 candidate-row re-analysis and 079 gate-preference comparison are exploratory causal checks on existing frozen development outputs, not new benchmarks or model training.
