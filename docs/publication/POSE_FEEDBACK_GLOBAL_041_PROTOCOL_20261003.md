# 041 pre-run protocol: globally coherent 2D/3D matching with safe correction

039 proved a 3D context alone is insufficient: its learned soft correspondences were within 1.5 mm of truth for only 30%/48% of top-ranked/oracle-best-eight candidate tissue points after 1,000 fresh synthetic presentations, and its free geometric fit badly regressed real weak-reference images. 040 showed the *same* fit with unavailable exact snapped correspondences could reduce synthetic top-one 2.499→1.254 mm and best-eight 1.001→0.422 mm. The new intervention targets global context and when to trust a fitted correction; it does not increase warp freedom or repeat failed direct-coordinate training.

Warm-start the scratch-lineage 039 descriptor, retain the frozen 019 direct pose/warp predictor, and add trainable whole-image self-attention and a coarse whole-volume atlas context branch before the same 2D/3D cost volume. A learned, evidence-dependent gate blends each raw geometric fit with its prior plane and begins near zero correction. Its training target is whether that raw fit actually improves physical error; thus the model can abstain from harmful corrections. Two fresh unique arbitrary-plane synthetic sections and one weak-affine real TRAIN section enter each of 2,000 batches. Real sections are drawn without replacement **within 041**; their acquired pixels are not re-augmented, but reuse across separate training experiments is allowed. Exact synthetic dense correspondences and pose errors supervise the matcher; real inherited Allen affines supervise only pose/gating at low weight, not expert arbitrary-plane truth or exact voxel correspondences. All donor/specimen/experiment IDs and the real schedule are frozen in receipts on `I:`. Synthetic images still have one independent background/artifact realization per new physical section, with brush optional.

Pre-output startup correction: the first schedule builder excluded real images used by any of twenty *past experiments*, leaving only 595 of 263,754 TRAIN images despite the intended 2,000 draws. This was an unnecessary cross-experiment exclusion, not a biological split requirement. It was stopped before creating an output tree or performing batch one. The corrected scheduler still has 2,000 distinct real TRAIN sections in this run and never uses development, calibration, or final-test donors; previous experiments may have seen some of the same unchanged acquired images. This amendment is frozen before any 041 training batch.

Freeze checkpoints at batches 0, 500, 1,000, 1,500 and 2,000. After the process exits, evaluate all actual top-eight branches on the unchanged 177 eligible sections from eight synthetic development identities and 64 weak-real sections from six development donors. Report physical top-one, score-selected and best-eight errors; raw-versus-gated corrections; near-true and appearance strata; correspondence recall; and worst real-donor regression. A development pass requires ≥0.25 mm selected and ≥0.20 mm best-eight synthetic improvement over frozen 019, with ≤0.20 mm worst-donor weak-real selected regression. Even a pass would not qualify calibrated probabilities, arbitrary-plane biological accuracy, GUI deployment, or DeepSlice superiority. No public or untouched final animal benchmark is opened here.
