# 166 TRAIN sampler: arbitrary planes that intersect tissue

The frozen 151 full-box offset stream used by experiment 162 yielded 665 blank images in 2,000 TRAIN draws. A blank image cannot identify a brain plane, so the new [166 sampler](../../training/arbitrary_plane_panel_geometry_train_166.py) draws each independent arbitrary antipodal plane normal, roll, thickness, appearance, background and artifacts as before, but passes the plane through a newly sampled physical atlas-interior point from its TRAIN deformation base. It does **not** deliberately repeat a plane with a changed background and does not reject chance coincidences. The finite-thickness rendering, subject map, conservative image frame, and v4 artifact path remain unchanged. Every draw retains its base/animal lineage, support-point index/hash, physical plane, independent seed branches and appearance record.

One bounded [TRAIN-only QC](../../training/qc_support_intersecting_planes_166_train.py) on 128 fixed fresh draws, with no retry or tissue-based selection, produced **0/128 blank** sections, median 20.06% valid tissue, 2/128 under 2% and 10/128 under 8%. All 128 points were in-frame and on their planes (maximum residual 3.64×10⁻¹² µm); section IDs and plane origins were distinct. For context only, the frozen 162 full-box stream had 665/2,000 blank, median 7.73% valid, and 1,011/2,000 under 8%. The two samples differ in size and seed; this is coverage QC, **not a model accuracy comparison**. Frozen rows, summary, source hashes and 162 input hashes: `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/support_intersecting_plane_qc_166_train_001`.

Selecting a point uniformly from tissue weights plane offsets toward larger/deeper cross sections. Sparse tangential cuts remain possible but less frequent, so future training/evaluation must retain an explicit sparse stratum and report when an image is inherently ambiguous. The 166 result establishes more efficient brain-intersecting TRAIN sampling, not all-angle localization success or real-slice validity. No DEV, expert-animal truth, public benchmark or GUI model was used; 151 and 162 remain frozen.
