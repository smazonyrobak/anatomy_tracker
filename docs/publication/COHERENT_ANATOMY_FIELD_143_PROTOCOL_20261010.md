# 143: coherent image–atlas field before fitting-to-pose feedback

142 made a correct atlas key locally available for 95.6% of eligible v4 sites but scored only 9.9% correctly. A separate eight-plan exact-pose control scored 43/389 (11.1%) within 0.5 mm, versus 23/306 (7.5%) on its blind-near branch; support-only exact pose scored 30/389 (7.7%). This diagnostic cannot establish a population estimate, but it rules out the blind proposal as the sole explanation. No new pose feedback may use the 142 residual, which is self-consistency of predicted matches rather than anatomical agreement.

Keep the randomly initialized 132 one-shot probabilistic-pose/local-warp lineage. Add a 64² contextual source–atlas field to the *same model*, taking the shared image feature plus raw/optional-brush inputs and rendering seven finite-thickness atlas slabs around each candidate state. The field jointly scores bounded 3-D correspondences over the whole image and predicts a smooth local offset, visibility and uncalibrated fit energy. Its local cost context and doubled atlas resolution are the substantive changes from the frozen-parent, independent 32² key scorer. No pretrained weights, old application weights or pseudolabels enter.

First train the field with independently drawn v4/v3 physical sections, known dense CCF correspondences and same-section exact, near and wrong candidate states. Sample a new plane and all appearance/artifact settings independently for each section; do not make systematic recolored pairs. Use synthetic truth only for TRAIN supervision and posthoc DEV measurement, not inference. Include hard wrong planes with comparable atlas support; supervise unmatched locations and prevent a large, smooth warp from appearing easy. Keep a matched support-only arm with identical source and optimizer exposure. Continue the shared parent with synthetic direct-pose/map supervision and acquired TRAIN appearance/weak-pose retention once the field supplies useful anatomy, then enable differentiable fitting-to-pose feedback within this same lineage. Do not freeze the source encoder in that joint phase.

Training scale is adaptive but meaningful: first 10,000 updates with at least 30,000 distinct accepted synthetic sections, then continue toward 25,000–30,000 updates/75,000–120,000 fresh sections only while frozen held-out metrics improve. Preserve optimizer state, source/data hashes, seeds, exact section provenance and best checkpoint. Sample donor-uniform acquired TRAIN appearances/weak references without calling their Allen affines precise anatomical truth. Do not tune on the public DeepSlice benchmark.

Evaluate checkpoints on the frozen 248 eligible v4 and 243 eligible v3 synthetic DEV sections and separate acquired coronal/sagittal DEV donors. Mechanism gates, in order: (1) exact-pose 0.5-mm correspondence on globally reachable tissue sites reaches at least 50% and beats support-only by at least 10 percentage points; (2) blind-near-branch correspondence reaches at least 35%; (3) anatomically correct versus support-matched wrong-plane fit ranking reaches at least 75%, at least 10 points over support-only; (4) enabling feedback improves selected v4 mapped-tissue error by at least 0.3 mm and ≤1.5-mm capture by at least 10 points against a matched no-feedback continuation, with no meaningful v3 angle/appearance regression or >0.2-mm donor-equal weak-reference regression. Report coverage, strain, abstentions and failures; do not hide a poor point estimate behind uncertainty. These are development gates, not animal-level confidence intervals or release criteria.

If exact-pose anatomy specificity remains poor despite substantial exposure, investigate source/atlas fidelity and real artifact domain gap before attempting more pose feedback. Physical heavy-oblique accuracy, calibration, electrode-site/region probabilities, GUI replacement and a fair final DeepSlice comparison remain unproven and out of scope for this mechanism gate.
