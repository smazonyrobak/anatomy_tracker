# Acquired TRAIN image visual comparison for synthetic realism

Six distinct donors were sampled without replacement from the 40-donor, 653-section Allen sagittal-ish **TRAIN-only** prepared image set. The midpoint section of each selected donor was shown at the same fixed 0–1 display range as the six 221 synthetic final inputs. The source summary binds every section to donor, specimen, experiment, section, and image SHA-256; both source array and geometry hashes were verified before rendering. This is a visual distribution check, not an accuracy test or a held-out-animal evaluation.

The acquired images show fine-grained tissue texture, irregular tissue edges, small outside-tissue specks, and a wide within-tissue exposure range. The six atlas-derived synthetic sections from `POSE_FRAMING_221_GENERATOR_VISUAL_QC_20261011.md` show correct large-scale anatomical variety and some damage/background variation, but visibly smoother tissue patterns and simpler damage geometry. This is a *qualitative* domain-gap signal from two small, differently sampled groups; it does not identify which discrepancy causes the current plane-ranking error. Before scaling synthetic exposure substantially, compare matched TRAIN acquisition strata and measure whether appearance augmentation improves a fresh animal-separated development gate without degrading old-TRAIN retention. These six acquired sections are mostly sagittal-ish and cannot validate synthetic steep-oblique realism.
