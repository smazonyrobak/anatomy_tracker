# 326: bounded acquired-orientation transfer probe

Freeze a new random initialization of the 6.23-million-parameter C4 one-pass joint model. Run exactly 1,000 optimizer batches, each with four independently sampled eligible v10 TRAIN synthetic cuts and one acquired TRAIN section. Alternate the acquired domain equally among Lupori coronal, Allen coronal, and Allen sagittal. Do not load any previous model weights, features, or pseudolabels. Each synthetic base, variant, and seed is drawn independently; chance-similar planes are permitted and no plane de-duplication is applied.

Use the unchanged 321 synthetic pose/map/support objective and its tolerant weak-affine acquired objective. Allen and human atlas registrations are **weak** coordinates, not measured physical cutting poses. Keep `AL1A` out of gradients. Freeze 10 Allen coronal TRAIN-inner donors from the preexisting 58-donor pool excluded from the 308/309 acquired gradients and eight Allen sagittal TRAIN-inner donors from the 40 available; exclude those eight from sagittal gradients. Choose readout donors by the script's fixed SHA-256 ranking of donor IDs and one lower-median section per donor, before any training. Record all animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs, source-image hashes, accepted/rejected synthetic draws, and exact source/data hashes. No DEV, CAL, FINAL, public benchmark, or GUI deployment.

Read out at batch 0 and batch 1,000 only. On the **identical frozen** 321 TRAIN-inner synthetic panels, compare batch-1,000 to 321's batch-1,000 model, not its mature batch-12,000 model. Pass all synthetic nonregression clauses: inner first mapped mean at most 3.920 mm and at most 21/128 over 5 mm; steep first mapped mean at most 4.004 mm; sparse first mapped mean at most 5.756 mm. On the acquired TRAIN-inner donors, require at least 8/10 first-choice AP-family and 9/10 top-16 AP-family capture, plus at least 6/8 first-choice ML-family and 6/8 top-16 ML-family capture. Also require sagittal first-choice family to improve by at least four donors relative to this run's batch-0 model. A missing/incomplete readout fails; do not reinterpret weak-affine angle as physical pose error.

This is a transfer-feasibility gate, not model selection or promotion. A pass would justify a larger matched architecture/learning experiment and physical validation planning; a failure would direct attention to capacity, objective, data, or the short training budget. Neither result establishes arbitrary-oblique accuracy, local map utility, calibrated uncertainty, or superiority to DeepSlice. Do not extend the run or change thresholds after seeing the result.
