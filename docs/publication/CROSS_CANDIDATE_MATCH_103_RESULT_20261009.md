# 103 cross-candidate matcher: the fresh v3 gate failed

The TRAIN-only pilot finished 4,000 accepted batches from 5,130 independent draws, with 4,000 distinct physical-section IDs and no real labels or pretrained features. Accepted normals covered all three nearest-axis families (AP 1,431, DV 1,334, ML 1,235); 1,883/4,000 were more than 35° from their nearest principal axis. The batch-mean loss fell only from 0.2581 in batches 1–1,000 to 0.2554 in batches 3,001–4,000. That is optimization evidence, not localization evidence. The final receipt, 14 source files, config, draw and training logs, and all five checkpoint hashes were independently checked.

Eight fresh synthetic deformation plans generated 256 independent v3 sections, one appearance/artifact draw per plane. Of these, 237 met the frozen valid-tissue rule (176 raw, 33 exact-black brush, 28 imperfect brush). The panel, plan receipts, 256 image hashes, output files and five matcher checkpoints verified. Step zero exactly reproduced the inherited 099 logits and support. These are synthetic descendants of one Allen atlas, not eight biological animals or physical all-angle validation.

Step 1,000 improved the old selector by only 0.128 mm and step zero by only 0.105 mm, below the preregistered 0.20-mm threshold. It improved against both comparators in only 2/8 plans; the appearance nonregression and lower-support conditions also failed. At that step it chose the physically best branch in only 31/237 sections versus 24/237 for the old selector, and mean selection regret was still about 1.76 mm. No 103 checkpoint is promoted, and the development-selected step is not an unbiased test estimate.

The same step's mapped errors were 4.550 mm for raw/no-brush, 2.281 for exact-black brush, and 3.359 for imperfect brush; their physical-best candidate errors were 2.742, 0.838 and 1.489 mm respectively. This is a **warning about the inherited pose-candidate generator under v3 input**, not proof that black backgrounds are harmful: the 094 pose and warp weights were frozen and never trained on v3, and the mode groups are independent, unpaired planes. Within the 176 eligible raw cases, 139 had exact-black exterior; even these had 4.751-mm selected and 2.999-mm physical-best mean errors. Raw no-brush candidate capture, not just non-black exteriors, needs targeted work. The v3 panel also has fewer mean valid pixels than the old 099 panel (9,829 versus 10,892) and a much larger raw share (74.3% versus 36.0%); direct numerical comparison of panel errors is therefore not a matched architecture effect.

Step-1,000 selected error by nearest-axis angle bin was 3.887/4.170/4.024/4.187 mm for 0–15°/15–30°/30–45°/45–54.7° (25/69/124/19 sections). There is no unique heavy-oblique collapse in this small synthetic panel: all bins are bad, and 19 maximally oblique eligible cases are too few to certify that extreme. Its physically best candidate remains around 2 mm even in each bin. Thus the current limitation is not a proven mathematical impossibility of arbitrary-plane imaging; it is a measured failure of v3-robust proposal capture and candidate selection, aggravated by an unvalidated simulator-to-physical gap.

Independent raw-row checks reproduced all five eight-plan-equal selected-error means from 1,185 section rows, and confirmed 16,590 candidate rows form exactly 14 branches per section per checkpoint with one consistent old, evidence and physical-best selection.
