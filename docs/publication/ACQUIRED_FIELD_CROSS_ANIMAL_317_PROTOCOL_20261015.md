# 317: is the real local-field image signal shared across TRAIN animals?

The [315 no-update assay](ACQUIRED_FIELD_IMAGE_SIGNAL_315_RESULT_20261015.md) showed a modest benefit of the human local field on 52 `AL1A` acquired sections. The [316 matched adaptation](SHARED_REAL_MAP_316_RESULT_AND_DECISION_20261015.md) then failed to turn 188 other acquired section/field pairs into a nontrivial map gain. Before another architecture or training run, apply exactly the same fixed-plane image-information comparison to all valid `CC2B`, `CC4B` and `CC4A` sections. These three animals supplied 316 gradients, so this is an information/label-consistency audit, **not independent model validation**.

Use the source Lupori ZIP and frozen split, no checkpoint, optimizer or pseudolabel. For every valid section, reproduce 315's 192-pixel max(red,green)/255 p99 input, approximate human QuickNII pose, 96-pixel VisuAlign local field, unweighted full-canvas affine projection, ±50-µm normalized five-offset atlas rendering, common tissue-and-atlas support mask eroded 7×7 (minimum 400 sites), sign-invariant smoothed Pearson and negative eight-neighbor MIND 3-pixel MSE. Compare the same four fields: zero, human affine-free local, its negative, and raw full human. Freeze the exact source image, plane and common scoring mask within each comparison. Record all animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs, four source hashes, eligibility, raw scores, per-animal and pooled summaries, script/protocol/split/atlas binding and output hashes. All code, source, cache and reports stay on `I:`.

Descriptive consistency gate: in **each** of the three animals, at least 90% of valid sections are eligible and, for **both** scores, human local beats zero and negative local in at least 60% of eligible sections with positive median paired margins. Passing would justify a separately gated real-image self-supervision mechanism test, not large training or a performance claim. Failure redirects effort to field/pose-reference quality and cross-animal contrast/anatomy mismatch before further map training. Do not inspect DEV/CAL/FINAL animals or run DeepSlice.
