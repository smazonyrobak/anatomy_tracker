# On-policy atlas evidence in the joint one-shot model: fixed development comparison

The full arbitrary-plane goal is still open. This is one substantive test of the current bottleneck, not a qualification run or a public benchmark. Both arms continue the same randomly initialized standalone lineage from the frozen candidate-energy batch-8,000 checkpoint. They see the same independently drawn synthetic physical sections and reserved real TRAIN sections, keep the same 16 pose modes × two reflections, and train the pose predictor and constrained atlas-conditioned mapper together. Neither imports old application weights, features or pseudolabels.

Both arms receive the same all-32 physical pose supervision, listwise branch-ranking targets, score-weighted physical loss, mapped visible-tissue loss on the *predicted score-selected* and reference-best **predicted** branches, and locally gated differentiable finite-slab atlas-fit loss. The fit-weight first-batch gradient target is 20% of the base pose gradient with no 0.01 floor. The sole matched difference is the **candidate scorer's evidence**: the atlas arm sees each candidate's rendered atlas slice and local image–atlas correlations at 64 pixels; the direct-scoring control sees zero atlas channels. Both still use the atlas in their shared local mapper and fitting loss. Thus this comparison isolates atlas evidence in global branch selection, not the value of the whole atlas-enabled model.

Each arm is scheduled for 8,000 three-image batches, with independently drawn synthetic planes (two per batch) and one distinct reserved real TRAIN section. The final batch-8,000 checkpoint is the predeclared readout; intermediate checkpoints are for recoverability and curve inspection, not for choosing the best result on the new panel. The new frozen 256-row panel uses eight held-out **synthetic deformation identities**, 32 independently sampled arbitrary planes each, one preassigned appearance per physical section and retained low-support rows. It is not eight independent biological animals. The same 64 weakly aligned images from six Allen development donors are the real-domain guardrail, not expert anatomical truth.

Primary engineering readout: equal-synthetic-identity mean *selected visible-tissue physical error* and selection regret (selected minus retrospective best-of-32 physical error). Report all scored cases and all low-support abstention candidates, median, 90th percentile, cases over 5 mm, plane-normal error and each identity's paired effect. Compare atlas scorer against the matched direct scorer and the frozen parent on the exact new panel. A meaningful gain requires at least 0.5 mm lower selected error and 25% lower selection regret versus direct, while not materially worsening best-of-32 coverage, the >5 mm tail or weak-real donor-equal error. Treat thresholds as development decisions, not statistical proof; with only eight synthetic identities and weak real labels, no biological superiority claim is possible. If candidate coverage itself remains millimetres off, reranking alone cannot qualify the model.

Do not access the public DeepSlice benchmark or final-test animals, claim calibrated probabilities, or install these experimental weights as the qualified GUI default. Evaluate only after the panel producer and both training arms exit and their receipts and raw files are frozen.
