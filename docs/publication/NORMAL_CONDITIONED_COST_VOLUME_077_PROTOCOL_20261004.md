# 077: on-policy wide-context pose correction pilot

The frozen 059 lineage selects about 2.656-mm-error synthetic planes on the reused 061 DEV panel, although its truth-best of 14 is 1.022 mm and a **truth-assisted** normal-fixed similarity fit can reach 0.338 mm. The 075 dense field improved without improving selected pose, and the frozen 076 field-agreement score worsened both selected error and top-eight availability. Do not train another scalar selector or extend 075 unchanged. Test whether a spatially coupled 3D atlas/image signal can **move** the parent's actual planes. The 066b/073 truth-assisted fits are geometric ceilings, not inference results.

Freeze the 059 parent, its pose ordering, atlas mapper and fitter. For each input, retain its blind eight highest-prior inherited pose/reflection branches and six highest-prior anchor branches, with no synthetic-truth candidate inserted. A small new image encoder reads the parent's full-slice features and five input channels. For each candidate, render a finite-thickness atlas context at 13 normal offsets from −6 to +6 mm on a 24×24 chart spanning twice the candidate field; apply the exact reflected-chart convention. A grouped whole-slice spatial correlation between a 12×12 image feature map and each atlas depth produces a 13×13×13 offset cost volume. A shared 3D context head outputs one coupled seven-vector: local in-plane translation (±3 mm per iteration), normal offset (±3 mm), two normal-tangent tilts (each ±15°), roll (±15°), and isotropic scale (×1/1.2–1.2). Re-render the updated candidate and apply the **same** head a second time. Process candidate pairs to bound GPU memory. The zero-initialized output makes checkpoint zero a parent-parity control.

Train only this updater for at most 2,000 batches on fresh, independently sampled eligible synthetic TRAIN sections from the 64 existing training deformation bases (two per batch), plus one distinct reserved weak-real TRAIN section per batch. Exclude the 059 real schedule; record all draw and source identities. Train on **all 14 actual proposals** with visible-tissue physical-coordinate error for synthetic sections and low-weight (0.15) inherited five-point weak-affine error for real sections. The original prior weights training examples but never learns a new score or changes candidate order. Synthetic truth supervises correction only; it does not create a candidate or enter inference. Save checkpoints 0, 1,000 and 2,000 with source, parent, schedule and output hashes, all on `I:`.

Evaluate those frozen checkpoints on the fixed 246-section/eight-identity 061 synthetic DEV panel and 64-section/six-donor weak-real DEV panel. Score the unchanged-parent selected branch **after correction** and the truth-best corrected 14 as a separate, unavailable-at-inference availability bound. Use the 075 evaluator's 1,024 surviving-tissue pixels and weak-real five-point physical metrics. Report identity-equal means, each raw/exact-black/imperfect-brush and low-/higher-support synthetic stratum, each real donor, and the near-true subset whose original 059 best-of-14 is ≤1 mm. Check checkpoint-zero physical parity against both the recomputed 059 parent and the frozen 075 checkpoint-zero parent rows, which use the same physical sampling, before interpreting trained steps. The batch-2,000 checkpoint is the sole advancement decision; batch 1,000 is diagnostic, not a selectable winner.

**Stop gate:** corrected identity-equal synthetic selected error ≤2.40 mm **and** truth-best corrected 14 ≤0.82 mm; no weak-real donor or synthetic appearance/support stratum selected error >0.20 mm above its matched 059 value; and near-true-subset corrected best-of-14 ≤its matched 059 value +0.10 mm. Failure stops this updater branch before larger exposure. A pass is only an exploratory development direction: confirm on fresh synthetic identities before scaling, then seek genuinely held-out biological-animal evidence. Neither weak affines nor synthetic identities establish calibrated electrode-region probabilities, GUI readiness, or superiority on the untouched public DeepSlice benchmark or final-test animals.

This differs from 073–074 scalar atlas search, 039–041 independent point matches plus fitting, and 072's single-render correction trained with truth-best fit feedback. Rotation-equivariant 2D–3D matching and iterative rendering in the repository's literature notes motivate the architecture, but their modalities and multi-slice inputs do not validate single mouse-histology localization here. Do not launch training or evaluation before code review.
