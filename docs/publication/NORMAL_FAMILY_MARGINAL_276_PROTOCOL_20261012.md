# 276: zero-training normal-family marginal readout

The 275 continuation is testing whether more independently drawn v9 planes help localization; its interim acquired sagittal first-choice orientation has degraded while some synthetic top-16 capture improves. A single top-scoring branch may not represent the most-supported **plane family** when the model spreads its mode mass across several similar normals. Before changing architecture or training labels, test that narrow inference hypothesis without fitting any weights or looking at a public benchmark.

After 275 exits and its frozen result is independently audited, load only its checkpoint selected by the already committed new-synthetic-panel rule. For each mode, classify its antipodal plane normal by the largest absolute AP, DV, or ML component. Sum the model's existing normalized mode mass within each of these three families; no new temperature, prior or label-driven parameter is permitted. Select the highest-mass family, then choose the highest original joint mode/reflection score **within** that family. Compare with the unchanged global top-one rule on exactly the same candidate set. These mode scores are uncalibrated; family sums are decision quantities, not defensible probabilities.

Read out paired rows on the 275 new 64-case full-sphere TRAIN-inner synthetic panel, frozen 270 64-case panel, and 28 coronal plus 22 sagittal acquired odd-hash TRAIN donor median sections frozen in 272. Synthetic truth supplies site error and normal-family correctness; acquired Allen affines supply only a weak normal-family/angle diagnostic. The acquired images have not trained 275, but their acquisition geometry contributed to the broader synthetic nuisance distribution; neither this nor its synthetic base subjects is independent biological validation.

The family-marginal choice is worth a later donor/animal-separated confirmation only if, against the same checkpoint's unmodified top-one, it increases acquired sagittal family correctness by at least five of 22 donors, loses no more than one of 28 acquired coronal donors, and worsens mean first-choice site error by no more than 0.2 mm on **each** synthetic panel. Otherwise retain the original top-one decision. Regardless of outcome, do not interpret raw mode mass as calibrated electrode-region probability, promote to GUI, or run DeepSlice; physical steep-oblique truth, joint local deformation, fit feedback and calibration remain required.
