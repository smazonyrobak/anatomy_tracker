# 112: first image-dependent fitting-evidence pilot

The central goal is not achieved by the 108–111 direct-pose continuations. Before feeding fitting results into the pose predictor, train the *existing* v3 atlas-conditioned mapper and fitted-candidate scorer, starting from frozen 111 model weights while keeping its image encoder and pose heads fixed. Use only fresh TRAIN synthetic full-angle sections, each with known physical plane and observed-pixel CCF map. A batch presents an exact plane, a nearby perturbation, a support-matched wrong branch proposed by the frozen pose predictor, and a different image evaluated at the exact plane. Keep candidate priors equal so the fit scorer must use image–atlas agreement rather than the pose prior. Supervise exact/near dense mapping, constrain deformation, and rank exact/near above wrong and matching-image above swapped-image. This bounded 512-batch pilot is a diagnostic, not a deployable model.

After the run exits, evaluate at the frozen parent, intermediate and final checkpoints on a fixed, identity-disjoint synthetic development panel. Require the mapper at the exact plane to improve on rigid placement, and require full atlas-intensity fitting to rank correct/near versus support-matched wrong candidates better than an atlas-support-only control. Inspect gradient *direction* toward the correct plane at several near starts, not merely nonzero gradient magnitude. Report valid-tissue spatial error, ranking, hard-candidate support and geometric gaps, and failures. Only if those gates show useful fitting evidence should a second phase send fitted energy back into the pose predictor's global mode/reflection probabilities while retaining direct pose supervision; continuous pose updates remain gated by a verified directional basin. No real-animal accuracy, calibration or GUI-readiness claim follows from 112.
