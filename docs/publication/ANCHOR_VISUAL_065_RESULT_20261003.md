# 065 pose-specific visual branch — frozen development result

The 8,000-batch pilot completed on `I:` and was evaluated only after exit. It saw 16,000 accepted independently drawn synthetic TRAIN sections (5,242 raw, 5,394 exact-black, 5,364 imperfect-brush) and 8,000 unique within-run weak-real TRAIN sections. All synthetic physical-section IDs and real animal/specimen/experiment/section tuples were retained; no deliberate paired-background versions were made. The separate anchor encoder/lateral pyramid and full-pose heads were trainable; the 16-mode old branch and atlas-conditioned local mapper/fitter/ranker were frozen. The initial 065 prediction exactly reproduced its 059 parent on the development panel.

On the already reused exploratory 061 panel (246 eligible sections from eight synthetic identities), the identity-equal visible-tissue rigid errors were:

| Frozen source | Selected pose | Truth-best of 14 poses | Best of six anchor poses |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 059 parent / 065 step 0 | 2.656 mm | 1.022 mm | 1.758 mm |
| 065 step 3,000 | 2.677 mm | 1.037 mm | 2.082 mm |
| 065 step 8,000 | 2.672 mm | 1.029 mm | 1.795 mm |

The predeclared gate required best-of-14 ≤0.770 mm, selected ≤2.400 mm, no >0.200-mm weak-real donor regression and no appearance collapse. It failed at both trained checkpoints. At step 8,000 the selected pose was an anchor in <1% of synthetic sections; old-branch candidates still dominate. Selected normal/centre/frame errors stayed at 31.83°/1.87 mm/45.36°, versus 31.91°/1.86 mm/45.57° for the parent. The raw, exact-black and imperfect-brush selected errors at step 8,000 were 2.776/2.542/2.586 mm; raw cases never selected an anchor. On 64 separate weak-real DEV sections from six donors, donor-equal five-point disagreement was 0.694 mm for the parent, 0.902 at step 3,000 and 0.739 at step 8,000; the worst donor regressed 0.230 mm at step 8,000. These are inherited Allen affine references, not expert arbitrary-plane truth.

Training-window means did improve late: synthetic near-anchor error 2.894→2.584 mm and predicted-anchor mapped error 2.961→2.757 mm from batches 1–500 to 7,501–8,000. The matched development panel did not improve. Therefore lower training loss is not grounds to extend or promote this branch. The scientific conclusion is narrow: making the 059 anchor encoder trainable with the same full-pose/fit objectives did **not** improve complete-plane capture or selection within this pilot. It does not prove image-only localization impossible; the prior exact-pose mapping and atlas-image information controls showed a much better attainable local fit. The next change must test a genuinely different global pose-capture mechanism with direct atlas evidence, rather than longer training of 065 or another scorer of these proposals. No uncertainty is calibrated, no biological animal-level result is claimed, and no GUI model or public DeepSlice benchmark is changed.
