# 226: more pose exposure and brighter synthetic tissue did not fix blind pose

The [frozen protocol](POSE_APPEARANCE_EXPOSURE_226_PROTOCOL_20261011.md) continued the project's randomly initialized 221 framed checkpoint for 1,200 batches. Each batch contained three independently drawn synthetic physical sections (3,600 accepted unique plane IDs), one Lupori TRAIN image and one alternating older coronal/sagittal weak-reference TRAIN image. The new framed slots used v4 and localized-strain/v7 appearance; the third slot rehearsed older v3. AL1A was excluded from gradients. The run exited normally after 391 seconds of training following setup. The audit found 1,200 consecutive finite training rows, 3,720 synthetic attempts, 120 rejected draws, 3,600 accepted unique IDs, no AL1A gradient row, and zero mismatches across seven output files, 16 source files, parent, protocol and split hashes.

The fixed readout regenerated and hash-matched 64 prior framed and 32 legacy cases, then drew 64 fresh framed v7 cases. It compared the unchanged parent and 0/400/800/1,200 checkpoints on identical inputs, yielding 800 rows. Step zero reproduced the parent on every case. An independent audit recomputed the row-level medians and top-16 counts and found zero mismatches across the readout's four outputs, eight source files, five checkpoints and protocol hash.

The fresh v7 first-choice improvement at 1,200 batches is **0.029 mm**, not the required **0.300 mm**. Top-16 capture of a <2-mm branch rises from 32/64 to 38/64, and truth-best-of-160 improves by 0.103 mm, but the selected plane remains about 3.81 mm wrong at the median. Its first-choice antipodal normal-axis median is 47.4°, versus 50.9° for the parent. A descriptive visibility split shows 15 low-support (<15% visible) cases improving from 4.43 to 2.95 mm, while 32 cases with 15–35% visibility worsen from 4.03 to 4.08 mm; this small post-hoc stratum does not override the failed whole-panel gate.

One protocol wording needs a precise limitation: bases 60–63 were absent from the **new framed-geometry** training slots, but the older v3 rehearsal slot used those base animal IDs 68 times. The fresh v7 cases have unique physical plane IDs and independent random appearances, yet their synthetic base animals are not entirely unseen by this continuation, and the parent lineage had already represented this 64-base family. This is TRAIN-inner case-level stress, **not** animal-level validation or physical steep-oblique evidence. The v7 and v4 framed panels each contain 64 cases, so small median differences are unstable; the large gap from 3.81-mm selected to 1.56-mm truth-best still shows both candidate coverage and ordering defects.

**Decision:** the predeclared primary gate fails at every checkpoint. Do not promote, extend this exposure/appearance recipe unchanged, calibrate its probabilities, integrate it into the GUI, or run the public DeepSlice benchmark. More synthetic presentations and brighter tissue alone did not make the direct pose selector accurate. The next targeted design should share pose learning across orientation anchors and use candidate-specific anatomical fit evidence on blind proposals, with a genuinely new synthetic-animal base family and acquired TRAIN guards before scaling. Keep full-sphere geometry; the present failure does not prove heavily oblique physical slices are intrinsically impossible. Trustworthy animal-separated physical-oblique truth is still needed before any all-angle performance claim.
