# Frozen protocol — practical source-derived atlas score on fresh sections

The reused-panel oracle-tissue diagnostic 302 found hard-candidate atlas intensity signal, but its tissue mask is unavailable at deployment. This no-training pilot tests one simple **image-only**, optional-brush-independent reliability and sign-invariant photometric score on 128 *new physical sections* from eight synthetic TRAIN-inner base animals 56–63. These are new draws, not new animals; 302 informed the fixed design. This is not validation of acquired histology or a shippable model.

- Before reading results, draw 16 independently seeded eligible v9 sections per base and record all attempts. Use the frozen 275 proposer, its prior-ranked top 16 pose/reflection branches, a central-depth 48×48 atlas bank using 299's renderer but a **fixed 50-µm nine-point slab/PSF for every candidate** (the synthetic section's hidden true thickness is not given to the score), 299's support-matched near/far pair definition, and the same 256 fixed sampled valid pixels for **error measurement only**. No candidate/pose/warp search or model update. Run the module with `python -m` from this worktree root so hashed and imported training sources are the same copy.
- Form a 48×48 source image by 4×4 averaging of input channel 0. Estimate candidate-independent reliability as its 5×5 local mean absolute deviation (MAD), quantile-normalized from image Q50 to Q90 and clipped to [0,1]. This uses no valid/visible/fragment truth, outline, brush flag, segmentation, or user-drawn mask. If reliability sums to zero, full and support-only scores fall back to frozen prior.
- For each candidate, support is the central atlas plane's support>0.95. On this candidate's supported cells, compute absolute reliability-weighted Pearson correlation between source and support-normalized atlas intensity, provided the effective weighted count is ≥32. Let coverage be supported reliability mass divided by total reliability mass. Full evidence is `coverage × correlation − 0.5 × (1 − coverage)`. The matched support-only evidence drops the correlation term. A uniform-weight full score is a descriptive ablation. All three select `argmax(prior + 4 × evidence)` with identical beam and constants; compare raw near/far evidence separately from selected-pose error.
- Save all 16 candidate priors, rigid-site errors, supports, three evidence vectors, selected indices, provenance, scores by brush/background/inversion/obliquity/base and hashes. The uniform-weight arm is a descriptive ablation; this gate does not establish that MAD reliability helps unless it also beats uniform weights on fresh confirmation. Near/far pairs are selected with synthetic truth for this conditional diagnostic and are not inference-time selection rates. Keep DEV/CAL/FINAL and public benchmark sealed. No prior external trained weights/features/pseudolabels.

The predeclared conjunction for a *promising practical diagnostic* is: ≥60 matched pairs including ≥30 without brush; full raw evidence prefers near in ≥75% of all and ≥70% of no-brush pairs; at least 10 percentage points more overall near wins than support-only; full selected mean rigid-site error improves ≥0.20 mm over frozen first choice and ≥0.15 mm over support-only; ≥6/8 base means are non-worse than frozen first choice; no-brush mean improves ≥0.10 mm. Failure rejects this fixed heuristic, not atlas information or arbitrary-plane feasibility. Passing motivates fresh animal-disjoint confirmation and design of a differentiable approximation for joint pose/deformation training, contingent on an explicit gradient check; this thresholded, argmax-based script itself is not such a training loss. Neither result licenses GUI promotion or calibrated probability claims.
