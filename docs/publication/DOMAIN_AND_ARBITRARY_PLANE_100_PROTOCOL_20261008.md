# 100: image realism and arbitrary-plane feasibility audit

This protocol follows the frozen 099 pilot; it does not change its data, optimizer, checkpoints, or scale-up readout. The aim is to decide whether a single observed section contains enough information to recover its plane, and whether our training images represent the observations we expect. A good result on one-template synthetic data is not evidence of real arbitrary-angle performance.

## What the active generator actually makes

The 099 TRAIN stream uses `arbitrary_plane_streaming_synthetic_v7_64.py` through `arbitrary_plane_one_shot_stream.py`, **not** the older synthetic generator whose domain-audit v2 documented scratches/compression. It samples an independent normal, roll, position, thickness, subject deformation, image warp, and appearance for each section; it does not deliberately reuse a plane with only a new background. The virtual population is 64 independent deformation bases with 64 global-affine descendants each, all rendered from one Allen template. Half the framing draws borrow a TRAIN near-coronal acquisition-size tuple even when the normal is arbitrary. This can create atypical crops for oblique sections; record and audit it rather than treating virtual IDs as independent animals.

Current active appearance: gain 0.6–1.4, gamma 0.6–1.6, inversion, 0.005–0.05 pixel noise, background mean 0–0.8 and slopes ±0.15, coarse illumination/background texture, and one of raw, exact-black, or imperfect-brush inputs. The brush error is a simple 1–8-pixel dilation or erosion. Current physical/artifact events are a smooth 4/8/16-grid warp, a narrow tear/gap with one-sided 1.5–6-pixel shift (22%), missing ellipse (16%, plus an independent 20% base ellipse), bright ridge called a fold (20%), circular ring called a bubble (15%), and a single axis-aligned brightness seam (25%). These are stochastic per section, but several are visual approximations rather than the physical phenomenon: the fold does not overlap tissue, the ring does not refract it, and the seam does not stitch displaced tiles.

There is no active compression, scratches, knife chatter, explicit defocus, multi-tile shading/seams, true overlapping folds, or independently displaced intact fragments. Border-padded warping plus masking needs direct visual inspection for repeated-edge artifacts. These omissions are hypotheses about a synthetic-to-real gap, not proof that adding every effect helps. The old v2 generator's richer list must not be cited as if the active TRAIN stream used it.

Before any million-presentation scale-up, render a small, once-only grid from the **actual** generator with, for each distinct independently drawn section, the atlas plane, clean subject render, observed artifacted input, optional brush, truth plane normal/position, and artifact receipt. Include steep obliques from all three nearest-cardinal families and small/partial sections. Show this to the user. Compare to a donor-separated sample of actual acquired sections by tissue-area/crop, intensity/background, edge and frequency statistics, disconnected-component size, and artifact prevalence. Use the comparison and visual inspection to prioritize a compact generator revision. In particular, address actual focus/stain/scanner variability, irregular tissue loss and detached fragments, and acquisition seams if they are prevalent; preserve exact observed-pixel-to-atlas targets and mark overlap/occlusion unobservable. Do not create paired appearances of the same training plane. Freeze versioned sources/seeds/receipts and retain a pre-revision synthetic confirmation set so the new generator cannot silently move the goalposts.

## Is any plane recoverable from one section?

The Allen CCF is a 3-D atlas, so arbitrary geometric reslicing is defined. But its nominal 10-µm isotropic volume was built from serial two-photon images acquired every 100 µm through-plane and interpolated; off-axis fine texture may not resemble a real cut equally well in every direction. [Wang et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8152789/) Arbitrary-orientation single-slice registration has worked for other 3-D medical images, not automatically for histology-to-population-atlas matching. [SLIV-Reg](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18683) DeepSlice's published mouse-histology evidence concerns coronal sections and recommends 10–20 same-block sections for angle estimation. [Carey et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10514056/) Therefore neither success nor impossibility for arbitrary single histology sections has been established here. A tiny, missing, or feature-poor fragment can be intrinsically ambiguous even with a perfect model; flexible warping can make wrong planes appear plausible.

On the newly frozen 099 identity-disjoint synthetic DEV panel, report section counts and performance by nearest AP/DV/ML plane-normal axis, and by `acos(max(abs(normal)))` bins 0–15°, 15–30°, 30–45°, 45–54.7°. Also stratify physical offset, retained tissue/valid fraction, bilateral visibility and disconnected components, artifact burden, and appearance. The 256 sections and eight synthetic deformation identities are exploratory, not enough for narrow error bars in every crossed stratum. Expand with **new independent planes and deformation identities**, not new appearances of the same section, if a stratum lacks support.

For each stratum separate: truth-in-beam recall; best physical error achievable among the frozen beam (oracle); actual selected plane/mapped-landmark error; and required warp magnitude. High oracle with poor selection means feature/ranking failure. Low oracle means global proposal/pose-coverage failure. If clean full-atlas renders also admit physically distant, similarly fitting planes under the **same bounded** photometric and warp allowance, flag true image ambiguity and a multimodal posterior. The false-plane fit tolerance must be fixed from acquisition noise/landmark uncertainty *before* inspecting the score. Synthetic-clean success but acquired-image failure instead indicates atlas/domain mismatch. Check orientation-dependent atlas texture against real oblique sections; a truly isotropic cytoarchitectural volume such as [STAM](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12422980/) is a potential independent stress-test source only after availability, licensing, and CCF mapping are verified, not a mid-run replacement or a substitute for multi-animal validation.

The present Product-5 real training/development sections are nearly all coronal (median 4.91° off AP, none over 30°) and have registered-affine weak references, not independent exact pose/dense warp truth. Thus they cannot validate the all-angle requirement. Acquire a separate animal-split, intentionally AP/DV/ML and steep-oblique cohort with measured cutting geometry or block-face/3-D reference and expert landmarks. Keep separate calibration and untouched final-test animals; report each orientation/visibility stratum's normal error modulo sign, physical landmark/electrode-site error, region assignment, 90% coverage, and accepted-risk curve with donor-level intervals. Fix acceptable error from electrode-site scientific use, not after seeing results. Only calibrated unseen-animal probabilities can support a claim such as “90% probability the site lies in TRN.”

Do not abandon full-sphere prediction because the current model fails. If a reliable operating boundary is demonstrated, keep arbitrary planes representable and return ambiguity/abstention. Offer optional rough cut family/angle, slice range or neighboring ordered sections to resolve hard cases, with broad validated cones that include heavy obliques around coronal, horizontal, and sagittal cuts. A hard near-coronal-only default would not satisfy the researcher's purpose. Public DeepSlice comparison and GUI promotion remain closed until the improved model and real animal-level development data justify them.
