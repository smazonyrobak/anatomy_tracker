# Frozen inherited atlas-fit readout: narrow gate passed, not deployable

The inherited atlas-fit selector improves branch choice after the mixed-pose continuation, meeting the [predeclared mechanism gate](SAGITTAL_MIXED_POSE_FITTED_DEV_READOUT_001_PROTOCOL_20261008.md). On 241 eligible sections from eight separate synthetic deformation plans, equal-plan mean full-resolution mapped-tissue error fell **3.158→2.936 mm** (0.222-mm gain), with 7/8 plans improving. The same checkpoint and eight image-prior branches were used by both paths; the fitted path changed branch in 118/241. On weak Allen real references, donor-equal five-point disagreement fell **0.652→0.627 mm** for 64 coronal sections/six donors and **2.425→1.809 mm** for 158 source-available sagittal sections/eight donors. The sagittal fit assumed an unmeasured, fixed 50-µm thickness. Neither weak affine set is expert anatomical truth.

The gate is a *necessary mechanism screen*, not a declaration of accuracy. The truth-best **among the same top-eight direct candidates** was 1.138 mm on the synthetic panel and 1.281 mm on the weak sagittal reference, leaving a large selection gap. The inherited pose update itself barely helped: leaving the top-prior branch selected but applying the refiner changed synthetic 3.158→3.148 mm, sagittal 2.425→2.420 mm and coronal 0.652→0.674 mm. This experiment supports the existence of some useful atlas-conditioned **ranking** evidence, not a well-trained feedback correction of the plane.

The synthetic improvements are uneven. Exact-black images improved 3.238→2.762 mm, imperfect-brush 3.101→3.012 mm, while raw-background images slightly worsened 3.180→3.185 mm. AP-, DV- and ML-nearest groups all improved, but the highest visible-tissue-support quartile **worsened 2.271→2.591 mm** despite having a 0.735-mm best-eight oracle. One of eight synthetic plans worsened by 0.291 mm. These were diagnostic strata outside the predeclared gate, not thresholds added after the result. They are scientifically important: the selector is least trustworthy in some of the better-observed sections, and a model trained to trust its current score blindly could regress. Do not promote this scoring path to the GUI or treat its normalized scores as probabilities.

This panel has independent synthetic deformation identities, but still derives from the *same Allen atlas* and has been read in other-model development; it is not physical oblique-animal validation. The real sagittal/coronal DEV sections were already used for batch-653 checkpoint selection on the direct metric, so this is a matched mechanism comparison, not an untouched final test. The source-availability exclusion remained exactly section `101345593`; the sealed holdout and public DeepSlice benchmark were not opened. Further work should train an observable reliability/fitting signal on TRAIN-only images and candidates, explicitly separating pre-warp agreement, post-warp agreement and deformation cost, then evaluate on a newly frozen identity-disjoint panel before letting fitting update pose. Artifact rates and physical steep-oblique transfer remain unresolved.
