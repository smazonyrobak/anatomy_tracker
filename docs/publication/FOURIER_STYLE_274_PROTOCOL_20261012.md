# 274: small label-free real-style continuation against an independent control

The 270 scratch-trained pose model improves on independently drawn full-sphere synthetic planes, but 272 fails gross sagittal TRAIN-family transfer and 273 rules out simple Gaussian low-pass filtering. Before increasing synthetic-only exposure, test a narrow label-preserving domain-adaptation idea. FDA (Yang and Soatto, CVPR 2020, https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Yang_FDA_Fourier_Domain_Adaptation_for_Semantic_Segmentation_CVPR_2020_paper.html) changes source-image Fourier amplitude while retaining phase and source labels. That result is from semantic segmentation, not proof of a histology registration benefit; this is a small matched pilot.

The v10 generator first makes one fully independent v9 physical plane, warp, artifacts, background and optional brush. On half of draws, it selects a random acquired coronal or sagittal **TRAIN** image, 50/50 by domain, and blends 40–80% of its central 9×9 non-DC Fourier amplitude into the synthetic intensity. Source phase and DC remain fixed. Only source-visible tissue is blended back; the original exterior, including exact black and optional brush semantics, remains unchanged. No Allen affine, pose label, learned feature, pseudolabel, or prior model weight from these acquired images enters the generator. A frozen preview of eight independent eligible planes is `I:/AnatomyTracker/previews/v10_fourier_style_274_002/atlas_and_input.png`; it is a visual plausibility check, not a validation set.

Split the 58 coronal and 40 sagittal TRAIN donors by even/odd first byte of SHA-256(`domain|donor_id`). Only even-hash donors supply unlabeled style images: take up to 16 evenly spaced section-number-ordered sections per donor into a GPU style bank (30 coronal and 18 sagittal donors). Only odd-hash donors from the frozen 272 median-section selection (28 coronal and 22 sagittal) are used for acquired transfer readouts. Preserve all donor/specimen/experiment/section/source hashes. This is still TRAIN-only and does not substitute for unseen-animal DEV or physical oblique truth.

Start **two** short pose-only continuations from the same 270 selected checkpoint and AdamW moments, each 2,000 batches ×4. The control sees new independent v9 planes; the style arm sees different new independent v10 planes. No physical plane is deliberately reused or appearance-paired across arms, so the draw streams are not exact paired controls. Keep the 270 pose loss, trainable modules, LR 5e-5, clipping, gradient base indices 0–55, and hold the 270 fresh/old TRAIN-inner synthetic panels fixed. Read out at batches 0, 1,000, and 2,000, including image-only top-16 and first-choice synthetic errors and held-style-donor acquired first-choice normal family/angle against weak Allen affines. The weak affines never enter gradients.

At batch 2,000, a style continuation justifies a larger *training* comparison only if it gains at least eight held-style sagittal correct families over the equal-budget control, loses at most two held-style coronal correct families, and loses at most four top-16 captures on the frozen fresh synthetic panel; it must also retain at least 40/64 old-panel top-16 captures. If not, reject this Fourier recipe. Even a pass would not promote a checkpoint, establish real localization, calibrate probability, justify full benchmark, or settle the joint deformation architecture.
