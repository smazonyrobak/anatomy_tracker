# Fitted-candidate evidence in the joint model: development screen

The prior on-policy atlas ranker failed: it viewed a rigid 64-pixel atlas slice before mapping, selected ~3.194 mm visible-tissue error, and did not beat its image-only control. True-mask raw post-mapping correlation also failed to beat the image prior. The next candidate scorer must *learn* from actual post-mapping evidence rather than treat that correlation as a likelihood. This is an efficacy screen in the current standalone random-initialized lineage, not a release or causal ablation.

Continue the frozen rank-009 atlas arm, retaining its 16 direct pose modes × two reflections, 256-pixel image encoder, finite-thickness atlas renderer and affine-free constrained local mapper. For the image prior's top eight actual predicted branches, map at 96 pixels in the original 256-pixel coordinate frame, re-render each deformed atlas slab, and score image/atlas features together with local displacement magnitude/roughness, atlas coverage, candidate pose and predicted pixel reliability. The chosen branch must then be mapped at full resolution for deliverable coordinates; the 96-pixel mapping is only the screening view. A synthetic valid-tissue target teaches the reliability channel, but **no synthetic mask is provided to the scorer at inference**. Real TRAIN sections have weak Allen pose references and no synthetic dense map; they train the shared image/pose features, not this post-fit head.

The new post-fit head begins randomly initialized. The remainder resumes the 009 checkpoint from this lineage; no old application weights, external pretrained features, legacy pseudolabels or DeepSlice data are used. Train for 12,000 three-image batches: two freshly drawn independent synthetic planes and one donor-balanced, previously unused real TRAIN image per batch. Preserve accepted/rejected synthetic draw provenance and real animal/specimen/experiment/section IDs. The all-32 physical pose loss stays active, while a listwise target based on the eight candidates' **mapped visible-tissue physical error** trains the fitted scorer. Score-weighted mapped accuracy connects its post-fit evidence back to the global pose and shared mapper; a tissue-reliability loss and displacement penalty discourage explaining wrong planes by ignoring tissue or excessive warping. This path does not equate raw local correlation with anatomical probability, and its outputs remain uncalibrated.

One full-beam forward/backward check must establish finite output and pose/head gradients before the long run. After the final checkpoint is frozen, evaluate on the already-consumed 256-section/eight-synthetic-identity arbitrary-plane development panel and the separate six-donor/64-image weak-real guardrail. Report *same-checkpoint* image-prior versus fitted-selection mapped and rigid visible-tissue error, top-eight reference-best coverage, tails over 5 mm, each identity's effect, low-support rows and real weak-reference error. The panel is for development only, not independent biological validation. A useful result requires at least 0.5 mm lower equal-identity selected mapped error and at least 25% lower selection regret than the same checkpoint's prior, no material worsening of top-eight coverage or the >5 mm tail, and no large real weak-reference regression. Passing this engineering gate would justify a matched control and longer joint pose/fit training; it would not establish calibrated probabilities or deployment readiness. Failure means redesign the feature/pose capture mechanism rather than scale this exact score unchanged.

Do not touch public DeepSlice labels or untouched final-test animals, claim real arbitrary-plane ground truth from near-coronal Allen sections, or promote these weights to the GUI. The model's existing generic `forward()` does not yet use the fitted scorer; inference wiring and GUI integration remain required after a positive development result.
