# Slide-artifact v2: mechanisms and QC, not a training approval

`training/arbitrary_plane_one_shot_slide_artifacts_v2.py` is a new TRAIN-only wrapper around the existing independent full-sphere v7 sampler. It does not modify the frozen 099/102 data or model. One call still draws one independently seeded physical plane and one appearance; it does not make paired black/gray copies. It retains the clean source cutting-plane state as the pose label, while the dense observed-pixel-to-CCF field follows local displacement.

The new mechanisms are independently moved surviving tissue with its original source location removed, overlap/occlusion masks, a geometric fold with overlaid tissue, bubble-like lens displacement and defocus, a shifted/defocused tile seam, and zero-padded resampling with explicit background fill rather than copied edge tissue. Raw inputs independently choose exact black, near-black, or inherited gray/confusing exteriors without asserting brush availability; exact-black exterior pixels remain zero after imaging effects. Existing v7 morphology/contrast/illumination/noise/partial-tissue variation remains inherited.

The only displayed QC is `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/one_shot_slide_artifacts_v2_qc_20261008_002/qc_grid.png`; its receipt records twelve attempted independent draws and six selected eligible, distinct planes. The grid deliberately selects for coverage of rare artifact *types*, so it is not an unbiased sample of frequency. Columns are the actual altered input, clean source atlas render, and valid/damaged-pixel overlay—not a predicted plane. The new generator and QC source hashes, exact random seeds, geometry/appearance parameters, source files, output hashes, and virtual-animal provenance are in `receipt.json` at that path. A separate preliminary `_001` QC directory was left untouched after cleanup was rejected; `_002` is the complete accepted review artifact.

Independent post-run checking confirmed six unique physical-section IDs, all six compressed sample hashes, finite valid CCF targets, and zero valid pixels at recorded fragment-overlap, fold-occlusion, or strong bubble-defocus locations. The generated fields contain 1,423 valid displaced-fragment pixels across the six selected cases. This is a **label-consistency and visual-mechanism check**, not a measured domain-realism or model-accuracy improvement.

Do not wire this source into million-scale training yet. Event probabilities in the code are deliberately high for stress coverage and have not been estimated from acquired sections; the grid cannot establish natural artifact prevalence. The virtual animals still reuse one Allen template, some source frames still borrow coronal acquisition geometry, and stylized folded/fragment shapes may differ from real mounted tissue. Compare a TRAIN-only acquired sample by background, focus, tissue continuity/fragmentation, artifact rate and crop before selecting training proportions. Then conduct a matched, identity-separated small development readout of plane proposal coverage, blindly selected mapped-tissue error and deformation validity. Preserve the existing pre-revision panel as a control and keep physically acquired oblique-animal validation separate.
