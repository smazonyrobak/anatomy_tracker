# 047 fitted-pose feedback pilot (pre-result protocol)

This pilot asks whether the existing scratch-lineage 046 slice/atlas matcher can provide useful pose/deformation feedback to the 019 one-pass model. For each image, the unchanged 019 model proposes its four highest-prior pose/reflection branches. Frozen 041 selects spatially spread tissue-query positions and coarse 3D atlas keys; frozen 046 scores oriented, finite-thickness atlas patches at those keys. A differentiable weighted plane fit combines those correspondences. The unchanged 019 mapper then predicts local tissue-to-atlas displacement at each fitted plane, and its fitted-candidate scorer combines image/atlas agreement and warp cost with the branch prior. The mapping and ranking losses train only the 019 pose head in this pilot. A fitting-evidence-only gradient must reach its coordinate outputs in batch one. All inference inputs are images/atlas/PSF, not synthetic truth.

The training run uses 500 batches of one newly drawn eligible arbitrary-plane TRAIN section each, with one independently randomized background/artifact realization per plane and preserved provenance; 019/041/046 weights and source hashes are recorded. Checkpoints are batches 0, 250 and 500. No model weights from outside this scratch lineage are introduced. This is an architecture-development pilot, not full exposure, real-animal validation, calibration, or GUI deployment.

After the run exits, compare those three checkpoints on the *same* frozen 037 synthetic DEV panel (177 eligible physical sections clustered by eight disjoint synthetic deformation identities). Report posterior-selected and physically best-four mean tissue-to-CCF error, posterior-selected rigid plane error, and appearance-stratified results for raw, exact-black and imperfect-brush sections. Batch 0 is the identical 019 initialization passed through the new inference path. The posterior-selected mapped mean must improve by at least 15% and reach at most 1.5 mm, with no appearance stratum worsening by more than 10%, before this stage is called promising. These criteria are only internal development gates; expert real-animal truth and calibrated probabilities remain mandatory before use.

The 037 panel has been examined in prior architecture stages, so success on it cannot independently establish generalization. No DeepSlice or final-test animal result is accessed in this pilot. The run and all outputs stay on `I:`; no rendered image is displayed or repeatedly inspected.
