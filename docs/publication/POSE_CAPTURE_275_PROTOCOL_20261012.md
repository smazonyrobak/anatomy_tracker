# 275: bounded v9 exposure extension with new planes and acquired-transfer guard

The 274 equal-budget v9 control improved the frozen synthetic first-choice site error from 3.015 to 2.525 mm, top-16 capture from 46/64 to 48/64, and old-panel top-16 from 49/64 to 53/64 in only 2,000 batches. Odd-hash TRAIN coronal weak-normal family stayed 28/28 and sagittal rose 11/22 to 13/22, but remains poor. The Fourier-style arm showed a directional sagittal gain yet failed its prespecified gate; do not use or scale that exact recipe. The new qualitative panel separates tiny-fragment proposal failure from larger-section misranking. This protocol asks whether *further ordinary v9 exposure* continues to improve both synthetic pose and acquired transfer, before redesigning the pose head. It is not a claim that more examples alone will solve registration.

Continue the project's own randomly initialized 274 **control** step-2,000 model and AdamW moments. Train only encoder, lateral feature path, pose modes, and normal-anchor pose/global heads, freezing local mapping and uncertainty. Use the unchanged physical-site/five-point/normal mixture loss and v9 independent-plane generator, with four newly drawn eligible physical sections per optimizer batch, TRAIN synthetic base indices 0–55, and no deliberately paired/reused section geometry. Run 8,000 additional batches (32,000 accepted presentations), LR 5e-5 for batches 1–4,000 and 3e-5 for 4,001–8,000, gradient clip 2. No acquired image, weak Allen affine, prior external model weight/feature/pseudolabel, DEV/CAL/FINAL case, or public benchmark enters gradients.

Before training, freeze a **new** 64-case v9 TRAIN-inner panel: eight independently drawn eligible planes from each of synthetic bases 56–63, with attempt logs, physical-plane IDs, and provenance. Keep both existing 270-fresh v9 and 268-old v8 panels fixed as regression checks. Keep the exact 28 coronal and 22 sagittal odd-hash TRAIN donor median sections from 272 as an acquired-image transfer guard. These donors are image-disjoint from 274's even-hash style bank, but the synthetic nuisance sampler used TRAIN acquisition geometry; this is not a fully independent animal-level validation set. Their Allen affines are weak registration metadata, not physical oblique ground truth.

Read out at added batches 0, 2,000, 4,000, 8,000. On synthetic panels record first-choice mean site error, top-16 ≤1.5-mm capture and mean normal-axis error; on acquired donors record first-choice cardinal normal family and median angle versus weak affine. Save all readout checkpoints. Select one checkpoint using only the **new** TRAIN-inner synthetic panel: highest top-16 count, tie-broken by lowest mean first-choice site error. Never select on acquired weak labels. The selected checkpoint justifies another bounded pose-stage continuation or joint-map training only if all of these hold: new-panel top-16 ≥52/64 and mean first-choice site error ≤2.3 mm; frozen 270-fresh top-16 ≥48/64; 268-old top-16 ≥50/64; odd-hash acquired coronal family ≥26/28 and sagittal ≥16/22. A failure blocks further v9-only scaling. A pass does **not** promote the model to GUI, prove physical arbitrary-oblique accuracy, calibrate uncertainty, or authorize DeepSlice benchmarking.

The all-plane promise remains a training-domain objective, not a demonstrated physical result. Sparse fragments may be intrinsically ambiguous and must ultimately trigger honest uncertainty/abstention or benefit from optional acquisition constraints, without silently narrowing permissible cutting angles. The trained local map, joint pose/deformation feedback, constraint conditioning, calibration on unseen animals, real oblique truth, and GUI integration remain separate required stages.
