# Global atlas-point proposal 024 — staged protocol

019/022/023 establish the immediate failure: the existing model's all-32 rigid proposal oracle averages 1.025 mm and supplies **no** candidate within 0.25 mm on the fixed 185-case synthetic DEV panel. An exact CCF-surface atlas render is clearly recognizable, but comparing planes about a millimetre away has a narrow and misleading local-similarity landscape. Neither a larger beam, 256-pixel rerender, oracle tissue mask nor the 020/021 ranker changes solved this. The next target is near-truth *proposal capture*.

[Brandstätter et al., WBIR 2024](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-73480-9_22) is the closest single-slice-in-volume parallel: it matches rotation-tolerant 2D slice features to 3D volume features, then fits a geometrically consistent pose rather than regressing the entire pose from one pooled image descriptor. It reports strong CT/MRI single-slice angle localization, while direct initialization in its comparison failed; this does **not** establish transfer to distorted mouse histology or to a fixed Allen atlas. [CoMIR](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/file/d6428eecbe0f7dff83fc607c5044b2b9-Paper.pdf) supports learned cross-modality local representations. This 024 path differs from the failed 013–015 absolute-CCF head (which regressed coordinates per pixel with no atlas search/consensus) and the failed image-key gallery (which retrieved coarse whole-plane cells but did not solve finite-frame pose).

Build the **proposal component** of the eventual single joint model, initialized from scratch: a 2D patch encoder for observed tissue and a 3D atlas-cube encoder with a shared 64-dimensional descriptor space. Synthetic observed-pixel→CCF correspondence supplies positives; other anatomical locations, including points from the same section, supply negatives. Independent random rotations/reflections of the atlas cube encourage orientation tolerance. No external model weights/features/pseudolabels. The image generator remains arbitrary-plane with independently randomized geometry, background/brush mode, deformation and artifacts. Retain exact source and synthetic subject provenance.

Use a short first stage of 3,000 batches × two fresh TRAIN sections × 32 surviving tissue points per section. The 192,000 point pairs are **not** 192,000 independent sections. Save the optimizer and random states and score the fixed synthetic DEV identities at 0, 1,000, 2,000 and 3,000 batches. For diagnostic retrieval, precompute a fixed, truth-independent bank of 100,000 CCF atlas-support positions at about 100 µm sampling. Query 32 known valid points per DEV section against this bank; GT validity is allowed only for this component diagnostic and must be replaced by a learned/optional-brush selection at inference. Report point recall@16 within 0.25/0.5/1 mm, grouped equally by synthetic identity, plus the fraction of sections with at least three spatially separated correct matches. A useful first gate is ≥20% recall@16 within 0.5 mm and ≥50% sections with three matches; failing it calls for redesign of descriptor/context scale, not automatic extension to a million presentations.

Only if this gate shows real retrieval should 024 proceed to robust full-frame geometric consensus and combine with the present atlas-conditioned warp/refinement in **one checkpoint/model**, then jointly train pose and deformation with fit feedback. That stage must be evaluated by near-truth pose capture, final selected tissue-coordinate error and donor-separated real weak-label nonregression. A patch-matching success alone is not a working replacement. Final expert-animal validation, calibrated trajectory/region probability, GUI integration and fair DeepSlice comparison remain downstream and must not be inferred from this pilot.
