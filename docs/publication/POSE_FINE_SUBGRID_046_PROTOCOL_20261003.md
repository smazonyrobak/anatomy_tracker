# 046 spatial fine matching and sub-grid correction

045 improves 1.5-mm key ranking but 044/045 show that the 041 search grid itself makes ≤0.5-mm truth keys available in only 28.1% of physically best top-32 lists. Simply increasing training batches for a classifier cannot cross that ceiling. This stage keeps the same shortlist and high-resolution finite-thickness rendering, but preserves the patch feature maps and computes a 5×5 local cross-modal correlation before predicting (a) a revised key score and (b) a continuous local three-axis offset from that key, bounded to ±1.5 mm. The offset is represented in the candidate plane's orthonormal frame and converted back to CCF coordinates, so it is valid for arbitrary cutting angles. This follows the coarse-to-fine idea of [LoFTR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Sun_LoFTR_Detector-Free_Local_Feature_Matching_With_Transformers_CVPR_2021_paper.html) and [2D3D-MATR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/papers/Li_2D3D-MATR_2D-3D_Matching_Transformer_for_Detection-Free_Registration_Between_Images_and_ICCV_2023_paper.pdf); neither has established performance on our histology problem.

Continue the scratch-lineage 045 checkpoint; initialize only the new pair/correction head randomly (zero final layer so batch zero reproduces 045 scores and zero offset). Frozen 019 and 041 generate actual off-plane proposals. Train 2,000 batches of two newly sampled, independently randomized synthetic TRAIN sections each (4,000 section presentations), with top-one, physically best and random proposals and one visible query each. Use a physical-distance soft list loss on the 32 proposed keys, negative-only lists, and smooth-L1 supervision for local offsets only where known synthetic tissue truth lies within 1.5 mm. No exact target is provided as an inference input. Save batch zero, 500, 1,000, 1,500 and 2,000; inspect matched development curves before selecting a checkpoint. No final animal or public benchmark is used.

Evaluate those frozen checkpoints on **all visible 16×16 queries** of the unchanged 177-section 037 synthetic DEV panel, stratified by raw/exact-black/imperfect-brush and equal-weighted across eight synthetic deformation identities. Report coarse-key top-one recall at 0.5/1.5 mm and corrected-point recall and mean physical error at the same thresholds for prior top-one and physically best-of-eight plane, plus the correction's effect on wrong matches. A useful stage needs corrected-point ≤0.5-mm recall ≥25% on physically best candidates, ≥60% within 1.5 mm, and no material fall in any appearance stratum; **it is still not a pose/GUI success**. If the offset merely moves wrong matches unpredictably, gate or abstain rather than accept a worse point estimate. The 037 panel is DEV reused for design; later independent identities and unseen biological animals are required.

Subsequent stages must aggregate spatially coherent corrected matches, fit pose and nonrigid tissue mapping jointly, and explicitly propagate fitting loss back into the probabilistic coordinate predictor. Otherwise the user's central joint-learning requirement remains unmet. Preserve uncertainty outputs, constraint support and animal provenance but do not numerically calibrate probabilities on synthetic DEV.
