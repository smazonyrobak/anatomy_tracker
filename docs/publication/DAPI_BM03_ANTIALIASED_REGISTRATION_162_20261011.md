# BM03 antialiased registration refinement (162)

`training/register_dapi_bm03_antialiased_162.py` made a bounded, same-modality BM03-to-DAPI-average refinement using only the TRAIN specimen. Its hash-frozen report, selected fixed-to-BM03 pull transform, and orthogonal/oblique visual QC are at `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/dapi_bm03_antialiased_pilot_162`. The original source files were unchanged; no CCF labels or model-training examples were made.

The source volumes were Gaussian-filtered before subsampling to approximately 68.8 × 100 × 68.8 µm, preserving the reconstructed coronal stack's actual 100-µm through-plane sampling. One affine refinement of the previous lower-resolution fit improved same-grid tissue Dice from 0.9743 to 0.9804 and eroded-tissue intensity correlation from 0.7344 to 0.7969. The affine singular values were 1.059, 1.024, and 1.015. Three-plane overlays and two synthetic oblique reslices show improved gross anatomical coincidence, but visible local differences remain in the cortex, ventricles, and tissue margins.

The source [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7359299/) establishes that the individual volumes were reconstructed from physical 100-µm **coronal** sections; the oblique QC panels are interpolated reslices, not physically acquired oblique-section validation. BM03's selected identity sform and unknown units still require the explicit in-memory millimetre/positive-axis/zero-origin assumption; the AP stack is axis 1, but its positive direction and world origin are unverified. The DAPI average includes all 12 source animals, including the sealed secondary specimens. Same-image fit metrics cannot prove correct local anatomical correspondence, and this transform is **not** a validated native-to-CCF map. Landmark review, coordinate-convention verification, and per-animal local-registration QC remain prerequisites for using these volumes as weak pose/deformation training material.
