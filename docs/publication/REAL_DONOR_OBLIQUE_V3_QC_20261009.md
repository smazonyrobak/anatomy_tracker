# TRAIN-donor virtual-oblique v3 data gate

`training/arbitrary_plane_reserved_real_oblique_v3.py` reslices a randomly oriented physical plane from one TRAIN donor's weak-Allen-registered serial image stack and passes that source to the physical-pose-preserving v3 slide artifactizer. It does not refit the pose to the artifactized raster. Each draw independently samples its plane, reflection, 25–100 µm slab, background, raw/exact-black/imperfect-brush mode, and artifact parameters. Source animal/specimen/experiment identities, the anchor section, conservative possible source-section span, and frozen source-file hashes are retained. The one incomplete serial stack (`animal_id=16207`) must be excluded from virtual-cut training rather than interpolated as though its missing section existed.

The bounded TRAIN-only QC at `I:/AnatomyTracker/reports/real_oblique_v3_qc_20261009_001` drew 24 cuts from 24 donors: 24 eligible, valid observed-tissue fractions 0.111–0.655 (median 0.273), 17 raw / 4 exact-black / 3 imperfect-brush. Six examples are shown in `atlas_virtual_artifact_111_grid.png` as atlas at the weak reference pose, donor virtual cut, artifactized input, and the frozen 111 model's guessed atlas plane. Analytic pose-to-source-image coordinate disagreement was at most 0.0032 µm at checked sentinel pixels; the v3 inverse-warp coordinate result disagreed with the source-grid reconstruction by at most 0.0005 µm on valid pixels. The frozen 111 direct predictor missed these six weak reference planes by 6.27–14.29 mm in mean five-point physical discrepancy. This is a genuine acquisition-domain failure, not a calibrated real-oblique accuracy estimate.

The virtual cuts contain coherent gross anatomy at varied orientations, but conspicuous stripes and contrast inherited from trilinearly reslicing approximately 100-µm-spaced coronal acquisitions. The weak Allen affines are not expert truth and the virtual cuts do not provide known *biological* deformation targets. These samples can test modest mixed-domain pose adaptation and image–atlas fit evidence; they cannot substitute for independently acquired oblique slides, animal-separated expert validation, or uncertainty calibration. The current model is not ready for GUI replacement.
