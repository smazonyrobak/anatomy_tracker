# 141: frozen top-four correspondence geometry ceiling

The 140 blind-fit gate failed. On its v4 truth-best near branches, 3,574/8,497 accessible fixed tissue sites had a close coarse key ranked first, 6,760/8,497 had one ranked among four, but only 938/8,497 had a final ≤0.5-mm fine match. A coarse key within 1 mm is not itself a fine key within 0.5 mm. Before changing or training another model, test whether retaining four coarse neighbourhoods can geometrically expose enough precise atlas keys to matter.

Freeze the 132 step-2,000 parent, both 140 step-4,000 heads, the unchanged 248 v4 and 243 v3 eligible synthetic DEV sections, and their already verified blind-16 original branch IDs. Use only the truth-best *original* branch when it is ≤1.5 mm, as an explicitly oracle diagnostic. The model must not receive its true pose, section coordinate labels, or a newly chosen branch. The atlas and source are exactly as in 140; no new training, reranking or appearance changes occur.

At each 16×16 fixed query on surviving observed tissue, compare oracle nearest-supported-key distances for (a) the whole rendered 7×32×32 atlas lattice, (b) the 3×5×5 neighbourhood of the head's highest-scoring coarse key, and (c) the union of the four highest-scoring coarse-key neighbourhoods. Report availability at ≤0.5, ≤0.75 and ≤1.5 mm, using the exact observed-pixel-to-CCF synthetic coordinate only for measurement. Duplicate/clamped keys do not affect a nearest-distance ceiling, but any later scored multi-neighbourhood matcher must deduplicate them before softmax and adjust its dustbin. Also reproduce the 140 hard-argmax fine hit (global ≤1-mm accessibility, no selected-key support requirement), report a separate support-qualified version, and show how often the top-four seeds have atlas support. Keep full-intensity and support-only heads separate on identical sections. Retain section/animal/specimen/experiment IDs, angle, and observed tissue fraction in every row.

The only go/no-go decision is geometric: build a *scored* top-four matcher only if the **full-intensity arm** increases ≤0.5-mm key availability by at least ten absolute percentage points **among sites that have a globally supported ≤0.5-mm key**, and reaches at least 60% of that global ceiling on v4. The support-only arm is a paired descriptive comparator, not a second chance at passing the gate. Report the v3 counterpart and four nearest-cardinal angle bins descriptively; do not tune the threshold after seeing data. Passing means merely that top-four is worth a bounded paired learning test, not that atlas intensity carries anatomical information. Any later test must use the same new draws for fresh full-intensity/support-only arms, report source/atlas-content ablations at fixed support, keep the 140 blind-fit and real-retention gates, and distinguish model-selected from oracle-best branches. If the ceiling fails, do not spend compute on top-four fitting; target descriptor precision and global proposal capture instead. The reused eight synthetic DEV deformation plans are not an independent biological holdout. No physical all-angle claim, calibrated uncertainty, public benchmark or GUI promotion follows.
